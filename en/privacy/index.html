Privacy & cookies

WorldWinePedia has no user accounts or forms, and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we store in your browser

Only three preferences in your browser's local storage: that you confirmed you are of legal age, the shipping country you chose and your language. They never leave your device and you can delete them in your browser settings.

Links to shops

When you click a link to a shop (Amazon or others) you leave this site: from then on that shop's privacy and cookie policy applies.
