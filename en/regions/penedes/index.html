Designation of Origin (DO)

Penedès

Penedès is a designation of origin in Catalonia, between Barcelona and Tarragona, known for its whites from Xarel·lo, Macabeo and Parellada and for a wide range of reds, rosés and sparkling wines. Its territory largely overlaps with the heartland of Cava production.

History

Viticulture in Penedès dates back to antiquity: Iberian and Roman remains related to wine have been found in the area, and in the Middle Ages monasteries and nobles maintained extensive vineyards. In the nineteenth century the region underwent a great expansion of vine growing, encouraged by French demand during the phylloxera crisis, until the pest reached Catalonia as well. Subsequent replanting focused largely on the white varieties that would form the basis of the sparkling wines of Sant Sadurní d'Anoia. In the second half of the twentieth century some pioneering wineries introduced French varieties and modern techniques, such as fermentation in temperature-controlled stainless steel tanks, which had a great influence on Spanish wine. Vilafranca del Penedès, the area's capital, is home to an important museum devoted to wine culture.

Territory and climate

The designation extends across the provinces of Barcelona and Tarragona, between the Mediterranean coast and the pre-coastal range. Three zones are traditionally distinguished: Penedès Marítim or Baix Penedès, near the sea and warmer; Penedès Central, the intermediate and largest zone; and Alt Penedès or Upper Penedès, higher and cooler, next to the mountains. The climate is Mediterranean, with mild winters and warm summers, gentler on the coast and cooler with greater temperature contrasts at altitude.

Soils

Calcareous soils predominate, with loamy to clayey textures and good water-holding capacity, along with areas with a greater presence of sand and gravel.

Grapes and styles

The traditional white trio consists of Xarel·lo, considered the area's emblematic variety, Macabeo and Parellada, the last more common in the higher zones. Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Riesling, White Grenache, Moscatel and Malvasía are also grown, the latter used in Sitges for a traditional sweet wine. Red varieties include Tempranillo (Ull de Llebre), Grenache, Carignan, Monastrell, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Pinot Noir, Syrah and the native Sumoll. Producers make young and aged whites, rosés, reds and traditional-method sparkling wines under the Clàssic Penedès label.

Relationship with Cava

Much of the vineyard area and many of the wineries of Penedès also lie within the production area of Cava, whose capital is Sant Sadurní d'Anoia. As a result, many wineries make both still wines under the Penedès DO and sparkling wines under the Cava DO. In recent years the designation has paid increasing attention to organic viticulture and to Xarel·lo as a variety capable of producing still whites with ageing potential.
