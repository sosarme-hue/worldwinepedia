Vacqueyras

Vacqueyras, Gigondas's neighbour in the Vaucluse, has been a Southern Rhône cru since 1990. It produces mainly generous, spicy Grenache and Syrah reds, along with smaller amounts of rosé and white.

Location

Vacqueyras lies in the Vaucluse department, north-east of Avignon, between the river Ouvèze and the Dentelles de Montmirail. The appellation covers the commune of Vacqueyras and part of neighbouring Sarrians. To the north it borders Gigondas, with which it shares landscape and grape varieties, although most of its vineyard lies at lower altitude, on terraces and stony plains at the foot of the hills. The village is traditionally associated with the medieval troubadour Raimbaut de Vaqueiras, who according to tradition was born there.

History

For decades the wines of Vacqueyras were sold as Côtes du Rhône and, from the mid-twentieth century, as Côtes du Rhône Villages, initially with the village name added. In 1990 they obtained their own appellation, which made Vacqueyras one of the crus of the Southern Rhône, alongside Châteauneuf-du-Pape, Gigondas, Tavel and Lirac, to which other villages have since been added.

Climate and soils

The climate is Mediterranean, with hot, dry summers, mild winters and rainfall concentrated in autumn and spring. The mistral often blows from the north, cooling the vineyard and reducing the risk of disease. The soils consist mainly of ancient alluvial terraces, with abundant rounded pebbles mixed with red clay, which retain heat and drain well. In the eastern part, closer to the Dentelles, there are sandy, limestone and marl soils, generally cooler.

Grapes and styles

The reds, which account for the great majority of production, are based on Grenache, accompanied by Syrah and Mourvèdre, and by smaller proportions of other Rhône varieties such as Cinsault. They are deeply coloured wines with aromas of ripe black fruit, spice, liquorice and garrigue herbs, and a generous structure. The rosés, made from similar varieties, are fruity and medium-bodied. The few whites come from White Grenache, Clairette, Bourboulenc, Roussanne, Marsanne and Viognier, and are usually broad and floral.

Comparison and drinking

Vacqueyras is often compared with Gigondas: it is usually regarded as somewhat more approachable in its youth and more moderately priced, although the best estates make wines capable of ageing for a decade. Many are aged in tank or large casks to preserve the fruit, and some in barrel. It is an appellation with many family growers and an important cooperative.
