Terra Alta

Terra Alta is a designation of origin in the far west of the province of Tarragona, around Gandesa. It is particularly known for its White Grenache whites, although it also makes Grenache and Carignan reds and traditional rancio and sweet wines.

History

Viticulture in Terra Alta has a long tradition, linked in the Middle Ages to the military orders, such as the Knights Templar, who held land in the area. In the early twentieth century the cooperative movement drove the construction of large wineries, among them those of Gandesa and El Pinell de Brai, designed by the architect Cèsar Martinell and known as cathedrals of wine for their Modernista architecture. In 1938 the area was the scene of the Battle of the Ebro, which left a deep mark on its villages. For much of the twentieth century production went into bulk wine; in recent decades estate bottling and a focus on White Grenache have given the area a clear identity.

Territory and climate

The designation occupies a high, rugged plateau at the western end of Catalonia, next to the border with Aragón, between the Ebro valley and the Pàndols and Cavalls ranges. It includes municipalities such as Gandesa, Batea, Corbera d'Ebre, Vilalba dels Arcs, Horta de Sant Joan and El Pinell de Brai. The climate is Mediterranean with a marked continental influence: hot, dry summers, cold winters and little rainfall. Two winds characterise the area: the dry, cold cierzo from the north-west, and the more humid garbí, which blows in from the sea to the south and moderates summer temperatures.

Soils

Calcareous soils predominate, poor in organic matter and of variable texture, with some clayey areas and others that are sandier. Their low fertility, combined with summer drought, keeps yields down.

Grapes and styles

White Grenache is the area's most representative variety. It gives medium to full-bodied whites with aromas of white fruit, flowers and Mediterranean herbs and an unctuous texture, suitable both for drinking young and for barrel fermentation or ageing. Macabeo, Parellada, Moscatel and Chardonnay are also grown. Among the reds, Grenache, Garnacha Peluda, Carignan, Tempranillo, Syrah, Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot stand out. Besides whites, reds and rosés, local tradition includes oxidatively aged rancio wines and mistelas.

Landscape

The Terra Alta landscape combines vineyards with olive and almond trees on rolling land surrounded by mountain ranges. The Modernista wineries, the remains of the Civil War and the stone villages are part of a heritage that has become integrated into the area's wine tourism.
