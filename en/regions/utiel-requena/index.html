Utiel-Requena

Utiel-Requena is a designation of origin in the interior of the province of Valencia, located on a high plateau with a continental climate. It is the heartland of the Bobal grape, used to make reds and rosés.

History

Viticulture on the Requena and Utiel plateau is very old: archaeological remains from the Iberian period related to winemaking have been found in the area. For centuries the district, located between Castile and the Valencian coast, produced wine for local consumption and for sale in Valencia and other cities. Beneath its old town, Requena preserves a network of excavated caves that were used as cellars and storerooms. In the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries the area specialised in deeply coloured bulk wines, including so-called doble pasta wines, made with a high proportion of grape skins and much prized for adding colour and body to other wines. In recent decades the modernisation of wineries and the appreciation of old-vine Bobal have transformed the designation's image.

Territory and climate

The designation occupies the western end of the province of Valencia, around Utiel, Requena, Caudete de las Fuentes, Venta del Moro and other municipalities, on a plateau bounded by the Cabriel and Magro rivers. The vineyards lie roughly between 600 and 900 metres above sea level. The climate is continental with some Mediterranean influence: cold winters with frequent frosts, warm summers, little rainfall and a marked difference between day and night temperatures. Late frosts and hailstorms are common risks.

Soils

Brown calcareous soils predominate, poor in organic matter, with clayey areas and others that are sandier or stonier, especially on the terraces near the rivers.

Grapes and styles

Bobal is the dominant variety. It is a deeply coloured red grape with good acidity, giving reds with red and black fruit, floral notes and pronounced tannins, as well as fresh, brightly coloured rosés. Tempranillo, Grenache, Garnacha Tintorera, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Syrah are also grown, and among the whites Macabeo, Merseguera, Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc. Producers make young and oak-aged reds, rosés and whites. Part of the territory, in the municipality of Requena, is also included in the Cava production area, and traditional-method sparkling wines are made in the district.

Old-vine Bobal

A significant part of the vineyard consists of old Bobal vines, bush-trained and dry-farmed. These low-yielding vines have become the basis for more concentrated wines and a central element of the designation's identity.
