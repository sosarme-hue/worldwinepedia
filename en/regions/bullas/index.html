Designation of Origin (DO)

Bullas

Bullas is a designation of origin in the north-west of the Region of Murcia, in mountainous country of medium to high altitude. Its main variety is Monastrell, used for reds and rosés of Mediterranean character.

History

Vines have been grown in north-western Murcia since Antiquity, and Roman remains linked to wine production have been found in the district. For centuries the town of Bullas and the surrounding villages lived largely from viticulture, and old traditional cellars with large earthenware jars still survive; one of them now houses the town's Wine Museum. As in other parts of the south-east, during the twentieth century production was geared mainly to bulk wine and the cooperatives. The designation of origin was recognised in the 1990s, and since then a group of wineries has committed to bottled wines that express the character of high-altitude Monastrell.

Territory and climate

The designation comprises Bullas and several nearby municipalities in north-western Murcia, such as Cehegín, Calasparra, Caravaca de la Cruz, Mula and Moratalla, among others. It is an area of valleys and slopes surrounded by mountain ranges, with vineyards lying roughly between 400 and 900 metres above sea level. The climate is Mediterranean with a continental influence, cooler than on the Murcian coast: summers are hot and dry, winters cool, rainfall scant and the difference between day and night temperatures notable, especially in the highest areas to the west.

Soils

Brown limestone soils predominate, generally poor and well drained, with some clay and, in places, sand or stones on the surface. The vineyards often appear on terraces and in small plots among almond and olive trees.

Grape varieties

The main variety is Monastrell, which occupies the great majority of the vineyard and is often grown as unirrigated bush vines. It is accompanied by Tempranillo, Syrah, Garnacha, Garnacha Tintorera, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Petit Verdot. The whites, not widely planted, include Macabeo, Airén, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Moscatel.

Styles

The designation mainly produces reds from Monastrell, on its own or blended, with ripe black fruit, notes of Mediterranean herbs and firm tannins, in young and barrel-aged versions. The altitude of some vineyards makes it possible to obtain fresher wines than in lower areas. Bullas also has a tradition of Monastrell rosés, fruity and deep in colour. A few whites are also made. It is a small designation, with a limited number of wineries, combining cooperatives with quality-minded family projects.
