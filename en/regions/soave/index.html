DOC and DOCG

Soave

Soave is one of Italy's best-known white wines, made mainly from Garganega in the hills east of Verona. The area also produces Soave Superiore and the sweet Recioto di Soave, both DOCG.

History

The town of Soave, overlooked by a medieval castle associated with the Scaligeri, the lords of Verona, is the centre of an area with a long tradition of vine-growing. Soave was recognised as a DOC in 1968 and over the following decades became one of Italy's most exported white wines. This success led to vineyards spreading onto the plain, creating a sharp difference between wines from the plain and those from the hills. To protect the latter, Recioto di Soave was granted DOCG status in 1998 and Soave Superiore in 2001. In 2018 the FAO included Soave's traditional vineyards among the Globally Important Agricultural Heritage Systems.

Territory and climate

The designation lies east of Verona, between the foothills of the Lessini mountains and the Adige plain. Soave Classico, the historic core, occupies the hills of the municipalities of Soave and Monteforte d'Alpone; the Colli Scaligeri subzone covers other hills within the designation. The climate is moderately continental, with cool winters, warm summers and good ventilation on the slopes, while the plain is warmer and more fertile.

Soils

The eastern hills, particularly the classic zone, have dark volcanic soils rich in basalt, whereas calcareous soils predominate in the western sector. Alluvial soils are found on the plain. Many producers believe that the volcanic soils give wines with marked tension and mineral notes.

Grapes

Garganega must make up at least 70% of the wine. It is a late-ripening variety, traditionally trained on pergolas, that gives wines with aromas of almond, white flowers, apple and citrus. It may be complemented by Trebbiano di Soave, a variety also known as Verdicchio, and by Chardonnay.

Styles

Soave and Soave Classico are dry, fresh, medium-bodied whites, although the best, especially from hillside vineyards, can develop for several years in bottle, acquiring notes of honey and nuts. Soave Superiore, from the hills only, requires lower yields and longer ageing. Recioto di Soave is a sweet wine made from Garganega grapes dried for several months, and may also be made in a sparkling version. The area also produces sparkling wines under the Lessini Durello DOC, from the Durella grape.
