Food and wine pairing

Choose what you are eating and we suggest wine styles, grapes and specific wines from the encyclopedia.

What are you eating?

🥩 Red meat and game

Full-bodied reds with some oak ageing: their tannins soften against the protein and fat of the meat. Structured reds for roasts and game; a medium-bodied red also works with a grilled steak.

Grapes that work well: Tempranillo · Cabernet Sauvignon · Syrah (Shiraz) · Nebbiolo · Malbec · Monastrell (Mourvèdre)

🍖 Pork and cured meats

Light, fruity reds or a rosé, which match the fat without overpowering it. With Iberian ham, a fino or manzanilla also works very well.

Grapes that work well: Grenache · Pinot Noir · Mencía · Tempranillo

🍗 Poultry

Full-bodied whites or light reds. With roast chicken, an oaked Chardonnay or a Pinot Noir; with duck, a slightly more structured red.

Grapes that work well: Chardonnay · Pinot Noir · Godello · Grenache

Grapes that work well: Albariño · Verdejo · Chardonnay · Godello · Sauvignon Blanc

🦐 Seafood

Fresh, saline whites, or a sparkling wine. The acidity and sea-breeze notes of Albariño or Muscadet suit oysters, clams and prawns very well.

Grapes that work well: Albariño · Xarel·lo · Sauvignon Blanc · Chardonnay

🍝 Pasta and pizza

With tomato sauces, reds with good acidity such as Sangiovese or Barbera, which match the acidity of tomato. With creamy or vegetable pasta, a fuller white.

Grapes that work well: Sangiovese · Grenache · Nebbiolo · Chardonnay

🍤 Fried food and tapas

Dry fortified wines (fino, manzanilla) or a sparkling wine: their acidity and bubbles cleanse the palate after each fried bite.

Grapes that work well: Palomino · Macabeo (Viura) · Xarel·lo · Chardonnay

🌶️ Spicy and Asian food

Aromatic whites, ideally off-dry, which soften the heat. Avoid very tannic, high-alcohol reds, which make it hotter.

Grapes that work well: Riesling · Gewürztraminer · Chenin Blanc · Muscat

🧀 Cheese

It depends on the cheese: full-bodied whites for fresh, mild cheeses; reds or fortified wines for aged ones; and a sweet wine with blue cheese.

Grapes that work well: Chardonnay · Tempranillo · Palomino · Pedro Ximénez

🍰 Desserts

A sweet wine, at least as sweet as the dessert: Muscat with fruit and cream desserts; Pedro Ximénez or Port with chocolate.

Grapes that work well: Muscat · Pedro Ximénez · Riesling
