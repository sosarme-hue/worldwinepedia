Domaine des Roches Neuves

Saumur-Champigny estate run by Thierry Germain, farmed biodynamically and known for its single-plot Cabernet Franc and Saumur Chenin Blanc.

Domaine des Roches Neuves is located in Varrains, in the heart of the Saumur-Champigny appellation, in the central Loire. In the early 1990s it was acquired by Thierry Germain, who came from a family of Bordeaux winegrowers, settled in the area and became one of its best-known producers.

In his early years Germain was influenced by the Foucault brothers of Clos Rougeard, leading figures of Saumur-Champigny. Over time he adopted biodynamic farming and very meticulous vineyard work, aiming for fresh, fine wines that express their site, far from heavy extraction and obvious oak.

The vineyard lies on tuffeau, the soft white limestone typical of the Saumur area, sometimes covered with clay and sand. Cabernet Franc is the main variety and gives reds ranging from the fresh, fruity Cuvée Domaine to single-plot selections such as Terres Chaudes, Les Mémoires, La Marginale and Clos de l'Échelier. The estate also makes Chenin Blanc whites in the Saumur appellation, such as L'Insolite and L'Échelier, with a taut, mineral profile.

Roches Neuves is today one of the estates that best represent the renewal of Saumur wines.
