Teliani Valley

One of Georgia’s leading wineries, based in Telavi, Kakheti. It mainly makes wines from the region’s classic appellations, such as Mukuzani, Tsinandali and Kindzmarauli, from Saperavi and Rkatsiteli.

Teliani Valley is one of Georgia’s largest wineries and one of the most present on international markets. It is based in Telavi, the capital of Kakheti, a town with a long winemaking tradition in the Alazani valley, at the foot of the Gombori range.

The winery works with its own vineyards and with grapes from growers in different parts of Kakheti. The main varieties are the region’s two great grapes, the red Saperavi and the white Rkatsiteli, together with Mtsvane Kakhuri and other local varieties, as well as some international ones.

Its range covers many of Kakheti’s classic appellations. The reds include Mukuzani, an aged dry Saperavi, Napareuli and Kindzmarauli, the latter a naturally semi-sweet Saperavi red. Among the whites, the most notable is Tsinandali, a dry blend of Rkatsiteli and Mtsvane. The winery makes most of its wines by the European method, in stainless steel tanks and in some cases with barrel ageing, and also produces qvevri wines following the Kakhetian tradition. Its style aims to offer an approachable, consistent version of the region’s great wines, which has made it a common gateway to Georgian wine for many foreign consumers.

Like many Kakheti wineries, it relies to a large extent on grapes from small growers in the valley’s villages, who still cultivate the traditional varieties on family plots.
