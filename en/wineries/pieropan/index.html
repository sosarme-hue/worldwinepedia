Winery

Pieropan

Family winery in Soave founded in 1880, a benchmark for Soave Classico and a pioneer of the area’s single-vineyard whites with Calvarino and La Rocca.

The Pieropan winery was founded in 1880 in the village of Soave, east of Verona, by Leonildo Pieropan, a doctor by profession. For several generations the family made wine in the town’s historic centre, at the foot of its medieval castle.

It was Leonida “Nino” Pieropan who, from the 1970s onwards, placed the winery among the most respected in the area. At a time when Soave was associated above all with high-volume wines, he focused on the hillside vineyards of the classic zone and on bottling his best plots separately: Calvarino, on soils of volcanic origin, and La Rocca, on limestone soils below the castle. Calvarino was first released in 1971 and is considered one of the first single-vineyard Soaves.

The leading variety is Garganega, joined in some wines by Trebbiano di Soave. The vines are traditionally trained on the Veronese pergola, and the family has adopted organic viticulture.

The winery is now run by Nino’s sons, Andrea and Dario Pieropan. The range also includes the entry-level Soave Classico and a sweet Recioto di Soave, and the family also makes reds in Valpolicella.
