Bodegas Matarromera

Winery in Valbuena de Duero founded in 1988 by Carlos Moro, the origin of a wine group with wineries in several Castilla y León appellations, including Ribera del Duero, Rueda, Toro and Cigales.

Bodegas Matarromera was founded in 1988 by agricultural engineer Carlos Moro in Valbuena de Duero, in the province of Valladolid, on the banks of the Duero in the western part of the Ribera del Duero appellation. From the outset the winery was conceived as a project for aged wines based on Tinto Fino, the local name for Tempranillo. The project coincided with the years in which Ribera del Duero was establishing itself as one of Spain’s great red wine regions, and the winery grew at the same pace as the appellation.

Building on Matarromera, Carlos Moro developed a business group with wineries in other Castilla y León appellations: Emina, in Ribera del Duero and Rueda; Valdelosfrailes, in Cigales; and Cyan, in Toro. The group has also stood out for its research, which has led to dealcoholised wines and grape-derived products such as extracts for cosmetics.

Matarromera’s vineyards lie on terraces of the Duero, with soils of rounded pebbles, sand and clay, and endure the extreme continental climate of the plateau: long, cold winters, hot summers and sharp temperature differences between day and night.

Its range includes Melior, a red with a short spell in oak, the Crianza, the Reserva and the Gran Reserva, as well as selection wines. The house style is classic Ribera: ripe black fruit, structure and well-integrated barrel ageing.
