El Coto de Rioja

Winery in Oyón, Rioja Alavesa, founded in 1970, whose reds El Coto Crianza and Coto de Imaz Reserva are among the best-selling Riojas in Spain.

El Coto de Rioja was founded in 1970 in Oyón, a village in Rioja Alavesa on the north bank of the Ebro, opposite Logroño. It was born during the boom in bottled Rioja, when demand for aged wines was growing fast in Spain, and from the start it focused on making classic-style reds with wide distribution.

Its best-known wine is El Coto Crianza, a Tempranillo aged in American oak barrels that has become one of the most familiar Rioja labels in Spanish bars, restaurants and supermarkets. It is joined by Coto de Imaz, the house brand for its Reservas and Gran Reservas, which receive longer ageing. The range is completed by young whites and rosés and some selection reds.

The winery has its own vineyards in different parts of the appellation and supplements its production with grapes from growers in the region. Tempranillo is the basis of practically all its reds, sometimes with contributions from other authorised varieties. Its premises in Oyón house a large barrel hall, needed for the long ageing required by the Reserva and Gran Reserva categories.

The El Coto style represents traditional American-oak Rioja: ripe red fruit, notes of vanilla and coconut, soft tannins and an easy-drinking profile, designed for everyday dining.
