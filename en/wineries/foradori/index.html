Foradori

Family winery in Mezzolombardo, Trentino, run by Elisabetta Foradori and her family, a benchmark for the Teroldego variety and biodynamic viticulture in Italy.

Foradori is a family winery located in Mezzolombardo, in the Campo Rotaliano, an alluvial plain north of Trento where the river Noce meets the Adige valley. This area is the home of Teroldego, a native red variety of Trentino that gives deeply coloured wines with dark fruit.

The key figure of the house is Elisabetta Foradori, who took over the family winery very young, in the 1980s, after her father’s death. Convinced of Teroldego’s potential, she spent years restoring its genetic diversity through massal selection of old vines, as opposed to the high-yielding clones that had spread in the area. She later converted the vineyard to biodynamic farming, and today her children are actively involved in running the estate.

The vineyards lie on alluvial gravel, sand and stones in the Campo Rotaliano, and on slopes in other nearby areas. Besides Teroldego, the family grows white varieties such as Nosiola and Manzoni Bianco. Many of its wines are sold under the Vigneti delle Dolomiti geographical indication.

One hallmark of the house is the use of clay amphorae to ferment and age some wines, such as the single-plot Teroldegos Morei and Sgarzon. Its range also includes the red Foradori and Granato, considered its most ambitious wine. The winery’s style aims for lively, precise wines that are true to their origin.
