Winery

Señorío de Sarría

Large agricultural estate in Puente la Reina, beside the Way of St James, with vineyards in Navarra's Valdizarbe and a range known for its rosés and single-vineyard reds.

Señorío de Sarría is a large estate in the municipality of Puente la Reina, a Navarrese town crossed by the Way of St James, where its two main routes meet. The property, of ancient origin, combines vineyards, woodland, crops and a small cluster of buildings, and has been devoted to viticulture and winemaking for decades.

Vineyards

The vineyards lie in the Valdizarbe sub-zone, in the centre of the Navarra D.O., a transitional area between the humid north of Navarra and the drier Ribera. The climate has Atlantic and continental influences, with cold winters and moderate summers, and the soils are clay and limestone. The estate is divided into numbered plots that give their names to several of its wines. Garnacha, Tempranillo, Graciano and Cariñena (known locally as Mazuelo) are grown alongside Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Chardonnay.

Wines

The winery makes a broad range, from young wines to reservas, but is particularly known for its series of single-vineyard wines identified by plot number. Among them stands out Viñedo Nº 5, a Garnacha rosé that represents the traditional style of Navarra rosé, fresh and fruity, made by the saignée method. Other wines in the series focus on varieties such as Graciano or Cariñena. The house style seeks fruity, balanced wines with the freshness typical of this part of Navarra.
