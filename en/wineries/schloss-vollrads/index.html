Schloss Vollrads

One of Germany’s oldest wine estates, in the Rheingau, with records of wine sales dating back to the 13th century, devoted exclusively to Riesling.

Schloss Vollrads stands on the hills above Winkel, in the municipality of Oestrich-Winkel, in the Rheingau. It is one of the oldest wine estates in the world with continuous activity: the earliest known document recording the sale of its wine dates from 1211. The castle, with its moated medieval tower and baroque manor house, is one of the most recognisable historic ensembles in the region.

For centuries the property belonged to the Greiffenclau family, one of the oldest in the Rheingau. After the death of the last count of the family in 1997, the estate passed to a regional bank, which undertook its modernisation. Since then Schloss Vollrads has regained its place as one of the region’s leading benchmarks, after a period of financial difficulty.

The estate is devoted exclusively to Riesling, grown on south-facing slopes above the Rhine, on loess, marl and quartzite soils. It makes wines in a wide range of styles, from dry to sweet, including Kabinett, Spätlese, Auslese and selected-harvest wines in vintages that allow them. Historically, the estate was known for indicating quality levels by means of coloured capsules.

Schloss Vollrads also hosts cultural and gastronomic events, and its restaurant and shop allow visitors to discover the estate and its wines.

Visits & wine tourism

The estate has a restaurant, wine shop and events space, and offers tastings and tours.
