Niepoort

Family house of Dutch origin founded in 1842, renowned for its Colheita and Vintage Ports and a pioneer of modern Douro reds and whites with wines such as Redoma.

Niepoort was founded in 1842 by Franciscus Marius van der Niepoort, of Dutch origin, and has remained in the hands of the same family for five generations. Unlike the large British houses, for a long time it was a firm of modest size, focused on long-aged Ports, and it became particularly famous for its Colheitas and its Garrafeira Ports, a house speciality in which the wine, after a period in wood, ages for years in large glass demijohns.

Under Dirk Niepoort, the company extended its activity into still Douro wines. Redoma was born in the early 1990s, followed by other reds such as Batuta and Charme, which helped to show the region’s potential for still wines in a fresh, elegant style. The house winery is at Quinta de Nápoles, in the valley of the Tedo, a tributary of the Douro, and works with its own vineyards and with old vines belonging to local growers.

Niepoort has also developed projects in other Portuguese regions, such as Bairrada and the Dão, and collaborations with producers in other countries. Its style is marked by a search for freshness, acidity and balance, in both its Ports and its still wines, with a measured use of new oak.
