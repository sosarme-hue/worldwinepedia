Winery

Bellavista

Franciacorta house based in Erbusco, created by Vittorio Moretti, one of the brands that has done most to build the reputation of the area’s traditional-method sparkling wines.

Bellavista is located in Erbusco, in the hills of Franciacorta, between the city of Brescia and Lake Iseo in Lombardy. Its name comes from the hill of the same name, which overlooks the surrounding landscape. The winery was created in the late 1970s by Vittorio Moretti, a construction entrepreneur, at a time when Franciacorta was beginning to define itself as an area for quality sparkling wine.

For decades winemaking was led by the oenologist Mattia Vezzola, who gave the house a recognisable style based on elegance, fine bubbles and the use of reserve wines. The sparkling wines are made by the traditional method, with second fermentation in bottle, from Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, to which Pinot Bianco is added in some wines. Part of the base wines is fermented and rested in small oak barrels.

The range includes Alma, its most widely available Franciacorta, the Satèn, with softer bubbles, the rosé, the Teatro alla Scala cuvée and the Vittorio Moretti Riserva, made only in the best vintages. Bellavista belongs to the Terra Moretti group, which owns other estates in Franciacorta and Tuscany and is now led by Francesca Moretti, the founder’s daughter.

The cellars and vineyards can be visited by appointment.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery offers guided cellar tours and tastings by appointment.
