Domaine Tempier

Historic Bandol estate in Provence, owned by the Peyraud family, closely linked to the championing of Mourvèdre and the creation of the appellation.

Domaine Tempier lies in Le Plan du Castellet, inland within the Bandol area on the Provençal coast between Marseille and Toulon. The estate belonged to the Tempier family and became known in the 20th century thanks to Lucien Peyraud, who married Lucie Tempier, known as “Lulu”, in the 1930s.

Lucien Peyraud was one of the driving forces behind the Bandol appellation and a staunch advocate of Mourvèdre, a late-ripening, demanding variety that finds one of its best terroirs on the sunny, breezy terraces of the area, close to the sea. Today the rules require Bandol reds to contain a majority of this grape and to be aged in wood for an extended period.

The Peyraud family table, with Lulu’s Provençal cooking, brought the house international fame among wine writers and importers. After the founding generation, the children and later other managers have maintained the domaine’s style.

Tempier makes a classic Bandol red and several single-vineyard cuvées, such as La Migoua, La Tourtine and Cabassaou, as well as a highly regarded rosé and a white. Its reds, aged in large oak casks, are characterised by structure, notes of Mediterranean herbs and spice, and great ageing potential, often needing several years in bottle before they open up.
