Delas Frères

Rhône house with 19th-century origins, part of the Louis Roederer group, with vineyards in Hermitage, Côte-Rôtie, Saint-Joseph and Crozes-Hermitage and a broad range from the south of the valley.

Delas Frères is one of the historic houses of the Rhône valley. Its origins go back to the first half of the 19th century, in the Tournon-sur-Rhône and Tain-l’Hermitage area, and the business took the name of the Delas family, who ran it for several generations. In the last quarter of the 20th century it passed to the Champagne house Deutz and, with it, to the Louis Roederer group, which has invested in renovating the vineyards and the winery.

Delas combines the roles of grower and négociant. It owns or farms vines on some of the best slopes of the northern valley, notably on the hill of Hermitage, where its wines from the lieux-dits Les Bessards and L’Ermite stand out, as well as in Côte-Rôtie, Saint-Joseph and Crozes-Hermitage. For its southern Rhône wines, such as its Côtes du Rhône, Ventoux, Gigondas and Châteauneuf-du-Pape, it also works with grapes from partner growers.

Among its best-known labels are the Côtes du Rhône Saint-Esprit, widely available in shops, the Crozes-Hermitage Les Launes and the Hermitage Marquise de la Tourette, made in both red and white.

The house style aims for precise, balanced wines in which northern Syrah shows defined fruit and spicy notes, while the southern wines emphasise Grenache and a fruity, approachable expression.
