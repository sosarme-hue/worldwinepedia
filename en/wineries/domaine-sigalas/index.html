Domaine Sigalas

A Santorini winery founded in 1991 by Paris Sigalas, near Oia in the north of the island. It is one of the benchmarks for the island’s Assyrtiko whites and also makes Vinsanto and reds from local grapes.

Domaine Sigalas was founded in 1991 by Paris Sigalas, a native of Santorini, and its winery is near Oia, in the north of the island. It has since become one of Santorini’s benchmark wineries and has helped make the island’s wines known internationally.

The winery works with its own vineyards and with grapes from local growers spread across different parts of Santorini. Many of these vines are very old, grow on their own roots in the volcanic soils and are trained in the traditional basket shape (kouloura) to protect them from the wind. The main variety is Assyrtiko, accompanied by the whites Athiri and Aidani and the reds Mandilaria and Mavrotragano.

Its range centres on dry whites from the Santorini PDO, such as the Santorini Assyrtiko, and single-vineyard wines such as Kavalieros, from a specific Assyrtiko plot. It also makes an oak-aged Assyrtiko in the traditional Nykteri style, sweet Vinsanto from sun-dried grapes and reds from Mavrotragano and Mandilaria, bottled as Cyclades geographical indication wines. The house style favours the expression of Assyrtiko’s acidity and saltiness, with wines that improve with several years in bottle.

Like the island’s other producers, Sigalas works with the low yields typical of Santorini’s old vines and with a vineyard area that has shrunk under pressure from tourism, which makes its wines a limited production.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery welcomes visitors and offers tastings of its wines.
