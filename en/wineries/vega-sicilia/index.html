Bodegas Vega Sicilia

Historic Ribera del Duero winery, founded in 1864, whose red Único is one of Spain’s most celebrated wines.

The history of Vega Sicilia begins in 1864, when Eloy Lecanda acquired the estate in Valbuena de Duero, in the province of Valladolid, and planted, alongside the local variety, vines brought from Bordeaux such as Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Malbec. In the early twentieth century the winery began making the long-aged wines that would make the house famous, long before the Ribera del Duero Designation of Origin was created in 1982.

Since 1982 the winery has belonged to the Álvarez family, which has preserved the house style and built a group with other wineries: Alión, also in Ribera del Duero; Pintia, in Toro; Macán, in Rioja, in partnership with the Rothschild family; and Oremus, in Tokaj (Hungary).

The estate vineyard lies near the Duero, on soils of varied composition, dominated by Tinto Fino (the local name for Tempranillo) together with Cabernet Sauvignon and, to a lesser extent, Merlot. The winery has its own cooperage.

Its main wines are Único, released after very long ageing in barrel, large vat and bottle; Valbuena 5º, sold in its fifth year; and Único Reserva Especial, a blend of several vintages. They are wines of great structure and longevity, highly sought after by collectors.
