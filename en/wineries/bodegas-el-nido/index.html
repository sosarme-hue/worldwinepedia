Winery

Bodegas El Nido

Jumilla winery created by the Gil family and Australian winemaker Chris Ringland, known for its concentrated reds Clio and El Nido.

Bodegas El Nido was founded in the early twenty-first century in Jumilla as a joint project between the Gil family, owners of Bodegas Juan Gil, and Australian winemaker Chris Ringland, known for his highly concentrated Barossa Valley Shiraz. Its aim was to make reds of the highest ambition in Jumilla from old vines with very low yields, in a style designed to compete in international markets.

Vineyards

The winery stands at the Cañada del Judío site, in the municipality of Jumilla, surrounded by hills. The climate is continental and semi-arid, with very hot summers, cold winters and scant rainfall, and the vineyards lie on calcareous, stony soils of low fertility. The winery works with old-vine Monastrell, the area's traditional variety, and with Cabernet Sauvignon, which reaches very high ripeness in this climate. Yields are very low and harvesting is done by hand.

Wines and style

The range consists of just two reds. Clio, named after the Greek muse of history, is a blend dominated by Monastrell. El Nido, the wine that gives the winery its name, is based on Cabernet Sauvignon, with a share of Monastrell. Both are aged for an extended period in new oak barrels. The style is very concentrated, with ripe fruit, deep colour and high alcohol, an opulent interpretation of the reds of south-eastern Spain.
