Château d’Esclans

Côtes de Provence estate relaunched by Sacha Lichine from 2006, creator of Whispering Angel rosé; since 2019 it has been majority-owned by Moët Hennessy.

Château d’Esclans lies in La Motte, in the Argens valley, inland in the Var département. In 2006 it was bought by Sacha Lichine, son of the writer and wine merchant Alexis Lichine, with the aim of making top-end Provence rosés. To lead winemaking he brought in the Bordeaux winemaker Patrick Léon.

The estate became internationally known through Whispering Angel, a Côtes de Provence rosé that became one of the most representative wines of the worldwide boom in Provence rosé from the 2010s onwards. The range also includes Rock Angel and the more ambitious rosés Les Clans and Garrus, the latter from a plot of old Grenache vines and aged in barrel.

The main varieties are Grenache, Cinsault and Rolle (Vermentino), together with Syrah and other Mediterranean grapes. Some of the fruit comes from the estate’s own vineyards and some from local growers the house works with.

In 2019 Moët Hennessy, the wines and spirits division of LVMH, acquired a majority stake in the company, and Sacha Lichine remained involved. The house style is characterised by very pale, dry, fresh rosés and, in the top cuvées, by greater texture and ageing potential.

The estate lies inland, away from the coast.
