Domaine Bernard Baudry

A family estate in Cravant-les-Coteaux, in the Chinon appellation, known for single-site Cabernet Francs that reflect the area’s different soils.

Domaine Bernard Baudry is located in Cravant-les-Coteaux, east of the town of Chinon, in Touraine, in the Loire Valley. Founded by Bernard Baudry, who trained as an oenologist before setting up as a grower, the estate is now also run by his son Matthieu, and both have continued the same approach: making wines that show the differences between the soils of the appellation.

In Chinon, the main red variety is Cabernet Franc, known locally as Breton. The estate’s vineyards are divided between the sand and gravel terraces near the Vienne river, which give lighter, fruitier reds, and the clay and limestone slopes (the tuffeau of Touraine), which produce more structured, age-worthy wines. The estate also makes a small amount of white from Chenin Blanc.

The vineyards are farmed organically and winemaking is low-intervention, with ageing that does not seek to mark the wine with oak. The range runs from the entry-level red, designed to be drunk young, to single-site wines such as Les Grézeaux, Clos Guillot and La Croix Boissée, which are considered among the benchmarks of the appellation and benefit from several years of cellaring.

Alongside names such as Charles Joguet, Olga Raffault and Philippe Alliet, the estate is among those that have helped spread the image of Chinon as an appellation for reds capable of ageing.
