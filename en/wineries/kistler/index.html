Kistler Vineyards

A Sonoma winery in California founded in 1978 and devoted almost exclusively to Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, with highly reputed single-vineyard wines.

Kistler Vineyards was founded in 1978 in Sonoma County, north of San Francisco, by Steve Kistler and Mark Bixler. From the outset, the winery specialised in Burgundy’s two great varieties, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, and became one of the benchmarks for Burgundian-style Californian whites.

Sonoma offers very favourable conditions for these varieties: fog and breezes from the Pacific cool the vineyards, especially in areas such as the Russian River Valley and the Sonoma Coast, allowing slow ripening and the preservation of acidity. Kistler works with its own vineyards and others selected in these areas, and makes numerous single-vineyard wines alongside blends from several sites.

In the cellar, the house applies Burgundy-inspired techniques, such as fermentation with native yeasts, barrel ageing on the lees and minimal intervention. Kistler’s Chardonnays are known for their broad texture and complexity, while its Pinot Noirs aim for precise fruit and finesse.

The winery is regarded as one of the houses that has done most for the international reputation of California Chardonnay, and it remains focused on a small number of varieties and sites.

Its range combines site-specific wines with blends under broader appellations, which offer a first approach to the house style. Both lines share the same emphasis on balance, texture and freshness rather than sheer power.
