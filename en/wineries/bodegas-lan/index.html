Bodegas LAN

Winery in Fuenmayor founded in 1972, whose name brings together the initials of the appellation’s three provinces —Logroño, Álava and Navarra— and whose flagship estate is Viña Lanciano.

Bodegas LAN was founded in 1972 in Fuenmayor, in Rioja Alta, at a time of strong expansion in the region. Its name is an acronym of the three provinces across which the Rioja Denominación de Origen Calificada extends: Logroño (now La Rioja), Alava and Navarra, a way of stressing that the winery wished to represent the region as a whole.

Its most emblematic vineyard is Viña Lanciano, an estate set in a meander of the Ebro near Fuenmayor, with soils of rounded pebbles and clay. The winery has divided this estate into plots that are harvested and vinified separately, and its most ambitious reds come from it. In addition to its own vineyards, it works with grapes from growers in different parts of Rioja.

Tempranillo is the main variety, accompanied by Mazuelo (Carignan), Garnacha and Graciano depending on the wine. LAN has paid particular attention to ageing, combining American and French oak barrels, and has used Russian oak barrels for some of its wines.

The range runs from LAN Crianza, a very widely sold wine, to the Reserva and Gran Reserva, by way of the Edición Limitada line, and culminates in Culmen, a red made only in selected vintages. The style combines the tradition of Rioja ageing with a search for fruit and concentration.
