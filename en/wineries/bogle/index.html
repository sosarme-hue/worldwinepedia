Bogle Vineyards

Family winery in Clarksburg, in the Sacramento River delta, known for affordable and widely available wines such as its Petite Sirah and Old Vine Zinfandel.

The Bogle family has farmed land in the Sacramento River delta, in northern California, since the nineteenth century. For generations it was devoted to agriculture, and in the late 1960s Warren Bogle and his son Chris began planting vines on their land in Clarksburg. In 1979 they made their first wines under their own label, and the winery has remained in family hands ever since, now run by the next generation.

The Clarksburg area, south of the city of Sacramento, has a warm climate during the day that is tempered in the afternoon by breezes coming in from San Francisco Bay through the delta. The soils are fertile and alluvial in origin. As well as its own vineyards, the winery works with grapes from growers in other parts of California, such as Lodi or the Central Coast, which allows it to produce a wide range labelled with the California designation.

Bogle became known above all for its Petite Sirah, a variety the family planted from the early years, and for its Old Vine Zinfandel, made from grapes from old vines. Its range also includes Chardonnay, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and blends. It is one of the Californian family wineries whose wines are most easily found in supermarkets and shops across the United States, with a fruity, generous and approachable style that has earned it a broad and loyal following.

Visits & wine tourism

It has a tasting room in Clarksburg open to the public.
