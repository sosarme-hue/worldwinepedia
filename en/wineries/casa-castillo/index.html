Winery

Casa Castillo

Family estate in Jumilla run by José María Vicente, one of the wineries that have redefined Monastrell with fresh wines expressing their soils.

Casa Castillo is an estate in the municipality of Jumilla, on a high plateau surrounded by mountain ranges. It belongs to the Vicente family, and it is José María Vicente who has led the viticultural and winemaking project that has given the winery standing in Spain and abroad.

Vineyards

The estate combines plots with very different soils: areas of rounded stones and limestone gravel, clay ground and sandy soils. Alongside Monastrell, the dominant variety, Garnacha and Syrah are grown. Some plots keep old Monastrell vines planted on their own roots (pie franco), ungrafted, which is possible on sandy soils unfavourable to phylloxera.

Style

Over time the winery has moved towards wines with less extraction and more freshness, with more measured use of oak and harvests that aim to preserve acidity, something especially relevant in a hot, dry climate such as Jumilla's. The range runs from Casa Castillo Monastrell, the house's most accessible wine, and the Garnacha El Molar, to single-plot wines such as Las Gravas, Valtosca and Pie Franco. Casa Castillo is often cited as an example of Monastrell's potential for fine, age-worthy wines.
