Château Smith Haut Lafitte

Classified growth of Graves in Martillac, in the Pessac-Léognan appellation, owned since 1990 by Daniel and Florence Cathiard and renowned for its reds and its Sauvignon Blanc-based white.

Château Smith Haut Lafitte lies in Martillac, south of Bordeaux, in the Pessac-Léognan appellation, created in 1987 in the northern part of the Graves. Its name combines that of a Scottish merchant, George Smith, who owned the estate in the 18th century, with the place name “Lafitte”, which refers to a small rise in the ground.

Its red wine was recognised as a Cru Classé de Graves in the classification of that area established in the mid-20th century. In 1990 the estate was bought by Daniel and Florence Cathiard, former top-level athletes, who undertook a thorough renovation: restructuring the vineyard, environmentally friendly farming practices, an in-house cooperage to make some of their barrels, and renovated cellars.

The family has also developed wine tourism and wellness activities on the property, including a hotel and a spa, and one of their daughters co-founded the cosmetics brand Caudalie, inspired by grape polyphenols.

The vineyard lies on a rise of deep gravel. For the reds, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot are grown; for the white, mainly Sauvignon Blanc, with some Sauvignon Gris and Sémillon. As well as the grand vin red and white, the estate makes the second wine Les Hauts de Smith, also in red and white.

Visits & wine tourism

The estate offers tours of its vineyards and cellars, including its cooperage, and has accommodation and restaurants on the property.
