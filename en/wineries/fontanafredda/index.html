Fontanafredda

Historic estate in Serralunga d’Alba linked to the House of Savoy, one of the largest vineyard properties in the Langhe and a producer of Barolo since the nineteenth century.

Fontanafredda’s history begins in 1858, when King Victor Emmanuel II of Savoy acquired the estate at Serralunga d’Alba for Rosa Vercellana, known as “la bela Rosin”, whom he later married. Their son, Emanuele Alberto Guerrieri, Count of Mirafiore, developed wine production on the estate in the following decades and made it one of the leading Barolo producers of the time.

After a long period owned by a bank during the twentieth century, the estate was acquired in 2008 by a group of Piedmontese entrepreneurs led by Oscar Farinetti, founder of Eataly.

Fontanafredda still looks like a small village, with its historic buildings, the former royal residence and the cellars surrounded by vineyards, among them the Vigna La Rosa cru. A significant part of its own vineyards is now farmed organically.

The range is broad: it includes several Barolos, from the Serralunga village wine to single-vineyard bottlings, as well as Barbaresco, Barbera, Dolcetto, Nebbiolo, the sweet, lightly sparkling Moscato d’Asti and traditional-method sparkling wines from Alta Langa.

Visits & wine tourism

The estate offers guided tours of the historic cellars and tastings, as well as dining and accommodation.
