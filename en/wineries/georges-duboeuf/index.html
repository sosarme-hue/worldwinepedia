Les Vins Georges Duboeuf

Romanèche-Thorins house founded by Georges Duboeuf, a key figure in the international spread of Beaujolais and Beaujolais Nouveau.

Georges Duboeuf (1933-2020), the son of growers in the Mâconnais, began by personally selling his family’s and neighbouring producers’ wines to restaurants in the region. In 1964 he set up his own négociant house in Romanèche-Thorins, in southern Beaujolais, which over the years became the area’s largest wine merchant.

Duboeuf played a decisive role in the international popularity of Beaujolais Nouveau, the new-vintage wine released on the third Thursday of November, and in promoting the ten Beaujolais crus, such as Morgon, Fleurie and Moulin-à-Vent. His floral labels became highly recognisable in many countries.

The house works with a large number of growers and cooperative wineries, and also sells wines from individual estates with their names on the label. The central variety is Gamay for the reds, together with Chardonnay for the whites of the Mâconnais and Beaujolais. Since Georges’s death his son Franck Duboeuf has continued at the helm.

In Romanèche-Thorins the family created a site devoted to wine and viticulture, now known as the Hameau Duboeuf.

Beaujolais lies south of Burgundy and north of Lyon, and the granite soils in the north of the region are particularly prized for Gamay.

Visits & wine tourism

In Romanèche-Thorins, the Hameau Duboeuf offers an educational visit about wine and the vine, with a tasting.
