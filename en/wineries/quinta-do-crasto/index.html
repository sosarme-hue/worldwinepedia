Quinta do Crasto

Family-owned quinta in the Cima Corgo, on the right bank of the Douro, known for its Douro reds from old vines and for its Ports.

Quinta do Crasto lies on the right bank of the Douro river, between Peso da Régua and Pinhão, in the Cima Corgo subregion. Its name derives from the Latin castrum, a fort, and the estate has a long winegrowing history. In the early twentieth century it was acquired by Constantino de Almeida, and today it is run by his descendants in the Roquette family.

For a long time the quinta’s output went mainly into Port. From the 1990s the family committed to still Douro DOC wines, which now make up the main part of its range, without giving up Ports such as Late Bottled Vintage and Vintage.

The estate preserves very old vineyards planted with many interplanted varieties, typical of the traditional Douro. Some of its best-known wines come from them, such as the Reserva Vinhas Velhas and the single-plot wines Vinha Maria Teresa and Vinha da Ponte. More recent plantings are dominated by varieties such as Touriga Nacional, Touriga Franca and Tinta Roriz. The family also farms Quinta da Cabreira, in the Douro Superior. The house style combines the region’s typical concentration with defined fruit and freshness, and its range runs from the Crasto red, for earlier drinking, to wines intended for long ageing.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers visits to the quinta with tastings, by prior booking.
