Winery

L'Aventure

Winery on the west side of Paso Robles, California, founded by Bordeaux winemaker Stephan Asseo and known for its blends of Syrah, Cabernet Sauvignon and Petit Verdot.

L’Aventure was founded in the late 1990s by Stephan Asseo, a French winemaker who had made wine for years in Bordeaux. Seeking more freedom than the rules of the French appellations allowed, particularly to blend varieties from different regions, he settled on the west side of Paso Robles, on California’s Central Coast, in the area that now forms the Willow Creek district.

The vineyard lies on slopes of calcareous and clay soils, at some altitude and under the influence of the cool breezes that come in from the Pacific through gaps in the coastal mountains. This setting, with warm days and cool nights, allows the grapes to ripen fully while retaining acidity. The estate is farmed for low yields and harvested by hand.

The house style is built on blending varieties that would never be mixed in France: Rhône Syrah with Bordeaux Cabernet Sauvignon and Petit Verdot, in the estate’s signature wine, the Estate Cuvée. The range also includes Rhône-inspired blends such as Côte à Côte, based on Grenache, Syrah and Mourvèdre. These are powerful, ripe and concentrated reds, but with a freshness that allows them to age.
