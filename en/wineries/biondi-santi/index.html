Biondi-Santi

Historic estate at Tenuta Greppo, in Montalcino, traditionally credited with creating Brunello di Montalcino in the late nineteenth century.

Tenuta Greppo, just outside Montalcino, is the birthplace of Biondi-Santi. In the nineteenth century Clemente Santi was already concerned with the quality of the estate’s wine, and his grandson Ferruccio Biondi-Santi selected large-berried Sangiovese vines, known as “Brunello”, to make a long-aged red from that variety alone, at a time when blending grapes was the custom. Tradition places the first vintage of this wine in 1888.

The following generations, notably Tancredi and later Franco Biondi-Santi, kept to a very classical style: Sangiovese on its own, ageing in large oak casks, and wines designed to evolve over decades. The estate is known for keeping old bottles and for periodically reconditioning its historic vintages.

Since 2016 the majority of the property has belonged to the French group EPI, owned by the Descours family, which has maintained the house’s traditional approach.

Production centres on Brunello di Montalcino, in its vintage version and as a Riserva made only in the best years, together with a Rosso di Montalcino.
