Txomin Etxaniz

Family winery in Getaria (Gipuzkoa), with vineyards facing the Bay of Biscay, whose white txakoli is one of the best known in the appellation.

Txomin Etxaniz is a family winery in Getaria, a coastal town in Gipuzkoa that gives its name to the Getariako Txakolina Designation of Origin. The family has grown vines and made txakoli for generations at the farmhouse that gives the house its name, and it was one of the wineries that drove the creation of the appellation in the late 1980s, when txakoli was beginning to move from a homemade wine to a bottled quality wine.

The vineyards lie on the slopes around Getaria, very close to the Bay of Biscay, in a damp Atlantic climate with abundant rainfall and mild temperatures. The vines are traditionally trained high, on pergolas or raised trellises, to help air the bunches and limit problems caused by humidity.

The main variety is the white Hondarrabi Zuri, joined by a small proportion of the red Hondarrabi Beltza. With them the winery makes its white Txomin Etxaniz, a young wine with moderate alcohol, lively acidity and often a slight spritz, traditionally poured from a height to enliven its freshness. The range also includes other wines, such as a sparkling one.

Txomin Etxaniz’s style is a good representation of classic Getaria txakoli: a light, saline and refreshing white, closely tied to the seafood cooking of the Basque coast.
