Ruffino

Tuscan house founded in 1877 in Pontassieve, one of the world’s best-known Chianti brands, with estates in Chianti Classico, Montalcino and other parts of the region.

Ruffino was founded in 1877 by the cousins Ilario and Leopoldo Ruffino in Pontassieve, east of Florence, where the company still has its headquarters. From the late nineteenth century its wines were widely distributed outside Italy, and the house became one of the names most associated with Chianti on international markets, at a time when Tuscan wine was often sold in the typical straw-covered fiasco.

Over the twentieth century the company passed through different hands; for many decades it was controlled by the Folonari family and later it became part of the American group Constellation Brands. Over time Ruffino brought together several estates of its own in Tuscany, including properties in Chianti Classico that supply its Riserva Ducale line, Poggio Casciano in the hills near Florence and Greppone Mazzi in Montalcino, where it makes Brunello.

Its most emblematic wine is Riserva Ducale, a Chianti Classico Riserva the house has made for many decades, together with its top version, Riserva Ducale Oro. The very broad range also includes Chianti, IGT reds such as Modus and wines from other Italian regions, among them a widely sold Prosecco. It is one of the Italian wineries with the greatest presence in shops and restaurants around the world.
