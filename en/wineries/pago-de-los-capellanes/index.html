Pago de los Capellanes

Family winery in Pedrosa de Duero, in the Burgos part of Ribera, founded in 1996 on land that once belonged to the parish chaplains, from whom it takes its name.

Pago de los Capellanes was founded in 1996 by the Rodero-Villa family in Pedrosa de Duero, a village in Burgos near Roa, in the central part of Ribera del Duero. The name recalls that the land where its vineyards were planted once belonged to the chaplains of the parish church, who administered it as part of their income.

The family had farming roots in the area and decided to commit to its own vineyards, planting and acquiring plots on the terraces of the Duero. Over the years the winery has identified some of its best sites, which it vinifies separately and which give their names to its most ambitious wines, such as El Nogal and El Picón. The soils vary between clay, limestone and sand, and the climate is continental, with cold winters, dry summers and sharp differences between day and night temperatures during ripening.

Tinto Fino, the local name for Tempranillo, is the dominant variety, and in some wines it has been complemented by small proportions of Bordeaux varieties such as Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot. The range follows Ribera’s traditional ladder, with a Roble, a Crianza and a Reserva, in addition to the single-site wines.

The style of Pago de los Capellanes is characterised by ripe fruit and ageing in French oak barrels, with medium- to full-bodied reds and polished tannins.
