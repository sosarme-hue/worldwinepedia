Winery

Ca' del Bosco

Erbusco winery founded by Maurizio Zanella, one of the houses that brought prestige to Franciacorta, Lombardy’s traditional-method sparkling wine.

The winery’s name comes from a small house surrounded by woodland, the “Ca’ del Bosc”, which Annamaria Clementi Zanella bought in the 1960s on the hills of Erbusco, in Franciacorta, south of Lake Iseo. Her son Maurizio Zanella turned the property into a winery at the end of that decade and, after getting to know Champagne, decided to devote himself to traditional-method sparkling wine.

Ca’ del Bosco became one of the benchmark houses of what was then a young appellation. Since the 1990s the Santa Margherita group has held a stake in the company, which is still run by Zanella.

The winery grows Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Blanc in vineyards across several Franciacorta municipalities. It is known for its attention to technical detail: among other things, it has developed a system for washing and drying the grapes before pressing, and it disgorges part of its production without added sulphites.

The Franciacorta range runs from Cuvée Prestige, its most widely available wine, to the vintage wines of the Vintage Collection and to Annamaria Clementi, its prestige cuvée, dedicated to the founder’s mother. It also makes still wines, such as the red Maurizio Zanella. The winery entrance is dominated by a large bronze gate by the sculptor Arnaldo Pomodoro.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers guided tours of the winery with a tasting, by prior booking.
