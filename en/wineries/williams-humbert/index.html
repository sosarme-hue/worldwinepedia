Winery

Williams & Humbert

Jerez house founded in 1877 by Alexander Williams and Arthur Humbert, known for brands such as Dry Sack and Canasta.

Williams & Humbert was founded in 1877 in Jerez de la Frontera by the Briton Alexander Williams, who had worked in the sherry trade, and his brother-in-law Arthur Humbert. Like other houses of British origin, it was set up with an eye to exporting to England, the great historic market for sherry, and its brands achieved wide international distribution during the twentieth century.

The bodegas

For a long time the house had bodegas in the centre of Jerez. Today it is based in large premises on the outskirts of the city, which hold tens of thousands of casks and a collection of soleras of different ages. Besides sherry, the company makes brandy, vinegar and other drinks, and for years it has belonged to a Jerez family that has also championed the revival of vintage wines, an unusual practice in a region dominated by the solera system.

Wines

Its most famous brand is Dry Sack, a medium sherry that was sold for decades in a bottle wrapped in a small burlap sack. Another historic brand is Canasta, a sweet cream. The range also includes finos, amontillados, olorosos and Pedro Ximénez, as well as long-aged wines certified as VOS and VORS. The sweet and medium blends combine oxidatively aged wines with Pedro Ximénez, in the tradition of wines intended for the British market.
