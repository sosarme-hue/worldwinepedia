La Rioja Alta, S.A.

Winery founded in Haro in 1890, a benchmark for traditional Rioja aged in American oak, using barrels from its own cooperage.

La Rioja Alta was born in 1890 in the Station District of Haro, driven by five families from La Rioja and the Basque Country who formed the Sociedad Vinícola de La Rioja Alta. Over time it adopted its current name and became one of the most representative houses of classic Rioja. Several of the founding families still hold shares in the company.

The winery owns vineyards in different parts of Rioja, both around Haro and Briones and in the eastern part of the appellation, where it grows Grenache. Over the years the group has added other wineries beyond Haro, such as Torre de Oña in Rioja Alavesa, Lagar de Cervera in Rías Baixas and Áster in Ribera del Duero.

The house style rests on long ageing in American oak barrels, which the winery makes and repairs in its own cooperage. Racking is done by hand, barrel by barrel, following the traditional candlelight method to separate the wine from its lees. This way of working produces elegant wines, with well-integrated ageing notes and good cellaring potential.

Its best-known labels include Viña Alberdi, Viña Arana, Viña Ardanza, Gran Reserva 904 and Gran Reserva 890, which are made only in the vintages the winery considers suitable for each level.

Visits & wine tourism

It organises guided tours of the Haro winery with a tasting of its wines, by prior booking.
