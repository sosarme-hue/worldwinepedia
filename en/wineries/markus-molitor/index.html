Winery

Weingut Markus Molitor

Family estate in the Middle Mosel, based at Haus Klosterberg, known for Riesling from steep-slope vineyards and for a system of coloured capsules that indicates the style of each wine.

The Markus Molitor estate is based at Haus Klosterberg, in the village of Wehlen, in the heart of the Middle Mosel. Markus Molitor took over the modest family holding at a very young age in the 1980s and gradually expanded it with plots in some of the region’s most renowned vineyards: Wehlener Sonnenuhr, Zeltinger Sonnenuhr, Graacher Himmelreich, Bernkasteler Badstube, Ürziger Würzgarten and Brauneberger Juffer, among others. Later he also added vineyards in the Saar, around Serrig.

Most of the plots lie on very steep slate slopes, largely worked by hand. In the cellar the approach is one of minimal intervention, with slow fermentations and ageing in the large traditional wooden casks of the Mosel.

The range is organised according to the ripeness levels of the German system (Kabinett, Spätlese, Auslese...) and, within them, with stars that mark higher-level selections. The colour of the capsule indicates the style: white for dry wines, green for off-dry (feinherb) wines and gold for wines with sweetness. Besides Riesling, the house is known for its Pinot Noir, unusual in the Mosel, and for releasing many wines after long ageing.

Its wines typically show the acid tension and mineral character associated with Mosel slate, and many of them are designed to develop over many years in bottle.
