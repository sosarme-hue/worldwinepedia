Castello di Ama

Estate in the hamlet of Ama, in Gaiole in Chianti, known for its single-vineyard Chianti Classico, its L’Apparita Merlot and its collection of contemporary art among the vines.

Castello di Ama is located in the small hamlet of Ama, in the commune of Gaiole in Chianti, at some altitude in the hills of Chianti Classico. The modern estate was born in the early 1970s, when several Roman families acquired the site intending to restore its vineyards and historic villas.

The winery is closely associated with Lorenza Sebasti, from one of the owning families, and her husband, the winemaker Marco Pallanti, who for decades led the making of the wines. Their work focused on identifying the best plots and making single-vineyard wines, an idea then uncommon in Chianti. Over time the house has centred its production on Sangiovese, complemented by small proportions of traditional varieties such as Malvasia Nera and Canaiolo, and of Merlot.

Its wines include Chianti Classico Ama, the San Lorenzo Gran Selezione and several single-vineyard Gran Selezione wines, as well as L’Apparita, a Merlot that was one of Italy’s first great wines from this variety. The estate is also known for the Castello di Ama per l’Arte Contemporanea project, which has invited international artists to create permanent works in the cellars, the villa and the gardens.

The house style aims for wines of elegance and freshness, in which the altitude of the vineyards translates into acidity and aromatic finesse.

Visits & wine tourism

The estate offers tours of the cellars and the contemporary artworks, with tastings; booking is required.
