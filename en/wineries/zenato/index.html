Winery

Zenato

Family winery founded in 1960 by Sergio Zenato on the shores of Lake Garda, known for its Lugana whites and its Valpolicella reds such as Ripassa and Amarone.

Sergio Zenato founded the winery in 1960 in the San Benedetto di Lugana area, in the commune of Peschiera del Garda, on the southern shore of Lake Garda. He was one of the producers who helped make Lugana known, a local white made from the grape called Trebbiano di Lugana or Turbiana.

Over time the family acquired vineyards in Valpolicella, in particular the Costalunga estate in Sant’Ambrogio di Valpolicella, which supplies its reds. The winery became internationally known for Ripassa, a Valpolicella made by the ripasso technique, that is, refermented on Amarone pomace, and for its Amarone della Valpolicella Classico, including a Riserva named after Sergio Zenato.

Since the founder’s death the company has been run by his children, Alberto and Nadia Zenato. The range also includes simpler wines from the Garda and Verona areas.

In its reds the house style seeks concentration and roundness, with ripe fruit and oak ageing, while the Luganas are characterised by their freshness and delicate aromatic profile.

The Lugana vineyards, on clay soils near the lake, benefit from the moderating influence of Lake Garda, which softens temperatures. In Valpolicella, the Costalunga vineyards lie on hillsides typical of the classic zone, where the grapes for Amarone are selected and then dried in lofts for several months, as local tradition requires.
