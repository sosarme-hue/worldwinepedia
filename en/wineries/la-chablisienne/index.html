La Chablisienne

Chablis cooperative winery founded in 1923, which brings together many local growers and makes wines from every Chablis appellation, from Petit Chablis to grand cru.

La Chablisienne was founded in 1923, at a difficult time for the Chablis vineyard, when local growers joined forces to sell their wines on better terms. Over time it moved from selling in bulk to vinifying, ageing and bottling its own wines, and today it is one of the appellation’s leading producers.

Its members farm vineyards spread throughout the Chablis area, which allows the cooperative to make wines from all four of the area’s appellations: Petit Chablis, Chablis, Chablis Premier Cru and Chablis Grand Cru. Among its best-known wines are the Chablis La Pierrelée, the old-vine Chablis Les Vénérables and a wide range of premiers crus such as Montée de Tonnerre, Fourchaume, Vaillons and Mont de Milieu.

The cooperative also owns Château Grenouilles, a large plot in the Grenouilles grand cru, the smallest of the seven Chablis grands crus, located beside the Serein river, from which it makes a dedicated wine.

All its wines are made from Chardonnay. The house handles each plot according to its origin, with tank vinification for the freshest wines and partial barrel ageing for some premiers and grands crus. Its style seeks to express the purity and minerality of Chablis, with a wide range running from everyday wines to wines for ageing.
