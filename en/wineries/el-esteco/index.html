El Esteco

Historic Cafayate winery founded in 1892 by the brothers David and Salvador Michel. It belongs to Grupo Peñaflor and is one of the leading producers of Torrontés in the Calchaquí Valleys.

El Esteco has its origins in the winery founded in 1892 in Cafayate by the brothers David and Salvador Michel, whose historic colonial-style building stands at the entrance to the town. For much of the twentieth century the house was known for the Michel Torino brand, and its Don David line pays tribute to one of the founders. Today the winery belongs to Grupo Peñaflor, one of Argentina’s largest wine groups, which also owns Trapiche.

Its vineyards lie around Cafayate, at about 1,700 metres above sea level, at the southern end of the Calchaquí Valleys. The dry climate, intense sunlight and cool nights make it possible to produce aromatic Torrontés whites and deeply coloured reds from Malbec, Cabernet Sauvignon and Tannat. The winery retains some old vineyards, whose grapes are used in specific wines, and has converted part of its vineyard area to organic farming.

El Esteco makes a broad range, from fresh young wines to estate and old-vine reds, as well as more experimental styles. Next to the winery is a hotel, Patios de Cafayate, which has contributed to the development of wine tourism in the area.

Because of its history and size, El Esteco is one of the wineries that have done most to make the wines of Cafayate, and Salta Torrontés in particular, known outside Argentina.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery offers tours and tastings, usually by prior booking.
