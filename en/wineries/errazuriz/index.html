Winery

Viña Errázuriz

Estate founded in 1870 by Maximiano Errázuriz in Panquehue, in the Aconcagua Valley. Run by Eduardo Chadwick, a descendant of the founder, it is one of the houses that have driven Chile’s top-end reds and coastal viticulture in Aconcagua.

Viña Errázuriz was founded in 1870 by Maximiano Errázuriz, a member of an influential Chilean family, who planted vineyards with French vines in Panquehue, in the inland Aconcagua Valley, then an unusual place for quality viticulture. Over time the estate became one of the largest of its day.

In the last decades of the twentieth century the management of the estate passed to Eduardo Chadwick, a descendant of the founder, under whose leadership the company undertook an ambitious quality programme and created top-end wines such as Don Maximiano Founder’s Reserve, a Cabernet Sauvignon-based blend, and Kai, based on Carmenère. The family also developed sister projects such as Seña, initially created in partnership with Robert Mondavi, and Viñedo Chadwick, in Alto Maipo. In 2004 a blind tasting held in Berlin, in which these wines were compared with great names from Bordeaux and Italy, had a wide impact and helped change the international perception of Chilean reds.

Errázuriz has also been a pioneer of viticulture in the valley’s coastal strip, known as Aconcagua Costa, planted in the first decades of the twenty-first century, the source of cool-climate whites and reds such as those of the Las Pizarras line. The Panquehue winery, which combines historic buildings with modern facilities, welcomes visitors.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery offers tours and tastings, usually by prior booking.
