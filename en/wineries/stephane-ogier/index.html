Domaine Stéphane Ogier

Family estate in Ampuis, Côte-Rôtie, run by Stéphane Ogier, making single-site wines from Côte-Rôtie, Condrieu and the historic Seyssuel vineyards.

Domaine Stéphane Ogier is based in Ampuis, the village that holds most of the Côte-Rôtie vineyard, at the northern end of the Rhône valley. The Ogier family had farmed vines in the area for generations and, like many local growers, long sold its grapes or wine to négociant houses. It was under Michel Ogier that the estate began bottling its own production, and his son Stéphane, who later took over, made it one of the leading names of Côte-Rôtie’s new generation.

The estate has expanded its holdings on the slopes of Côte-Rôtie and developed a range of single-site wines that show the differences between the appellation’s lieux-dits. It also makes Condrieu from Viognier and has taken part in reviving the slopes of Seyssuel, on the left bank of the Rhône north of Vienne, a vineyard renowned in Roman times that had disappeared and whose wines are sold under the Collines Rhodaniennes geographical indication.

The range is completed by Syrah reds from vines outside the prestigious appellations and more affordable Côtes du Rhône wines. The house style seeks fine, aromatic Syrah reds, with measured extraction and oak ageing that supports the fruit, and whites with broad texture and freshness.
