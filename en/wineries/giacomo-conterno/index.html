Winery

Giacomo Conterno

Monforte d’Alba winery, an emblem of traditional Barolo, famous for its Barolo Riserva Monfortino and for the Cascina Francia estate in Serralunga d’Alba.

The house takes its name from Giacomo Conterno, who in the early twentieth century shaped the family business in Monforte d’Alba and created Monfortino, a long-aged Barolo designed to last for decades. His son Giovanni Conterno consolidated the winery’s reputation and in 1974 acquired Cascina Francia, an estate in Serralunga d’Alba that has been the heart of production ever since. Since the early 2000s it has been run by Roberto Conterno, the founder’s grandson.

Giacomo Conterno is regarded as one of the most representative wineries of the traditional Barolo style: long fermentations and macerations, extended ageing in large oak casks and no pursuit of new-oak effects. Monfortino is made only in vintages the family considers worthy of it and is released after many years of ageing. Cascina Francia also yields a Barolo bearing the estate’s name and a highly regarded Barbera d’Alba.

In recent years the winery has added other vineyards in Serralunga d’Alba, such as Cerretta and Arione, and has acquired the historic Nervi house in Gattinara, in northern Piedmont, where Nebbiolo gives wines of a different profile. Despite this expansion, production remains limited and focused on a small number of wines.

The wines are generally considered to need long cellaring before they show their full complexity.
