Winery

Prunotto

Alba winery that grew out of a cooperative founded in 1904, took the name of Alfredo Prunotto and has belonged to the Antinori family since 1989.

Prunotto’s origins lie in the “Ai Vini delle Langhe” cooperative, founded in Alba in 1904. In 1923 Alfredo Prunotto took over the winery, which came to bear his name, and began exporting its wines outside Italy. Years later the business passed to other owners, who kept the name.

In 1989 the Antinori family began working with Prunotto, first on distribution and later as owners. From then on the winery acquired its own vineyards in some of Piedmont’s best areas, including plots in the Bussia cru of Monforte d’Alba, in the Barolo zone, and at Costamiole, near Agliano Terme, in the Barbera d’Asti area.

Prunotto was one of the wineries that, in the 1960s, began making and labelling single-vineyard wines in the Langhe, a practice that was rare at the time.

The current range includes Barolo and Barbaresco, several Barbera d’Alba and Barbera d’Asti wines such as Fiulot, as well as Dolcetto, Nebbiolo, Moscato d’Asti and the white Arneis. The style combines local tradition with careful, modern winemaking.
