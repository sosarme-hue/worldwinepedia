Bodegas y Viñedos Pujanza

Family winery in Laguardia, in Rioja Alavesa, founded in the late 1990s and focused on Tempranillo reds from high-altitude vineyards at the foot of the Sierra de Cantabria.

Bodegas y Viñedos Pujanza is a family winery located in Laguardia, in Rioja Alavesa. It was founded in the late 1990s by Carlos San Pedro, a member of a family of local growers, with the idea of making wines solely from its own vineyards.

Its vines lie on the slopes rising towards the Sierra de Cantabria, at a higher altitude than much of Rioja. The mountains shelter the vineyards from cold northerly winds, while the altitude lengthens the ripening cycle and helps preserve acidity. The soils are mostly poor clay-limestone with little water retention.

The winery works almost exclusively with Tempranillo, the emblematic variety of Rioja Alavesa, and organises its range by origin: from multi-vineyard wines such as Hado to single-plot reds such as Finca Valdepoleo or Norte, the latter from higher-altitude vineyards.

Pujanza's style moves away from the Rioja tradition of long ageing and blends from varied sources to focus on the expression of the vineyard: reds of precise fruit, good structure and marked acidity, with French oak ageing that seeks not to dominate the wine. Rioja Alavesa, on the left bank of the Ebro, is known for its limestone soils and a climate with a marked Atlantic influence.
