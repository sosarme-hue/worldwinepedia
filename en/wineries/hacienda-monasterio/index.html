Hacienda Monasterio

Estate in Pesquera de Duero where Danish winemaker Peter Sisseck has worked since the early 1990s, whose reds combine Tinto Fino with Bordeaux varieties.

Hacienda Monasterio is located in Pesquera de Duero, in the province of Valladolid, on a south-facing slope above the Duero valley, in one of the most traditional areas of Ribera. The project got under way in the early 1990s, when its vineyards were planted and the winery built, and from the start it had Danish winemaker Peter Sisseck, who would later create Dominio de Pingus and who has remained linked to the estate as technical director and partner.

The vineyard surrounds the winery and sits on clay and limestone soils with varying amounts of stone, at high altitude and with the continental climate of the plateau. In addition to Tinto Fino, the local name for Tempranillo, Bordeaux varieties such as Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot were planted, as well as some Malbec, which in the house wines add complementary nuances to the main variety.

The range is short. The main red, known simply as Hacienda Monasterio, is made from estate grapes and aged in barrel; in selected vintages a Reserva is also made and, on occasion, a Reserva Especial. The estate’s viticulture places the emphasis on soil care and on obtaining balanced grapes, in line with Sisseck’s work on his other projects.

The style seeks a balance between the power of Ribera and freshness, with ripe fruit and fine tannins.
