Dal Forno Romano

Family winery in the Val d’Illasi, east of Valpolicella Classica, known for its highly concentrated Amarone and Valpolicella.

Dal Forno Romano is located in Cellore d’Illasi, in the Val d’Illasi, one of the eastern valleys of the Valpolicella appellation, outside the Classica zone. The Dal Forno family had grown vines in the area for generations, but it was Romano Dal Forno who, from the 1980s, began to bottle his own wines with the ambition of placing them among Italy’s great reds. His meeting with Giuseppe Quintarelli is considered to have been decisive in that choice.

The house style is based on an extreme pursuit of concentration: high-density vineyards and very low yields, long drying of the grapes in ventilated rooms and extended ageing in new oak barriques. The winery, built by the family, is designed for precise control of every stage of production.

Production is limited to a few wines: Amarone della Valpolicella; the Valpolicella Superiore, which comes from the Monte Lodoletta estate and is also made with a proportion of dried grapes; and, in some vintages, a Recioto called Vigna Seré. The varieties are the area’s traditional ones, above all Corvina and Rondinella, alongside other native grapes.

Today Romano Dal Forno works with his sons. Their wines are among the most concentrated and long-lived Amarones.
