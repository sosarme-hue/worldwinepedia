Bruno Giacosa

Neive house associated with Bruno Giacosa, one of the great classical interpreters of Barolo and Barbaresco, with wines from vineyards such as Falletto, Asili and Rabajà.

The Giacosa family traded in grapes and wine in Neive, in the Barbaresco zone, and it was Bruno Giacosa (1929-2018) who turned the house into one of Piedmont’s benchmarks. For decades he worked mainly by buying grapes from growers in some of the finest crus of the Langhe, which he knew with remarkable precision, and vinifying them separately to express the character of each vineyard.

Over time the family acquired its own vineyards. The best known is Falletto, in Serralunga d’Alba, within the Barolo zone; in Barbaresco it works plots in famous crus such as Asili and Rabajà. For this reason the house has used two business names: Casa Vinicola Bruno Giacosa for the wines made from bought-in grapes and Azienda Agricola Falletto for those from its own vineyards. In vintages considered exceptional, some wines are released as Riserva with the distinctive red label.

The house style is classical: long macerations, ageing in large oak casks and wines of great structure, designed to evolve for many years in bottle. Besides Barolo and Barbaresco, it makes Barbera, Dolcetto, Nebbiolo d’Alba and a white from Roero Arneis, as well as a traditional-method sparkling wine. Since Bruno Giacosa’s death the company has been run by his daughter, Bruna Giacosa.
