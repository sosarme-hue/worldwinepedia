Domaine François Chidaine

Estate in Montlouis-sur-Loire, Touraine, a benchmark for Loire Chenin Blanc, with biodynamically farmed vineyards in Montlouis and the Vouvray area.

Domaine François Chidaine is based in Montlouis-sur-Loire, in Touraine, on the south bank of the Loire opposite Vouvray. François Chidaine comes from a family of Montlouis growers and, starting from the family vines, built an estate that has become one of the leading names of the appellation and of Loire Chenin Blanc in general.

The estate farms vineyards in Montlouis and in the Vouvray area on the opposite bank of the river, on clay, flint and limestone soils lying over the tuffeau chalk characteristic of Touraine. The vineyards are farmed according to the principles of biodynamic agriculture. Some of the wines from the north bank are sold as Vin de France, because they are made in the Montlouis winery, outside the Vouvray area.

The range focuses on single-plot Chenin whites, such as Les Bournais, Les Choisilles and Clos Habert, which range from dry styles to wines with residual sugar depending on the vintage, and on traditional-method sparkling wines. It is completed by Touraine wines, such as a Sauvignon Blanc and a red.

The house style seeks precise whites with great tension, in which ripeness coexists with acidity and a marked mineral character, aged in large used wooden casks that do not dominate the fruit.
