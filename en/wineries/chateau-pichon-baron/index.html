Château Pichon Baron

Pauillac second growth, known for its turreted château facing Latour and owned by AXA Millésimes since 1987.

Château Pichon Baron, whose full official name is Château Pichon Longueville Baron, arose from the 19th-century division of the old Pichon Longueville estate, in the south of Pauillac. One part went to Baron Raoul de Pichon Longueville, who had the neo-Renaissance château with its two turrets built, today one of the most photographed buildings in the Médoc; the other part gave rise to Pichon Comtesse, which lies across the road.

In the 1855 classification it was recognised as a Deuxième Grand Cru Classé. After a period of some decline, it was bought in 1987 by the insurer AXA, whose subsidiary AXA Millésimes undertook a thorough renovation of the vineyard and cellars, with new facilities built in the 1990s.

The best plots lie on a plateau of deep gravel close to Château Latour, planted mainly with Cabernet Sauvignon, together with Merlot and small areas of Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot.

As well as the grand vin, the estate makes Les Griffons de Pichon Baron and Les Tourelles de Longueville. The house style is marked by powerful, deep reds, typical of Pauillac, with ripe fruit and a structure intended for ageing.

Pichon Baron is part of AXA Millésimes, the insurer’s group of wine estates, which also includes properties such as Château Suduiraut in Sauternes and Quinta do Noval in Portugal’s Douro.
