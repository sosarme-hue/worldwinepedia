Château Prieuré-Lichine

Fourth growth of the 1855 classification in Cantenac, in the Margaux appellation, a former priory that owes its current name to Alexis Lichine.

Château Prieuré-Lichine is located in Cantenac, within the Margaux appellation. It originated as a priory of Benedictine monks who grew vines in the area, hence the first part of its name. In the 1855 classification it was listed as a fourth growth, then under the name Château Le Prieuré.

In 1951 it was acquired by Alexis Lichine, a Russian-born wine writer and merchant based in the United States, author of reference works on French wine. Lichine added his surname to the château's name, enlarged the vineyard through purchases and exchanges of plots, and was one of the first Médoc owners to open his estate to visitors. After his death his son Sacha ran the property until its sale in the late 1990s.

The vineyard is divided into numerous plots scattered across several communes of the Margaux appellation, on gravel soils. It is planted with Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Petit Verdot. The house style seeks the elegance and perfume associated with Margaux, with fine tannins and precise fruit.

The range includes the grand vin, Château Prieuré-Lichine, and a second wine, Confidences de Prieuré-Lichine, as well as a white wine. Together they allow one to appreciate, at different levels, the character of one of the most famous appellations of the Médoc, known for the delicacy of its reds.

Visits & wine tourism

The estate, a pioneer in welcoming visitors in the Médoc, offers tours and tastings.
