Tenuta dell'Ornellaia

Bolgheri estate founded in 1981 by Lodovico Antinori, now owned by the Frescobaldi family, known for its Bordeaux-style red Ornellaia.

Tenuta dell’Ornellaia was founded in 1981 by Lodovico Antinori on land in Bolgheri, in the municipality of Castagneto Carducci on the Tuscan coast. Inspired by the example of neighbouring Sassicaia and by the wines of Bordeaux, he planted Bordeaux varieties on soils of varied origin between the sea and the hills. The first vintage of the Ornellaia wine was 1985.

In the following decades the property changed hands: after a period in which the Frescobaldi family and the American group Mondavi were both involved, the Frescobaldi took full control in the mid-2000s.

The vineyards are planted mainly with Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. The flagship wine, Ornellaia, is a blend of these varieties under the Bolgheri Superiore DOC. The estate also makes Le Serre Nuove dell’Ornellaia, also from Bolgheri, and Le Volte dell’Ornellaia, a simpler Toscana IGT red. Masseto, a Merlot now run as a separate winery, was born on the same estate.

Since 2009 the house has commissioned a contemporary artist each year to design a special label for a limited series of large-format bottles, as part of the “Vendemmia d’Artista” project.
