Winery

Herederos del Marqués de Riscal

One of the pioneering wineries of modern Rioja, founded in Elciego (Rioja Alavesa) in 1858, which introduced Bordeaux techniques to the region; also a pioneer in Rueda.

The winery was founded in 1858 by Guillermo Hurtado de Amézaga, of the family of the Marquises of Riscal, in Elciego, in Rioja Alavesa. After a stay in Bordeaux, Hurtado de Amézaga wanted to apply at home the winemaking and barrel-ageing methods he had seen in France, and he was advised by Bordeaux technicians. Riscal thus became one of the houses that drove the birth of modern, long oak-aged Rioja.

The winery works with its own and contracted vineyards around Elciego. Its best-known wine is Marqués de Riscal Reserva, based on Tempranillo, whose label wrapped in a golden wire mesh is one of the most recognisable images in Spanish wine. The range also includes more modern-style wines, such as Barón de Chirel, and gran reservas.

In 1972 Riscal set up in Rueda, where it was one of the wineries that championed Verdejo and a fresh, aromatic style of white wine, helping to develop the appellation, created in 1980.

In 2006 the so-called City of Wine of Marqués de Riscal opened, a complex that includes a hotel designed by architect Frank Gehry, with its distinctive roof of coloured titanium ribbons, which has become an icon of wine tourism in Rioja.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers guided tours of the winery and the City of Wine, with a tasting, as well as a hotel and restaurants.
