Domäne Wachau

Growers’ cooperative in the Wachau based in Dürnstein, which brings together vines from many families across many of the region’s terraces and makes mainly dry Grüner Veltliner and Riesling.

Domäne Wachau is a cooperative that brings together a large number of winegrowing families in the Wachau. It was born in the twentieth century as a growers’ cooperative for the region and was long known as Freie Weingärtner Wachau; in the late 2000s it adopted its current name. In terms of the vineyard area controlled by its members it is the largest producer in the region and a cornerstone of its wine economy, as it enables many smallholders to keep the terraces under cultivation.

The cooperative is based in Dürnstein, at the foot of the castle hill, and its best-known building is the Kellerschlössel, a small eighteenth-century baroque palace built over cellars carved into the rock. The grapes come from vineyards throughout the Wachau, including some of the most highly regarded, such as Achleiten, Kellerberg, Loibenberg and Singerriedel.

Production focuses on dry Grüner Veltliner and Riesling, in the Steinfeder, Federspiel and Smaragd categories, with a range running from wines drawn from terraces across the region to individual single-vineyard wines. It also makes wines from other varieties, sparkling wines and some sweet wines. In recent decades the cooperative has strengthened its commitment to quality, with detailed work in the vineyards alongside its members and greater attention to the expression of each plot.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers tours of its premises and of the Kellerschlössel cellars, and has a wine shop in Dürnstein.
