Craggy Range

Winery founded in the late 1990s by the Peabody family, based at the foot of Te Mata Peak in Hawke’s Bay. It makes single-vineyard wines from the Gimblett Gravels and Martinborough.

Craggy Range was created in the late 1990s by the Australian businessman Terry Peabody and his family, in collaboration with the viticulturist Steve Smith, Master of Wine, who was searching New Zealand for sites capable of producing single-vineyard wines of high quality. The winery, with its restaurant and cellar door, stands at Havelock North, at the foot of the rocky faces of Te Mata Peak, one of the best-known peaks in the region.

The house works with its own vineyards in two main regions. In Hawke’s Bay it owns plots in the Gimblett Gravels, the source of its best-known reds: Le Sol, a Syrah, Sophia, a Merlot-based blend, and The Quarry, based on Cabernet Sauvignon. In Martinborough it farms the Te Muna vineyard, planted on stony terraces near the town, which gives its Pinot Noir and Sauvignon Blanc, as well as the top Pinot Noir Aroha.

From the outset the winery has set out to show the differences between specific sites, with single-vineyard wines made separately and barrel ageing tailored to each. Its style combines ripe fruit with freshness and fine tannins, and its Gimblett Gravels reds are intended to develop for years in bottle. Craggy Range remains in the hands of the founding family.

Visits & wine tourism

The Havelock North winery has a cellar door and restaurant at the foot of Te Mata Peak.
