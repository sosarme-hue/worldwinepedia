Winery

Davino

A family winery in Ceptura, in Dealu Mare, one of the pioneers of quality Romanian wine after the fall of communism, known for red blends based on Fetească Neagră such as Flamboyant.

Davino is a family winery located in Ceptura, in the Dealu Mare appellation, in Prahova county. It was created after the fall of communism by the Balaban family, led by Dan Balaban, and became one of the first private Romanian wineries focused from the outset on quality wines made from grapes from its own vineyards.

Its vines are on the hills of Ceptura, on south-facing slopes at the foot of the Carpathians, with varied soils and a continental climate of warm summers and long autumns. The winery works with low yields and hand harvesting, and has committed both to Romanian varieties, especially Fetească Neagră and Fetească Albă, and to international ones such as Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Sauvignon Blanc.

Davino is known above all for its red blends. The most famous is Flamboyant, an oak-aged red combining Fetească Neagră with Bordeaux varieties, designed for long ageing in bottle. Alongside it, the winery has made other blends and varietal wines from Fetească Neagră and Fetească Albă. The range also includes the Domaine Ceptura line, with whites, rosés and reds, and other selection wines. The winery’s style seeks to combine the ripe fruit of Dealu Mare with structure, freshness and ageing potential, and it has contributed significantly to the reputation of Romanian reds.
