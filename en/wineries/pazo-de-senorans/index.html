Pazo de Señoráns

Family winery set in a historic manor house in the Val do Salnés, in Meis (Pontevedra), known for its lees-aged Albariños.

Pazo de Señoráns is the project of Marisol Bueno and Javier Mareque, who in the late 1970s acquired a manor house in the parish of Vilanoviña, in the municipality of Meis, in the heart of the Val do Salnés. Pazos are Galician country houses, often surrounded by gardens, vines and granaries, and Señoráns preserves that traditional ensemble. The family was involved during the years in which the Rías Baixas Designation of Origin was established, in 1988, and the winery is today one of the area’s benchmarks.

The Val do Salnés is the subzone of Rías Baixas closest to the Atlantic, with a cool, damp climate, abundant rainfall and soils of granitic origin. Here Albariño is traditionally trained on pergolas, to air the bunches and protect them from humidity. The winery works with its own vineyards around the manor and with grapes from Salnés growers.

Its best-known wine is Pazo de Señoráns Albariño, a young white made in tank with a period of rest on its lees. The house also makes Selección de Añada, which spends an extended period on its fine lees before release, highlighting Albariño’s capacity to age.

The Pazo de Señoráns style is one of precise Albariños with good acidity and texture, focused more on fruit and salinity than on aromatic exuberance. The winery also produces traditional Galician spirits and liqueurs.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers guided tours of the manor, its gardens and the winery, with a tasting, by prior booking.
