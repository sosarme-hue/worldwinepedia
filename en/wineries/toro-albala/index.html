Winery

Toro Albalá

Winery in Aguilar de la Frontera, in the Montilla-Moriles D.O., known for its long-aged sweet Pedro Ximénez wines, especially the Don PX range.

Toro Albalá is a family winery located in Aguilar de la Frontera, in the province of Córdoba, within the Montilla-Moriles D.O. Its name comes from the founding family's surname; the family set up the winery in an old building in the town and has kept it running for several generations.

Pedro Ximénez

Like the rest of the designation, the winery works almost exclusively with Pedro Ximénez. With it, it makes finos and amontillados through biological and oxidative ageing, but its reputation rests mainly on its sweet wines. To make them, the grapes are spread out in the sun after harvest to raisin and concentrate their sugars; the resulting very dense must is fortified with grape spirit and aged in oak casks.

Don PX

The Don PX range is the house's hallmark. Some of these wines are bottled with a vintage after spending decades in cask, something unusual in an area dominated by the criaderas and solera system. With time these wines gain concentration and complexity, with notes of raisins, figs, coffee, cocoa and spice. Toro Albalá also holds stocks of very old wines released in small quantities.
