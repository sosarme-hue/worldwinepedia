Winery

Bodegas Valdespino

One of the oldest houses in Jerez, linked to the Macharnudo vineyard district, known for the Inocente fino and the Tío Diego amontillado; it belongs to Grupo Estévez.

Valdespino is one of the longest-established firms in Jerez de la Frontera. The Valdespino family has been linked to the city and its vineyards for centuries, and the winery long enjoyed a reputation among connoisseurs for wines of great quality. In the late twentieth century it became part of Grupo Estévez, which moved production to its Jerez facilities and has preserved the house's historic soleras and brands.

Macharnudo

Valdespino's most distinctive feature is its relationship with the Macharnudo vineyard district, one of the most highly regarded in the Jerez area, located north of the city. Its hills of albariza, the white chalky soil typical of the region, retain the winter rains and release the moisture to the vines during the summer. The winery is one of the few that have traditionally championed the identity of the vineyard on their labels, in a region where the style of each wine has depended more on ageing than on the vineyard of origin.

Winemaking and wines

Valdespino maintains practices that have become rare in Jerez, such as fermenting part of its musts in oak casks rather than in steel tanks. Its best-known wines are the fino Inocente, from Macharnudo and aged for a long time under flor, and the amontillado Tío Diego. The range also includes palo cortados, olorosos, Moscatels and very long-aged wines certified as VOS and VORS.
