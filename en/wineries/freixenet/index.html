Winery

Freixenet

Major cava house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, known worldwide for widely available sparkling wines such as Cordón Negro and Carta Nevada, now part of the Henkell Freixenet group.

Freixenet was born in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia in the early 20th century from the union of two wine families, the Ferrers and the Salas. The name comes from the family farmhouse «La Freixeneda». For decades the company was run by the Ferrer family, who turned it into one of the world's largest producers of traditional-method sparkling wine.

Expansion

From the second half of the 20th century Freixenet pursued an intense export strategy and took cava to many international markets. Its Christmas advertising campaigns became very popular in Spain. The group also added wineries in other Spanish designations and in other countries. In 2018 the German group Henkell acquired a majority stake, giving rise to Henkell Freixenet.

Style and wines

The house works with the classic cava varieties —Macabeo, Xarel·lo and Parellada— and makes its sparkling wines by the traditional method, with a second fermentation in bottle. Its best-known labels are Cordón Negro, in its characteristic black bottle, and Carta Nevada, in a frosted bottle. Alongside them are longer-aged cavas, rosés and other sparkling wines.

Visits & wine tourism

The house offers visits to its cellars in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, including a tour of the facilities and a tasting.
