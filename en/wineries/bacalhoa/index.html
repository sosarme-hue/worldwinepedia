Bacalhôa Vinhos de Portugal

Wine group based in Azeitão, on the Setúbal peninsula, heir to the former J. P. Vinhos, producing widely sold brands such as JP Azeitão and the Quinta da Bacalhôa red.

Bacalhôa Vinhos de Portugal is based in Azeitão, on the Setúbal peninsula, south of Lisbon. The company grew out of the former J. P. Vinhos (João Pires), one of the firms that drove the modernisation of Portuguese wine in the last decades of the twentieth century, and took its current name from Quinta da Bacalhôa, whose Renaissance palace and gardens are among the most notable monuments in the area. The group has been associated with the businessman José Berardo.

Quinta da Bacalhôa is home to a vineyard of Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot planted in the second half of the twentieth century, which produces a Bordeaux-inspired red that was one of the first prestige Portuguese wines made from these varieties.

Over the years the group has grown to bring together estates and wineries in several regions of Portugal, among them Caves Aliança in Bairrada and Quinta do Carmo in the Alentejo. On the Setúbal peninsula its activity ranges from high-volume wines, such as the JP Azeitão range, to oak-aged reds and traditional Moscatel de Setúbal, of which it keeps old reserves.

The Azeitão area enjoys a Mediterranean climate tempered by the nearby Atlantic and the Arrábida hills, with sandy soils on the plain and limestone on the slopes, suited both to Castelão and to international varieties and Muscat.

Visits & wine tourism

The Azeitão winery and the Quinta da Bacalhôa palace can be visited on guided tours that include a tasting.
