Mar de Frades

Winery in the Val do Salnés devoted to Albariño, whose white is recognisable by its blue bottle and by a label on which a ship appears when the wine reaches serving temperature.

Mar de Frades is a winery in the Val do Salnés, the subzone of the Rías Baixas Denominación de Origen closest to the ocean, located near the Ría de Arousa, in the province of Pontevedra. Its name refers to the sea and to friars, alluding to the monastic tradition that accompanied winegrowing in Galicia for centuries. It belongs to the Zamora Company group, which also owns Bodegas Ramón Bilbao in Rioja.

The winery works with Albariño, the appellation’s emblematic white variety, from its own vineyards and from growers in Salnés. Its flagship estate is Finca Valiñas, a vineyard near the winery that gives its name to one of its wines. The soils are of granitic origin, light and acidic, and the climate, humid and tempered by the Atlantic, favours whites with marked acidity and aromatic expression.

The house’s best-known wine is Mar de Frades Albariño, distinguished by its blue bottle and by a heat-sensitive label on which the silhouette of a ship appears when the wine is chilled to the right drinking temperature. The range also includes Finca Valiñas, an Albariño with longer ageing on lees, and a sparkling wine made from the same variety.

The Mar de Frades style seeks fresh, saline and aromatic whites that reflect the nearby sea and the Atlantic character of Salnés.
