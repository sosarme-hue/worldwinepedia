Winery

L.A. Cetto

One of Mexico’s largest wineries, founded by the Cetto family, of Trentino origin, with extensive vineyards in the Valle de Guadalupe. It is particularly known for having popularised Nebbiolo and Petite Sirah in Baja California.

The origins of L.A. Cetto go back to Angelo Cetto, an immigrant from Trentino, in northern Italy, who arrived in Mexico in the first half of the twentieth century and settled in Baja California, where he began growing vines and making wine. His son, Luis Agustín Cetto, whose initials give the company its name, greatly expanded the business, and later generations of the family have turned it into one of the country’s largest wine producers. The winery also makes brandy and other drinks.

The company has extensive vineyards in the Valle de Guadalupe and other parts of Baja California, on sandy soils of granitic origin and in a semi-arid Mediterranean climate. For decades its winemaking was in the hands of the Italian winemaker Camillo Magoni, who played a prominent role in spreading Italian varieties in the valley, such as Nebbiolo, which has become one of the region’s most characteristic grapes, although in Baja California it gives wines very different from those of Piedmont, riper and with softer tannins.

The range is very broad, from everyday wines to private reserves and more ambitious bottlings. Among reds, Petite Sirah, one of the most widely available Mexican wines, the Nebbiolo Reserva Privada and wines from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Malbec, Tempranillo and Zinfandel stand out; among whites, Chenin Blanc, Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay and Fumé Blanc. Rosés and sparkling wines are also made. Thanks to its size and distribution, L.A. Cetto has played an important role in spreading Mexican wine both at home and abroad.
