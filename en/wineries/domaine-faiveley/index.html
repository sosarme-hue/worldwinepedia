Domaine Faiveley

Family-owned house in Nuits-Saint-Georges founded in 1825, one of Burgundy’s largest vineyard owners, in the Côte d’Or and the Côte Chalonnaise.

Pierre Faiveley founded the house in 1825 in Nuits-Saint-Georges, in the heart of the Côte de Nuits. Over the generations the family acquired vineyards until it became one of Burgundy’s largest landowners, with plots in many appellations, including several Côte de Nuits Grands Crus such as Chambertin-Clos de Bèze, and in the Côte de Beaune.

An important part of its vineyard is in the Côte Chalonnaise, especially in Mercurey, where the family is one of the leading landowners. This combination allows the house to offer everything from affordable wines to Grands Crus.

In 2005 Erwan Faiveley, of the seventh generation, took over and launched a technical renewal of winemaking, expanding the estate with new acquisitions, including vineyards in Gevrey-Chambertin. The house remains family-owned.

Faiveley is known above all for its Pinot Noir reds, traditionally firm and built for ageing, which in recent years have gained in fruit expression and finesse; it also makes Chardonnay whites.

Nuits-Saint-Georges, the village where the house is based, gives its name to one of the best-known village appellations of the Côte de Nuits, which has no Grands Crus but many Premiers Crus. Its reputation is closely linked to the tradition of the négociant houses based in the town, of which Faiveley is one of the oldest.
