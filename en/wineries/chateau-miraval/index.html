Château Miraval

Estate in Correns, inland in the Var, whose Côtes de Provence rosé, made in partnership with the Perrin family since the 2012 vintage, achieved wide international recognition.

Château Miraval lies in Correns, a village inland in the Var, in the area known as Provence Verte, renowned for its commitment to organic farming. The estate, of ancient origin, stretches across a valley surrounded by woodland, with terraced vines and olive groves. For part of the 20th century it also housed a recording studio used by well-known musicians.

In the late 2000s the property was bought by the actors Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie, whose media profile contributed to the wine’s fame. Winemaking was entrusted to the Perrin family, owners of Château de Beaucastel, in partnership with whom the Miraval rosé was launched with the 2012 vintage. Since then, ownership of the estate has been the subject of changes and legal disputes among its shareholders, while the Perrins have remained in charge of the wines.

The Miraval rosé, in the Côtes de Provence appellation, is based on Cinsault, Grenache, Syrah and Rolle (Vermentino), and combines estate grapes with those of local growers. The range has expanded to include other rosés, whites and a rosé Champagne.

The house style reflects modern Provence rosé: pale colour, freshness and delicate fruit, with a little more texture thanks to Rolle and to partial oak ageing of a portion of the wine.
