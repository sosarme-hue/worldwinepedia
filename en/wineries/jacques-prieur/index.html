Domaine Jacques Prieur

Meursault domaine with vineyards in some of the most celebrated grands crus of the Côte d’Or, both white and red, from Montrachet to Musigny and Chambertin.

Domaine Jacques Prieur is based in Meursault, in the Côte de Beaune, and is one of the Burgundy estates with the most prestigious vineyard holdings. Founded by the Prieur family, since the late 1980s it has also had the involvement of the Labruyère family, who have driven its modernisation while keeping vineyard management and winemaking at the estate itself.

Its distinctive feature is the breadth of its vineyards, spread along the whole Côte d’Or. It owns plots in white grands crus such as Montrachet, Chevalier-Montrachet and Corton-Charlemagne, and in red grands crus such as Musigny, Chambertin and Clos de Vougeot. To these are added many premiers crus in Meursault, Puligny-Montrachet, Beaune and Volnay, including two monopoles: the Clos de Mazeray in Meursault and the Clos des Santenots, classified as Volnay 1er Cru.

The house works with Chardonnay and Pinot Noir and vinifies each plot separately, with ageing in oak barrels, aiming for concentrated wines with good ageing potential that reflect the character of each terroir. Its whites tend to show richness and texture, and its reds structure and depth. Alongside the grands and premiers crus, the range includes regional and village-level wines, which offer a first approach to the house style from more modest plots of vines.
