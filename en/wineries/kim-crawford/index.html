Kim Crawford

New Zealand brand founded in 1996 by the winemaker Kim Crawford and his wife Erica, whose Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc is one of the best-selling in the world.

Kim Crawford was born in 1996 as a small project of the New Zealand winemaker Kim Crawford and his wife Erica. In its early days the company had neither vineyards nor a winery of its own: it bought grapes from growers and made wine in rented facilities, a flexible model that allowed it to grow quickly and to select fruit from different areas.

The wine that made the brand famous was its Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc, lively and highly aromatic in style, which met with notable success in North America. The brand was acquired in the early 2000s by a wine group and is now part of the portfolio of Constellation Brands, which has made it one of the most widely distributed New Zealand labels internationally. The founders are no longer involved in the company.

Marlborough, in the north-east of the South Island, offers the conditions that explain the character of these wines: long hours of sunshine, cool nights that preserve acidity and well-drained alluvial soils in the Wairau Valley and its side valleys. The result is whites with intense aromas and marked acidity that are very recognisable in the glass.

Besides Sauvignon Blanc, the range includes Pinot Gris, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and rosé, as well as some more ambitious selections. The overall style favours fresh fruit and aromatic clarity, with winemaking carried out mostly in stainless steel tanks.
