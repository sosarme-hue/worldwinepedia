Maison Joseph Drouhin

Family-owned Beaune house founded in 1880, with its own vineyards in Chablis, the Côte de Nuits and the Côte de Beaune, and an offshoot in Oregon.

Joseph Drouhin, a native of Chablis, founded a wine trading house in Beaune in 1880. His son Maurice and later his grandson Robert Drouhin steadily expanded the estate vineyard, with plots in some of the most highly regarded sites of the Côte d’Or and in Chablis. Today the house is still family-owned and run by the fourth generation.

The house’s cellars run beneath the historic centre of Beaune and make use of very old galleries, some of them medieval. One of its most emblematic vineyards is the Clos des Mouches, a Beaune Premier Cru from which the house makes both a white and a red. In Chablis it operates under the name Drouhin Vaudon.

The family was among the first in Burgundy to adopt organic and biodynamic viticulture in its own vineyards, from the 1990s. In 1987 it created Domaine Drouhin Oregon in the United States, devoted to Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

The Drouhin style is associated with wines of great finesse and balance, favouring the expression of fruit and terroir over oak.

In addition to its own vineyards, the house buys grapes and wine from growers throughout Burgundy, as is usual for négociants-éleveurs.
