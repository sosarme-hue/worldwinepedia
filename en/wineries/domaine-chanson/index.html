Domaine Chanson

One of Burgundy’s oldest houses, founded in Beaune in 1750 and part of the Champagne Bollinger group since 1999, with its own vineyards in the Côte de Beaune.

Domaine Chanson, long known as Chanson Père et Fils, is one of the oldest wine houses in Burgundy. Its origins date back to 1750, when a wine business was founded in Beaune which, over the generations, passed into the hands of the Chanson family and took its name. In 1999 the house was acquired by the Champagne Bollinger group, which undertook a major renovation of the vineyards and winery.

The house is headquartered in Beaune, and one of its hallmarks is the Bastion de l'Oratoire, a former bastion of the town’s ramparts with thick stone walls, used for ageing the wines in bottle and barrel.

Chanson owns vineyards in some of the most renowned sites of the Côte de Beaune, above all in Beaune, Savigny-lès-Beaune, Pernand-Vergelesses and the hill of Corton, including several premiers crus and grand cru plots. One of its emblematic wines is the Beaune 1er Cru Clos des Mouches, made in both white and red. Alongside the estate wines, the house still buys grapes and wine for its négociant range, which covers other parts of Burgundy, from Chablis to the Côte Chalonnaise.

The house style seeks precise wines that express their origin: taut, age-worthy Chardonnay whites and Pinot Noir reds with clear fruit and fine structure.
