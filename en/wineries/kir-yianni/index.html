Winery

Kir-Yianni

A winery in Greek Macedonia founded in 1997 by Yiannis Boutaris, with estates in Naoussa and Amyndeon. It is one of the leading references for Xinomavro, with wines such as Ramnista Naoussa.

Kir-Yianni was created in 1997 by Yiannis Boutaris, a member of the family that founded the Boutari house, who decided to devote himself to his own project centred on his vineyards in Naoussa. The winery’s name, which could be translated as “Mr Yiannis”, alludes to its founder. From the outset it has stood out for detailed vineyard work and for its search for a modern, precise expression of Xinomavro.

The main estate is in Yiannakohori, on the slopes of Mount Vermio, within the Naoussa appellation. There the vineyard is divided into numerous plots with different soils and exposures, which are often vinified separately. The winery also owns a second estate in Amyndeon, a higher, cooler plateau in north-western Macedonia, where Xinomavro for rosé and sparkling wines is grown, along with white varieties.

Its most emblematic wine is Ramnista, a barrel-aged Xinomavro from Naoussa. The range also includes blends of Xinomavro with international varieties such as Syrah or Merlot, sold as geographical indication wines, rosés from Amyndeon and whites. The house style seeks to preserve Xinomavro’s acidity and tension, softening its tannins through harvesting at just the right ripeness and careful ageing.

Its work with Xinomavro, in aged reds as well as in rosés and sparkling wines, illustrates the versatility of a variety often considered difficult.
