Winery

Helan Qingxue

A Ningxia winery at the foot of the Helan Mountains, whose red Jia Bei Lan was one of the first wines from the region to gain international recognition. Its winemaker, Zhang Jing, is one of the best-known figures in Chinese wine.

Helan Qingxue is a medium-sized winery on the eastern foothills of the Helan Mountains, in Ningxia. Its name refers to the snow that covers the peaks of this range in winter. It was created in the 2000s, in the early years of the region’s viticultural expansion, and from the beginning focused on making quality reds from Bordeaux varieties.

The winery’s technical director has been the winemaker Zhang Jing, who made her name with the red Jia Bei Lan. In the early 2010s this wine was one of the first from Ningxia to catch the attention of international critics, which did much to put the region on the world wine map.

The vineyards are farmed on stony and sandy soils, in the dry, sunny, continental climate of the area, with large differences between day and night temperatures. As elsewhere in Ningxia, the vines are buried every autumn to protect them from the winter cold, and viticulture depends on irrigation. The range focuses on barrel-aged reds from Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, sometimes with other Bordeaux varieties, and is completed by a few whites. The style seeks to combine the region’s ripe fruit with structure and freshness. Like other pioneering wineries in Ningxia, it has helped to show that the region can produce reds of international standard.
