José Maria da Fonseca

Family house founded in 1834 in Azeitão, on the Setúbal peninsula, known for its Moscatel de Setúbal, the Periquita red and Lancers rosé.

José Maria da Fonseca was founded in 1834 in Vila Nogueira de Azeitão, at the foot of the Arrábida hills, and is one of the oldest wine companies in Portugal. Its founder, who gave the house its name, planted vineyards in the area and developed two wines above all: Moscatel de Setúbal and a Castelão red from an estate called Cova da Periquita, the origin of the Periquita brand, which in time became one of the best-known Portuguese wines. The company is still owned by his descendants, the Soares Franco family.

In the twentieth century the house launched Lancers rosé, initially intended for the American market. Beyond the Setúbal peninsula, the company owns vineyards in the Alentejo, where it owns the historic José de Sousa winery in Reguengos de Monsaraz, known for keeping alive winemaking in clay amphorae.

Moscatel de Setúbal is one of its specialities. The house keeps very old stocks of this wine in its Azeitão cellars and sells versions of different ages, such as Alambre, as well as Moscatel Roxo, made from the region’s rare pink-skinned variety. Its historic headquarters in Vila Nogueira de Azeitão, known as the Casa-Museu, welcomes visitors.

Its range also includes reds and whites from the Setúbal peninsula and the Alentejo, from everyday wines to more ambitious bottlings.

Visits & wine tourism

Its historic headquarters in Vila Nogueira de Azeitão offers guided cellar tours with tastings.
