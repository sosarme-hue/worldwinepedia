Clos des Papes

Avril family domaine in Châteauneuf-du-Pape, with vineyards spread over numerous parcels across the appellation and a long-lived red and white.

Clos des Papes belongs to the Avril family, who have been present in Châteauneuf-du-Pape for centuries and have been involved in village life for generations. The domaine’s name evokes the history of the village, linked to the Avignon popes, who had their summer residence there. Paul Avril was a leading figure in the appellation in the 20th century, and his son Paul-Vincent Avril has run the domaine since the late 1980s, now joined by the next generation.

One of the house’s particularities is the dispersal of its vineyard, divided into numerous parcels spread over different sectors of the appellation, with soils of rounded pebbles, clay, sand and limestone. For the Avrils, this diversity makes it possible to achieve a balanced blend every year, offsetting differences in ripening between areas.

The domaine essentially makes two wines, a red and a white. The red is based on Grenache, with a significant share of Mourvèdre, plus Syrah and other authorised varieties. The white blends several of the appellation’s white varieties and is known for its ability to age, something less common among southern Rhône whites.

The style of Clos des Papes favours finesse and freshness over power, with low yields and ageing that does not seek to mark the wine with oak.
