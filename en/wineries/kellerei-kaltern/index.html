Kellerei Kaltern – Cantina Kaltern

A cooperative winery in Caldaro (Kaltern), in Alto Adige, bringing together growers from around Lake Caldaro and producing a wide range of whites and reds.

Caldaro, Kaltern in German, is one of the most important wine-growing municipalities in Alto Adige (South Tyrol), the German-speaking province of northern Italy. Located on the so-called Wine Road south of Bolzano, it surrounds Lake Caldaro, whose name is traditionally associated with light reds made from Schiava (Vernatsch).

As in much of Alto Adige, local viticulture is largely organised around cooperatives, in which many small growers bring their grapes to a shared winery. Kellerei Kaltern is the town’s leading cooperative and is the result of the union of several historic cooperatives in the municipality.

Its members’ vineyards are spread across slopes and terraces at different altitudes around the lake, with varied soils of limestone, moraine and alluvial origin. This diversity allows the cultivation of white varieties — Pinot Bianco, Pinot Grigio, Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Gewürztraminer — as well as reds — Schiava, Pinot Nero, Lagrein, Cabernet and Merlot.

The range is organised into several lines, from classic varietal wines to named selections from specific sites. The general house style seeks clean wines with precise fruit and good freshness, in keeping with the alpine character of Alto Adige.

As in other Alto Adige cooperatives, the winery advises its members on vineyard work and sorts the grapes according to their origin, assigning them to its different lines.
