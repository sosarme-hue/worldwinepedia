Winery

Viñas del Vero

One of the founding wineries of modern Somontano, based near Barbastro; it belongs to González Byass and makes wines such as Secastilla and Blecua.

Viñas del Vero was founded in the 1980s, when the Somontano D.O., at the foot of the Pyrenees in Huesca, was beginning to build its modern identity. Its name comes from the river Vero, which runs through the area and the town of Barbastro. It was one of the wineries that, together with others in the area, demonstrated Somontano's potential for wines from both native and international varieties. Since 2008 it has belonged to the Jerez-based González Byass group.

Vineyards

Somontano has a continental climate moderated by the nearby mountains, with cold winters, warm summers and cool nights that favour acidity and aroma. The winery works with its own vineyards spread across different parts of the area, on sedimentary soils of clay and limestone. It grows Chardonnay, Gewürztraminer, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Tempranillo, Syrah and Pinot Noir, as well as Garnacha, long present in the northern valleys.

Wines

The range includes widely distributed varietal wines such as its Gewürztraminer and Chardonnay, which helped make Somontano known throughout Spain. In the Secastilla valley, in the north of the appellation, the winery recovered old Garnacha vines from which it makes Secastilla. At the top stands Blecua, a red made in a dedicated winery on the estate from a selection of the best plots.
