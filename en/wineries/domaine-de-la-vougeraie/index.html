Domaine de la Vougeraie

A Burgundy domaine created in 1999 by the Boisset family from its own vineyards, farmed biodynamically, with a wide range of wines from the Côte de Nuits and the Côte de Beaune.

Domaine de la Vougeraie was born in 1999, when the Boisset family, one of Burgundy’s major wine groups, decided to bring together under a single name the vineyards it owned in the Côte d’Or and the surrounding area. The domaine’s name pays tribute to the village of Vougeot and its famous Clos, and the winery is in Premeaux-Prissey, south of Nuits-Saint-Georges.

The domaine brings together a highly fragmented vineyard holding, as is usual in Burgundy, ranging from regional appellations to grands crus of the Côte de Nuits and the Côte de Beaune. Its plots include two monopoles: the Clos Blanc de Vougeot, a premier cru vineyard planted with white grapes since the time of the Cistercian monks, and the Clos de Thorey in Nuits-Saint-Georges.

The vineyards are farmed according to biodynamic principles, and winemaking seeks to express each plot, with careful vinification and barrel ageing that supports without dominating. The reds are made from Pinot Noir and the whites from Chardonnay, following the rules of the Burgundy appellations.

The broad and diverse range makes it possible to travel through much of the hierarchy of Burgundy’s climats, from village wines to some of the most highly regarded vineyards of the Côte.
