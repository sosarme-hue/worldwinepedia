Campo Viejo

Rioja brand created in 1959 and made in a modern winery on the outskirts of Logroño; it is one of the most widely distributed Rioja labels internationally.

Campo Viejo was born in 1959 in Logroño as a Rioja wine brand and, over the decades, became one of the most widespread Rioja labels on export markets. Its name evokes the traditional vineyard landscape of the region. Today it belongs to the international group Pernod Ricard, which brings together several wineries in Rioja and other parts of Spain.

At the beginning of the twenty-first century the brand opened a large winery on the outskirts of Logroño, built largely underground and making use of the slope of the land, to reduce its impact on the landscape and make it easier to control temperature during ageing. The facility houses a large barrel hall and is also designed to receive visitors.

The grapes come from vineyards spread across the different zones of the Denominación de Origen Calificada, with Tempranillo as the main variety, accompanied by Garnacha, Mazuelo (Carignan) and Graciano in the longer-aged wines. The range follows Rioja’s classic ladder, from a young, fruity Tempranillo to Reservas and Gran Reservas, as well as whites, rosés and a Cava.

The house style aims for approachable wines with clear fruit and well-integrated oak, easy to understand and to match with food. Thanks to its wide presence in supermarkets and online shops, Campo Viejo is for many consumers their first introduction to the wines of Rioja.

Visits & wine tourism

The Logroño winery offers guided tours with tastings, focusing on how its wines are made and aged.
