Casa Valduga

Family winery in the Vale dos Vinhedos, founded by descendants of Trentino immigrants who arrived in the Serra Gaúcha in 1875. It is particularly known for its traditional-method sparkling wines and for its pioneering role in the region’s wine tourism.

The Valdugas arrived in the Serra Gaúcha in 1875, in the first wave of immigration from northern Italy, and settled in the area that now forms the Vale dos Vinhedos, in Bento Gonçalves. For generations the family grew vines and made wine on a small scale. In the second half of the twentieth century, a new generation of brothers turned the family activity into a commercial winery and committed to European varieties and quality wines, at a time when the region was still dominated by American grapes.

The house has specialised above all in traditional-method sparkling wines, with a second fermentation in bottle and lees ageing in underground cellars, made from Chardonnay and Pinot Noir. Casa Valduga was also one of the first wineries in the region to open its doors to wine tourism, with accommodation and a restaurant on the estate itself, and it helped turn the Vale dos Vinhedos into a tourist destination. The company has grown into a family group with several brands and vineyards in different parts of southern Brazil beyond the Serra Gaúcha.

Its range includes brut, extra brut and nature sparkling wines, rosés and long-aged cuvées, among which Casa Valduga 130 stands out, created to commemorate one hundred and thirty years since the family’s arrival in Brazil. It also makes still wines from Chardonnay, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and other varieties, as well as sparkling Moscatel. The style of its sparkling wines combines freshness, fruit and the bread and pastry notes that come from lees ageing.

Visits & wine tourism

The Vale dos Vinhedos estate offers guided tours, tastings, a restaurant and accommodation.
