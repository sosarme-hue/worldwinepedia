Winery

Bodega Pisano

Family winery in Progreso, Canelones, founded by immigrants of Italian origin and run by the Pisano brothers. Its RPF line is one of the benchmarks for Uruguayan Tannat.

The Pisano family, of Italian origin, has been engaged for several generations in winegrowing in the Progreso area, in the department of Canelones, north of Montevideo. Like many wineries in the region, for decades it made wines for the domestic market. Transformation came in the 1990s, when the new generation, three brothers who divided between them the work of the vineyard, the winemaking and the sales, decided to steer the company towards quality wines and exports, at a time when few Uruguayan producers were doing so.

The winery works with its own vineyards in Progreso, on the clay and somewhat calcareous soils typical of Canelones, in a temperate, humid climate influenced by the Río de la Plata and the Atlantic. It is a medium-sized business that has kept its family character and a style of its own, often with wines of notable concentration.

Tannat is the central variety of its production. Among its lines, RPF stands out, the initials of “Reserva Personal de la Familia” (the family’s personal reserve), bringing together varietal wines from Tannat, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and other varieties selected by the family. The house also makes more ambitious blends, young reds, whites, rosés and sparkling wines, as well as sweet Tannat wines. Pisano has been one of the wineries that has done most to make Uruguayan Tannat known on international markets.
