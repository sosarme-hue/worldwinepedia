Winery

Domaine de Beaurenard

Coulon family estate in Châteauneuf-du-Pape, farmed biodynamically, producing red and white wines from this appellation and from Rasteau.

Domaine de Beaurenard is a family property based in Châteauneuf-du-Pape, in the southern Rhône valley, run for several generations by the Coulon family. In addition to its Châteauneuf-du-Pape vineyards, the domaine owns vines in Rasteau and also makes Côtes du Rhône.

The vineyard is farmed biodynamically, one of the features that most clearly identify the house. In Châteauneuf-du-Pape the plots are spread across different soil types within the appellation, from the famous rounded pebbles to areas of sand and clay, and are planted with the many authorised varieties, with Grenache as the basis of the reds.

The Châteauneuf-du-Pape range includes a classic red and white and the Boisrenard cuvée, in both colours, from old vines and conceived as the estate’s most ambitious wine. The reds are aged in a combination of vessels, such as vats, large casks and barrels, to balance Grenache’s fruit with the structure brought by Syrah and Mourvèdre.

The domaine’s style is associated with generous yet balanced wines that seek freshness and precision within the richness typical of the southern Rhône’s Mediterranean climate, and which age well.

Châteauneuf-du-Pape, whose name recalls the presence of the popes in Avignon in the 14th century, was one of France’s first appellations of origin, and the domaine belongs to the families with a long history in the village.
