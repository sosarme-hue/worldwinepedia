Oller del Mas

An estate with a castle on the outskirts of Manresa, owned by the same family for centuries, with vineyards in Pla de Bages.

Oller del Mas is an estate with a castle on the outskirts of Manresa, the capital of Bages, owned by the same family for many generations. Its vineyards are part of the Pla de Bages D.O.

It makes whites and reds from local varieties such as Picapoll and from international ones, and combines wine with other farming and tourism activities on the estate.
