Winery

Cricova

A large Moldovan state-owned winery founded in 1952 in Cricova, near Chișinău, famous for its kilometres of underground galleries carved in limestone and its traditional-method sparkling wines.

Cricova is one of Moldova’s best-known wineries and one of the most unusual in the world. It is located in the town of the same name, a few kilometres from the capital, Chișinău, in the Codru region, and it is a state-owned company. It was founded in 1952, in the Soviet era, making use of old underground limestone quarries from which stone had been extracted for building for centuries.

These quarries form a network of galleries many kilometres long, tens of metres below ground, where temperature and humidity remain stable all year round. The galleries are laid out like a genuine underground city, with streets named after wines along which visitors can travel by vehicle. This environment is ideal for maturing sparkling wines and storing bottles, and the winery keeps a large collection of old wines from various countries.

Cricova is known above all for its traditional-method sparkling wines, with second fermentation and bottle ageing in the galleries, in styles ranging from brut to medium-sweet. It also produces still white and red wines from varieties such as Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Fetească Albă, Pinot Noir, Cabernet Sauvignon and Fetească Neagră. Because of its history and its facilities, the winery is one of Moldova’s main tourist attractions and a national symbol of the country’s wine tradition.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers guided tours of its underground galleries, which are visited by vehicle, with tastings of its wines; booking ahead is advisable.
