Winery

Ceretto

Alba family house with vineyards in great Barolo and Barbaresco crus, known for its Blangè Arneis white and its links with contemporary art.

The company was founded in Alba by Riccardo Ceretto in the period between the two world wars. His sons Bruno and Marcello Ceretto transformed it from the 1960s onwards: instead of simply buying grapes, they began to acquire vineyards in some of the most prestigious crus of the Langhe, a commitment to the idea of the cru that was then unusual in the area.

This gave rise to estates such as Bricco Rocche, in Castiglione Falletto, and Bricco Asili, in Barbaresco, later joined by plots in Barolo crus such as Brunate and Prapò. The house is headquartered at the Monsordo Bernardina estate on the outskirts of Alba. The family also owns the chapel of the Madonna delle Grazie, among the vineyards of La Morra, known as the “Barolo Chapel” for its brightly coloured decoration by the artists Sol LeWitt and David Tremlett.

Ceretto enjoyed particular success with Blangè, an Arneis white that helped to make the variety known beyond Piedmont. Today the company is in the hands of the third generation of the family, who have converted the estate vineyards to organic farming with practices inspired by biodynamics.

Besides Barolo and Barbaresco from several vineyards, the range includes Langhe Nebbiolo, Barbera d’Alba, Dolcetto d’Alba and Moscato d’Asti. The style aims for precise wines that express their place of origin.
