Pago de Carraovejas

Peñafiel winery founded in the late 1980s by Segovian restaurateur José María Ruiz, with its own vineyards on the slopes facing the castle.

Pago de Carraovejas was born in the late 1980s on the initiative of José María Ruiz, a well-known restaurateur from Segovia who wanted to make a Ribera del Duero red designed to accompany traditional Castilian cooking, especially roast lamb. For this he chose a hillside site in Peñafiel, in the valley of the Botijas stream, facing the town’s castle.

The winery’s vineyards lie on slopes with different aspects and altitudes, on limestone and clay soils, which makes it possible to work plots with different profiles. The main variety is Tinto Fino (Tempranillo), joined by Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot in some wines.

Over time the family has built a wine group, known as Alma Carraovejas, which includes projects in other Spanish regions, such as Ossian in the Segovia area, Viña Meín in Ribeiro and wineries in Rioja and Ribera del Duero. The Peñafiel estate also houses a gastronomic restaurant.

The wine that bears the house name, Pago de Carraovejas, is a barrel-aged red with a ripe, structured profile. The range also includes small-production plot selections. The style seeks to combine the power of Ribera del Duero with freshness and precision.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers tours of the estate and winery with a tasting, as well as its restaurant, by prior booking.
