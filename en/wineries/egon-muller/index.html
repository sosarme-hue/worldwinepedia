Weingut Egon Müller – Scharzhof

Family estate in the Saar valley, owner of most of the Scharzhofberg vineyard and famous for its Prädikat Rieslings with sweetness, among the most sought-after white wines in the world.

The Scharzhof, at the foot of the Scharzhofberg near Wiltingen, belonged in the Middle Ages to a religious institution in Trier. After the French occupation of the left bank of the Rhine, church property was auctioned off, and in 1797 the estate was bought by Jean-Jacques Koch, an ancestor of the Müller family, which has run it ever since. It is now headed by Egon Müller, the fourth of the family to bear that name.

The Scharzhofberg is a south-facing slate slope in the Saar valley, one of the coolest tributaries of the Mosel. This climate, at the limit of Riesling ripening, produces wines of very high acidity and great delicacy. The estate owns most of the vineyard, with a significant proportion of old ungrafted vines, and also manages the Le Gallais estate, with vines in the Wiltinger Braune Kupp.

The house is best known for its Rieslings with residual sugar in the traditional Prädikat categories: Kabinett, Spätlese, Auslese and, in years when noble rot allows, Beerenauslese, Trockenbeerenauslese and Eiswein, made in very small quantities. Winemaking is traditional, with fermentation in tanks and large wooden casks and a style that seeks to convey the vineyard transparently. Egon Müller’s wines are renowned for their exceptional longevity. The family has also been involved in a wine project in Slovakia.
