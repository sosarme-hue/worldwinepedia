Marie-Thérèse Chappaz

A small estate in Fully, in the Valais, farmed biodynamically by Marie-Thérèse Chappaz and famed for its sweet Petite Arvine and Marsanne wines from grapes shrivelled on the vine.

Marie-Thérèse Chappaz is one of Switzerland’s most highly regarded winegrowers. She took over family vines in Fully, on the right bank of the Rhône in the Valais, and gradually expanded her estate with plots in Fully and the neighbouring villages. Her vineyards lie on steep, terraced slopes, with granite and gneiss soils around Fully and other plots on soils of different origin, which allows her to work with a wide variety of terroirs.

For years she has farmed her vines according to the principles of biodynamic agriculture, an unusual choice in the Valais when she adopted it and one that made her a reference for other producers in the region. In the cellar she seeks to intervene as little as possible and to respect the character of each plot.

Her fame rests above all on her sweet wines, made from Petite Arvine and Ermitage grapes, as Marsanne is called in the Valais, which are left on the vine until late autumn so that they shrivel and, in some years, are affected by noble rot. She is a member of Grain Noble ConfidenCiel, an association of Valais producers that sets strict rules for sweet wines from grapes naturally concentrated on the vine. She also makes dry wines from Fendant, Petite Arvine, Pinot Noir, Gamay, Humagne Rouge and other varieties, precise and expressive in style, in limited quantities.
