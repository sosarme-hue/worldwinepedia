Segura Viudas

Cava producer in the Alt Penedès, based in an old farmhouse in Torrelavit, known for its Brut Reserva and its Reserva Heredad; it belongs to the Freixenet group.

Segura Viudas is based in an old masía, or farmhouse, in the municipality of Torrelavit, in the Alt Penedès, surrounded by vineyards and with origins going back to medieval times. The estate, known as Heredad Segura Viudas, gives its name to the house's most emblematic Cava. For decades the winery has belonged to the Freixenet group, one of the world's largest sparkling wine producers, though it keeps its own identity and range.

Vineyards and winemaking

The Alt Penedès is an area of calcareous and clay soils, with a Mediterranean climate softened by altitude and by distance from the sea. Here the traditional Cava varieties are grown: Macabeo, which brings fruit and body; Xarel·lo, which gives structure and ageing potential; and Parellada, lighter and more floral. All the house's Cavas are made by the traditional method, with a second fermentation in bottle and ageing on the lees in underground cellars.

Range

Segura Viudas is best known for its Brut Reserva, a Cava widely distributed abroad, especially in the United States, which offers a dry, fruity style at an affordable price. At the top of the range stands the Reserva Heredad, presented in a bottle decorated with metal pieces recalling the estate's coat of arms, which comes from a selection of grapes and a longer ageing period. The winery also makes rosé and semi-dry Cavas and other long-aged wines.
