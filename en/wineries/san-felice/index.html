Winery

San Felice

A winery in Castelnuovo Berardenga, in the south of Chianti Classico, based in the old hamlet of San Felice and known for its Chianti Classico Il Grigio and its work with forgotten Tuscan varieties.

San Felice is based in the hamlet of the same name, a small settlement of medieval origin in the municipality of Castelnuovo Berardenga, at the southern end of Chianti Classico near Siena. The complex, which brings together the winery, the vineyards and restored historic buildings, is today also a hotel.

In this part of Chianti Classico the climate is somewhat warmer, and the soils combine clays, limestone and the characteristic alberese, which favours good ripening of Sangiovese. The winery makes a range of Chianti Classico from the basic wine to the Riserva Il Grigio, its best-known label, and the Gran Selezione. It also produces Vigorello, regarded as one of the first Tuscan reds made outside the rules of the appellation, the so-called “Super Tuscans”.

San Felice is also known for its work recovering almost-vanished native Tuscan varieties, in collaboration with university institutions, which has brought grapes such as Pugnitello back into cultivation. Outside Chianti, the group owns the Campogiovanni estate in Montalcino, devoted to Brunello.

The house style seeks Sangiovese reds with precise fruit and good acidity, with oak ageing that supports the structure of the variety.

The winery is one of the historic producers of the Castelnuovo Berardenga area, one of the southernmost and sunniest parts of Chianti Classico, where Sangiovese tends to give riper, rounder wines than in the higher northern communes.

Visits & wine tourism

The hamlet of San Felice houses a hotel and offers the opportunity to visit the winery and taste its wines.
