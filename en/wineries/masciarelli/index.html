Masciarelli

Winery founded in 1981 by Gianni Masciarelli in San Martino sulla Marrucina (Chieti), one of those that brought prestige to Montepulciano and Trebbiano d’Abruzzo.

Gianni Masciarelli founded the winery in 1981 in San Martino sulla Marrucina, in the province of Chieti, starting from his family’s vineyards. In a region then known mainly for high-volume wines, he set out to show that Montepulciano and Trebbiano d’Abruzzo could produce quality wines capable of ageing.

Together with his wife, the Croatian winemaker Marina Cvetic, Masciarelli gradually expanded the vineyards across the four provinces of Abruzzo, between the Maiella massif and the Adriatic Sea, paying particular attention to lowering yields. After Gianni’s death in 2008, Marina Cvetic and later their daughter Miriam have led the company.

The winery is organised into several lines. The Classica range, the most widely available, includes a Montepulciano d’Abruzzo, a Trebbiano d’Abruzzo and a Cerasuolo d’Abruzzo, the region’s traditional rosé. The Marina Cvetic line and the Villa Gemma wines represent the house’s most ambitious wines.

The family also owns Castello di Semivicoli, a historic residence surrounded by vineyards that operates as accommodation.
