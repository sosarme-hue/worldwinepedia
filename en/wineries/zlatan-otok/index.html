Winery

Zlatan Otok

The Plenković family winery in Sveta Nedjelja, on the south coast of the island of Hvar, known for its Plavac Mali reds from very steep slopes above the sea.

Zlatan Otok, “golden island”, is the winery founded by Zlatan Plenković on the island of Hvar in Dalmatia. The Plenković family, natives of the island, had grown vines for generations, and Zlatan Plenković turned that tradition into one of Croatia’s best-known private wineries, which is still in family hands today.

The heart of the estate is at Sveta Nedjelja, on the south coast of Hvar, where the vines occupy very steep, stony slopes dropping straight into the Adriatic. There the combination of direct sunshine, light reflected from the sea and limestone that stores heat allows Plavac Mali to reach great ripeness. The vines are largely tended by hand, often on small terraces,. Farming these slopes, fully exposed to the sun and with little soil between the stones, demands great effort and gives low yields, but grapes of great concentration.

The house’s emblematic wine is Zlatan Plavac Grand Cru, from the slopes of Sveta Nedjelja, a powerful, concentrated red aged in barrel. The range includes other Plavac Mali reds and whites from Dalmatian varieties such as Pošip, Bogdanuša and Maraština. The winery’s style seeks to reflect the intensity of the Hvar sun and the Mediterranean character of its vineyards.
