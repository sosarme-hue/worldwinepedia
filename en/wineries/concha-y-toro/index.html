Viña Concha y Toro

Founded in 1883 by Melchor de Santiago Concha y Toro in Pirque, in the Maipo Valley, it is Chile’s largest wine company and one of the biggest in the world. Its range runs from Casillero del Diablo to the top red Don Melchor.

Viña Concha y Toro was founded in 1883 by Melchor de Santiago Concha y Toro, a Chilean politician and businessman, who imported French vines and planted them on his estate at Pirque, at the foot of the Andes in Alto Maipo. The manor house and park from that period still survive and are part of the estate’s visitor tour.

Over the twentieth century the company grew to become Chile’s largest wine producer and exporter. Today it owns vineyards in most of the country’s valleys and manages brands at very different levels. Its most widely sold wine is Casillero del Diablo, whose name comes from a legend: according to tradition, to deter those who were stealing his best wines, Don Melchor spread the rumour that the devil lived in his cellar. At the other end of the range is Don Melchor, a Cabernet Sauvignon from the Puente Alto vineyard, which since 2019 has been made at its own dedicated winery within the group.

The company also makes lines such as Marques de Casa Concha, named after the family’s noble title, and Terrunyo. Outside Chile it owns the Trivento winery in Mendoza and Fetzer in California. Together with the Rothschild family of Baron Philippe de Rothschild it created the Almaviva project in Puente Alto. Concha y Toro has been one of the companies that have contributed most to the international presence of Chilean wine.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery offers tours and tastings, usually by prior booking.
