Winery

Silver Heights

A small family winery in Ningxia run by the winemaker Emma Gao, who trained in Bordeaux. It was one of the first wineries in the region to attract international attention, with Bordeaux-style reds such as The Summit.

Silver Heights is a family project created in Ningxia by the Gao family. Its winemaker, Emma Gao, studied oenology in Bordeaux and worked at Bordeaux wineries before returning to China, where she began making wine in the second half of the 2000s, at first in very modest premises. The early vintages attracted the attention of critics and international professionals at a time when Ningxia wine was still little known outside China, and the winery became one of the names that put the region on the map.

The vineyards lie on the eastern foothills of the Helan Mountains, on stony and sandy soils, in the dry, continental climate typical of Ningxia. As elsewhere in the region, winters are so cold that the vines must be buried every autumn to protect them from frost, and viticulture depends on irrigation. The winery works with moderate yields and pays particular attention to vineyard work.

Production is small and focuses on reds from Bordeaux varieties, above all Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, aged in barrel. The best-known wine is The Summit, its flagship red, and the range includes other reds and some Chardonnay whites. The house style, influenced by its winemaker’s Bordeaux training, seeks balance and freshness rather than power, with a measured use of oak.
