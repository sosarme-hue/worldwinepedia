Weingut Franz Hirtzberger

Family estate in Spitz, at the western end of the Wachau, renowned for its Riesling and Grüner Veltliner Smaragd from vineyards such as Singerriedel and Honivogl.

The Hirtzberger family has grown vines in Spitz an der Donau for several generations. Franz Hirtzberger, who ran the estate for decades, placed it among the most prestigious in Austria and also played a leading role in the Vinea Wachau association, of which the estate is a member. It is now run by his son, also named Franz Hirtzberger.

Spitz lies in the western part of the Wachau, the coolest part of the region, open to the air currents that flow down from the Waldviertel. The estate’s vines occupy very steep dry-stone terraces on gneiss and other primary rocks. The most emblematic vineyard is the Singerriedel, a terraced slope rising above the village, which the estate devotes mainly to Riesling. Other important vineyards are the Honivogl, known for its Grüner Veltliners, Hochrain, Setzberg and Axpoint. Cultivating these terraces requires very intensive manual work.

Production focuses on dry Riesling and Grüner Veltliner, in the Federspiel and Smaragd categories, together with small quantities of other varieties such as Weissburgunder and Grauburgunder. Hirtzberger’s Smaragd wines are known for their concentration, ripe fruit and texture, supported by the acidity typical of the western Wachau, and for their ability to develop for many years in bottle. Winemaking, in tanks and casks, aims to preserve the purity of the fruit and the character of each vineyard.
