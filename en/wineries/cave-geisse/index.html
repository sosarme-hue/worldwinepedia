Cave Geisse

Winery in Pinto Bandeira, in the Serra Gaúcha, founded by the Chilean winemaker Mario Geisse and devoted exclusively to traditional-method sparkling wines made from its own estate grapes.

Mario Geisse, a Chilean-born winemaker, came to Brazil in the 1970s to work in sparkling wine production in the Serra Gaúcha, at one of the foreign companies then setting up in the region. Convinced of the area’s potential for this style, he soon afterwards acquired land in Pinto Bandeira, a district of the highlands lying higher than much of the region, and planted vines with the aim of making his own sparkling wines. Thus Cave Geisse was born; it is now run by the family, with the involvement of the next generation.

The conditions in Pinto Bandeira, with temperatures somewhat cooler than in the lower parts of the Serra Gaúcha, soils of basaltic origin and good ventilation, yield grapes with high acidity and moderate sugar, ideal as a sparkling base. The winery works exclusively with grapes from its own vineyards, mainly Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, and makes all its wines by the traditional method, with a second fermentation in bottle and often lengthy lees ageing.

The range includes brut, extra brut and nature sparkling wines, Blanc de Blancs, rosés and long-aged vintage cuvées. The house style is dry, fresh and precise, with fine bubbles and the complexity that comes from contact with the lees. Cave Geisse is regarded as one of the wineries that have helped bring prestige to Brazilian sparkling wine and to the Pinto Bandeira geographical indication, which focuses on this type of wine.
