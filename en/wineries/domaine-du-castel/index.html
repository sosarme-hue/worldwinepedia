Domaine du Castel

A family winery in the Judean Hills, near Jerusalem, created by Eli Ben Zaken from a vineyard planted in the late 1980s. It was one of the pioneers of small-production wines in Israel, with Bordeaux-style reds and Chardonnay whites.

Domaine du Castel grew out of the personal project of Eli Ben Zaken, who in the late 1980s planted a small vineyard of Bordeaux varieties in the Judean Hills, west of Jerusalem. The first wines, made in very small quantities in the early 1990s, attracted attention for their quality and showed the potential of these hills for Bordeaux-style reds. Castel thus became one of the country’s first “boutique” wineries and a model for many later projects. The Ben Zaken family has been involved in running the winery throughout its history.

The vineyards lie on slopes in the Judean Hills, several hundred metres above sea level, on limestone and terra rossa soils. The altitude brings cooler nights than on the coastal plain, which helps keep the grapes in balance in a Mediterranean climate of hot, dry summers.

The range is short. The main red is the Grand Vin, a barrel-aged Bordeaux-style blend based on Cabernet Sauvignon, accompanied by Petit Castel, of a somewhat more approachable profile. Among the whites, the “C” Blanc du Castel, made from barrel-fermented Chardonnay, stands out, and the winery also produces a rosé. The wines are kosher. The house style seeks elegance and balance, with careful use of oak.
