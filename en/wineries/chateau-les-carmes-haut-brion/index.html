Château Les Carmes Haut-Brion

Small estate in the Pessac-Léognan appellation located within the city of Bordeaux, next to Haut-Brion, known for the high proportion of Cabernet Franc in its vineyard.

Château Les Carmes Haut-Brion is an unusual estate: its small vineyard lies within the municipal boundaries of Bordeaux itself, surrounded by urban areas and next to the property of Château Haut-Brion. It belongs to the Pessac-Léognan appellation, in the Graves area.

Its name recalls the Carmelite friars (the Grands Carmes of Bordeaux), who owned and farmed this land from the sixteenth to the eighteenth century, until the French Revolution nationalised and sold it. In the nineteenth century the estate passed to a family of Bordeaux merchants whose descendants kept it until 2010, when it was acquired by the businessman Patrice Pichet. Since then a new winery of contemporary design has been built and quality has been given a strong boost.

The vineyard sits on gravel and clay and, unlike most estates on the Left Bank, gives a very prominent role to Cabernet Franc, alongside Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot. The urban setting also creates a microclimate slightly warmer than that of the nearby rural areas.

The house style is characterised by aromatic, fresh wines with fine tannins and great elegance, in which Cabernet Franc brings floral and spicy notes. The estate is not included in the Graves classification, but it has earned a prominent place among the reds of Pessac-Léognan.
