Tenuta di Biserno

Estate on the Tuscan coast, at Bibbona, created by the brothers Lodovico and Piero Antinori, focused on reds from Bordeaux varieties.

Tenuta di Biserno is located in Bibbona, in the province of Livorno, on the stretch of the Tuscan coast north of Bolgheri. The estate was created by Lodovico Antinori, founder of Tenuta dell’Ornellaia, together with his brother Piero Antinori, who for decades headed the house of Marchesi Antinori. It is a family project of their own and is not part of the Marchesi Antinori company.

The vineyards were planted on the hills of Bibbona, in a setting similar to Bolgheri: a Mediterranean climate tempered by sea breezes, good sunshine and varied soils with clay, sand and stones. As at other estates on the Tuscan coast, the main focus is on Bordeaux varieties, in particular Cabernet Franc, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Petit Verdot.

The estate’s flagship wine is Biserno, a red blend of these varieties sold under the Toscana geographical indication. The range includes other reds from the same area, such as Il Pino di Biserno, and more accessible wines, such as Insoglio del Cinghiale.

The house style seeks broad, ripe reds with generous fruit and polished tannins, in the vein of the great wines of the Tuscan coast, with barrel ageing and notable ageing potential in its top wines.
