Château Calon-Ségur

Saint-Estèphe third growth classified in 1855, famous for the heart on its label, linked to the story of the Marquis de Ségur.

Château Calon-Ségur lies in Saint-Estèphe, the northernmost of the great communes of the Médoc, on the left bank of the Gironde. It is one of the oldest estates in the area: the name Calon already appears in the Middle Ages, and in the 18th century the property passed to the Marquis Nicolas-Alexandre de Ségur, who also owned Lafite and Latour.

According to a remark attributed to him, the marquis said that although he made wine at Lafite and Latour, his heart was at Calon. This is the origin of the heart that adorns the château’s label and has become one of the most recognisable symbols of Bordeaux. In the 1855 classification Calon-Ségur was ranked as a third growth.

For much of the 20th century the estate belonged to the Gasqueton family, and since 2012 it has been owned by a French insurance group, which has funded an extensive renovation: vineyard restructuring, a new winery and a stricter selection for the grand vin.

The vineyard, largely enclosed by a stone wall, lies on gravel over clay and limestone. Cabernet Sauvignon dominates, completed by Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. The house style combines the tannic firmness typical of Saint-Estèphe with precise fruit and notable elegance, in very long-lived wines. The estate also makes a second wine, Le Marquis de Calon Ségur.
