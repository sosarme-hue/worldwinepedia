Bodegas Arzuaga Navarro

Family winery in Quintanilla de Onésimo, in the Valladolid part of Ribera del Duero, founded by Florentino Arzuaga, which combines aged reds with a hotel and restaurant on the estate.

Bodegas Arzuaga Navarro was created in the 1990s by businessman Florentino Arzuaga in Quintanilla de Onésimo, on the Valladolid stretch of the Duero known as the ‘Golden Mile’, home to some of the appellation’s most prestigious wineries. It is still a family business, in which his descendants are involved.

The property revolves around the La Planta estate, a large area of scrubland, pine forest and vineyard where vine growing coexists with a game reserve with deer and wild boar. The vineyards sit on soils of clay, limestone and rounded pebbles, at high altitude and with the continental climate typical of the Duero plateau.

Tinto Fino, the local name for Tempranillo, is the main variety, and in some wines it is complemented by small proportions of Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, depending on the vintage. The range includes a young oak-aged red, the Crianza, the Reserva and the Gran Reserva, as well as selection wines.

Arzuaga is also one of the best-known wine tourism projects in Ribera del Duero: next to the winery stands a hotel with spa and restaurant, where regional cuisine and produce from the estate accompany the house wines. The style of its reds is that of aged Ribera: ripe fruit, body and a noticeable oak presence.

Visits & wine tourism

The estate has a hotel, spa and restaurant, and the winery runs guided tours with tastings.
