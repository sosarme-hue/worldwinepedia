Winery

Meerlust

Historic Stellenbosch estate near False Bay, owned by the Myburgh family since the eighteenth century. It is best known for Rubicon, one of South Africa’s first Bordeaux blends.

Meerlust is one of the oldest estates in the Cape. Its land, in the southern part of the Stellenbosch district, a few kilometres from False Bay, was granted in the late seventeenth century, and in the mid-eighteenth century it passed to the Myburgh family, who have kept it ever since. The group of Cape Dutch buildings, with the manor house, cellars and other farm buildings, is regarded as an important architectural heritage. The proximity of the sea and the breezes coming off the bay influence the climate of its vineyards.

For much of the twentieth century the estate sold its wine to other companies. In the 1970s Nico Myburgh drove the replanting of the vineyards with Bordeaux varieties and the making of the estate’s own wines. The best-known result is Rubicon, whose first vintage was 1980 and which is among the first Bordeaux-style blends produced in South Africa. It is made from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Cabernet Franc, sometimes with a little Petit Verdot, and is aged in barrel before a long period in bottle.

Today the estate is run by Hannes Myburgh, Nico’s son. Besides Rubicon, Meerlust makes Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, the latter one of the house’s most highly regarded whites, as well as a sparkling wine. Its style is marked by restraint, freshness and ageing potential.

Visits & wine tourism

The estate has a tasting room open to the public.
