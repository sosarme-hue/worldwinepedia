Winery

Castello Banfi

Large Montalcino estate founded in 1978 by the American brothers John and Harry Mariani, known for its Brunello and for its research into Sangiovese clones.

Castello Banfi was founded in 1978 by John and Harry Mariani, owners of the American importer Banfi Vintners, which had been distributing Italian wine in the United States for decades. The brothers acquired a large property in the southern part of the Montalcino municipality, around the medieval castle of Poggio alle Mura, which is now the estate’s headquarters.

Banfi’s arrival represented a large-scale investment in an area that was then far less well known than it is today. The company replanted much of the land and carried out a research programme on Sangiovese clones, in collaboration with universities, to select those best suited to Montalcino’s soils and climate.

Besides Brunello di Montalcino, the estate produces Rosso di Montalcino and Toscana IGT wines in which Sangiovese is blended with international varieties, as well as whites and sparkling wines. The Mariani family also owns a winery in Piedmont.

The restored castle of Poggio alle Mura houses hospitality facilities and a museum devoted to glass and wine.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers winery tours and tastings, as well as dining and accommodation around the castle.
