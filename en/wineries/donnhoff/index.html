Weingut Hermann Dönnhoff

Family estate in Oberhausen that has placed the Nahe among the great Riesling regions, with precise wines from vineyards such as Hermannshöhle, Brücke and Felsenberg.

The Dönnhoff family has been settled in Oberhausen, on the banks of the Nahe, since the eighteenth century, and for a long time combined winegrowing with other farming activities. It was Helmut Dönnhoff who, from the 1970s onwards, turned the estate into one of the most highly regarded in Germany, enlarging its vineyards by buying plots in the best sites of the middle Nahe. It is now run by his son Cornelius Dönnhoff.

The house’s most famous vineyard is the Niederhäuser Hermannshöhle, a slope of complex soils with slate and volcanic rock, considered one of Germany’s great vineyards. The estate is also the sole owner of the Oberhäuser Brücke, a small vineyard beside the bridge over the river, whose cool, damp microclimate favours wines with sweetness and Eiswein. Other leading vineyards are the Schlossböckelheimer Felsenberg, Norheimer Dellchen and Kirschheck.

Dönnhoff makes dry Rieslings, including Grosses Gewächs from several vineyards, and Rieslings with sweetness in the Kabinett, Spätlese and Auslese categories, as well as nobly sweet wines and Eiswein in suitable years. It also produces Weissburgunder and Grauburgunder. The wines are marked by their precision, their balance between fruit and acidity and their expression of the geological diversity of the Nahe, and are largely fermented and aged in traditional wooden casks.
