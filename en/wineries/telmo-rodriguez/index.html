Compañía de Vinos Telmo Rodríguez

Company founded in 1994 by winemaker Telmo Rodríguez and Pablo Eguzkiza to make wines in various Spanish regions, centred on reviving traditional vineyards and varieties.

Telmo Rodríguez grew up at Remelluri, his family’s estate in Rioja Alavesa, and trained as a winemaker in Bordeaux. In 1994, together with his fellow student Pablo Eguzkiza, he founded the Compañía de Vinos Telmo Rodríguez, a project without a single winery that set out to work in different regions of Spain to rescue old vineyards, local varieties and styles at risk of disappearing.

The company has developed wines in very diverse areas. In Rioja it makes the Lanzaga range, with the red LZ as its entry-level wine; in Rueda, the white Basa; in Toro, Dehesa Gago; in Valdeorras, the Godello Gaba do Xil; and in Málaga, the sweet Molino Real, which helped to revive the Moscatel of the Málaga mountains. It has also worked in Ribera del Duero and Cigales, among other regions.

The house philosophy gives more importance to the vineyard and the village than to cellar practices. Rodríguez and Eguzkiza have publicly advocated a classification of Spanish wines based on origin —region, village and plot—, inspired by the Burgundian model. In the vineyard they favour organic farming, manual work and, where possible, the preservation of bush-trained vines.

Their wines range from affordable, widely distributed labels to limited single-plot wines, all in a style that favours freshness and transparency of origin.
