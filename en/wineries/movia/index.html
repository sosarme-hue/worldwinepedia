Winery

Movia

The Kristančič family estate in Goriška Brda, with vines on both sides of the Italian-Slovenian border, known for its biodynamic farming, its macerated whites and its Puro sparkling wine.

Movia is the estate of the Kristančič family in Ceglo, in Goriška Brda, at the western tip of Slovenia. The family has grown vines in these hills since the nineteenth century, and the border drawn after the Second World War left part of its vineyards in Slovenia and part in the Italian Collio, a situation the estate retains today. Under the direction of Aleš Kristančič, Movia has become one of the best-known Slovenian producers internationally.

The vineyards, on the flysch marl and sandstone soils typical of the area, are farmed according to biodynamic principles. In the cellar the house practises minimal intervention: spontaneous fermentations, long ageing in wood and very little sulphur. It is one of the wineries that have done most to make the long skin-macerated whites of the border region known.

Its best-known wines include Puro, a sparkling wine sold undisgorged, with its lees in the bottle, so that the consumer disgorges it on opening, and Lunar, a Rebula white made from whole berries macerated in barrel without intervention, following the phases of the moon. The range also includes aged whites and reds, such as the Veliko Belo and Veliko Rdeče blends, and wines from varieties such as Rebula, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Merlot, all with a highly distinctive style.
