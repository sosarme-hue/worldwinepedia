Winery

La Crema

Sonoma winery founded in 1979 and owned since 1993 by Jackson Family Wines, specialising in Chardonnay and Pinot Noir from cool-climate areas.

La Crema was born in 1979 in Sonoma County under the name La Crema Viñera, with the ambition of making Burgundy-inspired wines in California. From the outset it focused on the two great Burgundian varieties, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, sourced from areas where the influence of the Pacific Ocean, with its morning fogs and breezes, moderates temperatures. The name of the house refers, in Spanish, to the idea of “the best” of the harvest, the cream of the crop, which sums up its original ambition of selecting fruit from the finest cool-climate sites.

In 1993 the winery was acquired by the Jackson family, founders of Kendall-Jackson, and became part of the Jackson Family Wines group. With that backing it expanded its production and its access to vineyards in several cool coastal areas: the Sonoma Coast, the Russian River Valley, Los Carneros, Monterey and, further north, Oregon’s Willamette Valley, among others. Its range is structured around wines with broad appellations, widely available in shops, and wines from specific areas or vineyards.

The house style combines ripe fruit with freshness, with ageing in oak barrels, largely French, and a use of wood adjusted to each wine. Its Sonoma Coast bottlings in particular have become some of the most widespread Californian Chardonnays and Pinot Noirs on the American market. The brand has tasting spaces in Sonoma County.

Visits & wine tourism

It has tasting spaces in Sonoma County; booking is recommended.
