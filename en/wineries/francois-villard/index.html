Domaine François Villard

Northern Rhône estate founded by François Villard, a former chef turned winegrower, with wines from Condrieu, Saint-Joseph, Saint-Péray and the Collines Rhodaniennes IGP.

François Villard trained and worked as a chef before turning to wine. In the late 1980s he planted his first vines in the Northern Rhône and gradually built up an estate based in Saint-Michel-sur-Rhône, in the Condrieu area, on the right bank of the river. He belongs to a generation of growers who helped renew the image of appellations such as Condrieu and Saint-Joseph.

The estate farms terraced vineyards on granite and other crystalline soils typical of this part of the valley. The range includes Viognier whites from Condrieu, Marsanne and Roussanne whites from Saint-Joseph and Saint-Péray, and Syrah reds from Saint-Joseph and Côte-Rôtie, as well as wines from the Collines Rhodaniennes IGP, sourced from vines outside the appellations or from young vines.

Villard is also one of the founding partners, together with Yves Cuilleron and Pierre Gaillard, of Les Vins de Vienne, a project devoted to reviving the historic vineyards around the city of Vienne.

The house style is associated with wines of ripe fruit and generous texture, aged in barrel, and with Syrah reds in which part of the stems is often retained. The names of the cuvées, frequently involving wordplay, are another recognisable feature of the estate.
