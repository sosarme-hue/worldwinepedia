Schuchmann Wines

A winery in Kisiskhevi, near Telavi, created by the German businessman Burkhard Schuchmann. It makes European-style wines and, under the Vinoterra label, traditional qvevri wines from Georgian varieties.

Schuchmann Wines is a winery in the village of Kisiskhevi, in the municipality of Telavi, at the heart of Kakheti. It was created in the late 2000s by Burkhard Schuchmann, a German businessman, who decided to invest in Georgian wine at a time when the sector was looking for new markets in Western Europe after the Russian embargo. Winemaking was entrusted to a Georgian team led by the oenologist Giorgi Dakishvili, associated with the Vinoterra project, devoted to traditional qvevri winemaking.

The winery works with its own vineyards in the Alazani valley and with grapes from local growers. The main varieties are the great grapes of Kakheti: the red Saperavi and the whites Rkatsiteli, Kisi and Mtsvane.

Its production is divided into two main lines. On the one hand, European-style wines, fermented in steel tanks and in some cases barrel-aged, including several from Kakheti’s classic appellations, such as Tsinandali, Mukuzani and Kindzmarauli. On the other, the Vinoterra range, made by the traditional Kakhetian method in buried qvevri: whites with long skin maceration, amber in colour and tannic in texture, and Saperavi reds. This dual approach allows the winery to show both Georgian tradition and a more international interpretation of the same varieties. The facilities include a hotel and spaces devoted to wine tourism.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery has a hotel and offers tours and tastings.
