Anselmo Mendes

Project of the winemaker Anselmo Mendes in the Monção e Melgaço subregion, one of the benchmarks for Alvarinho and for age-worthy Vinho Verde.

Anselmo Mendes is one of Portugal’s best-known winemakers, with a long career as a consultant to wineries in different regions of the country. His personal project focuses on the Monção e Melgaço subregion, at the northern tip of the Vinho Verde designation, beside the river Minho, which separates it from Galicia. There he has devoted much of his career to studying the Alvarinho (Albariño) variety.

Monção e Melgaço has a somewhat warmer, drier climate than the rest of Vinho Verde, thanks to the hills that shelter it from direct Atlantic influence. The soils are mainly granitic, with areas of alluvial deposits near the river. These conditions allow Alvarinho to ripen well, and here it gives wines with more body and ageing potential than the light traditional Vinho Verde.

Anselmo Mendes’s work has been marked by experimentation with different winemaking techniques: lees ageing, barrel fermentation and maturation, skin maceration and plot selection. His range runs from fresh, direct Alvarinhos such as Muros Antigos to more ambitious single-plot and oak-aged wines such as Parcela Única and Curtimenta.

Besides Alvarinho, the house works with Loureiro and other varieties of the region, and also makes some reds. Its contribution has helped to change the image of Vinho Verde, showing that it can produce complex, long-lived whites.
