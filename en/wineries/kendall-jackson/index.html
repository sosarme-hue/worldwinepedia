Winery

Kendall-Jackson

Winery founded in 1982 by Jess Jackson, known above all for its Vintner’s Reserve Chardonnay and the origin of the family group Jackson Family Wines.

Jess Jackson, a San Francisco lawyer, bought a property in Lake County, north of Napa, in the early 1970s with the idea of growing grapes. In 1982 he launched the Kendall-Jackson brand and its first Vintner’s Reserve Chardonnay, a white with generous fruit, a creamy texture and a touch of sweetness that was an immediate success and helped to shape American taste for Chardonnay in the 1980s and 1990s.

Jackson’s central idea was to blend grapes from different cool areas along the Californian coast, from Mendocino to Santa Barbara, to obtain a consistent, balanced wine sold under the California appellation. To secure supply, the company gradually bought vineyards along the coast, and today it farms a large area of its own in several counties.

Kendall-Jackson gave rise to Jackson Family Wines, a family group bringing together numerous wineries in California, Oregon and other countries. After Jess Jackson’s death in 2011, the company remained in the hands of his widow, Barbara Banke, and their children. Kendall-Jackson’s headquarters and visitor centre are near Santa Rosa, in Sonoma, surrounded by gardens and vegetable plots.

Besides Vintner’s Reserve, the winery makes more ambitious lines from specific vineyards and a range that includes Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon. The group has also promoted sustainable viticulture programmes on its estates.

Visits & wine tourism

Its visitor centre near Santa Rosa offers tastings and garden tours.
