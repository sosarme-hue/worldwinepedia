Château Haut-Bages Libéral

Pauillac fifth growth classified in 1855, with some of its vines next to Château Latour, in a classic Cabernet Sauvignon-based style.

Château Haut-Bages Libéral lies in Pauillac, in the Médoc, on the left bank of the Gironde, one of the most prestigious communes in Bordeaux. Its name combines that of the Bages plateau, south of the town of Pauillac, with that of the Libéral family, wine merchants who owned the estate in the 18th century. In the 1855 classification it was ranked as a fifth growth.

In the second half of the 20th century it was bought by the Villars family, linked to other Médoc estates, and since the early years of this century it has been run by Claire Villars-Lurton. Under her management the estate has turned its viticulture towards organic and biodynamic practices, with particular attention to soil life and biodiversity.

The vineyard is divided into several plots. The best known adjoins the vines of Château Latour, in the south of Pauillac, on deep gravel; others lie on the Bages plateau, on gravel with clay and limestone. Cabernet Sauvignon dominates, accompanied by Merlot and a small proportion of Petit Verdot.

The house wines follow the classic Pauillac style: dark fruit, notes of cedar and graphite, a firm structure and good ageing potential, often offering a balance between quality and prestige much appreciated by wine lovers. The estate also makes a second wine, La Fleur de Haut-Bages Libéral.
