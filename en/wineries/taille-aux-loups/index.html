Domaine de la Taille aux Loups

Jacky Blot’s estate in Montlouis-sur-Loire, in Touraine, devoted to Chenin Blanc in all its styles, from sparkling to sweet.

Domaine de la Taille aux Loups was created in the late 1980s by Jacky Blot, who had previously worked in the wine trade, in Husseau, a hamlet of Montlouis-sur-Loire. The Montlouis appellation lies on the south bank of the Loire, opposite Vouvray, between the river and the Cher, and shares with its neighbour an exclusive focus on Chenin Blanc and clay and flint soils over limestone tuffeau.

Blot helped give Montlouis a new image, with demanding vineyard work, hand harvesting in successive passes and barrel fermentation without seeking a marked oak character. The estate makes the full range of styles that Chenin allows: dry still wines from plots such as Clos de Mosny or Les Hauts de Husseau, sparkling wines and, in favourable vintages, sweet wines. Among the sparklers, Triple Zéro stands out, made without chaptalisation and with no sugar added at bottling or at dosage.

The family also runs Domaine de la Butte, in the red Bourgueil appellation, devoted to Cabernet Franc. Jacky Blot’s son Jean-Philippe has joined the work at both estates. The house style is marked by taut, precise whites in which Chenin’s natural acidity balances the ripeness of the grapes.
