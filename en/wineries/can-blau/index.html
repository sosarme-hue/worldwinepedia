Winery

Bodegas Can Blau

Montsant winery belonging to Gil Family Estates, known for its reds made from Cariñena, Syrah and Garnacha.

Bodegas Can Blau is the project in the Montsant D.O. of Gil Family Estates, the group that grew out of Bodegas Juan Gil in Jumilla. The Gil family, which had achieved great success with Monastrell in Murcia, extended its activity to other Spanish appellations with the aim of making wines that expressed the varieties and soils of each area, and the Montsant was chosen for its wealth of old vineyards.

The Montsant

The Montsant surrounds the Priorat and shares much of its winegrowing history, although its soils are more varied: besides slate there are calcareous clays, decomposed granite and sands. The climate is Mediterranean, influenced by the Montsant range, which shelters the area from the north wind, with hot, dry summers. Notable varieties include Cariñena and Garnacha, often from old vines, along with more recently introduced ones such as Syrah.

Wines

The winery works with grapes from various villages in the appellation and has focused on blends of Cariñena, Syrah and Garnacha, whose proportions vary with the wine and the vintage. Its range includes Blau, the youngest and fruitiest red; Can Blau, the wine that gives the house its name, aged in barrel; and Mas de Can Blau, a selection from old vines. The style seeks to combine ripe fruit, Mediterranean character and freshness.
