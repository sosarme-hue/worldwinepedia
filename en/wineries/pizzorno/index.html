Pizzorno Family Estates

Family winery in Canelón Chico, Canelones, founded in 1910 by the Italian immigrant Próspero Pizzorno. It is one of the historic houses of Uruguayan Tannat.

The story of Pizzorno begins with Próspero Pizzorno, an Italian immigrant who settled in Canelón Chico, in the department of Canelones, and founded the winery there in 1910. Like many other families of European origin in the area, he planted vines on the smallholdings near Montevideo and made wine for the local market. For decades the winery, like most of the Uruguayan industry, produced everyday wines.

From the end of the twentieth century, with new generations of the family in charge, the company undertook a profound renewal: the vineyard was replanted with selected clones, the winery was modernised and production was redirected towards quality wines intended for export as well. The house remains a medium-sized family business, working mainly with grapes from its own vineyards in Canelón Chico, on the clay soils characteristic of southern Uruguay.

Tannat is central to its production, both in varietal wines and in blends with Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon or Cabernet Franc. The range runs from young, fruity reds to barrel-aged reservas and more ambitious wines, and also includes whites from Sauvignon Blanc and Chardonnay, rosés and sparkling wines. The house style seeks a balance between the tannic structure of Tannat and the freshness brought by the maritime climate of Canelones, with oak use adapted to each wine.
