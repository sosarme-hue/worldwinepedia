Descendientes de J. Palacios

Project founded in 1999 by Álvaro Palacios and his nephew Ricardo Pérez Palacios in Corullón, decisive in the rediscovery of hillside Mencía in Bierzo.

Descendientes de J. Palacios was born in 1999, when Álvaro Palacios, known for his work in Priorat, and his nephew Ricardo Pérez Palacios settled in Bierzo, drawn by the old Mencía vines covering the slopes of Corullón. The winery’s name pays tribute to José Palacios, Álvaro’s father and founder of the family winery Palacios Remondo in Rioja.

The project focuses on steep vineyards on slate soils, many of them old bush vines worked largely by hand, sometimes with the help of horses or mules. The winery has applied biodynamic farming principles and has recovered abandoned plots on the hillsides around Corullón.

The range follows a hierarchy inspired by Burgundy: Pétalos del Bierzo is the entry-level wine, made from grapes from various plots; Villa de Corullón is the village wine, from vineyards within the municipality; and at the top stand single-site wines such as Las Lamas, Moncerbal and La Faraona, made in very limited quantities.

The house style seeks to express hillside Mencía with freshness, a floral and mineral profile and fine tannins, steering clear of over-extraction. Its work helped Bierzo gain international attention in the early twenty-first century.
