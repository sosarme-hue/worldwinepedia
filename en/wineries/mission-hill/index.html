Mission Hill Family Estate

Okanagan Valley winery in West Kelowna, above Okanagan Lake, owned since 1981 by Anthony von Mandl. It is known for its architecture and for Oculus, its Bordeaux-style red.

Mission Hill was founded in 1966 by a group of businessmen on a hill above Okanagan Lake, in what is now West Kelowna. In 1981 it was bought by Anthony von Mandl, a Vancouver businessman, who undertook a long transformation of the winery with the aim of making quality wines in a region then dominated by wines from hybrid varieties. Over time the company acquired vineyards in different parts of the valley, from around Kelowna to the south near Oliver and Osoyoos, to have suitable climates for each variety.

The winery is known for its architecture. The current complex, completed in the early 2000s to a design by the American architect Tom Kundig, includes a bell tower, barrel cellars carved into the volcanic rock of the hill, an amphitheatre and open views over the lake, and it has become one of British Columbia’s main wine tourism destinations.

The range is broad, covering whites from Chardonnay, Pinot Gris and Riesling, reds from Pinot Noir, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah, and icewines. At the top sits a collection of prestige wines led by Oculus, a Bordeaux-style blend dominated by Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon, made from grapes from the south of the valley. The house style seeks to combine the ripeness offered by the southern Okanagan with the freshness of its climate.

Visits & wine tourism

The West Kelowna estate offers guided tours, tastings, a restaurant and cultural events, with views over Okanagan Lake.
