Domaine Gauby

Family estate in Calce, Roussillon, run by Gérard Gauby and his son Lionel, a benchmark for biodynamic viticulture and a fresh style in southern France.

Domaine Gauby is located in Calce, a small village in the hills north-west of Perpignan, in the Pyrénées-Orientales department. Gérard Gauby, from a local family of growers, began bottling his own wines in the 1980s, when most of Roussillon’s grapes went to cooperatives and to fortified sweet wines. Over time he became one of the names who changed the region’s image. Today he runs the estate with his son Lionel.

Calce’s vineyards are spread across hills of limestone, schist and marl, surrounded by garrigue and woodland. The family farms organically and biodynamically and attaches great importance to the biodiversity of the surroundings, with plots interspersed with natural vegetation. After an early period of very concentrated wines, the house moved towards a style with less extraction, earlier harvests and more freshness, which has influenced other producers in southern France.

The range, mostly released under the Côtes Catalanes designation, includes entry-level whites and reds such as Les Calcinaires, the Vieilles Vignes cuvées and single-site wines such as the white Coume Gineste or the red La Muntada. The varieties are those traditional to Roussillon: Grenache in its various forms, Carignan, Syrah, Mourvèdre, Macabeu and Muscat, among others.

Gauby’s wines are characterised by their tension, their texture and their expression of Calce’s limestone soils.
