Bodegas Hermanos Sastre

Family winery in La Horra, in the Burgos part of Ribera del Duero, known for its Viña Sastre brand and for Pesus, an old-vine red.

Bodegas Hermanos Sastre is a family winery in La Horra, a village in the province of Burgos at the heart of Ribera del Duero, in an area with a long winegrowing tradition where a remarkable number of old bush-trained vineyards survive. The Sastre family had grown vines for generations and, like many growers in the district, sold its grapes or bulk wine until it decided to bottle under its own label in the last decades of the twentieth century, as the appellation was expanding.

The winery works exclusively with its own vineyards, divided into numerous plots around La Horra, some of them with very old vines. The soils combine clay, limestone and sand, at high altitude and with the continental climate typical of the Burgos side of Ribera, with cold winters and dry summers with cool nights.

Tinto Fino, the local name for Tempranillo, is the basis of all its wines. The range begins with Viña Sastre Roble, a red with a short spell in barrel, and continues with the Crianza, the Pago de Santa Cruz selection and Regina Vides. At the top stands Pesus, made from old vines in which small amounts of other varieties grow alongside Tinto Fino.

The house style seeks reds with ripe fruit and structure, with oak ageing as a complement, true to the character of high-altitude Ribera.
