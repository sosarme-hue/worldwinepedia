Château de Fieuzal

Château in Léognan, in the Pessac-Léognan appellation, classified as a Cru Classé de Graves for its red wine and also known for its white.

Château de Fieuzal lies in the municipality of Léognan, south of Bordeaux, in the Pessac-Léognan appellation, created in 1987 for the northern and most highly regarded part of the Graves. It appears in the classification of Graves wines, drawn up in the 1950s, as a Cru Classé for its red wine.

Since the early years of this century the property has belonged to the Irish Quinn family, who have invested in the vineyard and the winery. Its vines grow on the gravels typical of the area, mixed with clay and sand, which favour drainage and ripening.

Fieuzal makes a red based on Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot with contributions from other Bordeaux varieties, and a barrel-aged white of Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon, which has played a large part in its reputation. Both have a second wine, L'Abeille de Fieuzal, more approachable and intended for earlier drinking.

The house style seeks balanced wines, with the freshness and the smoky, mineral nuances often associated with the wines of Pessac-Léognan.

Léognan is one of the most highly regarded municipalities of Pessac-Léognan and is home to several of the classified châteaux of the Graves. Its landscape alternates vineyards, pine forests and small streams, and the vines occupy the best-drained gravel rises, where they find the most favourable conditions for ripening.
