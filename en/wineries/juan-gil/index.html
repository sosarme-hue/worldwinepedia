Winery

Bodegas Juan Gil

Family winery in Jumilla founded in 1916, the origin of the Gil Family Estates group and one of the brands that has done most to make Monastrell known on the international market.

Bodegas Juan Gil was founded in 1916 by Juan Gil Giménez in Jumilla (Murcia). For much of the 20th century the winery sold wine mainly in bulk and locally, as was usual in the district. From the late 1990s the family's fourth generation renewed the vineyards and the winery and committed to bottling quality wines based on Monastrell, Jumilla's traditional variety.

Vineyard

Jumilla has a semi-arid continental climate, with very hot summers, cold winters and little rain. The vineyards lie on high plateaus of roughly 400 to 800 metres, on poor, stony limestone soils. Many Monastrell vines are old and bush-trained, and some are ungrafted, since the sandy soils of certain plots held back the spread of phylloxera.

Wines and group

The house's best-known wine is Juan Gil Etiqueta Plata (Silver Label), a barrel-aged Monastrell that has become a benchmark for the designation. The range also includes younger versions, blended reds and Moscatels. The winery's success gave rise to Gil Family Estates, which brings together wineries in several designations, including Calatayud, Rueda, Montsant and Rías Baixas.
