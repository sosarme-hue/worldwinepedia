Château Lynch-Bages

Fifth Growth (Cinquième Grand Cru Classé) in Pauillac, owned by the Cazes family since the 1930s and known for a generous Cabernet Sauvignon style.

Château Lynch-Bages stands on the Bages plateau, next to the town of Pauillac. Its name combines that of the place with the Lynch family, of Irish origin, who owned the estate for much of the 18th century. In the 1855 classification it was ranked Cinquième Grand Cru Classé (Fifth Growth).

Jean-Charles Cazes took charge of the property in the 1930s and it has belonged to his family ever since. His grandson Jean-Michel Cazes drove the modernisation of the cellars and the château’s international reputation from the 1970s onwards; today the next generation runs it. The family has also restored the small hamlet of Bages, with shops and a restaurant.

The vineyard, on gravel, is planted mainly with Cabernet Sauvignon, along with Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. As well as the grand vin, the estate makes the second wine Echo de Lynch-Bages and a white, Blanc de Lynch-Bages. New cellars were inaugurated in 2020.

Lynch-Bages is appreciated for offering the character of Pauillac — black fruit, cedar notes and firm tannins — in a generous style and at a quality that many enthusiasts consider above its official rank.

The Cazes family also owns other estates in Bordeaux.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers tours of the estate with a tasting, by prior booking; the hamlet of Bages has shops and a restaurant.
