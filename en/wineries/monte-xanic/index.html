Monte Xanic

Valle de Guadalupe winery founded at the end of the 1980s by a group of partners, regarded as one of the pioneers of Mexican quality wine. Its red Gran Ricardo and its white Chenin Colombard are among its best-known wines.

Monte Xanic was born at the end of the 1980s in the Valle de Guadalupe, near the village of Francisco Zarco, on the initiative of a group of partners that included Hans Backhoff. At a time when Mexican wine was dominated by large companies and much of the fruit went into brandy, the project set out to make quality wines from its own vineyards using modern techniques, and it became one of the first medium-sized wineries in Baja California with this aim. Its example encouraged many of the projects that later emerged in the valley. The name Xanic comes from an indigenous language of western Mexico and is usually translated as “the flower that blooms after the first rain”.

The winery works with vineyards in the Valle de Guadalupe and in other valleys of Baja California, on sandy soils of granitic origin, in a semi-arid Mediterranean climate moderated by the Pacific Ocean. Water scarcity calls for careful irrigation. The Backhoff family has remained involved in the winery’s technical direction.

The range covers varietal wines and blends. Among reds, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah and Malbec stand out, along with Gran Ricardo, a Bordeaux-style blend that is the house’s most prestigious wine and pays tribute to one of its founders. Among whites, Chenin Colombard is one of Mexico’s most popular wines, alongside a Sauvignon Blanc and a Chardonnay. The overall style combines the ripe fruit typical of the valley’s climate with a pursuit of balance and freshness.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery offers guided tours and tastings in the Valle de Guadalupe.
