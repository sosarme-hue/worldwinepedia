Domaine Bruno Clair

Family estate in Marsannay-la-Côte, at the northern end of the Côte de Nuits, with vineyards ranging from Marsannay sites to the Chambertin-Clos de Bèze grand cru.

Domaine Bruno Clair is based in Marsannay-la-Côte, the first wine village of the Côte de Nuits as one heads south from Dijon. Its origins lie in the old Clair-Daü family estate, a historic property in the area that was divided among the heirs. Bruno Clair created his own domaine from part of those vines and expanded it with other plots along the Côte.

The Clair family played an important role in the history of Marsannay: it was here that the well-known Pinot Noir rosé was developed, and the village later obtained its own village appellation. The domaine still makes several single-site Marsannays, both red and white. Marsannay is, in fact, one of the few village appellations of the Côte de Nuits that allows all three colours: red, white and rosé.

Beyond Marsannay, the estate owns vines in Gevrey-Chambertin, notably its plot in the Chambertin-Clos de Bèze grand cru and the Clos du Fonteny premier cru, as well as in Morey-Saint-Denis, Chambolle-Musigny and Vosne-Romanée. In the Côte de Beaune it farms vineyards in Savigny-lès-Beaune, Pernand-Vergelesses and Corton-Charlemagne, among others.

The house style is associated with wines of great purity, lightly marked by oak and made to age, in which the expression of each plot is the aim. The family remains at the head of the domaine today.
