Bodegas Beronia

Winery in Ollauri, Rioja Alta, founded in 1973 and owned since the 1980s by González Byass; it is known for its classic-style Crianzas and Reservas.

Bodegas Beronia was founded in 1973 in Ollauri, a small village in Rioja Alta near Haro, by a group of wine-loving friends from the Basque Country who decided to make their own Riojas. The name recalls the Berones, the pre-Roman people who lived in the area before the arrival of Rome. In the early 1980s the winery was acquired by the Jerez-based group González Byass, which has kept it ever since as its Rioja house.

The winery stands among vineyards, in an area of clay-limestone soils and Atlantic-influenced climate well suited to Tempranillo. In addition to its own vines, it works with grapes from growers around Ollauri and other villages of Rioja Alta. Alongside Tempranillo it uses Garnacha, Mazuelo (Carignan) and Graciano in its reds, and Viura for the whites.

Beronia is known above all for its barrel-aged wines: Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva of classic profile, with ripe fruit and spicy notes from the oak. The range is completed by more modern reds, single-varietal wines, whites and rosés. The company also makes a Verdejo in the Rueda appellation.

Thanks to its group’s commercial network, its wines are widely available in shops and restaurants, both in Spain and in markets such as the United Kingdom.
