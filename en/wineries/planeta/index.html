Planeta

Sicilian family company that emerged in the 1990s in the Menfi area, with wineries spread across different parts of the island, from Etna to Vittoria and Noto.

The Planeta family has farmed for generations in south-western Sicily, around Menfi and Sambuca di Sicilia. In the early 1990s, encouraged by Diego Planeta, a key figure in modern Sicilian viticulture, the younger members of the family decided to make and bottle their own wines. The first winery was built on the Ulmo estate, by Lake Arancio, and the first vintages came out in the middle of that decade.

Planeta soon drew attention with wines from international varieties, such as its Chardonnay, which helped to change the image of Sicilian wine abroad. Over time the company has focused more and more on native grapes and on the island’s diversity.

Today Planeta has wineries in several areas: Menfi; Vittoria, where it makes Cerasuolo di Vittoria from Nero d’Avola and Frappato; Noto; Etna; and Capo Milazzo, on the north-east coast. Each is devoted to the varieties and styles typical of its territory, such as Nero d’Avola, Moscato, Carricante and Nerello Mascalese.

The company remains family-owned and has also developed wine tourism and olive oil production.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers tours and tastings at several of its Sicilian wineries, as well as accommodation, by prior booking.
