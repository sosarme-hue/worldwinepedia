Winery

Sogrape

Portuguese family wine group founded in 1942, creator of Mateus Rosé and owner of Casa Ferreirinha, the Ferreira and Sandeman Port houses and estates in the Dão, Vinho Verde and Alentejo.

Sogrape was founded in 1942 by Fernando van Zeller Guedes together with other partners, and its first great success was Mateus Rosé, a lightly sparkling rosé in a distinctively shaped bottle that became one of the best-selling Portuguese wines in the world. The company is still owned by the Guedes family and is one of the country’s largest wine groups.

In 1987 Sogrape acquired A. A. Ferreira, a Port house linked to Dona Antónia Adelaide Ferreira, the «Ferreirinha», who in the nineteenth century was one of the largest landowners in the Douro. With it came Casa Ferreirinha, the brand of still Douro wines to which Barca Velha belongs: created by the winemaker Fernando Nicolau de Almeida, its first vintage was 1952, and it is released only in years considered exceptional. Later the group also took over the Sandeman Port house.

Beyond the Douro, Sogrape owns estates in other Portuguese regions, such as Quinta dos Carvalhais in the Dão, Quinta de Azevedo in Vinho Verde and Herdade do Peso in the Alentejo, as well as wineries in countries such as Spain, Argentina, Chile and New Zealand. Its range runs from widely distributed wines such as Mateus and Duque de Viseu to top-end wines such as Barca Velha.

Visits & wine tourism

Several of its properties welcome visitors, including the Ferreira and Sandeman lodges in Vila Nova de Gaia.
