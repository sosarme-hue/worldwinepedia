Leeuwin Estate

Family estate in Margaret River founded in the 1970s by Denis and Tricia Horgan. Its Art Series Chardonnay is one of Australia’s best-known whites.

Leeuwin Estate was created in the 1970s by Denis and Tricia Horgan on a property near the Margaret River coast, in the south-west of Western Australia. The name comes from Cape Leeuwin, the south-western tip of the continent, named after a Dutch ship that sailed along those coasts in the seventeenth century. In its early days the project was advised by the Californian producer Robert Mondavi, who had identified the site as suitable for quality winegrowing.

The estate is best known for its Art Series range, whose labels reproduce works commissioned from contemporary Australian artists. The Art Series Chardonnay, made since the early 1980s, soon became a benchmark for Australian Chardonnay because of its intensity and ageing potential. The range also includes Cabernet Sauvignon, Shiraz, Riesling and a Sauvignon Blanc–Semillon blend, together with the Prelude Vineyards line and the Siblings range.

The Horgan family still runs the company, with the second generation also involved. Leeuwin Estate is equally well known for the open-air concerts it has hosted on the estate for decades, which have helped make Margaret River known as a tourist destination. Its vineyards lie on the region’s gravelly soils, a short distance from the Indian Ocean, whose influence moderates temperatures during ripening.

Visits & wine tourism

The estate has a cellar door and restaurant, and hosts events and concerts on certain dates.
