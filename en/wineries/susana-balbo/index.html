Susana Balbo Wines

Family winery in Agrelo, Luján de Cuyo (Mendoza), founded in 1999 by winemaker Susana Balbo, maker of the Críos, Signature and BenMarco ranges.

Susana Balbo is one of the best-known figures in Argentine wine. She graduated in oenology in the early 1980s, was one of the first women to practise the profession in the country and worked for years as a winemaker for various wineries, in Argentina and abroad, before creating her own project. In 1999 she founded Susana Balbo Wines in Agrelo, in the Luján de Cuyo department, one of the traditional areas for Mendoza Malbec.

The winery is a family business in which her children, José and Ana Lovaglio Balbo, take part. It makes wines from its own vineyards and from growers it works with in different parts of Mendoza, especially Luján de Cuyo and the Uco Valley, whose high altitudes favour fresh, aromatic wines.

The range is organised into several lines. Críos, whose name refers to the founder’s children, brings together approachable varietal wines such as Malbec, Cabernet Franc and Torrontés. Above it are the Signature and BenMarco lines and the house’s top wines. The style seeks wines with expressive fruit, balance and freshness, and the winery has also stood out for its work with Torrontés, Argentina’s most characteristic white variety, which it vinifies in a dry, fresh and aromatic style.
