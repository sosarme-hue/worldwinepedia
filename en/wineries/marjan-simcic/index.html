Winery

Marjan Simčič

A family estate in Goriška Brda, with vineyards in Slovenia and the Italian Collio, renowned for its Rebula whites and the single-site wines of its Opoka line.

The Marjan Simčič estate is located in Goriška Brda, in western Slovenia, and belongs to a family that has grown vines in these hills for several generations. Like other families in the area, the Simčičs have vineyards on both sides of the border between Slovenia and Italy, in Brda and in the Collio, two parts of the same landscape of hills separated only by the political line drawn after the Second World War.

Marjan Simčič, who took over the family holding, has become one of the region’s benchmark producers. His vineyards lie on opoka, the flysch of marl and sandstone characteristic of the area, and are farmed with environmentally respectful methods and moderate yields. The estate has paid particular attention to Brda’s traditional varieties, especially Rebula and Sauvignonasse, while also working with Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Gris and Merlot.

The range is organised into several lines, from fresh wines for early drinking to age-worthy wines. The most ambitious is Opoka, named after the region’s soil, which brings together wines from specific plots, made with slow fermentations, often with skin maceration in the case of the whites, and long ageing in wood. These wines seek to express the texture, minerality and ageing potential of the whites of Brda.
