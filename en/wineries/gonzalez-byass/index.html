Winery

González Byass

Jerez house founded in 1835 by Manuel María González, creator of Tío Pepe fino and owner of a wide heritage of soleras in Jerez's historic centre.

González Byass was founded in 1835 by Manuel María González Ángel in Jerez de la Frontera. His uncle, José Ángel de la Peña, whom the family called «Tío Pepe» (Uncle Joe), helped him in his early days, and the fino wine that would become the house's emblem was named in his honour. The Byass name comes from Robert Blake Byass, the winery's commercial agent in England, who became a partner. Today the company is still owned by descendants of the founding family.

The bodegas

In the centre of Jerez, next to the cathedral and the Alcázar, the house keeps a complex of historic bodegas where thousands of casks rest under the criaderas and solera system. Among its buildings is La Concha, a circular bodega with a 19th-century iron structure. The famous Tío Pepe neon sign on Madrid's Puerta del Sol has become a popular landmark.

Wines

Besides Tío Pepe, a fino aged biologically under a veil of flor and made from Palomino, the house produces a full range of sherries: amontillados such as Viña AB, palo cortados such as Leonor, olorosos, creams and Pedro Ximénez such as Néctar, as well as very old wines certified VORS, among them Matusalem. The group also owns wineries in other Spanish designations.

Visits & wine tourism

The historic bodegas in the centre of Jerez can be visited on guided tours that end with a tasting of the wines.
