Cantine San Marzano

Salento cooperative founded in 1962 in San Marzano di San Giuseppe, near Taranto, known for its Sessantanni Primitivo di Manduria.

Cantine San Marzano was born in 1962 as a cooperative founded by a small group of growers in San Marzano di San Giuseppe, in the province of Taranto, in northern Salento. Over time the number of members grew considerably and the winery became one of the best-known wine companies in Puglia.

The members’ vineyards lie within the Primitivo di Manduria appellation and in other parts of Salento, on a plain with a hot, dry climate and red clay soils over limestone. Many are old bush vines, the region’s traditional training system, which gives low yields and concentrated grapes.

The house’s emblematic variety is Primitivo, genetically identical to California’s Zinfandel. Its best-known wine is Sessantanni, a Primitivo di Manduria from old vines. The range also includes Salento Primitivo and Negroamaro under various brands, such as Il Pumo and Talò, the Collezione Cinquanta red and wines from other varieties, as well as rosés and sparkling wines.

San Marzano’s style is geared to warm, generous reds with ripe fruit and a smooth texture, typical of southern Italy.

The cooperative structure allows it to work with many small family vineyards, some of which have been farmed by the same families for generations. Its growth has been accompanied by a strong presence on international markets.
