Winery

R. López de Heredia Viña Tondonia

Historic house in the Station District of Haro, founded in 1877 and regarded as one of the leading exponents of classic, long barrel-aged Rioja.

The winery was founded in 1877 by Rafael López de Heredia y Landeta in Haro, next to the railway station that at the time linked La Rioja with the northern ports and with France. It is one of the houses that shaped the so-called Station District, home to several of the region’s oldest wineries. Since its foundation it has remained in the hands of the same family, which has run it over several generations.

Its most famous vineyard is Tondonia, an estate on a bend of the Ebro, on the right bank of the river, which gives its name to the house’s best-known wines. The winery also farms other estates of its own, such as Bosconia, Cubillo and Gravonia, which likewise lend their names to its labels.

López de Heredia is renowned for its loyalty to traditional methods: fermentation in large oak vats, very long ageing in used barrels repaired in its own cooperage, manual racking and fining with egg whites. The wines are released many years after the harvest, when the winery judges them ready. The result is red and white wines with a tertiary profile, marked acidity and great ageing potential, often used as a benchmark for understanding the historic Rioja style.

Its underground cellars and the contemporary building designed by Zaha Hadid for the winery shop are now part of Haro’s architectural landscape.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery offers guided tours of its historic cellars with a tasting, by prior booking.
