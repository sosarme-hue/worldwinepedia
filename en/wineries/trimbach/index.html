Maison Trimbach

Family-owned house in Ribeauvillé dating back to 1626, known for its dry Rieslings and for Clos Sainte Hune.

The Trimbach family is documented in Riquewihr from 1626, the date the house takes as its origin. In the late 19th century it settled in Ribeauvillé, at the foot of the Vosges, where it is still based. The house remains in the hands of the family, which has worked in it for more than a dozen generations.

Trimbach combines its own vineyards with grapes bought from local growers, and makes all of Alsace’s classic varieties: Riesling, Gewurztraminer, Pinot Gris, Pinot Blanc, Sylvaner and Muscat, as well as Pinot Noir. Its hallmark is a dry, taut, food-friendly style, with wines that often need time in bottle to show their full potential, in contrast to sweeter-style Alsace.

Its most celebrated wines are two Rieslings: Clos Sainte Hune, from a small plot in the Grand Cru Rosacker in Hunawihr, owned by the family for a very long time, and Cuvée Frédéric Emile, from vineyards in the Osterberg and Geisberg Grands Crus in Ribeauvillé. The basic range, known as Classic, is very easy to find.

The wines are fermented and aged in steel tanks and in large old oak casks, which add no oak flavours.

Ribeauvillé is one of the historic wine villages on the Alsace Wine Route.
