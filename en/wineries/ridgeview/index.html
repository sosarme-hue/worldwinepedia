Winery

Ridgeview

Family winery in East Sussex, founded in 1995 at the foot of the South Downs and devoted from the outset to traditional-method sparkling wine. Its wines are named after London districts, such as Bloomsbury and Cavendish.

Ridgeview was founded in 1995 by Mike and Chris Roberts, who planted vines at Ditchling Common, in East Sussex, a short distance from the hills of the South Downs. The estate lies north of the village of Ditchling, in a landscape of gentle hills in the interior of the county. From the start they decided to focus exclusively on traditional-method sparkling wines made from the Champagne varieties, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier, at a time when few English producers did so. Together with Nyetimber, it is regarded as one of the wineries that demonstrated southern England’s potential for this style.

The company remains in family hands: the second generation, with Tamara Roberts leading the business and Simon Roberts as winemaker, has taken over. As well as its own vineyard, Ridgeview works with grapes from partner growers in southern England and has made sparkling wine for other estates without their own winery, thus contributing to the development of the industry.

A hallmark of the house is its choice of London district names for its cuvées. Bloomsbury, Chardonnay-led, is the best-known wine; Cavendish relies more on the red varieties, and Fitzrovia is a rosé. The range also includes vintage and long-aged wines. The style combines fresh fruit, lively acidity and the complexity brought by lees ageing.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery offers guided tours and tastings, as well as a space for enjoying its wines beside the vineyard.
