Winery

Aveleda

Family company based at Quinta da Aveleda, in Penafiel, one of the leading Vinho Verde producers and creator of the Casal Garcia brand.

Aveleda takes its name from the quinta of the same name in Penafiel, in the inland part of the Vinho Verde region, which has belonged to the Guedes family for generations. The company dates the beginning of its winemaking to 1870, when Manoel Pedro Guedes promoted wine production on the estate. It is still run by descendants of the family today.

In 1939 the house launched Casal Garcia, a young, light white Vinho Verde with a slight spritz, which became one of the most widely distributed Portuguese wines on the international market and one of the region’s most recognisable brands. Alongside it, Aveleda makes wines under the quinta’s name, including varietals and blends of Alvarinho and Loureiro, as well as sparkling wines and grape spirits.

The estate is also known for its historic gardens, with lakes, woodland and romantic follies, which form part of the visit. In recent years the company has extended its presence to other Portuguese regions, such as the Douro and Bairrada, although Vinho Verde remains the core of its business. Its style focuses on fresh, aromatic wines with lively acidity, true to the Atlantic character of the Minho.

For decades the house has been one of the leading ambassadors of Vinho Verde abroad, and its range runs from widely distributed wines to bottlings made in more limited quantities.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers tours of the winery and the quinta’s gardens, with tastings.
