Isole e Olena

Chianti Classico estate formed by joining two old properties, known for its Chianti Classico and for Cepparello, a benchmark pure Sangiovese.

Isole e Olena is located at the western edge of Chianti Classico, in the municipality of Barberino Tavarnelle, between Florence and Siena. Its name joins those of two neighbouring old properties, Isole and Olena, which the De Marchi family, originally from Piedmont, acquired in the mid-20th century and united into a single estate that also includes a small historic hamlet.

From the 1970s the estate was run by Paolo De Marchi, who became one of the most influential figures in modern Chianti Classico. He worked on clonal selection and replanting the vineyards, championed the quality of Sangiovese and in 1980 created Cepparello, a red made exclusively from this variety which, since it did not then comply with the Chianti rules, was released as a table wine and is now classified as IGT Toscana. In 2022 the family sold the estate to the French group EPI, owned by the Descours family.

The vineyards lie on slopes at a relatively high altitude for the area, on galestro and alberese soils. Besides Sangiovese, the estate grows traditional varieties such as Canaiolo and some international ones, such as Chardonnay, Syrah and Cabernet Sauvignon.

The range includes the Chianti Classico, Cepparello, a Chardonnay, reds from international varieties and a Vin Santo. The house style seeks the elegance, acidity and ageing potential of high-altitude Sangiovese.
