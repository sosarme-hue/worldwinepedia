Winery

Gaja

Family winery founded in Barbaresco in 1859 which, under Angelo Gaja, made a decisive contribution to the international standing of Langhe Nebbiolo wines.

Giovanni Gaja founded the winery in the village of Barbaresco in 1859. During the first generations the family became known locally for the quality of its wines, and it was above all from the 1960s, when Angelo Gaja joined, that the house gained an international profile.

Angelo Gaja introduced practices that were then unusual in the Langhe, such as lowering yields, ageing in small barrels alongside traditional casks, and planting international varieties such as Chardonnay and Cabernet Sauvignon. He also championed single-vineyard wines such as Sorì San Lorenzo, Sorì Tildin and Costa Russi, which became benchmarks for Nebbiolo.

The family owns vineyards in Barbaresco and in the Barolo area, and extended its activity to Tuscany with the Pieve Santa Restituta estate in Montalcino and Ca’ Marcanda in Bolgheri. More recently it has also turned its attention to Etna.

Angelo Gaja’s children now work at the winery. The house Barbaresco, made from Nebbiolo from different plots in the municipality, remains the winery’s emblematic wine.
