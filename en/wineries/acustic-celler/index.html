Winery

Acústic Celler

Winemaker Albert Jané's project in the Montsant D.O., devoted to old-vine Garnacha and Carignan, with wines that aim for freshness and a sense of place.

Acústic Celler is the personal project of Albert Jané, a winemaker from a family with a long winemaking tradition in Penedès. His choice of Montsant reflects the wealth of old vineyards in this designation, which encircles Priorat like a ring and shares much of its history and grape varieties.

Vineyards

Montsant offers a great diversity of soils —limestone, clay, granitic sand and, in some areas, slate— and varied altitudes, allowing wines of different profiles. Acústic works mainly with old-vine Garnacha and Carignan, often bush-trained and in small, low-yielding plots. The winery's philosophy stresses careful, low-intervention viticulture.

Style

The project's name refers to the idea of an «acoustic» wine, that is, one without artifice, in which the vineyard is heard above the technique. The wines seek a balance between the ripeness typical of a Mediterranean climate and freshness, with moderate use of oak. The range includes reds such as Acústic and Braó, and whites made from traditional local varieties such as Garnacha Blanca and Macabeo. It is one of the wineries that best illustrate Montsant's potential as a more affordable alternative to neighbouring Priorat.
