Baron Philippe de Rothschild

Bordeaux wine company of the Château Mouton Rothschild family, creator of Mouton Cadet, one of the most widely distributed Bordeaux brands.

Baron Philippe de Rothschild is the wine company built around Baron Philippe de Rothschild (1902-1988), who ran Château Mouton Rothschild in Pauillac for more than sixty years. Philippe was an innovator: he introduced château bottling in the 1920s and, from 1945, commissioned artists to illustrate Mouton’s labels. In 1973 Mouton Rothschild was promoted to Premier Grand Cru Classé, the only change made to the 1855 classification in that category.

In the 1930s the baron created Mouton Cadet, a Bordeaux appellation wine made from grapes from different parts of the region, which in time became one of the best-selling Bordeaux brands in the world. The range now includes a red, a white and a rosé, among other wines.

After Philippe’s death the company was run by his daughter, Baroness Philippine de Rothschild, and then by her descendants. The group is also involved in projects outside France, such as Opus One in California, created with Robert Mondavi, and Almaviva in Chile.

Mouton Cadet offers an approachable, fruity style of Bordeaux, designed to be enjoyed young.

The company is based in Pauillac, in the Médoc, close to the family’s estates.
