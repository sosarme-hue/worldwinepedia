Winery

Guido Berlucchi

House in Borgonato, Corte Franca, where in 1961 Guido Berlucchi and the winemaker Franco Ziliani made the first traditional-method sparkling wine in Franciacorta.

Berlucchi’s history is bound up with Palazzo Lana, a historic residence in Borgonato, in the municipality of Corte Franca, owned by Guido Berlucchi’s family. In the late 1950s Berlucchi, who was making still wine on the estate, engaged the young winemaker Franco Ziliani, who suggested making a sparkling wine in the Champagne manner.

The first bottles came out in 1961 under the name “Pinot di Franciacorta”. That wine is regarded as the starting point of modern Franciacorta, an area of hills south of Lake Iseo that in the following decades would become one of Italy’s leading appellations for traditional-method sparkling wine.

Over time the Ziliani family came to head the company, which is now run by Franco’s children. Berlucchi grows Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Blanc in its Franciacorta vineyards and is one of the largest producers in the appellation.

The “’61” range, recalling the year of the first bottles, gathers its most widely available Franciacorta wines, in versions such as Brut, Satèn and Rosé. The house also makes vintage and extended-ageing cuvées.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers tours of the historic cellars of Palazzo Lana, with a tasting, by prior booking.
