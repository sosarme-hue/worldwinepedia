Quinta do Vallado

One of the oldest quintas in the Douro, near Peso da Régua, once owned by Dona Antónia Adelaide Ferreira and still in the hands of her descendants; it makes Douro wines and Ports.

Quinta do Vallado lies by the Corgo river, very close to where it flows into the Douro and to the town of Peso da Régua, in the Baixo Corgo subregion. Its documented origins go back to 1716, making it one of the oldest quintas in the valley. In the nineteenth century it belonged to Dona Antónia Adelaide Ferreira, the famous «Ferreirinha», one of the most important figures in Douro history, and it is still owned by her descendants today.

For generations, the quinta’s grapes and wine went mainly into Port made by other houses. From the 1990s the family began bottling its own still Douro DOC wines, and later Ports as well, especially tawnies. Alongside the vineyards of the original estate, which include plots of old interplanted vines, the family farms Quinta do Orgal, in the Douro Superior.

Its range includes Douro reds and whites at several levels, varietal wines such as its Touriga Nacional, old-vine wines and Ports. The style seeks to balance the ripeness of Douro fruit with freshness and elegance. The quinta has a modern winery next to its historic buildings and a wine hotel, and it is one of the reference points for wine tourism around Régua.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers winery tours with tastings and has accommodation on the quinta itself.
