Domaine Yves Cuilleron

Family estate in Chavanay, in the northern Rhône, making Condrieu, Saint-Joseph and Côte-Rôtie, as well as Collines Rhodaniennes geographical-indication wines.

Yves Cuilleron’s estate is in Chavanay, a village on the right bank of the Rhône between Condrieu and Saint-Pierre-de-Bœuf, at the northern end of the Saint-Joseph appellation. The family had grown vines for several generations, but it was Yves Cuilleron who, taking over in the late 1980s, expanded the vineyard and gave the estate its present profile.

His work has focused on reclaiming abandoned terraces on the steep granite and schist slopes of the northern Rhône, held up by dry-stone walls. Today the estate makes wines from the three main appellations of the area: Condrieu, with Viognier as the only variety; Saint-Joseph, red from Syrah and white from Marsanne and Roussanne; and Côte-Rôtie, the great Syrah red of the right bank. The range is completed by a series of varietal wines under the Collines Rhodaniennes geographical indication, sold as “Les Vignes d’à Côté”, from vines located next to the appellations.

Many of the wines are made by plot or lieu-dit, with the name of the site on the label. The house style seeks to express variety and soil, with barrel ageing for the more ambitious wines. Yves Cuilleron was also one of the founders, together with Pierre Gaillard and François Villard, of Les Vins de Vienne, a project devoted to reviving historic vineyards around that town.
