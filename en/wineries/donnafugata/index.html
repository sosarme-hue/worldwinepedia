Winery

Donnafugata

Sicilian winery founded in 1983 by the Rallo family in Marsala, with estates at Contessa Entellina, Pantelleria, Vittoria and Etna, known for the Ben Ryé passito.

Donnafugata was founded in 1983 by Giacomo and Gabriella Rallo. Giacomo came from a family with more than a century of experience in Marsala wine, and Gabriella brought her family’s land at Contessa Entellina, in the interior of western Sicily, where the new company planted its first vineyards. The historic cellars in Marsala remain its headquarters.

The name, “woman in flight”, refers to Giuseppe Tomasi di Lampedusa’s novel The Leopard, in which Donnafugata is the estate of the Princes of Salina, and evokes the story of Queen Maria Carolina, who took refuge in this part of Sicily in the early nineteenth century. The image of a woman with windswept hair appears on its labels, known for their artistic design.

In 1989 the family began working on the island of Pantelleria, where it grows Zibibbo (Muscat of Alexandria) on terraces as low bush vines, a growing method recognised by UNESCO. Estates at Vittoria, for Cerasuolo and Frappato, and on Etna were added later.

The company is now run by José and Antonio Rallo, the founders’ children. Its best-known wines include the Ben Ryé passito, the red Mille e una Notte, Sedàra and the white Anthìlia.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers tours and tastings at the historic cellars in Marsala and at some of its other estates, by prior booking.
