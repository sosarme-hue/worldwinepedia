The Sadie Family Wines

Winery of winemaker Eben Sadie on the Paardeberg, in the Swartland, a key figure in the renewal of South African wine with his Columella and Palladius blends and his old-vine series.

The Sadie Family Wines is the project of winemaker Eben Sadie, who settled on the slope of the Paardeberg, a granite massif in the Swartland, in the early 2000s, after working at other wineries in South Africa and abroad. His work, together with that of other producers in the region, played a decisive part in making the Swartland known and in revaluing the old vineyards of the Cape. The winery is a small family business.

The house’s two founding wines are Columella, a Syrah-based red completed with other varieties of Mediterranean origin, and Palladius, a white blend in which Chenin Blanc is combined with numerous varieties. Both come from various Swartland vineyards, many of them old, bush-trained and dry-farmed on granite, shale and sandy soils.

Later Sadie created the Old Vine Series, a collection of wines each from a single old vineyard, often of undervalued varieties such as Cinsault, Palomino, Semillon or Chenin Blanc, located in the Swartland and other parts of the Cape. The stated aim of this series is to show the heritage value of these vineyards and to contribute to their preservation. Winemaking is low-intervention, with spontaneous fermentations, gentle extraction and ageing in large vessels or used barrels, in search of fresh wines that express their site.
