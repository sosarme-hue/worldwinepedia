Domaine des Lambrays

Morey-Saint-Denis estate in the Côte de Nuits, owner of almost all of the Clos des Lambrays Grand Cru and part of the LVMH group since 2014.

Domaine des Lambrays is based in Morey-Saint-Denis, in Burgundy’s Côte de Nuits, between Gevrey-Chambertin and Chambolle-Musigny. Its name is tied to the Clos des Lambrays, a walled vineyard on the slope above the village, almost all of which the estate owns; only a small parcel belongs to another producer.

The Clos des Lambrays was raised to Grand Cru status in 1981, joining Morey’s other great vineyards: Clos de Tart, Clos de la Roche, Clos Saint-Denis and part of Bonnes-Mares. The estate changed hands several times in the twentieth century; in 1996 it was bought by the German Freund family and in 2014 it passed to the LVMH group, which has undertaken a renewal of vineyard and winery.

Besides the Grand Cru, the domaine makes Morey-Saint-Denis wines, including some Premiers Crus, and Puligny-Montrachet whites from parcels in the Côte de Beaune.

The house style is characterised by fine, aromatic Pinot Noir reds, focused more on elegance and the expression of terroir than on power, with long ageing potential. The upper and lower parts of the Clos des Lambrays have different soils and exposures, allowing the estate to vinify different sectors separately before blending.
