Winery

Recaredo

Family house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia specialising in long-aged Brut Nature sparkling wines made from its own biodynamically farmed grapes, today released under the Corpinnat collective brand.

Recaredo was founded in 1924 by Josep Mata Capellades in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, the historic capital of Catalan sparkling wine. The house remains in the hands of the Mata family, which has kept to a very clear path: traditional-method sparkling wines made exclusively from the grapes of its own vineyards.

Vineyard

The vineyards lie in Alt Penedès, mainly on limestone soils in the valley of the Bitlles river. The winery grows the area's traditional varieties —Xarel·lo, Macabeo and Parellada— and applies biodynamic farming principles, with certification for this kind of viticulture. Xarel·lo, regarded as the Penedès variety with the greatest ageing capacity, plays a central role in its wines.

Style

All its sparkling wines are Brut Nature, that is, made without a sweetened dosage, and are characterised by long ageing on the lees. Recaredo keeps artisanal practices such as the use of a cork stopper during the second fermentation and manual disgorgement. Its labels include Terrers and the single-vineyard Serral del Vell.

Corpinnat

In 2019 Recaredo left the Cava D.O., together with other prestigious wineries, to join Corpinnat, a collective brand that requires, among other things, organic, hand-harvested grapes from the heart of Penedès and longer minimum ageing than Cava.
