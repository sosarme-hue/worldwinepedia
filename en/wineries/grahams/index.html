Winery

W. & J. Graham’s

Port house founded in 1820 by a family of Scottish merchants and owned by the Symington family since 1970, known for its rich-style Ports and for Quinta dos Malvedos.

Graham’s has its origins in the textile business of the brothers William and John Graham, Scottish merchants based in Porto. According to the house’s history, in 1820 they accepted a payment in Port wine and from then on entered the trade, which eventually became their main business. In 1890 the house acquired Quinta dos Malvedos, in the Cima Corgo, near the confluence with the Tua river, which remains the heart of its Vintage Ports. Its ageing lodge in Vila Nova de Gaia was built in the same period.

In 1970 the Symington family, involved in the Port trade since the late nineteenth century, bought Graham’s, which has since been part of Symington Family Estates. The house draws on its own quintas and on Douro growers with whom it has worked for generations.

Graham’s style is associated with Ports of ripe fruit and generous sweetness, with a structure that allows long ageing in the case of its Vintage Ports. Its range covers the Six Grapes ruby, Late Bottled Vintage, 10, 20, 30 and 40-year-old tawnies, single-harvest Ports, classic Vintage Ports and Quinta dos Malvedos Vintage in some undeclared years. Its Gaia lodge is one of the best-known visitor centres in the city.

Visits & wine tourism

Its lodge in Vila Nova de Gaia offers guided tours with tastings, by prior booking.
