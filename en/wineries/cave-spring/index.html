Cave Spring Vineyard

Family winery on the Niagara Peninsula, based in the village of Jordan with vineyards on the benches of the Niagara Escarpment. It is one of the benchmarks for Canadian Riesling.

Cave Spring is a business of the Pennachetti family, who in the late 1970s planted European varieties on the slopes of the Niagara Escarpment, at a time when they were still uncommon in Ontario. Together with winemaker Angelo Pavan, the family created the winery in the 1980s and based it in the village of Jordan, in the town of Lincoln, in historic buildings in the village centre.

The vineyard that gives the house its name, Cave Spring Vineyard, lies in the Beamsville Bench sub-appellation, on a terrace at the foot of the escarpment, on clay and loam soils with limestone and dolomite. The proximity of Lake Ontario and the air circulation along the escarpment create a climate favourable to mid-ripening varieties, and the winery has specialised in Riesling, making dry, off-dry, late-harvest and icewine versions. It also produces Chardonnay, Gamay, Pinot Noir and Cabernet Franc, as well as traditional-method sparkling wines.

The range of wines from its historic vineyard is identified by the initials CSV, standing for Cave Spring Vineyard, and brings together the wines from its oldest-vine plots. The house style favours acidity, expression of the escarpment’s limestone terrain and moderate ripeness.

Its Rieslings are often cited as a good example of the character of the Beamsville Bench and usually develop well in bottle over several years.
