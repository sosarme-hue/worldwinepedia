Château La Fleur-Pétrus

Pomerol estate on the appellation’s plateau, close to Pétrus, owned by Établissements Jean-Pierre Moueix since the mid-twentieth century.

Château La Fleur-Pétrus lies on the upper part of the Pomerol plateau, on Bordeaux’s Right Bank, in the sector where some of the appellation’s most renowned properties are concentrated, including Pétrus and Lafleur, which it borders. Like the rest of Pomerol, it is not subject to any official classification.

In 1950 it was acquired by Jean-Pierre Moueix, the Libourne merchant who played a decisive role in Pomerol’s international reputation, and it still belongs to the family house, Établissements Jean-Pierre Moueix, which also runs other Right Bank properties. Over the decades the vineyard has been enlarged with neighbouring parcels.

The soils combine gravel and clay, in proportions that vary from one parcel to another. The vineyard is planted mainly with Merlot, with some Cabernet Franc.

La Fleur-Pétrus’s style stands out for finesse and freshness rather than opulence: reds with precise fruit, elegant tannins and great ageing potential, true to the classic taste of the Moueix house. As at the house’s other properties, harvesting is by hand and the parcels are vinified separately, in pursuit of precision rather than extraction.
