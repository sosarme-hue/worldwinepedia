Finca Antigua

Estate of the Martínez Bujanda family in Los Hinojosos (Cuenca), within the La Mancha D.O., with its own high-altitude vineyards and a wide range of varietal and oak-aged wines.

Finca Antigua belongs to the Martínez Bujanda family, with a long winemaking tradition in Rioja, who decided to invest in La Mancha, attracted by the possibilities of a large estate of its own vineyards. The property lies in the municipality of Los Hinojosos, in the province of Cuenca, in the north-eastern sector of the La Mancha D.O.

Vineyard

The estate sits at around 900 metres, a high elevation for the La Mancha plateau that brings cool nights and slower ripening. The climate is extreme continental, with cold winters, very hot summers and little rain. Soils are poor, with limestone and stones. Since it owns its vineyards, the winery controls the whole process, from vine to bottle.

Wines

The winery makes a broad range that includes single-variety wines from Tempranillo, Syrah, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Garnacha, among others, barrel-aged wines and a few whites, including a Moscatel. The style aims for fruity, well-built wines offering good value, reflecting the change La Mancha has undergone in recent decades, from a bulk-wine area to a region of bottled wines with an identity of their own.
