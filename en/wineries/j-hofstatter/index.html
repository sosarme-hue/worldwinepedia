Winery

J. Hofstätter

Family winery in Tramin, Alto Adige, founded in the early 20th century, known for its Gewürztraminer and Pinot Nero, which also owns the Dr. Fischer estate in Germany’s Saar.

J. Hofstätter is one of the best-known family wineries in Alto Adige. It was founded in 1907 by Josef Hofstätter in Tramin (Termeno), the South Tyrolean wine-route village from which Gewürztraminer takes its name. In the mid-20th century the winery passed by marriage to the Foradori family, who still run it and have joined their surname to that of the house.

The business is based on several estates of its own on both sides of the Adige valley. They include the Kolbenhof, on the slopes above Tramin, associated with its Gewürztraminer, and the Barthenau estate in the Mazon area on the opposite bank, one of the benchmark areas for Pinot Nero in Italy. The range covers the typical Alto Adige varieties: Pinot Bianco, Chardonnay, Gewürztraminer, Lagrein and Pinot Nero, among others.

In recent years the family has extended its activity beyond Italy with the purchase of the Dr. Fischer estate in the Saar valley, within Germany’s Mosel region, devoted to Riesling. These wines are sold under their own label, though under the family’s management.

Hofstätter’s style combines the aromatic precision characteristic of Alto Adige’s mountain viticulture with more ambitious single-vineyard wines that can age well, alongside a broad range of fresh, approachable varietal wines.
