Winery

Boutari

One of Greece’s oldest and best-known wine houses, founded in Naoussa in 1879 by Yiannis Boutaris. Its Xinomavro Naoussa is a classic of Greek wine, and the company has wineries in several regions of the country.

Boutari was founded in 1879 in Naoussa, in Greek Macedonia, by Yiannis Boutaris, and has since been bound up with the history of Xinomavro and the Naoussa appellation. It was one of the first Greek companies to bottle wine under its own brand and to sell it nationally and internationally, at a time when most of the country’s wine was sold in bulk. During the twentieth century the house grew to become one of Greece’s largest wine groups.

Besides its historic winery in Naoussa, the company has developed facilities in other Greek wine regions, such as Santorini, Mantinia in the Peloponnese and Crete, allowing it to make wines from numerous native varieties: Xinomavro, Assyrtiko, Moschofilero and Agiorgitiko, among others. It works both with its own vineyards and with grapes bought from growers in each area.

Its best-known wine is Boutari Naoussa, a wood-aged Xinomavro red that for decades has been many consumers’ gateway to the reds of northern Greece. The house also makes a Grande Reserve Naoussa, with longer ageing, from a selection of grapes from the area. The style of these wines is classic: reds with lively acidity and firm tannins, with developed notes that become more pronounced with time in bottle. The group’s range includes whites, rosés, sparkling wines and sweet wines from different regions.
