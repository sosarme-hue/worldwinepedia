Winery

Hatzidakis

Santorini winery founded in 1997 by Haridimos Hatzidakis in Pyrgos, known for its old-vine Assyrtiko, its organic viticulture and its traditional Nykteri.

Hatzidakis was founded in 1997 by Haridimos Hatzidakis in Pyrgos, one of the inland villages of Santorini. Haridimos, who had worked as a winemaker on the island, started with small plots of old vines and a winery dug into the volcanic rock, a traditional solution on Santorini that provides cool, stable temperatures.

From the outset the house committed to organic viticulture and to minimal-intervention winemaking, with spontaneous fermentations and little use of technology. On Santorini the vines are traditionally trained into baskets, or kouloura, coiled at ground level to protect them from the strong winds, on soils of volcanic ash and pumice where phylloxera never took hold, which means very old root systems have survived.

The central variety is Assyrtiko, which on the island gives whites of high acidity, firm body and marked mineral character. Hatzidakis makes several Assyrtikos, among them single-plot old-vine wines, Nykteri, a traditional Santorini style aged in wood, and sweet Vinsanto. It also works with less widespread local varieties such as Aidani and Mavrotragano.

After the death of Haridimos Hatzidakis in 2017, the winery has carried on under the direction of his family and his team, maintaining the style that made it known.
