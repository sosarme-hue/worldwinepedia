Château Saint-Pierre

Fourth growth of the 1855 classification in Saint-Julien, owned since the 1980s by the family of Henri Martin, who also own Château Gloria.

Château Saint-Pierre is a fourth growth in the 1855 classification, located in Saint-Julien-Beychevelle, in the heart of the Saint-Julien appellation. It is one of the smallest classified estates in the Médoc.

Its history during the 19th century was marked by divisions between heirs, which fragmented the vineyard. In 1982 it was acquired by Henri Martin, a leading figure in Médoc wine and the creator of Château Gloria, who set about reuniting the property. Today it is run by his descendants, the Triaud family, who also manage Château Gloria.

The vineyard lies on the deep gravel soils typical of Saint-Julien, in plots close to some of the commune's great growths. It is planted mainly with Cabernet Sauvignon, complemented by Merlot and some Cabernet Franc.

The style of Saint-Pierre reflects that of Saint-Julien: a balance between the structure and depth of Pauillac wines and the finesse of those of Margaux. Its reds combine black fruit, firm but polished tannins and good ageing potential. In good vintages, its grand vin is considered one of the commune's classified growths offering the best value relative to its reputation. Saint-Julien, located between Pauillac and Margaux, is the only one of the great Médoc communes without a first growth, yet it has a very high proportion of classified growths.
