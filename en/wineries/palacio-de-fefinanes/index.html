Bodegas del Palacio de Fefiñanes

Historic winery housed in the Palacio de Fefiñanes, in Cambados, whose Albariño de Fefiñanes is one of the oldest bottled labels of the variety.

The winery occupies part of the Palacio de Fefiñanes, a seventeenth-century manorial complex that presides over the square of the same name in Cambados (Pontevedra), one of the town’s most recognisable monumental spaces. The palace has belonged to the same family for generations, which has kept it linked to winemaking.

In the first half of the twentieth century the winery began selling its wine in bottle under the brand Albariño de Fefiñanes, long before the Rías Baixas Designation of Origin was created in 1988. For this reason it is considered one of the pioneering labels of bottled Albariño, and its image, bearing the palace’s coat of arms, is very recognisable.

The winery makes its wines from grapes grown in the Val do Salnés, the most Atlantic subzone of Rías Baixas, with a cool, damp climate and granitic soils, where Albariño is traditionally trained on pergolas. Besides the young-style Albariño de Fefiñanes, the range includes a version with longer lees ageing, an oak-aged Albariño and a grape spirit.

The house style is characterised by classically styled Albariños, fresh and balanced, with fruit and a marked saline sensation, which remain closely tied to the history of Cambados.
