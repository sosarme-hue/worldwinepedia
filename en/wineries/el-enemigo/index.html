El Enemigo

Mendoza project of Alejandro Vigil and Adrianna Catena, known for its Bonarda, Malbec and Cabernet Franc and for the top-end Gran Enemigo line.

El Enemigo is the brand of Bodega Aleanna, a project created by winemaker Alejandro Vigil, known for his long career as technical director at Catena Zapata, and by historian Adrianna Catena, daughter of Nicolás Catena. The name refers to an idea the founders themselves have often explained: that each person’s main adversary lies within. The labels, with their literary aesthetic, reinforce that personal tone.

The project made its name with wines from varieties that at the time had less of a role than Malbec among Mendoza’s quality wines, such as Bonarda and Cabernet Franc, in addition to Malbec itself and Chardonnay. The grapes come from different parts of the province, from eastern Mendoza to the high-altitude vineyards of the Uco Valley, in particular Gualtallary, in Tupungato, and other sites such as El Cepillo or Los Chacayes.

The range is organised on two main levels: the El Enemigo wines, varietals with small proportions of other grapes, and the Gran Enemigo wines, single-plot or single-site reds based on Cabernet Franc. The style seeks freshness, tension and an expression of place, with ageing that combines barrels, large casks and, for some of the whites, ageing under a veil of yeast. Vigil has also opened a space devoted to wine and food in the Chachingo area of Maipú, which has become a well-known stop for visitors to Mendoza.
