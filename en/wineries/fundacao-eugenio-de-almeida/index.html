Fundação Eugénio de Almeida

Évora foundation established in 1963 that makes, at the Adega da Cartuxa winery, some of the best-known wines of the Alentejo, such as Cartuxa, EA and Pêra-Manca.

The Fundação Eugénio de Almeida is a private cultural, social and educational institution based in Évora, the historic capital of the Alentejo. It was established in 1963 by Vasco Maria Eugénio de Almeida, heir to an important family of landowners in the region, and devotes the proceeds of its economic activities, wine among them, to its social purposes.

The foundation’s winemaking is concentrated at the Adega da Cartuxa, housed in an old building on the outskirts of Évora, near the Carthusian monastery of Santa Maria Scala Coeli, which gives the brand its name. The vineyards lie around the city, on granite and schist soils, in a Mediterranean climate with a strong continental influence, very hot, dry summers and cool winters.

The range is organised into several lines. EA groups young, approachable wines; Cartuxa includes a Colheita and a Reserva of greater structure; and Pêra-Manca, a name of ancient origin associated with the wines of Évora, designates the house’s most ambitious wines. Pêra-Manca red is made only in years the foundation considers exceptional, and it has become one of the most sought-after Portuguese reds.

The main varieties are the traditional ones of the Alentejo, such as Aragonez (Tempranillo), Trincadeira and Alicante Bouschet for the reds, and Antão Vaz and Arinto for the whites.

Visits & wine tourism

The Adega da Cartuxa, in Évora, has a wine tourism centre offering guided tours of the winery and tastings.
