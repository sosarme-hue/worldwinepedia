Winery

Feudi di San Gregorio

Irpinia winery founded in 1986 in Sorbo Serpico (Avellino), devoted to Campania’s native varieties: Aglianico, Fiano, Greco and Falanghina.

Feudi di San Gregorio was founded in 1986 in Irpinia, the hilly and mountainous inland area of Campania, in the province of Avellino. Its founders, from local families, set out to promote the region’s traditional grapes at a time when they were little known outside southern Italy.

The winery is based in Sorbo Serpico, in a modern building surrounded by vineyards. It works with its own vineyards and those of local growers across Irpinia’s three main appellations: Taurasi, for Aglianico reds, and Fiano di Avellino and Greco di Tufo, for whites. Many of these vineyards lie at considerable altitude, with cold winters and soils of volcanic and clay-limestone origin.

The company has paid attention to studying the area’s old vines, some of them pre-phylloxera, and to preserving their genetic heritage.

The range includes whites from Fiano, Greco and Falanghina, Taurasi and Aglianico reds such as Rubrato, as well as traditional-method sparkling wines made from native grapes.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers winery tours with tastings and has a restaurant, by prior booking.
