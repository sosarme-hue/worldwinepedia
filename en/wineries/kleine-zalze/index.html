Kleine Zalze

Stellenbosch winery especially known for its Chenin Blancs, from the widely available Cellar Selection range to the Family Reserve.

Kleine Zalze is a historic estate on the outskirts of Stellenbosch, one of the oldest wine areas in South Africa, where viticulture dates back to the time of the first Dutch settlers at the end of the seventeenth century. In its current form the winery is owned by the Basson family, who drove its modern development and turned it into one of the South African producers most present on export markets.

The estate forms part of a complex that includes vineyards, a residential area with a golf course and a restaurant, and the winery works both with its own vineyards and with grapes from growers in different parts of the Coastal Region, which allows it to make wines in a variety of styles.

Its recognised speciality is Chenin Blanc, the most widely planted white variety in South Africa, traditionally known in the country as Steen. The winery offers it at several levels: the Cellar Selection range, often from bush vines, provides fresh, fruity, widely distributed whites; the Vineyard Selection and Family Reserve come from selected plots, frequently of old vines, and spend time in barrel to gain texture and complexity.

Alongside Chenin Blanc, Kleine Zalze makes Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, traditional-method sparkling wines and reds from Cabernet Sauvignon, Shiraz and Pinotage. The house style combines ripe fruit, freshness and a measured use of oak, with a value for money that explains its popularity.

Visits & wine tourism

It has a tasting room and restaurant on the estate, on the outskirts of Stellenbosch.
