Winery

Anthonij Rupert Wyne

South African wine group based at the historic L'Ormarins estate in Franschhoek, making wines from several Cape regions under brands such as Anthonij Rupert, Cape of Good Hope, Optima, Protea and L'Ormarins.

Anthonij Rupert Wyne is based at L'Ormarins, a historic estate in the Franschhoek valley at the foot of the Groot Drakenstein mountains, in the Western Cape. The property belongs to the Rupert family, and the winery is named after Anthonij Rupert, brother of businessman Johann Rupert, who drove the family’s wine project.

As well as the Franschhoek vineyards, the group works with estates in different parts of the Cape, such as Riebeeksrivier in the Swartland and Altima in the Elandskloof valley, a cool, high-lying enclave. This diversity makes it possible to grow very different varieties on the terroirs that suit them best: Chenin Blanc and Syrah in the warm Swartland, Sauvignon Blanc and Chardonnay in cooler areas, and Bordeaux varieties in Franschhoek.

Production is organised into several brands. Anthonij Rupert covers the most ambitious wines; Cape of Good Hope is dedicated to single-vineyard wines; Optima is a red blend; Protea groups more approachable varietal wines; and L'Ormarins signs the traditional-method sparkling wines, known in South Africa as Cap Classique.

The L'Ormarins estate is also home to the Franschhoek Motor Museum, which houses the family’s car collection.

Visits & wine tourism

The L'Ormarins estate welcomes visitors, with a tasting room and the Franschhoek Motor Museum.
