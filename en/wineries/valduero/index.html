Bodegas Valduero

Family winery in Gumiel del Mercado, in the Burgos part of Ribera del Duero, known for its ageing galleries dug into the hillside and its long-lived reds.

Bodegas Valduero is a family business based in Gumiel del Mercado, a village in the province of Burgos in the northern part of Ribera del Duero, near Aranda de Duero. It was born in the 1980s, as the appellation was beginning to consolidate, from a family with a farming tradition in the district, and is still run by its descendants.

Its most striking feature is the underground galleries dug into the hillside next to the winery, which extend beneath the hill and offer stable temperature and humidity, well suited to barrel ageing and bottle maturation. This tradition links with that of the underground cellars found in many Ribera villages, where for centuries wine was made and stored below ground.

The winery works with its own vineyards around Gumiel del Mercado, at high altitude, on clay and limestone soils. Tinto Fino, the local name for Tempranillo, is the basis of its reds. The range follows Ribera’s classic ladder, with Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva, and also includes selection wines with very long ageing and a single-variety red from old vines, Una Cepa.

Valduero’s style is that of traditional Ribera: structured reds with black fruit, notes of oak and spice, intended to develop for years in bottle. The house places particular importance on bottle rest before releasing its wines.
