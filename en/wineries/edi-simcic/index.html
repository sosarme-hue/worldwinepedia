Winery

Edi Simčič

A family estate in Vipolže, in Goriška Brda, run by Edi Simčič and his son Aleks, known for its aged whites and for Duet Lex, a red from Bordeaux varieties.

The Edi Simčič estate is located in Vipolže, in the hills of Goriška Brda, near the Italian border. The family has grown vines in the area for generations, and in the last decades of the twentieth century Edi Simčič began bottling his own wines with a clear focus on quality. Today he runs the winery with his son Aleks Simčič, and it has become one of the most highly regarded in Slovenia.

The vineyards, on the flysch marl and sandstone soils characteristic of Brda, are farmed for low yields with careful vine work. The family’s plots are spread over several hills around Vipolže, with differing exposures and altitudes. In the cellar the house favours slow fermentations, prolonged ageing in oak barrels and casks, and long resting periods before release, so that its wines reach the market with some maturity.

Its whites include wines from Rebula, Sauvignon Blanc, Sauvignonasse and Chardonnay, as well as aged blends; the Chardonnay from the Kozana plot is one of its best-known wines. Among the reds, the house benchmark is Duet Lex, a blend of Merlot with Cabernet Franc and Cabernet Sauvignon aged at length in barrel. The winery’s style combines concentration, texture and good acidity, with wines designed to develop in bottle for years.
