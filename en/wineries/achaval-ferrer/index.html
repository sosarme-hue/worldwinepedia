Winery

Achaval-Ferrer

Mendoza winery founded in the late 1990s by a group of Argentine and Italian friends, known for its single-estate Malbecs from old vines in different parts of Mendoza.

Achaval-Ferrer was born in 1998 at the initiative of a group of Argentine partners, among them Santiago Achával and Manuel Ferrer, who gave the winery its name, together with Italian wine professionals such as the winemaker Roberto Cipresso. Their aim was to make wines reflecting the character of specific Mendoza vineyards, at a time when most Argentine reds were made as blends from different origins.

The winery became known for its single-estate Malbecs, made separately from old vines located in different areas: Finca Altamira, in La Consulta in the Uco Valley; Finca Bella Vista, in Perdriel; and Finca Mirador, in Medrano. Comparing these wines, made from the same variety in a similar way, illustrates the influence of soil, altitude and climate on Malbec. Alongside them the house makes a Mendoza Malbec and a blend called Quimera.

The winery’s style is based on low yields, hand harvesting and barrel ageing that seeks to respect the fruit. Its headquarters are in Perdriel, in Luján de Cuyo, on the banks of the Mendoza River. Since the early 2010s the winery has belonged to an international drinks group, which has maintained its range of single-estate wines.

Its single-estate wines are often regarded as an early example of Argentina’s interest in expressing place of origin.
