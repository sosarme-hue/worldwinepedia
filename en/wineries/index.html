Wineries

Historic houses and benchmark estates: their history, vineyards, style and wines.

Acústic Celler

Montsant · Winemaker Albert Jané's project in the Montsant D.O., devoted to old-vine Garnacha and Carignan, with wines that aim for freshness and a sense of place.

Alvear

Montilla-Moriles · Montilla winery founded in 1729, one of Spain's oldest, specialising in Montilla-Moriles D.O. wines made from Pedro Ximénez, from finos to sweet solera wines.

Bodega Mustiguillo

Utiel-Requena · Toni Sarrión's winery on the Utiel-Requena plateau, a pioneer in championing Bobal as a quality variety and holder of the El Terrerazo Vino de Pago.

Bodega Numanthia

Toro · Winery in the Toro D.O., in Valdefinjas (Zamora), known for concentrated Tinta de Toro reds from old vines.

Bodegas Ateca

Calatayud · Calatayud D.O. winery belonging to the Gil Family Estates group, focused on high-altitude old-vine Garnacha and known for the Atteca and Honoro Vera wines.

Bodegas Barbadillo

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry · Family house in Sanlúcar de Barrameda founded in 1821, closely associated with manzanilla and known for Solear and its seasonal en rama manzanilla releases.

Bodegas Borsao

Campo de Borja · Cooperative winery in the Campo de Borja D.O., at the foot of the Moncayo, known for good-value Garnachas with wide international distribution.

Bodegas El Grifo

Lanzarote · Lanzarote winery founded in 1775, one of the oldest in the Canary Islands, known for its wines from Malvasía Volcánica grown in pits dug into volcanic ash.

Bodegas Emilio Moro

Ribera del Duero · Family winery in Pesquera de Duero, with several generations of grape-growing tradition, making Tinto Fino reds from its own vineyards.

Bodegas Enrique Mendoza

Alicante · Family winery in Alicante, one of the benchmarks of the Alicante D.O., known for its Monastrell reds and its Moscatels from the Marina Alta.

Bodegas Jorge Ordóñez & Co.

Málaga · Málaga winery founded by the importer Jorge Ordóñez to revive the Moscatel wines of the Axarquía, both dry and sweet.

Bodegas José Pariente

Rueda · La Seca winery founded in 1998 by Victoria Pariente, a benchmark for Rueda Verdejo whites made from old vines.

Bodegas Juan Gil

Jumilla · Family winery in Jumilla founded in 1916, the origin of the Gil Family Estates group and one of the brands that has done most to make Monastrell known on the international market.

Bodegas Lustau

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry · Jerez house founded in 1896, known for its broad range of styles, its collection of almacenista wines and for ageing wines in all three towns of the Sherry Triangle: Jerez, El Puerto de Santa María and Sanlúcar.

Bodegas Martín Códax

Rías Baixas · Winery founded in Cambados in 1986 by a large group of Salnés growers, whose Albariño is one of the most widely available from Rías Baixas.

Bodegas Muga

Rioja · Family winery in Haro founded in 1932, known for fermenting and ageing its wines entirely in wood and for running its own cooperage.

Bodegas Protos

Ribera del Duero · Winery founded in Peñafiel in 1927 by a group of growers, with galleries dug beneath the castle hill and a presence in Rueda as well.

Bodegas Ramón Bilbao

Rioja · Haro winery founded in 1924 by Ramón Bilbao Murga, known for widely distributed Rioja wines such as its Tempranillo Crianza.

Bodegas Vega Sicilia

Ribera del Duero · Historic Ribera del Duero winery, founded in 1864, whose red Único is one of Spain’s most celebrated wines.

Bodegas del Palacio de Fefiñanes

Rías Baixas · Historic winery housed in the Palacio de Fefiñanes, in Cambados, whose Albariño de Fefiñanes is one of the oldest bottled labels of the variety.

CVNE (Compañía Vinícola del Norte de España)

Rioja · House founded in Haro in 1879 by the Real de Asúa brothers, behind classic Rioja brands such as Cune, Imperial, Viña Real and Monopole.

Casa Castillo

Jumilla · Family estate in Jumilla run by José María Vicente, one of the wineries that have redefined Monastrell with fresh wines expressing their soils.

Clos Mogador

Priorat · René Barbier's family estate in Gratallops, one of the founding projects of modern Priorat, known for its eponymous red made from Garnacha, Carignan and other varieties.

Codorníu

Cava · One of the oldest and most emblematic Cava houses, in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, linked to the first traditional-method sparkling wines made in Spain and to Modernista cellars listed as a monument.

Descendientes de J. Palacios

Bierzo · Project founded in 1999 by Álvaro Palacios and his nephew Ricardo Pérez Palacios in Corullón, decisive in the rediscovery of hillside Mencía in Bierzo.

Enate

Somontano · Winery in Salas Bajas (Huesca), one of the driving forces of modern Somontano, known for combining wine and contemporary art on its labels.

Familia Torres

Penedès · Family-owned Penedès winery founded in 1870, one of Spain's best-known wineries internationally, with vineyards in several Catalan designations and other Spanish regions.

Finca Antigua

La Mancha · Estate of the Martínez Bujanda family in Los Hinojosos (Cuenca), within the La Mancha D.O., with its own high-altitude vineyards and a wide range of varietal and oak-aged wines.

Freixenet

Cava · Major cava house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, known worldwide for widely available sparkling wines such as Cordón Negro and Carta Nevada, now part of the Henkell Freixenet group.

Félix Solís

Valdepeñas · Family wine group based in Valdepeñas, one of Spain's largest wine producers, known for the Viña Albali brand of the Valdepeñas D.O.

González Byass

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry · Jerez house founded in 1835 by Manuel María González, creator of Tío Pepe fino and owner of a wide heritage of soleras in Jerez's historic centre.

Gramona

Penedès · Historic family house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, a benchmark for long-aged sparkling wines based on Xarel·lo and a founding member of the Corpinnat collective brand.

Herederos del Marqués de Riscal

Rioja · One of the pioneering wineries of modern Rioja, founded in Elciego (Rioja Alavesa) in 1858, which introduced Bordeaux techniques to the region; also a pioneer in Rueda.

J. Chivite Family Estates

Navarra · Navarra house with a long family tradition and roots documented in the seventeenth century, known for its Colección 125 range and its rosés.

La Rioja Alta, S.A.

Rioja · Winery founded in Haro in 1890, a benchmark for traditional Rioja aged in American oak, using barrels from its own cooperage.

Marqués de Murrieta

Rioja · Historic Rioja winery based on the Ygay estate, southeast of Logroño, whose origins go back to the mid-nineteenth century and Luciano de Murrieta.

Pago de Carraovejas

Ribera del Duero · Peñafiel winery founded in the late 1980s by Segovian restaurateur José María Ruiz, with its own vineyards on the slopes facing the castle.

Pazo de Señoráns

Rías Baixas · Family winery set in a historic manor house in the Val do Salnés, in Meis (Pontevedra), known for its lees-aged Albariños.

Perelada

Empordà · Historic Alt Empordà winery linked to the castle of Peralada, producing still wines under the Empordà D.O. and sparkling wines under the Cava D.O.

R. López de Heredia Viña Tondonia

Rioja · Historic house in the Station District of Haro, founded in 1877 and regarded as one of the leading exponents of classic, long barrel-aged Rioja.

Rafael Palacios

Valdeorras · Winery founded in 2004 by Rafael Palacios in O Bolo (Ourense), devoted to Godello whites from mountain vineyards in the Bibei valley.

Raimat

Costers del Segre · Large wine estate near Lleida, acquired in 1914 by Manuel Raventós of the Codorníu family, and a pioneer of the Costers del Segre D.O.

Recaredo

Penedès · Family house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia specialising in long-aged Brut Nature sparkling wines made from its own biodynamically farmed grapes, today released under the Corpinnat collective brand.

Tinto Pesquera (Familia Fernández Rivera)

Ribera del Duero · Winery created by Alejandro Fernández in Pesquera de Duero, whose Tinto Pesquera played a decisive role in making Ribera del Duero known worldwide.

Toro Albalá

Montilla-Moriles · Winery in Aguilar de la Frontera, in the Montilla-Moriles D.O., known for its long-aged sweet Pedro Ximénez wines, especially the Don PX range.

Txomin Etxaniz

Getariako Txakolina · Family winery in Getaria (Gipuzkoa), with vineyards facing the Bay of Biscay, whose white txakoli is one of the best known in the appellation.

Álvaro Palacios

Priorat · Álvaro Palacios's winery in Gratallops, one of the leading names in the revival of Priorat in the late 1980s, focused on old-vine Garnacha and Carignan grown on llicorella slate.
