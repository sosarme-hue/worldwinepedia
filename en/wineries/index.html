Wineries

Historic houses and benchmark estates: their history, vineyards, style and wines.

Abadía Retuerta

· Estate in Sardón de Duero, next to a monastery founded in the twelfth century, whose wines, made since the mid-1990s, are sold under the Vino de la Tierra de Castilla y León designation.

Achaval-Ferrer

Luján de Cuyo · Mendoza winery founded in the late 1990s by a group of Argentine and Italian friends, known for its single-estate Malbecs from old vines in different parts of Mendoza.

Acústic Celler

Montsant · Winemaker Albert Jané's project in the Montsant D.O., devoted to old-vine Garnacha and Carignan, with wines that aim for freshness and a sense of place.

Agustí Torelló Mata

Cava · Family house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia specialising in long-aged Cavas, famous for Kripta, presented in an amphora-shaped bottle.

Allegrini

Valpolicella · Family winery in Fumane, in Valpolicella Classica, known for its Amarone and for IGT reds such as Palazzo della Torre and La Poja.

Alois Lageder

Trentino-Alto Adige · Alto Adige family winery founded in 1823 in Bolzano and now based in Magrè, known for its commitment to biodynamic farming and for its mountain whites.

Alvear

Montilla-Moriles · Montilla winery founded in 1729, one of Spain's oldest, specialising in Montilla-Moriles D.O. wines made from Pedro Ximénez, from finos to sweet solera wines.

Ameztoi

Getariako Txakolina · Family winery in Getaria, on the coast of Gipuzkoa, making Txakoli from Hondarrabi Zuri in vineyards overlooking the Bay of Biscay, also known for its Rubentis rosé.

Anselmo Mendes

Vinho Verde · Project of the winemaker Anselmo Mendes in the Monção e Melgaço subregion, one of the benchmarks for Alvarinho and for age-worthy Vinho Verde.

Ao Yun

· A Moët Hennessy project in the mountains of north-western Yunnan, near Meili Snow Mountain, with vineyards at more than two thousand metres. It makes a single top-end red based on Cabernet Sauvignon, whose first vintage was 2013.

Artadi

Rioja · Winery in Laguardia run by Juan Carlos López de Lacalle, which grew out of a growers’ association in 1985 and is known for its single-vineyard wines from Rioja Alavesa; in the 2010s it left the Rioja DOCa.

Ata Rangi

Martinborough · Family winery in Martinborough founded in 1980 by Clive Paton, one of the pioneers of New Zealand Pinot Noir.

Au Bon Climat

Santa Barbara · Winery founded in 1982 by Jim Clendenen in Santa Barbara County, a reference for Burgundian-style Californian Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, with acidity and restrained alcohol.

Aveleda

Vinho Verde · Family company based at Quinta da Aveleda, in Penafiel, one of the leading Vinho Verde producers and creator of the Casal Garcia brand.

Bacalhôa Vinhos de Portugal

Setúbal · Wine group based in Azeitão, on the Setúbal peninsula, heir to the former J. P. Vinhos, producing widely sold brands such as JP Azeitão and the Quinta da Bacalhôa red.

Baron Philippe de Rothschild

Bordeaux · Bordeaux wine company of the Château Mouton Rothschild family, creator of Mouton Cadet, one of the most widely distributed Bordeaux brands.

Barone Ricasoli

Chianti Classico · Historic house of the castle of Brolio, in Gaiole in Chianti, linked to Baron Bettino Ricasoli, who in the nineteenth century proposed the grape formula for Chianti.

Bellavista

Franciacorta · Franciacorta house based in Erbusco, created by Vittorio Moretti, one of the brands that has done most to build the reputation of the area’s traditional-method sparkling wines.

Benanti

Etna · Family winery in Viagrande, on the eastern slope of Etna, one of those that drove the revival of the volcano’s wines from the late 1980s.

Beringer Vineyards

Napa Valley · Winery founded in 1876 in St. Helena by the brothers Jacob and Frederick Beringer; it is one of the oldest continuously operating wineries in Napa.

Bessa Valley Winery

Thracian Valley · A Thracian Valley winery near Pazardzhik, created in the early 2000s by Count Stephan von Neipperg, owner of châteaux in Saint-Émilion, and Karl-Heinz Hauptmann. It is known for its Enira red.

Biondi-Santi

Brunello di Montalcino · Historic estate at Tenuta Greppo, in Montalcino, traditionally credited with creating Brunello di Montalcino in the late nineteenth century.

Bisol

Prosecco · Valdobbiadene winegrowing family with roots documented back to the sixteenth century, specialising in hillside Prosecco Superiore and Cartizze; part of the Lunelli group since 2014.

Blandy’s

Madeira · Madeira house founded in 1811 by John Blandy and still owned by his family, one of the best-known producers of the island’s fortified wine.

Bodega Bouza

· Uruguayan family winery based in Melilla, on the outskirts of Montevideo, known for its Albariño and Tannat and for its wine-tourism offering.

Bodega Catena Zapata

Bodega Chacra

Patagonia · Small winery in Río Negro, Patagonia, founded in 2004 by Piero Incisa della Rocchetta. It is devoted mainly to old-vine Pinot Noir, farmed according to biodynamic principles.

Bodega Colomé

Salta · Historic winery in the Calchaquí Valleys of Salta, whose origins go back to 1831. It is known for its very high-altitude vineyards, among the highest in the world.

Bodega Garzón

Maldonado · Winery in the department of Maldonado, near the village of Garzón and the Atlantic coast, driven by the Argentine businessman Alejandro Bulgheroni. Its hillside vineyards on granitic soils have made the Albariño and Tannat of this area known abroad.

Bodega Mustiguillo

Bodega Numanthia

Bodega Pisano

Bodegas Alto Moncayo

Bodegas Arzuaga Navarro

Bodegas Ateca

Calatayud · Calatayud D.O. winery belonging to the Gil Family Estates group, focused on high-altitude old-vine Garnacha and known for the Atteca and Honoro Vera wines.

Bodegas Barbadillo

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry · Family winery in Sanlúcar de Barrameda founded in 1792, best known for La Gitana manzanilla.

Bodegas Jorge Ordóñez & Co.

Bodegas José Pariente

Bodegas Juan Gil

Bodegas Lustau

Bodegas Mauro

· Winery in Tudela de Duero created by Mariano García and his family, with a first vintage in 1980; its vineyards lie outside the boundaries of the Ribera del Duero DO and its wines are sold under the Vino de la Tierra de Castilla y León designation.

· Winery making old-vine wines in several areas of central and south-eastern Spain, known for Volver (La Mancha) and Tarima (Alicante).

· Winery in Parras de la Fuente, Coahuila, regarded as the oldest in the Americas: its origins go back to the estate founded in 1597.

· Family company of Italian origin founded in 1969 at Yenda, in the Riverina region of New South Wales, known around the world for the [yellow tail] brand.
