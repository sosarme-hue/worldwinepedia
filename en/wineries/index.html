Wineries

Historic houses and benchmark estates: their history, vineyards, style and wines.

Achaval-Ferrer

Luján de Cuyo · Mendoza winery founded in the late 1990s by a group of Argentine and Italian friends, known for its single-estate Malbecs from old vines in different parts of Mendoza.

Acústic Celler

Montsant · Winemaker Albert Jané's project in the Montsant D.O., devoted to old-vine Garnacha and Carignan, with wines that aim for freshness and a sense of place.

Allegrini

Valpolicella · Family winery in Fumane, in Valpolicella Classica, known for its Amarone and for IGT reds such as Palazzo della Torre and La Poja.

Alois Lageder

Trentino-Alto Adige · Alto Adige family winery founded in 1823 in Bolzano and now based in Magrè, known for its commitment to biodynamic farming and for its mountain whites.

Alvear

Montilla-Moriles · Montilla winery founded in 1729, one of Spain's oldest, specialising in Montilla-Moriles D.O. wines made from Pedro Ximénez, from finos to sweet solera wines.

Ata Rangi

Martinborough · Family winery in Martinborough founded in 1980 by Clive Paton, one of the pioneers of New Zealand Pinot Noir.

Au Bon Climat

Santa Barbara · Winery founded in 1982 by Jim Clendenen in Santa Barbara County, a reference for Burgundian-style Californian Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, with acidity and restrained alcohol.

Aveleda

Vinho Verde · Family company based at Quinta da Aveleda, in Penafiel, one of the leading Vinho Verde producers and creator of the Casal Garcia brand.

Baron Philippe de Rothschild

Bordeaux · Bordeaux wine company of the Château Mouton Rothschild family, creator of Mouton Cadet, one of the most widely distributed Bordeaux brands.

Benanti

Etna · Family winery in Viagrande, on the eastern slope of Etna, one of those that drove the revival of the volcano’s wines from the late 1980s.

Beringer Vineyards

Napa Valley · Winery founded in 1876 in St. Helena by the brothers Jacob and Frederick Beringer; it is one of the oldest continuously operating wineries in Napa.

Biondi-Santi

Brunello di Montalcino · Historic estate at Tenuta Greppo, in Montalcino, traditionally credited with creating Brunello di Montalcino in the late nineteenth century.

Bisol

Prosecco · Valdobbiadene winegrowing family with roots documented back to the sixteenth century, specialising in hillside Prosecco Superiore and Cartizze; part of the Lunelli group since 2014.

Blandy’s

Madeira · Madeira house founded in 1811 by John Blandy and still owned by his family, one of the best-known producers of the island’s fortified wine.

Bodega Catena Zapata

Luján de Cuyo · Family winery in Mendoza founded in 1902 by the Italian immigrant Nicola Catena. Under Nicolás Catena Zapata it pioneered high-altitude Malbec and the international rise of Argentine wine.

Bodega Chacra

Patagonia · Small winery in Río Negro, Patagonia, founded in 2004 by Piero Incisa della Rocchetta. It is devoted mainly to old-vine Pinot Noir, farmed according to biodynamic principles.

Bodega Colomé

Salta · Historic winery in the Calchaquí Valleys of Salta, whose origins go back to 1831. It is known for its very high-altitude vineyards, among the highest in the world.

Bodega Mustiguillo

Utiel-Requena · Toni Sarrión's winery on the Utiel-Requena plateau, a pioneer in championing Bobal as a quality variety and holder of the El Terrerazo Vino de Pago.

Bodega Norton

Luján de Cuyo · Historic Luján de Cuyo winery founded in 1895 by the British engineer Edmund James Palmer Norton. Since 1989 it has belonged to the Austrian Swarovski family.

Bodega Numanthia

Toro · Winery in the Toro D.O., in Valdefinjas (Zamora), known for concentrated Tinta de Toro reds from old vines.

Bodegas Ateca

Calatayud · Calatayud D.O. winery belonging to the Gil Family Estates group, focused on high-altitude old-vine Garnacha and known for the Atteca and Honoro Vera wines.

Bodegas Barbadillo

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry · Family house in Sanlúcar de Barrameda founded in 1821, closely associated with manzanilla and known for Solear and its seasonal en rama manzanilla releases.

Bodegas Borsao

Campo de Borja · Cooperative winery in the Campo de Borja D.O., at the foot of the Moncayo, known for good-value Garnachas with wide international distribution.

Bodegas El Grifo

Lanzarote · Lanzarote winery founded in 1775, one of the oldest in the Canary Islands, known for its wines from Malvasía Volcánica grown in pits dug into volcanic ash.

Bodegas Emilio Moro

Ribera del Duero · Family winery in Pesquera de Duero, with several generations of grape-growing tradition, making Tinto Fino reds from its own vineyards.

Bodegas Enrique Mendoza

Alicante · Family winery in Alicante, one of the benchmarks of the Alicante D.O., known for its Monastrell reds and its Moscatels from the Marina Alta.

Bodegas Jorge Ordóñez & Co.

Málaga · Málaga winery founded by the importer Jorge Ordóñez to revive the Moscatel wines of the Axarquía, both dry and sweet.

Bodegas José Pariente

Rueda · La Seca winery founded in 1998 by Victoria Pariente, a benchmark for Rueda Verdejo whites made from old vines.

Bodegas Juan Gil

Jumilla · Family winery in Jumilla founded in 1916, the origin of the Gil Family Estates group and one of the brands that has done most to make Monastrell known on the international market.

Bodegas Lustau

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry · Jerez house founded in 1896, known for its broad range of styles, its collection of almacenista wines and for ageing wines in all three towns of the Sherry Triangle: Jerez, El Puerto de Santa María and Sanlúcar.

Bodegas Martín Códax

Rías Baixas · Winery founded in Cambados in 1986 by a large group of Salnés growers, whose Albariño is one of the most widely available from Rías Baixas.

Bodegas Muga

Rioja · Family winery in Haro founded in 1932, known for fermenting and ageing its wines entirely in wood and for running its own cooperage.

Bodegas Protos

Ribera del Duero · Winery founded in Peñafiel in 1927 by a group of growers, with galleries dug beneath the castle hill and a presence in Rueda as well.

Bodegas Ramón Bilbao

Rioja · Haro winery founded in 1924 by Ramón Bilbao Murga, known for widely distributed Rioja wines such as its Tempranillo Crianza.

Bodegas Vega Sicilia

Ribera del Duero · Historic Ribera del Duero winery, founded in 1864, whose red Único is one of Spain’s most celebrated wines.

Bodegas del Palacio de Fefiñanes

Rías Baixas · Historic winery housed in the Palacio de Fefiñanes, in Cambados, whose Albariño de Fefiñanes is one of the oldest bottled labels of the variety.

Boekenhoutskloof

Franschhoek · Winery in the Franschhoek valley that since the 1990s has become one of South Africa’s most influential producers, with wines such as The Chocolate Block and an old-vine Semillon.

CVNE (Compañía Vinícola del Norte de España)

Rioja · House founded in Haro in 1879 by the Real de Asúa brothers, behind classic Rioja brands such as Cune, Imperial, Viña Real and Monopole.

Ca' del Bosco

Franciacorta · Erbusco winery founded by Maurizio Zanella, one of the houses that brought prestige to Franciacorta, Lombardy’s traditional-method sparkling wine.

Casa Castillo

Jumilla · Family estate in Jumilla run by José María Vicente, one of the wineries that have redefined Monastrell with fresh wines expressing their soils.

Casa Lapostolle

Colchagua Valley · Winery founded in Chile in 1994 by Alexandra Marnier Lapostolle, of the French family that created Grand Marnier, and her husband Cyril de Bournet. Its flagship wine is Clos Apalta, made in the Colchagua Valley.

Castello Banfi

Brunello di Montalcino · Large Montalcino estate founded in 1978 by the American brothers John and Harry Mariani, known for its Brunello and for its research into Sangiovese clones.

Caymus Vineyards

Napa Valley · Family winery in Rutherford founded in 1972 by the Wagner family, known for a ripe, generous style of Cabernet Sauvignon and for its Special Selection.

Champagne Bollinger

Champagne · Family-owned house in Aÿ founded in 1829, known for a Pinot Noir-led champagne style and for fermenting part of its wines in barrel.

Champagne Louis Roederer

Champagne · Family-owned Reims house dating back to 1776, creator of Cristal and owner of an extensive vineyard of its own in Champagne.

Chateau Ste. Michelle

Columbia Valley · The best-known winery in Washington State, based in Woodinville near Seattle, with vineyards in the Columbia Valley. It has been decisive in making Washington Riesling and reds known.

Château Cheval Blanc

Saint-Émilion · Great Saint-Émilion estate on the border with Pomerol, famous for the high proportion of Cabernet Franc in its wine.

Château Haut-Brion

Pessac-Léognan · Premier Grand Cru Classé in Pessac-Léognan, the only château outside the Médoc included in the 1855 classification of reds; owned by the Dillon family since 1935.

Château Lynch-Bages

Médoc · Fifth Growth (Cinquième Grand Cru Classé) in Pauillac, owned by the Cazes family since the 1930s and known for a generous Cabernet Sauvignon style.

Château Margaux

Médoc · Premier Grand Cru Classé in the 1855 classification, located in the Margaux appellation of the Médoc and owned by the Mentzelopoulos family since 1977.

Château de Beaucastel

Châteauneuf-du-Pape · Perrin family estate in Châteauneuf-du-Pape, known for growing all thirteen permitted varieties and for the weight of Mourvèdre in its red.

Château d’Esclans

Provence · Côtes de Provence estate relaunched by Sacha Lichine from 2006, creator of Whispering Angel rosé; since 2019 it has been majority-owned by Moët Hennessy.

Château d’Yquem

Sauternes · The most famous estate in Sauternes and the only Premier Cru Supérieur in the 1855 classification, renowned for its sweet wines made from nobly rotten grapes.

Clos Mogador

Priorat · René Barbier's family estate in Gratallops, one of the founding projects of modern Priorat, known for its eponymous red made from Garnacha, Carignan and other varieties.

Cloudy Bay

Marlborough · Marlborough winery founded in 1985 whose Sauvignon Blanc played a decisive part in the international fame of New Zealand wine. Today it is part of the Moët Hennessy group.

Codorníu

Cava · One of the oldest and most emblematic Cava houses, in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, linked to the first traditional-method sparkling wines made in Spain and to Modernista cellars listed as a monument.

Craggy Range

Hawke's Bay · Winery founded in the late 1990s by the Peabody family, based at the foot of Te Mata Peak in Hawke’s Bay. It makes single-vineyard wines from the Gimblett Gravels and Martinborough.

Descendientes de J. Palacios

Bierzo · Project founded in 1999 by Álvaro Palacios and his nephew Ricardo Pérez Palacios in Corullón, decisive in the rediscovery of hillside Mencía in Bierzo.

Domaine Drouhin Oregon

Willamette Valley · Winery founded in 1987 in the Dundee Hills by the Burgundy house Joseph Drouhin, one of the first French investments in Oregon and a reference for the region’s Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

Domaine Faiveley

Côte de Nuits · Family-owned house in Nuits-Saint-Georges founded in 1825, one of Burgundy’s largest vineyard owners, in the Côte d’Or and the Côte Chalonnaise.

Domaine Huet

Loire · Vouvray estate founded in 1928, a benchmark for Loire Chenin Blanc, with three historic vineyards and biodynamic farming.

Domaines Ott

Provence · Provençal house founded by Marcel Ott in the early 20th century, a pioneer of quality rosé and owned by Louis Roederer since 2004.

Domäne Wachau

Wachau · Growers’ cooperative in the Wachau based in Dürnstein, which brings together vines from many families across many of the region’s terraces and makes mainly dry Grüner Veltliner and Riesling.

Donnafugata

Sicily · Sicilian winery founded in 1983 by the Rallo family in Marsala, with estates at Contessa Entellina, Pantelleria, Vittoria and Etna, known for the Ben Ryé passito.

Dr. Konstantin Frank

Finger Lakes · Family winery founded in 1962 on the shores of Keuka Lake by the scientist Konstantin Frank, who showed that the European vine could be grown in the cold climate of the north-eastern United States.

Duckhorn Vineyards

Napa Valley · St. Helena winery founded in 1976 by Dan and Margaret Duckhorn, a pioneer of quality Merlot in Napa and also known for its Sauvignon Blanc.

E. Guigal

Northern Rhône · Family-owned house in Ampuis founded in 1946, a benchmark for Côte-Rôtie and one of the best-known producers in the Rhône Valley.

El Esteco

Salta · Historic Cafayate winery founded in 1892 by the brothers David and Salvador Michel. It belongs to Grupo Peñaflor and is one of the leading producers of Torrontés in the Calchaquí Valleys.

Enate

Somontano · Winery in Salas Bajas (Huesca), one of the driving forces of modern Somontano, known for combining wine and contemporary art on its labels.

Esporão

Alentejo · Wine company founded in 1973 on the Herdade do Esporão, in Reguengos de Monsaraz, one of the leading names in the Alentejo with wines such as Esporão Reserva and Monte Velho.

Familia Torres

Penedès · Family-owned Penedès winery founded in 1870, one of Spain's best-known wineries internationally, with vineyards in several Catalan designations and other Spanish regions.

Familia Zuccardi

Valle de Uco · Mendoza family business started in 1963 by Alberto Zuccardi in Maipú. Today it is one of the benchmark wineries of the Uco Valley, with its Piedra Infinita winery in Paraje Altamira.

Famille Hugel

Alsace · Family-owned house in Riquewihr dating back to 1639, one of the best known in Alsace and a driving force behind the rules for Vendanges Tardives and Sélection de Grains Nobles.

Felton Road

Central Otago · Winery at Bannockburn, in Central Otago, known for its single-block Pinot Noirs and biodynamic viticulture. Its first wines were released in the late 1990s.

Ferrari Trento

Trentino-Alto Adige · Traditional-method sparkling wine house founded in Trento in 1902 by Giulio Ferrari and owned by the Lunelli family since 1952, a benchmark for the Trento DOC.

Feudi di San Gregorio

Campania · Irpinia winery founded in 1986 in Sorbo Serpico (Avellino), devoted to Campania’s native varieties: Aglianico, Fiano, Greco and Falanghina.

Finca Antigua

La Mancha · Estate of the Martínez Bujanda family in Los Hinojosos (Cuenca), within the La Mancha D.O., with its own high-altitude vineyards and a wide range of varietal and oak-aged wines.

Fontanafredda

Barolo · Historic estate in Serralunga d’Alba linked to the House of Savoy, one of the largest vineyard properties in the Langhe and a producer of Barolo since the nineteenth century.

Freixenet

Cava · Major cava house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, known worldwide for widely available sparkling wines such as Cordón Negro and Carta Nevada, now part of the Henkell Freixenet group.

Félix Solís

Valdepeñas · Family wine group based in Valdepeñas, one of Spain's largest wine producers, known for the Viña Albali brand of the Valdepeñas D.O.

Gaja

Barbaresco · Family winery founded in Barbaresco in 1859 which, under Angelo Gaja, made a decisive contribution to the international standing of Langhe Nebbiolo wines.

González Byass

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry · Jerez house founded in 1835 by Manuel María González, creator of Tío Pepe fino and owner of a wide heritage of soleras in Jerez's historic centre.

Gramona

Penedès · Historic family house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, a benchmark for long-aged sparkling wines based on Xarel·lo and a founding member of the Corpinnat collective brand.

Grosset

Clare Valley · Small Clare Valley winery founded in 1981 by Jeffrey Grosset. It is a benchmark for dry Australian Riesling with its Polish Hill and Springvale wines.

Guido Berlucchi

Franciacorta · House in Borgonato, Corte Franca, where in 1961 Guido Berlucchi and the winemaker Franco Ziliani made the first traditional-method sparkling wine in Franciacorta.

Gérard Bertrand

Languedoc-Roussillon · Languedoc wine group created by Gérard Bertrand, with numerous estates in the south of France and a strong commitment to biodynamic viticulture.

Henri Bourgeois

Sancerre · Family of growers in Chavignol, in Sancerre, with a wide range of Loire Sauvignon Blanc and a sister estate in Marlborough (New Zealand).

Henschke

Eden Valley · Family winery at Keyneton, in the Eden Valley, in the hands of the Henschke family since the nineteenth century. It is known for its single-vineyard Shiraz Hill of Grace and Mount Edelstone.

Herederos del Marqués de Riscal

Rioja · One of the pioneering wineries of modern Rioja, founded in Elciego (Rioja Alavesa) in 1858, which introduced Bordeaux techniques to the region; also a pioneer in Rueda.

J. Chivite Family Estates

Navarra · Navarra house with a long family tradition and roots documented in the seventeenth century, known for its Colección 125 range and its rosés.

José Maria da Fonseca

Setúbal · Family house founded in 1834 in Azeitão, on the Setúbal peninsula, known for its Moscatel de Setúbal, the Periquita red and Lancers rosé.

Kanonkop

Stellenbosch · Family estate on the slopes of the Simonsberg, in Stellenbosch, regarded as one of the great references for Pinotage and also known for its Bordeaux blend Paul Sauer.

Kendall-Jackson

Sonoma · Winery founded in 1982 by Jess Jackson, known above all for its Vintner’s Reserve Chardonnay and the origin of the family group Jackson Family Wines.

Klein Constantia

Constantia · Constantia estate that was part of Simon van der Stel’s historic property. It is famous for having revived Vin de Constance, the legendary sweet Muscat wine of the Cape.

La Rioja Alta, S.A.

Rioja · Winery founded in Haro in 1890, a benchmark for traditional Rioja aged in American oak, using barrels from its own cooperage.

Leeuwin Estate

Margaret River · Family estate in Margaret River founded in the 1970s by Denis and Tricia Horgan. Its Art Series Chardonnay is one of Australia’s best-known whites.

Les Vins Georges Duboeuf

Beaujolais · Romanèche-Thorins house founded by Georges Duboeuf, a key figure in the international spread of Beaujolais and Beaujolais Nouveau.

Luís Pato

Bairrada · Family producer in Bairrada, in Óis do Bairro, whose work with the Baga grape, in single-vineyard and sparkling wines, did much to build the region’s international reputation.

M. Chapoutier

Northern Rhône · Tain-l’Hermitage house dating back to 1808, a major landowner on the hill of Hermitage and a pioneer of biodynamics and of braille labels.

Maison Joseph Drouhin

Burgundy · Family-owned Beaune house founded in 1880, with its own vineyards in Chablis, the Côte de Nuits and the Côte de Beaune, and an offshoot in Oregon.

Maison Louis Jadot

Burgundy · Beaune house founded in 1859, one of Burgundy’s major négociants-éleveurs, with its own vineyards from the Côte de Nuits to Beaujolais.

Maison Louis Latour

Côte de Beaune · Family-owned Burgundy house with vineyards in Aloxe-Corton since the late 18th century, closely linked to the Grand Cru Corton-Charlemagne.

Maison Trimbach

Alsace · Family-owned house in Ribeauvillé dating back to 1626, known for its dry Rieslings and for Clos Sainte Hune.

Marchesi Antinori

Tuscany · Florentine family involved in wine since 1385, one of Italy’s most influential houses and the creator of Tignanello, one of the first great “Super Tuscans”.

Marchesi Frescobaldi

Tuscany · Old Florentine family with centuries of winemaking tradition and several Tuscan estates, including Castello di Nipozzano in Chianti Rufina and Castelgiocondo in Montalcino.

Marqués de Murrieta

Rioja · Historic Rioja winery based on the Ygay estate, southeast of Logroño, whose origins go back to the mid-nineteenth century and Luciano de Murrieta.

Masciarelli

Abruzzo · Winery founded in 1981 by Gianni Masciarelli in San Martino sulla Marrucina (Chieti), one of those that brought prestige to Montepulciano and Trebbiano d’Abruzzo.

Masi Agricola

Valpolicella · The Boscaini family’s company, established in Valpolicella since 1772, specialising in the appassimento technique and known for its Costasera Amarone and for Campofiorin.

Mastroberardino

Campania · Historic family winery in Atripalda, in Irpinia, credited with saving varieties such as Aglianico, Fiano and Greco from oblivion after the Second World War.

Meerlust

Stellenbosch · Historic Stellenbosch estate near False Bay, owned by the Myburgh family since the eighteenth century. It is best known for Rubicon, one of South Africa’s first Bordeaux blends.

Mionetto

Prosecco · Prosecco house founded in Valdobbiadene in 1887 by Francesco Mionetto, now one of the most widely distributed Prosecco brands in the world.

Moric

Burgenland · Project of winegrower Roland Velich in Burgenland, devoted to old-vine Blaufränkisch from Lutzmannsburg and Neckenmarkt in a style that favours finesse over power.

Moët & Chandon

Champagne · Champagne house founded in 1743 in Épernay by Claude Moët, now part of the LVMH group and one of the most widely distributed houses in the world.

Niepoort

Port · Family house of Dutch origin founded in 1842, renowned for its Colheita and Vintage Ports and a pioneer of modern Douro reds and whites with wines such as Redoma.

Opus One Winery

Napa Valley · A joint venture between Baron Philippe de Rothschild and Robert Mondavi, whose first vintage was 1979. It makes a single grand Bordeaux-style red in Oakville.

Pago de Carraovejas

Ribera del Duero · Peñafiel winery founded in the late 1980s by Segovian restaurateur José María Ruiz, with its own vineyards on the slopes facing the castle.

Pascal Jolivet

Sancerre · Upper Loire house founded in 1987, specialising in Sauvignon Blanc from Sancerre and Pouilly-Fumé.

Pazo de Señoráns

Rías Baixas · Family winery set in a historic manor house in the Val do Salnés, in Meis (Pontevedra), known for its lees-aged Albariños.

Penfolds

Barossa Valley · Historic house founded in 1844 at Magill, near Adelaide, and now with its main winery in the Barossa. It is famous for Grange and for its tradition of blending grapes from several regions.

Perelada

Empordà · Historic Alt Empordà winery linked to the castle of Peralada, producing still wines under the Empordà D.O. and sparkling wines under the Cava D.O.

Pieropan

Soave · Family winery in Soave founded in 1880, a benchmark for Soave Classico and a pioneer of the area’s single-vineyard whites with Calvarino and La Rocca.

Pio Cesare

Piedmont · Family house founded in Alba in 1881 by Cesare Pio, with cellars in the town’s historic centre and classic Langhe wines such as Barolo, Barbaresco and Barbera d’Alba.

Planeta

Sicily · Sicilian family company that emerged in the 1990s in the Menfi area, with wineries spread across different parts of the island, from Etna to Vittoria and Noto.

Produttori del Barbaresco

Barbaresco · Cooperative founded in 1958 in the village of Barbaresco, bringing together local growers and making only Nebbiolo wines, including several highly regarded single-vineyard bottlings.

Prunotto

Piedmont · Alba winery that grew out of a cooperative founded in 1904, took the name of Alfredo Prunotto and has belonged to the Antinori family since 1989.

Quinta do Crasto

Douro · Family-owned quinta in the Cima Corgo, on the right bank of the Douro, known for its Douro reds from old vines and for its Ports.

Quinta do Vallado

Douro · One of the oldest quintas in the Douro, near Peso da Régua, once owned by Dona Antónia Adelaide Ferreira and still in the hands of her descendants; it makes Douro wines and Ports.

R. López de Heredia Viña Tondonia

Rioja · Historic house in the Station District of Haro, founded in 1877 and regarded as one of the leading exponents of classic, long barrel-aged Rioja.

Rafael Palacios

Valdeorras · Winery founded in 2004 by Rafael Palacios in O Bolo (Ourense), devoted to Godello whites from mountain vineyards in the Bibei valley.

Raimat

Costers del Segre · Large wine estate near Lleida, acquired in 1914 by Manuel Raventós of the Codorníu family, and a pioneer of the Costers del Segre D.O.

Ramos Pinto

Port · Port house founded in 1880 by Adriano Ramos Pinto, known for its historic advertising posters, its studies of Douro grape varieties and Quinta da Ervamoira.

Recaredo

Penedès · Family house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia specialising in long-aged Brut Nature sparkling wines made from its own biodynamically farmed grapes, today released under the Corpinnat collective brand.

Ridge Vineyards

Sonoma · Californian winery founded in 1962, famous for its Monte Bello Cabernet Sauvignon from the Santa Cruz Mountains and for its old-vine Zinfandels from Sonoma, such as Lytton Springs and Geyserville.

Robert Mondavi Winery

Napa Valley · Winery founded in 1966 in Oakville by Robert Mondavi, a key figure in the modernisation of Californian wine and the spread of quality varietal wines.

Schloss Johannisberg

Rheingau · Historic Rheingau estate on a hill above the Rhine, linked by tradition to the discovery of Spätlese and known for its Rieslings identified by coloured seals.

Silver Oak Cellars

Napa Valley · Winery founded in 1972 by Justin Meyer and Ray Duncan that devotes itself exclusively to Cabernet Sauvignon, aged in American oak, with one wine from Napa Valley and another from Alexander Valley.

Soalheiro

Vinho Verde · Family winery in Melgaço, in the Monção e Melgaço subregion, a pioneer of bottled Alvarinho in the area and a benchmark for the variety in Portugal.

Sogrape

Douro · Portuguese family wine group founded in 1942, creator of Mateus Rosé and owner of Casa Ferreirinha, the Ferreira and Sandeman Port houses and estates in the Dão, Vinho Verde and Alentejo.

Stag's Leap Wine Cellars

Napa Valley · Winery founded in 1970 by Warren Winiarski in what is now the Stags Leap District, whose 1973 S.L.V. Cabernet Sauvignon was the top-ranked red at the 1976 Judgment of Paris.

Symington Family Estates

Port · Family group of Scottish origin, one of the leading Port producers, owner of historic houses such as Graham’s, Dow’s, Warre’s and Cockburn’s and of many quintas in the Douro.

Tablas Creek Vineyard

Paso Robles · Paso Robles winery founded in 1989 by the Perrin family of Château de Beaucastel and the importer Robert Haas, devoted to Rhône varieties grown from plant material brought from France.

Tasca d'Almerita

Sicily · Sicilian family that has owned the Regaleali estate, in the interior of the island, since the nineteenth century, producing wines from several areas, from Etna to the island of Salina.

Taylor’s (Taylor, Fladgate & Yeatman)

Port · Port house founded in 1692, one of the oldest in the trade, known for its Vintage Ports, for popularising Late Bottled Vintage and for its Quinta de Vargellas in the Douro Superior.

Tenuta San Guido

Bolgheri · The Incisa della Rocchetta family estate in Bolgheri, home of Sassicaia, the Cabernet-based red that brought international fame to the Tuscan coast.

Tenuta dell'Ornellaia

Bolgheri · Bolgheri estate founded in 1981 by Lodovico Antinori, now owned by the Frescobaldi family, known for its Bordeaux-style red Ornellaia.

The Eyrie Vineyards

Willamette Valley · A pioneering Willamette Valley winery founded by David and Diana Lett, who in the 1960s planted the area’s first Pinot Noir and the first Pinot Gris in the United States.

The Sadie Family Wines

Swartland · Winery of winemaker Eben Sadie on the Paardeberg, in the Swartland, a key figure in the renewal of South African wine with his Columella and Palladius blends and his old-vine series.

Tinto Pesquera (Familia Fernández Rivera)

Ribera del Duero · Winery created by Alejandro Fernández in Pesquera de Duero, whose Tinto Pesquera played a decisive role in making Ribera del Duero known worldwide.

Tormaresca

Puglia · The Antinori family’s project in Apulia, set up in 1998, with two estates: Bocca di Lupo, in Castel del Monte, and Masseria Maime, in Salento.

Toro Albalá

Montilla-Moriles · Winery in Aguilar de la Frontera, in the Montilla-Moriles D.O., known for its long-aged sweet Pedro Ximénez wines, especially the Don PX range.

Trapiche

Mendoza · One of the oldest and largest wineries in Argentina, with origins in Mendoza in 1883. It is part of Grupo Peñaflor and makes a broad range, from everyday wines to single-vineyard Malbecs.

Txomin Etxaniz

Getariako Txakolina · Family winery in Getaria (Gipuzkoa), with vineyards facing the Bay of Biscay, whose white txakoli is one of the best known in the appellation.

Tyrrell's

Hunter Valley · Family winery in the Hunter Valley founded in 1858 by Edward Tyrrell. It is one of the great references for Hunter Semillon and was a pioneer of Chardonnay in Australia.

Veramonte

Casablanca Valley · Casablanca Valley winery created in the 1990s by Agustín Huneeus. Known for its cool-climate whites and Pinot Noir, it is part of the Spanish group González Byass.

Veuve Clicquot Ponsardin

Champagne · Reims house founded in 1772 and shaped by Barbe-Nicole Ponsardin, the “widow Clicquot”, credited with the riddling table; famous for its yellow label.

Villa Maria

Marlborough · Company founded in 1961 in Auckland by George Fistonich, of Dalmatian descent, which became one of New Zealand’s largest producers, with a major presence in Marlborough.

Viña Almaviva

Maipo Valley · Winery born of a partnership between Concha y Toro and Baron Philippe de Rothschild, with vineyards in Puente Alto, in Alto Maipo. It makes a single grand vin, Almaviva, whose first vintage was 1996.

Viña Concha y Toro

Maipo Valley · Founded in 1883 by Melchor de Santiago Concha y Toro in Pirque, in the Maipo Valley, it is Chile’s largest wine company and one of the biggest in the world. Its range runs from Casillero del Diablo to the top red Don Melchor.

Viña De Martino

Maipo Valley · Family winery founded in 1934 by the Italian immigrant Pietro De Martino in Isla de Maipo. In recent decades it has become one of the most active Chilean houses in recovering old vineyards and fresher styles.

Viña Errázuriz

Aconcagua Valley · Estate founded in 1870 by Maximiano Errázuriz in Panquehue, in the Aconcagua Valley. Run by Eduardo Chadwick, a descendant of the founder, it is one of the houses that have driven Chile’s top-end reds and coastal viticulture in Aconcagua.

Viña Montes

Colchagua Valley · Chilean winery created in the late 1980s by the winemaker Aurelio Montes and three partners. Based in Apalta, in the Colchagua Valley, it is known for reds such as Montes Alpha M, Purple Angel and Folly.

Viña Santa Rita

Maipo Valley · Historic Maipo Valley estate founded in 1880 by Domingo Fernández Concha in Alto Jahuel. The property retains a nineteenth-century manor house, a park and a museum of pre-Columbian art.

W. & J. Graham’s

Port · Port house founded in 1820 by a family of Scottish merchants and owned by the Symington family since 1970, known for its rich-style Ports and for Quinta dos Malvedos.

Weingut Bründlmayer

Kamptal · Family estate in Langenlois, in the Kamptal, known for its Rieslings from the Heiligenstein, its Grüner Veltliners from loess and its traditional-method sparkling wines.

Weingut Dr. Bürklin-Wolf

Pfalz · Large family estate in the Mittelhaardt, in the Pfalz, with vines in the finest vineyards of Forst, Deidesheim and Ruppertsberg, farmed biodynamically and classified according to its own system.

Weingut Dr. Loosen

Mosel · Family estate in the Middle Mosel run by Ernst Loosen, with old vines on some of the region’s best slopes, such as Erdener Prälat and Wehlener Sonnenuhr.

Weingut Egon Müller – Scharzhof

Mosel · Family estate in the Saar valley, owner of most of the Scharzhofberg vineyard and famous for its Prädikat Rieslings with sweetness, among the most sought-after white wines in the world.

Weingut F.X. Pichler

Wachau · Family estate in Oberloiben, in the Wachau, renowned for its Grüner Veltliner and Riesling Smaragd from vineyards such as Kellerberg, Loibenberg and Steinertal.

Weingut Franz Hirtzberger

Wachau · Family estate in Spitz, at the western end of the Wachau, renowned for its Riesling and Grüner Veltliner Smaragd from vineyards such as Singerriedel and Honivogl.

Weingut Gunderloch

Rheinhessen · Estate in Nackenheim, on Rheinhessen’s Roter Hang, founded in 1890 and known for its Rieslings from the Nackenheimer Rothenberg vineyard, on red Permian soils.

Weingut Hermann Dönnhoff

Nahe · Family estate in Oberhausen that has placed the Nahe among the great Riesling regions, with precise wines from vineyards such as Hermannshöhle, Brücke and Felsenberg.

Weingut Joh. Jos. Prüm

Mosel · Family estate in Wehlen, in the Middle Mosel, emblematic for its Rieslings from the Wehlener Sonnenuhr vineyard, with delicate sweetness and great longevity.

Weingut Juliusspital

Franken · Winery of the hospital foundation established in Würzburg in 1576 by Prince-Bishop Julius Echter, one of Germany’s largest wine estates and a benchmark for Franconian Silvaner.

Weingut Künstler

Rheingau · Family estate in Hochheim, at the eastern end of the Rheingau, known for its dry Rieslings from vineyards such as Hölle, Kirchenstück and Domdechaney.

Weingut Robert Weil

Rheingau · Estate in Kiedrich, in the Rheingau, founded in 1875 and devoted almost entirely to Riesling, with the Kiedricher Gräfenberg vineyard as its emblem.

Weinlaubenhof Kracher

Burgenland · Family estate in Illmitz, in the Seewinkel by Lake Neusiedl, internationally known for its nobly sweet wines.

Wynns Coonawarra Estate

Coonawarra · Historic Coonawarra winery whose triple-gabled building, erected in 1891 by John Riddoch, is the symbol of the region. It is best known for its Black Label Cabernet Sauvignon.

Yalumba

Barossa Valley · Winery founded in 1849 at Angaston by Samuel Smith and still in the hands of his descendants. It is one of Australia’s oldest family houses and a pioneer of Viognier in the country.

d'Arenberg

McLaren Vale · Family winery in McLaren Vale, founded in 1912 by Joseph Osborn and now run by his great-grandson Chester Osborn. It is known for its old-vine Shiraz and Grenache and for its unusual wine names.

Álvaro Palacios

Priorat · Álvaro Palacios's winery in Gratallops, one of the leading names in the revival of Priorat in the late 1980s, focused on old-vine Garnacha and Carignan grown on llicorella slate.
