Viña Cono Sur

Chilean winery founded in 1993 by Concha y Toro, based in Chimbarongo, Colchagua, and known for its Pinot Noir and for the bicycle on its labels.

Viña Cono Sur was created in 1993 by the Concha y Toro group as an independent winery geared towards exports and a younger image of Chilean wine. Its name refers to the “Southern Cone” of the Americas, the southern part of the continent formed by Chile, Argentina and Uruguay. Its headquarters and main winery are in Chimbarongo, in the Colchagua Valley, surrounded by vineyards.

From the start Cono Sur set its sights on Pinot Noir, a variety that was then little planted in Chile, and became one of its leading producers in the country, with vineyards in cool areas such as Casablanca, San Antonio or the Biobío Valley further south. Its range also includes Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Riesling, Gewürztraminer, Cabernet Sauvignon, Carmenère and other varieties, with grapes from various Chilean valleys.

The bicycle that appears on many of its labels recalls the fact that the winery’s workers used to get around the vineyards by bicycle, and it has become a symbol of the house and of its interest in sustainability, with practices such as cover crops and the use of animals in pest control. Its affordably priced Bicicleta line is one of its best sellers abroad, while wines such as 20 Barrels and Ocio represent its more ambitious ranges, made in smaller quantities from selected plots.
