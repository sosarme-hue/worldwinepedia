Château Haut-Bailly

Classified growth of Graves in Léognan, in the Pessac-Léognan appellation, devoted exclusively to red wine and known for its elegance and finesse.

Château Haut-Bailly lies in Léognan, south of Bordeaux, in the Pessac-Léognan appellation, created in 1987 within the Graves area. It is among the reds in the classification of Graves wines drawn up in the 1950s and, unlike many of its neighbours, makes only red wine.

For much of the twentieth century it belonged to the Sanders family. In 1998 it was bought by the American banker Robert G. Wilmers, whose family still owns it, while management remained in the hands of Véronique Sanders, a descendant of the previous owners. Since then the estate has renewed its winery and vineyard.

The vineyard occupies a rise of gravel and sand over a clay-limestone subsoil. It is planted with Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, with some Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, and retains a parcel of century-old vines in which the different varieties are interplanted, as was customary before phylloxera.

The estate makes its grand vin, Château Haut-Bailly, and a second wine, La Parde de Haut-Bailly, later renamed Haut-Bailly II. The house style favours finesse, silky texture and balance over power.
