Winery

Bodegas Ateca

Calatayud D.O. winery belonging to the Gil Family Estates group, focused on high-altitude old-vine Garnacha and known for the Atteca and Honoro Vera wines.

Bodegas Ateca is located in the town of the same name in the province of Zaragoza, in the Jalón river valley, within the Calatayud D.O. It is part of Gil Family Estates, the group of the Gil family, originally from Jumilla, which brings together wineries in several Spanish designations.

High-altitude vineyards

Calatayud is one of the highest winegrowing areas in Aragon: many vineyards lie above 700 metres and some approach 1,000. The climate is extreme continental, with cold winters, hot summers and wide day-night temperature swings that help the grapes keep their acidity. Soils are poor, with slate, stones and limestone. Many old bush-trained Garnacha vines survive in this setting, and they form the core of the winery's work.

Wines

The winery makes a range that runs from young, very affordable Garnachas such as Honoro Vera Garnacha to barrel-aged old-vine wines such as Atteca and its more selective version, Atteca Armas. The style aims to show Garnacha's ripe fruit together with the freshness brought by altitude.
