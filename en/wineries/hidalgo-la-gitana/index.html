Winery

Bodegas Hidalgo-La Gitana

Family winery in Sanlúcar de Barrameda founded in 1792, best known for La Gitana manzanilla.

Bodegas Hidalgo-La Gitana was founded in 1792 in Sanlúcar de Barrameda, the town at the mouth of the Guadalquivir where manzanilla is made. It has remained in the hands of the Hidalgo family for generations, making it one of the oldest family wineries in the Jerez area. Its cellars stand in the town's historic centre.

Manzanilla

Manzanilla is a biologically aged wine made from Palomino that can only be aged in Sanlúcar. The proximity of the sea and the river gives the town high humidity and mild temperatures, conditions in which the flor —the layer of yeast that covers the wine in the cask— stays alive and thick all year round. The result is a wine even paler, lighter and more saline than the fino of Jerez or El Puerto. Ageing takes place in the criaderas and solera system, in which wines of different ages are gradually blended in stages.

Wines

The house's best-known wine is La Gitana, one of the most widely available manzanillas, whose label reproduces the portrait of a Gypsy woman. The winery also makes the manzanilla pasada Pastrana, from a specific vineyard and with longer ageing, and a range of older wines such as the amontillado Napoleón, olorosos, palo cortados and Pedro Ximénez. Hidalgo-La Gitana also owns vineyards in albariza districts near Sanlúcar.
