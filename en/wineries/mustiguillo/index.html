Winery

Bodega Mustiguillo

Toni Sarrión's winery on the Utiel-Requena plateau, a pioneer in championing Bobal as a quality variety and holder of the El Terrerazo Vino de Pago.

Mustiguillo is the project of Toni Sarrión, who in the late 1990s set out to show that Bobal, the main red grape of the Utiel-Requena plateau, could produce fine wines and not just bulk or blending wine. The winery is on the El Terrerazo estate, in the interior of the province of Valencia.

El Terrerazo

The estate lies at about 800 metres, with a continental climate of cold winters, hot summers and a sharp contrast between day and night. Soils are limestone and clay, with plenty of stones. The site's particular conditions led to its recognition as Vino de Pago El Terrerazo, the Spanish category for wines from a specific estate with singular characteristics; its wines are therefore not labelled under the Utiel-Requena D.O., although the estate lies in that district.

Wines

The winery works mainly with Bobal, often from old vines, and has also revived the white variety Merseguera. Its range includes Mestizaje, a fresher, more approachable red and white, Finca Terrerazo, the estate's flagship Bobal, and Quincha Corral, from a plot of old vines. The house style seeks freshness, polished tannins and moderate use of oak.
