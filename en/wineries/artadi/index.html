Winery

Artadi

Winery in Laguardia run by Juan Carlos López de Lacalle, which grew out of a growers’ association in 1985 and is known for its single-vineyard wines from Rioja Alavesa; in the 2010s it left the Rioja DOCa.

Artadi has its origins in 1985, when a group of growers in Laguardia, in Rioja Alavesa, joined forces to make and sell their own wines under the name Cosecheros Alaveses. Over time the project passed into the hands of the López de Lacalle family, and Juan Carlos López de Lacalle became its main driving force. In time the Artadi brand, created for its wines, came to give its name to the whole winery.

From the 1990s Artadi moved towards more concentrated wines from old Tempranillo vines, and later towards the idea of the single vineyard. Its best-known wines bear the names of specific plots in Laguardia and Elvillar, such as Viña El Pisón, one of the most highly regarded vineyards in the area, along with others such as La Poza de Ballesteros and El Carretil. Viñas de Gain is its entry-level red.

The winery argues that wines should be identified by their village and vineyard, in the manner of Burgundy, rather than by ageing times. Because of disagreements with the model of the Denominación de Origen Calificada, Artadi announced in the mid-2010s that it was leaving the Rioja DOCa, so its wines from later vintages are not sold with that designation, although they still come from the same vineyards in Rioja Alavesa.

The current style seeks freshness, precision and the expression of limestone soils, with less pronounced oak than in its early years.
