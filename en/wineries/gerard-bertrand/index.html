Gérard Bertrand

Languedoc wine group created by Gérard Bertrand, with numerous estates in the south of France and a strong commitment to biodynamic viticulture.

Gérard Bertrand grew up at Domaine de Villemajou, the family estate in the Corbières, where his father, Georges Bertrand, was a grower and wine merchant. Before devoting himself fully to wine he was a professional rugby player. After his father’s death in 1987 he took over the estate and, over the years, founded his own company, which has become one of the leading producers in Languedoc-Roussillon.

The group brings together a series of estates across various appellations in the south of France, such as Corbières, Minervois, La Clape and Limoux, and is headquartered at Château l’Hospitalet, in the La Clape massif near Narbonne. Gérard Bertrand has driven the conversion of his vineyards to biodynamic farming and is one of its most visible advocates in the region.

The range is very broad: from IGP Pays d’Oc varietal wines and widely available rosés such as Côte des Roses and Gris Blanc, to estate wines and top cuvées such as Clos d’Ora, in the Minervois.

The house style seeks to highlight the diversity of Mediterranean terroirs and varieties — Grenache, Syrah, Mourvèdre, Carignan, Cinsault — with wines of generous fruit and a sunny profile.

The group also makes sparkling wines in Limoux.

Visits & wine tourism

Château l’Hospitalet, near Narbonne, welcomes visitors, with tastings, a restaurant and accommodation.
