Winery

Bodegas Jorge Ordóñez & Co.

Málaga winery founded by the importer Jorge Ordóñez to revive the Moscatel wines of the Axarquía, both dry and sweet.

Jorge Ordóñez, born in Málaga into a family linked to the wine trade, is best known as an importer of Spanish wines into the United States, where he helped make wineries from many regions known. With his Málaga winery he set out to revive the tradition of Moscatel wines from the Axarquía district, in the east of the province.

Mountain vineyards

The Axarquía vineyards sit on very steep slate slopes, often so sharply inclined that all work is done by hand or with the help of mules. Old vines of Muscat of Alexandria survive there, a variety traditionally also used to make raisins, which are dried in the sun on the characteristic drying beds known as paseros.

Wines

The winery makes two main lines. On the one hand, dry Moscatel whites such as Botani, which show the aromatic, fresh side of the variety, under the Sierras de Málaga D.O. On the other, a series of numbered sweet wines —among them Nº 1 Selección Especial— made from overripe Moscatel grapes, developed with the collaboration of the Austrian producer Alois Kracher, a specialist in sweet wines.
