Oyster Bay

New Zealand brand created in 1990 by the Delegat family, known internationally for its Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc.

Oyster Bay is the main brand of Delegat, a New Zealand family company whose origins go back to 1947, when the Croatian immigrant Nikola Delegat bought a small property near Auckland and began making wine. His children, Jim and Rosemari Delegat, gradually turned that modest business into one of the country's largest producers.

The Oyster Bay brand was born in 1990 with a clear aim: to take a fresh, aromatic and recognisable style of New Zealand wine to international markets, starting from vineyards in Marlborough, at the north-eastern tip of the South Island. The region, sunny yet cool, with cold nights and stony alluvial soils, had proved to be an exceptional place for Sauvignon Blanc, and Oyster Bay's soon became one of the best-selling New Zealand wines in countries such as the United Kingdom, Australia and the United States.

The house works with its own vineyards and those of partner growers in Marlborough and in Hawke's Bay, on the North Island. Besides Sauvignon Blanc, the range includes Chardonnay, Pinot Gris, Pinot Noir, Merlot, rosés and sparkling wines. The style is consistent across them all: wines with clear-cut fruit, fresh and balanced, generally made without a marked influence of oak so as to preserve the aromatic expression of the grape.

Oyster Bay is thus a good example of the commercial success that turned New Zealand, within a few decades, into a world reference for aromatic whites.
