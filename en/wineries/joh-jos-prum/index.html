Weingut Joh. Jos. Prüm

Family estate in Wehlen, in the Middle Mosel, emblematic for its Rieslings from the Wehlener Sonnenuhr vineyard, with delicate sweetness and great longevity.

The Prüm family has been present in Wehlen for centuries. The Joh. Jos. Prüm estate was born in 1911, when, following a division of inheritance, Johann Josef Prüm created his own estate with part of the family vineyards. His son Sebastian Prüm consolidated the house’s prestige; it was later run for decades by Manfred Prüm and is now in the hands of his daughter Katharina Prüm. Other estates in the region also bear the Prüm name, but should not be confused with this one.

The heart of the vineyard holdings is the Wehlener Sonnenuhr, a very steep, south-facing slope of blue slate opposite the village of Wehlen, named after the large sundial the Prüm family had built among the vines in the nineteenth century. The estate also owns vines in the Graacher Himmelreich, Zeltinger Sonnenuhr and Bernkasteler Badstube, many of them old.

J.J. Prüm makes almost exclusively Rieslings with residual sugar, in the Kabinett, Spätlese and Auslese categories and, in favourable years, nobly sweet wines. The wines ferment slowly and are aged in tanks and casks, in a very distinctive style: when young they can show reductive notes, and with age they develop great complexity and balance. The house is known for the ageing capacity of its wines, even in the lightest categories.
