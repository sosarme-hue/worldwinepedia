Bret Brothers & La Soufrandière

Project of brothers Jean-Philippe and Jean-Guillaume Bret in the southern Mâconnais, combining the family estate La Soufrandière and a small négociant house, Bret Brothers.

Brothers Jean-Philippe and Jean-Guillaume Bret run two complementary businesses in Vinzelles, in the southern Mâconnais (Burgundy). La Soufrandière is the family estate, with its own vineyards mainly in Pouilly-Vinzelles and the surrounding area, which the brothers farm organically and biodynamically. Bret Brothers is their négociant label, under which they vinify grapes bought from growers in various appellations of the area, such as Pouilly-Fuissé, Saint-Véran and the village Mâcons.

Almost all their production centres on Chardonnay, the great white grape of the Mâconnais. The brothers work by climat, bottling individual plots separately to show the differences in soil and exposure between them, a practice that helped renew the image of a region traditionally associated with simple blended whites.

Their style aims for precise, fresh wines, with proper ripeness and ageing that respects the fruit.

Their labels span the range of southern Mâconnais appellations, from Mâcon-Vinzelles and Mâcon-Igé to Pouilly-Vinzelles, Saint-Véran and Pouilly-Fuissé, including some of the premiers crus recognised in the latter appellation in 2020.

The southern Mâconnais, dominated by the limestone rocks of Solutré and Vergisson, offers a mosaic of clay and limestone soils and very varied exposures, which makes the plot-by-plot approach practised by the Bret brothers particularly meaningful.
