Cullen Wines

Family winery in Margaret River founded in 1971 by Kevin and Diana Cullen and run by their daughter Vanya, a pioneer of biodynamic viticulture in Australia.

Cullen is one of the founding wineries of Margaret River. In 1971 the physician Kevin Cullen and his wife Diana planted their first vines at Wilyabrup, in the central part of the region, shortly after the area's first pioneers. Diana Cullen was in charge of winemaking for many years, and later their daughter Vanya Cullen took over as winemaker and managing director.

Under her leadership Cullen became one of the leading wineries in the application of biodynamic farming in Australia, with its vineyards certified according to these principles, and an example of environmental commitment, with measures to reduce and offset its emissions. The search for wines that express their place with as little intervention as possible lies at the heart of its philosophy.

Like the rest of Margaret River, Cullen's vineyards benefit from a Mediterranean climate tempered by the Indian Ocean. The house's most emblematic wine is Diana Madeline, a Cabernet Sauvignon-based blend with other Bordeaux varieties, named after the founder. Its white counterpart is Kevin John, a Chardonnay that pays tribute to Kevin Cullen. The range also includes Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon blends and single-site wines.

Cullen's style is marked by elegance, freshness and texture, with fine-grained reds capable of developing for many years in bottle.

Visits & wine tourism

It has a cellar door and restaurant on the estate at Wilyabrup.
