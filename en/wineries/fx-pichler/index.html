Winery

Weingut F.X. Pichler

Family estate in Oberloiben, in the Wachau, renowned for its Grüner Veltliner and Riesling Smaragd from vineyards such as Kellerberg, Loibenberg and Steinertal.

The Pichler family has grown vines for several generations in Oberloiben, part of the municipality of Dürnstein at the eastern end of the Wachau. It was Franz Xaver Pichler who, from the 1970s and 1980s onwards, gave the estate an international reputation, making it one of the benchmark names in Austrian wine. It is now run by his son Lucas Pichler. The estate is a member of the Vinea Wachau association.

Its vineyards are on some of the region’s most highly regarded terraces: the Dürnsteiner Kellerberg, a south-facing gneiss slope above the Danube; the Loibner Loibenberg, on dry-stone terraces; the Loibner Steinertal, a cooler side valley with primary-rock soils; and others such as Liebenberg. Viticulture is manual on terraces that in many cases can only be worked on foot.

Production focuses on dry Grüner Veltliner and Riesling, in the Federspiel and, above all, Smaragd categories. The house’s Smaragd wines are known for their concentration, aromatic richness and firm acidity, and for their ability to develop over many years in bottle. In the best years the estate also makes a selection wine called Unendlich, from both Riesling and Grüner Veltliner, using the ripest grapes from its finest plots. Fermentations take place in tanks and casks, without seeking new-oak notes.
