Domaine Alain Voge

A Cornas estate in the northern Rhône, linked to the figure of Alain Voge, producing Syrah from Cornas and Marsanne whites from Saint-Péray.

Domaine Alain Voge is based in Cornas, on the right bank of the Rhône opposite Valence. Alain Voge, from a family of local growers, was for decades one of the leading figures of the appellation and helped to make known both the reds of Cornas and the whites of neighbouring Saint-Péray. In recent years the estate has continued its work with a new management team while keeping its identity.

In Cornas, Syrah is the only permitted variety and is grown on steep granite slopes, often terraced. The estate’s reds are made by plot or by vine age, from wines from younger vines, more approachable, to old-vine cuvées designed for ageing.

In Saint-Péray, the southernmost appellation of the northern Rhône, overlooked by the ruins of the castle of Crussol, the estate makes whites from Marsanne, a variety that gives broad wines with an unctuous texture and notes of white fruit, flowers and almond.

The vineyards are farmed along organic and biodynamic lines, and winemaking seeks to express the purity of the fruit and the character of the granite soils, with ageing that respects the balance of the wines.

The estate is a common point of reference for anyone wishing to explore both the Syrah reds and the Marsanne whites of this southern end of the northern Rhône, two styles that show the diversity of the area.
