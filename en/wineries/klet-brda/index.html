Klet Brda

A wine cooperative in Goriška Brda, based in Dobrovo, which brings together many local growers and is one of Slovenia’s largest wine producers.

Klet Brda is the wine cooperative of Goriška Brda and is based in Dobrovo, the area’s central village, also known for its castle. Founded in the twentieth century, during the Yugoslav period, it brings together a large number of growers from the Brda hills, who deliver their grapes to it, and it is among the largest wine producers in Slovenia. It has played a decisive role in the local economy, where many families maintain small vineyard plots.

Its members’ vines are spread across the Brda hills, on the flysch marl and sandstone soils known as opoka, at varied exposures and altitudes. This diversity allows the cooperative to produce a wide range, from fresh, fruity everyday wines to plot selections and aged wines.

Production is organised into several lines. One of the most widely available is Quercus, which brings together fresh varietal whites and reds from grapes such as Rebula, Sauvignonasse, Pinot Gris, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Merlot, made to show the fruit and freshness of the area. The Bagueri line groups wines from selected vineyards with longer ageing. The cooperative also makes sparkling wines and blends, and has helped to make Rebula and the wines of Brda known on international markets.
