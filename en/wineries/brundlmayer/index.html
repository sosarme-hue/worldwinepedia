Winery

Weingut Bründlmayer

Family estate in Langenlois, in the Kamptal, known for its Rieslings from the Heiligenstein, its Grüner Veltliners from loess and its traditional-method sparkling wines.

The Bründlmayer family has grown vines in Langenlois for generations. Willi Bründlmayer, who took over the estate in the early 1980s, made it one of the best-known in Austria and a benchmark for dry Kamptal whites; the next generation of the family now shares in its management. The estate is a member of the Österreichische Traditionsweingüter association, which is devoted to vineyard classification.

Its vines are spread across some of the Kamptal’s best sites. The most famous is the Zöbinger Heiligenstein, a hill of Permian sandstone and conglomerates where the estate grows old-vine Riesling. Other leading vineyards are the Gaisberg, on crystalline schists, and the Lamm, Käferberg and Loiserberg, on the loess terraces of Langenlois, which are very well suited to Grüner Veltliner. In some plots the estate has used a lyre training system, which opens the canopy into two planes to improve the grapes’ exposure.

The range includes dry Grüner Veltliner and Riesling, from wines drawn from terraces across the area to long-lived single-vineyard wines, as well as Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Zweigelt and other varieties. Bründlmayer is also one of Austria’s most highly regarded producers of traditional-method sparkling wine, with long ageing on the lees. The house style seeks balance, freshness and vineyard expression, with moderate use of oak.
