Herdade do Mouchão

Historic estate in the Alto Alentejo, owned by the Reynolds family, of English origin, regarded as one of the cradles of Alicante Bouschet in Portugal and known for its traditional, long-lived reds.

Herdade do Mouchão lies in the Alto Alentejo, in an area of rolling plains, cork-oak woodland and very hot summers. The estate belongs to the Reynolds family, of English origin, who came to Portugal in the nineteenth century drawn by the cork trade and eventually devoted themselves to farming and wine as well.

Mouchão is known for having been one of the first Portuguese properties to plant Alicante Bouschet, a red-fleshed variety of French origin that found an adopted home in the Alentejo. Over time this grape has become the house’s signature and one of the region’s emblematic varieties.

The estate’s recent history was shaped by the revolution of 1974: like other large Alentejo properties, Mouchão was occupied during the agrarian reform, and the family did not regain control until years later, which meant rebuilding the vineyards and the winery.

The house keeps to very traditional methods. Part of the fruit is still trodden in marble lagares, and the wines age in large old wooden vats before spending a long time in bottle. Its main wine, Mouchão red, is made mostly from Alicante Bouschet; the range also includes Dom Rafael, in a more approachable style, and Ponte das Canas.
