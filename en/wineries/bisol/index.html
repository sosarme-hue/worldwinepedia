Bisol

Valdobbiadene winegrowing family with roots documented back to the sixteenth century, specialising in hillside Prosecco Superiore and Cartizze; part of the Lunelli group since 2014.

The Bisol family’s presence on the hills of Valdobbiadene is documented from the sixteenth century, and the house recalls that history in its brand, Bisol1542. The modern business developed in the twentieth century in Santo Stefano di Valdobbiadene, at the heart of the Conegliano Valdobbiadene Prosecco Superiore DOCG.

The winery works with its own vineyards spread across the area’s steep slopes, including part of the Cartizze hill, the appellation’s most prestigious subzone. The main variety is Glera, the Prosecco grape, grown here on slopes where much of the work is done by hand.

In 2014 the Lunelli group, owner of Ferrari in Trento, acquired a majority stake in the business, while the Bisol family remained involved in its management. The house is also known for the Venissa project on the island of Mazzorbo, in the Venetian lagoon, where the old Dorona variety was revived.

Its range includes several Valdobbiadene Prosecco Superiore wines, such as Crede, Cartizze and single-vineyard bottlings, as well as traditional-method sparkling wines.
