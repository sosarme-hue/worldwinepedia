Domaine Zind-Humbrecht

Alsace domaine based in Turckheim, born of the union of the Zind and Humbrecht families, known for its biodynamically farmed grand cru and clos wines.

Domaine Zind-Humbrecht was born in 1959 from the union of two Alsace winegrowing families, when Léonard Humbrecht married Geneviève Zind. The Humbrecht family had been growing vines in Gueberschwihr for generations, and the marriage brought together the vineyards of both families. Over the years Léonard Humbrecht acquired parcels in some of the finest terroirs of Alsace, and the domaine settled in Turckheim, near Colmar.

His son Olivier Humbrecht, who in 1989 became the first Frenchman to earn the title of Master of Wine, has run the domaine since the late 1980s. Under his direction the vineyard was converted to biodynamics, and the house has distinguished itself by its work on the expression of each soil.

Zind-Humbrecht owns vines in several grands crus, such as Rangen — home to the Clos Saint Urbain — Brand, Hengst and Goldert, as well as in highly reputed clos such as the Clos Windsbuhl in Hunawihr, and the Clos Jebsal and Clos Hauserer in Turckheim and Wintzenheim. It makes Riesling, Pinot Gris, Gewurztraminer and Muscat, among other varieties.

The house’s wines are often concentrated and broad in texture, with a level of residual sugar that varies by parcel and vintage. To guide consumers, the labels include an index indicating the degree of perceived sweetness.
