Marqués de Murrieta

Historic Rioja winery based on the Ygay estate, southeast of Logroño, whose origins go back to the mid-nineteenth century and Luciano de Murrieta.

Luciano de Murrieta, a military man by training who had become familiar with Bordeaux winemaking, began making and selling aged Rioja wines in the mid-nineteenth century, and the winery dates its foundation to 1852. Some years later he acquired the Ygay estate, southeast of Logroño, which has been the heart of the house ever since. For his contributions he was granted the title of Marquis of Murrieta.

In the 1980s the winery passed to the Cebrián-Sagarriga family, which has kept the house’s classic approach and renovated its facilities, including the Ygay castle that presides over the estate. The family also runs Pazo Barrantes, in Rías Baixas.

The Ygay estate brings together in a single property the vineyard from which the winery makes its wines, on clay and pebble soils in the Rioja Alta area. Tempranillo, Grenache, Mazuelo (Carignan) and Graciano are grown, along with Viura for the whites.

Its best-known labels are Marqués de Murrieta Reserva, the white Capellanía, the modern red Dalmau and Castillo Ygay Gran Reserva Especial, a long-aged wine made only in certain vintages. The style is that of classic Rioja, barrel-aged and built for the cellar.
