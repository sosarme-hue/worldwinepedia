Wolf Blass

Barossa winery founded in 1966 by the German-born winemaker Wolfgang Blass, known for its colour-coded label ranges, from Yellow Label to Black Label.

Wolfgang Blass, a German-trained winemaker, arrived in Australia in the early 1960s and in 1966 founded his own company in the Barossa. From the outset he championed a style of red that was softer and more approachable in its youth than many Australian wines of the time, with generous fruit and well-integrated oak, and he became popular both for his wines and for his outgoing personality.

The house settled at Nuriootpa, in the northern Barossa, and grew quickly into one of the best-known brands in the country. One of its hallmarks is the organisation of the range by label colour: Yellow Label and Red Label group everyday wines, while the Grey, Platinum and Black Label tiers are the more ambitious reds, often blends from several regions of South Australia.

The practice of combining grapes from different areas, deeply rooted in Australian tradition, allows the winemaking team to craft wines with a recognisable style vintage after vintage. Wolf Blass is now part of the Treasury Wine Estates group, which also includes other historic houses of the country.

Overall, the winery is a good example of modern Australian wine: fruity, polished Cabernet Sauvignon and Shiraz reds alongside clean Chardonnay and Riesling whites, designed to appeal to a broad public.
