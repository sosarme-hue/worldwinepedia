Winery

Ao Yun

A Moët Hennessy project in the mountains of north-western Yunnan, near Meili Snow Mountain, with vineyards at more than two thousand metres. It makes a single top-end red based on Cabernet Sauvignon, whose first vintage was 2013.

Ao Yun is the result of a search undertaken by the French group Moët Hennessy to make a top-end red in China. After studying various parts of the country, the group chose a mountainous area in the north-west of Yunnan province, in the foothills of the Himalayas, near Meili Snow Mountain and in the valleys through which the upper Mekong flows. The first vintage was 2013. The wine’s name is usually translated as “flying above the clouds”.

The vineyards are spread among several villages on the valley slopes, at altitudes above two thousand metres. They are small plots, often terraced, farmed in collaboration with local farming families, who do much of the work by hand. Altitude offsets the low latitude: days are sunny, nights cool and the growing season long. The low atmospheric pressure at this height and the remoteness of the area made it necessary to adapt winemaking techniques and logistics.

Ao Yun makes a single wine, a red bearing the estate name, based on Cabernet Sauvignon and completed with Cabernet Franc and, depending on the year, other varieties. It is aged in barrel and aims for a style of concentrated fruit and freshness, with firm tannins. Because of its origin, its altitude and the backing of a large international group, the project has drawn considerable attention to Yunnan’s potential as a wine area.
