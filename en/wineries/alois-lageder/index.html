Alois Lageder

Alto Adige family winery founded in 1823 in Bolzano and now based in Magrè, known for its commitment to biodynamic farming and for its mountain whites.

Johann Lageder founded the business in Bolzano in 1823 as a wine-trading company. During the twentieth century the following generations turned it into a winery with its own vineyards and moved to Magrè sulla Strada del Vino, in southern Alto Adige, where the family owns the historic Casòn Hirschprunn estate and Tòr Löwengang.

The company is now run by the Lageder family, in which Alois Lageder, of the fifth generation, and his son Alois Clemens have played leading roles. Since the 1990s the winery has turned to organic and then biodynamic farming in its own vineyards, and encourages the growers it works with to follow the same path.

Alto Adige is an alpine area of hillside vineyards, with warm summers and cool nights, favouring aromatic whites with good acidity. Lageder works with a wide range of varieties, such as Pinot Grigio, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Gewürztraminer, Pinot Noir and Lagrein.

Its production is organised into a line of widely available varietal wines, such as its Pinot Grigio, and into estate and single-vineyard wines from its own vineyards.
