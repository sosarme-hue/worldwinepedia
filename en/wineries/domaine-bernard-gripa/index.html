Domaine Bernard Gripa

Family estate in Mauves, on the right bank of the northern Rhône, known for its red and white Saint-Joseph and its Saint-Péray whites.

Domaine Bernard Gripa is based in Mauves, a village in the Ardèche department opposite Tournon, on the right bank of the northern Rhône. Mauves is part of the historic core of the Saint-Joseph appellation, and the Gripa family has grown vines in the area for several generations. Bernard Gripa made the estate’s name, and his son Fabrice has since taken over.

The vines are spread across two appellations. In Saint-Joseph, the granite slopes, often terraced, are planted with Syrah for the reds and Marsanne and Roussanne for the whites. A little further south, in Saint-Péray, a small appellation next to Cornas devoted exclusively to whites, the estate grows Marsanne and Roussanne on granite and limestone soils.

The house style is classic: Syrah reds with precise fruit, spicy notes and firm structure, and broad yet fresh whites, with measured use of oak. The range includes red and white Saint-Joseph, the old-vine cuvée Le Berceau, and the Saint-Péray wines Les Pins and Les Figuiers.

Gripa is regarded as one of the benchmark producers of Saint-Péray, a little-known appellation that has regained prestige in recent decades.
