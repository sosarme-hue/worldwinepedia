Beringer Vineyards

Winery founded in 1876 in St. Helena by the brothers Jacob and Frederick Beringer; it is one of the oldest continuously operating wineries in Napa.

The German-born brothers Jacob and Frederick Beringer founded the winery in 1876 in St. Helena. Jacob, who had worked at the Charles Krug winery, took charge of winemaking, and tunnels were dug into the hillside of volcanic rock for ageing, keeping a cool, stable temperature. In the 1880s Frederick had the Rhine House built, a large house in a style inspired by those of his native region that has become the emblem of the estate.

During Prohibition the winery carried on by making sacramental wine, which allowed it to maintain a continuity of operation that few Napa wineries can claim. The Beringer family kept ownership until 1971, and since then the winery has passed through several owners; today it is part of the Treasury Wine Estates group.

Beringer makes a very wide range, from widely distributed wines with the California appellation to selections from Napa vineyards. Its most ambitious wine is the Private Reserve Cabernet Sauvignon, made from grapes from several vineyards in the valley, including mountain parcels. The house has also been known for its Private Reserve Chardonnay and for wines from Knights Valley, in Sonoma.

Located on St. Helena’s main street, Beringer was one of the first Napa wineries to open its doors to the public after Prohibition, and its tunnels and the Rhine House remain one of the valley’s classic visits.

Visits & wine tourism

The historic St. Helena estate welcomes visitors, with tastings and tours of the Rhine House and cellars.
