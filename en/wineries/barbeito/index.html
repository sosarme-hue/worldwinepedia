Vinhos Barbeito

Small Madeira house founded in 1946 by Mário Barbeito de Vasconcelos, known for a precise, fresh style of Madeira made without added caramel.

Vinhos Barbeito was founded in 1946 by Mário Barbeito de Vasconcelos, at a difficult time for Madeira wine, whose traditional markets had shrunk considerably. Barbeito was also a scholar of the island’s history and assembled an important documentary collection on the discoveries and navigation. After his death the company passed to his family.

Since the early 1990s the technical direction has been in the hands of his grandson, Ricardo Diogo Freitas, who has given the house a very clear identity. His aim is a Madeira of great precision, in which acidity and freshness balance the aromatic richness of ageing. To that end, Barbeito gave up the use of caramel to adjust colour, a common practice in other wines of the island, and keeps intervention in the cellar to a minimum.

A large part of its range is aged by the canteiro method, that is, in casks placed in warm rooms of the lodge, without artificial heating. The house is known for its single-variety wines, its Colheitas and its single-cask or small-blend lots, often named after members of the family.

Barbeito works with grapes bought from island growers, with whom it maintains long-term relationships, and makes wines from the traditional varieties, such as Sercial, Verdelho, Boal and Malmsey, as well as Tinta Negra.
