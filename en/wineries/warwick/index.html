Warwick Wine Estate

Estate on the slopes of the Simonsberg, in Stellenbosch, linked for decades to the Ratcliffe family and known for its Bordeaux blend Trilogy.

Warwick lies on the slopes of the Simonsberg, north-east of the town of Stellenbosch, one of South Africa's most highly regarded areas for reds from Bordeaux varieties. The estate was bought in the 1960s by Stan Ratcliffe, who initially sold the grapes to other wineries.

His wife, Norma Ratcliffe, of Canadian origin, began making wine on the estate in the 1970s and became one of the first women winemakers in the country, in a sector then dominated by men. Self-taught at first, she later completed her training and helped to reveal the potential of Cabernet Franc in South Africa, a variety then uncommon in the country. Later, her son Mike Ratcliffe drove the growth of the brand on international markets.

The house's emblematic wine is Trilogy, a barrel-aged blend of Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Franc and Merlot whose proportions vary with the vintage. The range also includes The First Lady, an approachable Cabernet Sauvignon that pays tribute to Norma Ratcliffe; Three Cape Ladies, a blend that includes Pinotage; varietal Cabernet Franc; and whites such as Sauvignon Blanc and Chardonnay.

Warwick's style aims for reds with ripe fruit and firm structure, with the freshness brought by the slopes of the Simonsberg, capable of developing well in bottle.

Visits & wine tourism

It has a tasting room on the estate, on the slopes of the Simonsberg.
