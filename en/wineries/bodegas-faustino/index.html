Bodegas Faustino

Family winery in Oyón, in Rioja Alavesa, whose origins date back to 1861 and whose Faustino I Gran Reserva, with its label inspired by a Rembrandt portrait, is one of the most recognisable classic Riojas.

The origins of Bodegas Faustino date back to 1861, when the Martínez family began making wine in Oyón, a village in Rioja Alavesa at the foot of the Sierra de Cantabria, opposite Logroño. For generations the family sold wine in bulk, and in the twentieth century it began bottling under its own brand, named after Faustino Martínez, one of the members of the dynasty. The company is still family-owned and has grown into a group with wineries in other Spanish appellations.

The house is known above all for its long-aged wines. Its most famous label, Faustino I Gran Reserva, is instantly recognisable by its frosted bottle and a label that reproduces a portrait by Rembrandt. Alongside it, the winery makes Faustino V Reserva and Faustino VII, a younger and very widely sold red, as well as whites and rosés.

Its own vineyards are spread across Rioja Alavesa and Rioja Alta, with Tempranillo predominating, complemented by Graciano and Mazuelo (Carignan) in the wines for ageing. The winery has extensive underground cellars and barrel halls, and is known for keeping a large stock of bottles from older vintages.

The Faustino style is classic Rioja: long oak ageing, extended periods in bottle before release, and wines with ripe fruit, spicy notes and a smooth texture, designed to accompany food.
