Meiomi

Californian brand created by Joe Wagner, known for a Pinot Noir blended from several coastal areas that has become one of the best-selling in the United States.

Meiomi was born in the mid-2000s, the creation of Joe Wagner, a member of the family that owns Caymus Vineyards in the Napa Valley. The idea was to make an approachable Pinot Noir of consistent quality from grapes grown in different parts of the California coast, rather than relying on a single vineyard or appellation. According to the brand itself, the name means “coast” in the language of the Wappo, an Indigenous people of northern California.

The wine is made from grapes sourced mainly from Monterey, Santa Barbara and Sonoma counties, which contribute complementary profiles: ripe fruit from some areas, freshness and acidity from others. For this reason it is labelled with the general designation of California rather than the name of a specific region. The success of the Pinot Noir, with its fruity, smooth style, led the range to be extended with a Chardonnay and other wines.

In 2015 the brand was sold to the Constellation Brands group, which has continued to develop it on a large scale. Meiomi has become one of the most widely available Pinot Noir labels in supermarkets and shops in the United States, and it is an example of the branded-wine model built on several areas of origin. There is no winery open to the public under its name.
