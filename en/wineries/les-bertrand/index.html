Les Bertrand

Family estate in Beaujolais making Gamay reds from crus such as Fleurie and Morgon and from the wider Beaujolais area, in a fresh, low-intervention style.

Les Bertrand is a family estate in Beaujolais, the region south of Burgundy, between Mâcon and Lyon, where Gamay is almost the only red variety. It should not be confused with Gérard Bertrand, an unrelated producer in the Languedoc.

Its range brings together wines from several of the ten Beaujolais crus, notably Fleurie and Morgon, along with reds from the Beaujolais and Beaujolais-Villages appellations, including a wine dedicated to Lantignié, one of the communes of the Beaujolais-Villages area, and a Beaujolais Nouveau. The labels, with striking names such as Coup de Foudre, Cuvée du Chaos and Dynamite, reflect a light-hearted approach to wine.

The estate belongs to the movement of Beaujolais producers who favour careful vineyard work and low-intervention winemaking, a tradition with deep roots in the region. As is common in the area, whole-bunch fermentation plays an important role and contributes to Gamay’s fruity expression.

The house style seeks fresh reds with lively fruit and soft tannins, showing the differences between the granite soils of the northern crus and the more southerly areas. They are wines mainly meant to be enjoyed young, although the crus can gain complexity with a few years in bottle.
