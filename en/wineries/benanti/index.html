Winery

Benanti

Family winery in Viagrande, on the eastern slope of Etna, one of those that drove the revival of the volcano’s wines from the late 1980s.

The Benanti family had vineyards on Etna from the late nineteenth century, but the modern winery was born in 1988, when Giuseppe Benanti decided to revive the family tradition and devote himself to making quality wines from the volcano’s native varieties. To that end he set up a programme to study the various vineyards and local grapes.

At that time Etna was little known outside Sicily, and Benanti was one of the wineries that showed the potential of its volcanic soils, high-altitude vineyards and old vines, often bush-trained on terraces of lava stone.

The winery works with vineyards on different slopes of the volcano. For its reds it mainly uses Nerello Mascalese, accompanied by Nerello Cappuccio, and for its whites Carricante, Etna’s great white grape, especially on the eastern side around Milo. Its white Pietramarina, from Carricante grown in Milo, is one of the house’s best-known wines.

The company is now run by Giuseppe’s sons, Antonio and Salvino Benanti. The range includes Etna Rosso, Etna Bianco and Etna Rosato, as well as contrada wines and sparkling wines.
