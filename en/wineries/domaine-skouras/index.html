Domaine Skouras

Peloponnese winery founded by George Skouras near Argos, known for its Agiorgitiko reds from Nemea and for Megas Oenos, one of the pioneering blends of modern Greek wine.

Domaine Skouras was founded by George Skouras, a winemaker trained in Dijon, who returned to Greece intending to make quality wines from the varieties of the Peloponnese. The winery is on the plain of Argos, in the Argolis region, a short distance from the Nemea area, Greece’s most important red wine designation.

Skouras belonged to the generation of producers who, in the last decades of the twentieth century, renewed Greek wine with better-chosen vineyards, modern winemaking and barrel ageing. One of its best-known wines is Megas Oenos, a blend of Agiorgitiko and Cabernet Sauvignon that combined the native variety with an international grape and became one of the landmarks of that renewal.

The house’s main variety is Agiorgitiko, the great red grape of Nemea, which gives wines of deep colour, ripe red fruit and soft tannins. Skouras uses it for the Nemea Saint George and for other reds from different altitudes, since in Nemea the height of the vineyards strongly influences the style. The winery also works with Moschofilero, Viognier and other varieties for its whites and rosés.

The Skouras style is marked by the search for balance between the ripeness the Peloponnese climate allows and the freshness that comes from higher sites.
