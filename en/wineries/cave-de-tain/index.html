Winery

Cave de Tain

Cooperative winery in Tain-l'Hermitage, in the northern Rhône, producing Hermitage, Crozes-Hermitage, Saint-Joseph, Cornas and Saint-Péray.

Cave de Tain is the cooperative winery of Tain-l'Hermitage, the town at the foot of the hill of Hermitage on the left bank of the Rhône. Founded in 1933 by a group of local growers, it now brings together many members who farm vines in the main appellations of the northern Rhône.

It is one of the most important players in the area: its members work plots in Hermitage, Crozes-Hermitage, Saint-Joseph, Cornas and Saint-Péray, and the cooperative has a significant presence on the hill of Hermitage itself. As everywhere in the northern Rhône, Syrah is the only red grape, while the whites are made from Marsanne and Roussanne.

The range is organised into several lines. The most representative is Nobles Rives, which covers the area's different appellations in red and white and makes it possible to compare the character of each: the direct fruit of Crozes-Hermitage, the structure of Hermitage or the intensity of Cornas. The winery has also encouraged more environmentally friendly viticulture among its members. Alongside the prestigious appellations, the cooperative also makes simpler wines, such as IGP Collines Rhodaniennes, intended for everyday drinking, and it has developed visitor facilities to introduce the public to the viticulture of the hill.

Visits & wine tourism

The winery has a shop and visitor area in Tain-l'Hermitage, with tasting options.
