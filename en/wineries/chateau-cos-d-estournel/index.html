Château Cos d’Estournel

Saint-Estèphe second growth, recognisable by the oriental-style pagodas of its winery and located opposite Lafite Rothschild.

Château Cos d’Estournel lies at the southern end of the Saint-Estèphe appellation, on a gravel hill separated by a small stream from Château Lafite Rothschild, which is already in Pauillac. The estate owes its fame to Louis-Gaspard d’Estournel, who in the early decades of the 19th century enlarged the vineyard and sold his wines in distant markets, including India. Fascinated by the East, he had a winery built adorned with pagodas, carved doors and other exotic motifs, which remains one of the most characteristic sights in the Médoc.

In the 1855 classification it was recognised as a Deuxième Grand Cru Classé, and during the 20th century it established itself as the most prestigious property in Saint-Estèphe. After belonging for decades to the Prats family, it was bought in 2000 by the businessman Michel Reybier, who led the construction of a new winery, opened at the end of that decade, in which the wine is moved by gravity.

The vineyard is planted mainly with Cabernet Sauvignon, with Merlot and small areas of Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. The estate also has some plots of white varieties, from which it makes a white wine, something unusual in the area.

As well as the grand vin, it produces the second wine Les Pagodes de Cos. The Cos d’Estournel style is associated with rich, deep, spicy reds, often considered closer to those of Pauillac than to those of its own appellation, and with great ageing potential.
