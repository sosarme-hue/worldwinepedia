Winery

Mullineux

Swartland winery founded in 2007 by Chris and Andrea Mullineux, renowned for its Syrah and its old vine Chenin Blanc whites.

Mullineux was founded in 2007 by the married couple Chris Mullineux, a South African, and Andrea Mullineux, a United States-born winemaker, who settled in Riebeek-Kasteel, in the heart of the Swartland. The region, north of Cape Town, was then known mainly for cereal farming and bulk wine production, but a group of young producers was beginning to discover the value of its dry-farmed old vines.

The winery focuses on two main lines. On the one hand there is Syrah, made in a fresh, spicy style with moderate use of new oak and often with partial whole-bunch fermentation. On the other there are whites based on Chenin Blanc from old bush vines, sometimes blended with other Mediterranean white varieties. To this range are added wines that highlight the soil type of each plot, such as granite, schist or iron-rich soils, and a sweet wine made from grapes dried in the sun.

In 2013 the Mullineux couple went into partnership with the businessman Analjit Singh, owner of the Leeu group, and the company became Mullineux & Leeu Family Wines. That collaboration also gave rise to the Leeu Passant project in Franschhoek, focused on historic vineyards in several areas of the Cape, and the Kloof Street range, made in a more approachable style.

Mullineux is regarded as one of the key names in the renewal of South African wine, with wines that seek to express their place of origin through a low-intervention approach to winemaking.
