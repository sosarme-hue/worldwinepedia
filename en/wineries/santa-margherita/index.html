Santa Margherita

Wine company founded in 1935 by Count Gaetano Marzotto in Fossalta di Portogruaro, famous worldwide for its Pinot Grigio.

Santa Margherita was born in 1935 in Fossalta di Portogruaro, in the eastern part of Veneto, near the border with Friuli. Its founder, Count Gaetano Marzotto, a textile industrialist, conceived the estate as a large-scale agricultural project; the company’s name pays homage to his wife, Margherita Lampertico. The Marzotto family still owns the group.

The house owes its international fame to Pinot Grigio. In the early 1960s it began making this variety as a white wine, quickly separating the juice from the skins, which in Pinot Grigio have a pinkish tone. The result, a light, fresh white, was hugely successful, above all in the United States, and helped make Pinot Grigio one of the world’s best-selling Italian wines. The grapes come mainly from vineyards in the Adige valley, in Alto Adige and the Valdadige area.

Over time Santa Margherita became a wine group with estates and brands in several Italian regions, including Alto Adige, Franciacorta, Tuscany, Sicily and Sardinia, and with a stake in the Franciacorta house Ca’ del Bosco. The Santa Margherita brand also covers Valdobbiadene Prosecco Superiore, Chianti Classico and other wines.

The house style is geared to clean, consistent, easy-drinking wines designed for a broad public.
