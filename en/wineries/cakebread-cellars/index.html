Cakebread Cellars

Family winery in Rutherford, in the Napa Valley, founded in 1973 by Jack and Dolores Cakebread and known for its Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Cabernet Sauvignon.

Cakebread Cellars was founded in 1973 by Jack and Dolores Cakebread, who bought a small property in Rutherford, right in the middle of the Napa Valley, beside Highway 29. Jack Cakebread was then running a family car repair shop in Oakland and was also a keen photographer; buying the property came about almost by chance during a trip to the valley. During the early years the family combined the winery with its other activities, travelling back and forth between Oakland and Napa.

Over time the winery grew and extended its vineyard holdings in different parts of the Napa Valley, from the valley floor to mountain slopes, as well as in the cool Los Carneros area and in the Anderson Valley, in Mendocino County. Its range rests on three main wines: a Napa Valley Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Cabernet Sauvignon, alongside single-vineyard reds and other varieties.

The founders’ sons, in particular Bruce Cakebread, later took over management and winemaking. From the start the house has placed great importance on the link between wine and food, with its own kitchen garden and culinary programmes at the winery. The style aims for wines with clear fruit and good acidity and a moderate use of oak, intended to accompany food, and the Chardonnay is today one of its most widely distributed wines.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers tastings and tours at its Rutherford winery, some paired with food, by prior reservation.
