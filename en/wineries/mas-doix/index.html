Winery

Mas Doix

Family winery in Poboleda working very old Garnacha and Cariñena vines in the northern Priorat, known for Les Crestes, Salanques and Doix.

Mas Doix is a family winery in Poboleda, in the northern Priorat, created by descendants of the village's winegrowing families who decided to bottle their own wines from inherited vineyards. Its name refers to the family farmhouse, and its roots lie in the area's winegrowing tradition before the revival of the 1990s: many of its vines were planted by earlier generations.

Vineyards

Poboleda lies at the foot of the Montsant range, in an area somewhat cooler than the centre of the Priorat. The family's vineyards grow on slopes and terraces of llicorella, and a large share consists of very old bush-trained Cariñena and Garnacha vines, some of them a century old. These vines give very low yields and highly concentrated grapes. Most of the work is done by hand because of the steepness of the land.

Wines

The range is organised by vine age and by plot. Les Crestes is the youngest and most approachable red, based on Garnacha. Salanques combines old-vine Garnacha and Cariñena, and Doix comes from the family's oldest vines, with Cariñena in the leading role. The winery also makes a single-plot wine in very small quantities. The house style combines the concentration typical of old vines with the freshness of this part of the area and careful barrel ageing.
