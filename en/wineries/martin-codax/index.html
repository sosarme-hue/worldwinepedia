Bodegas Martín Códax

Winery founded in Cambados in 1986 by a large group of Salnés growers, whose Albariño is one of the most widely available from Rías Baixas.

Martín Códax was founded in 1986 in Cambados (Pontevedra), the traditional capital of Albariño, when a large group of growers from the Val do Salnés decided to join forces to make and sell their wine. The winery takes its name from Martín Codax, a medieval Galician troubadour whose cantigas de amigo, evoking the sea of Vigo, are among the oldest surviving compositions of Galician-Portuguese lyric poetry.

The winery stands on Mount Burgáns, in Cambados, overlooking the Arousa estuary. It works with grapes from hundreds of small growers, who farm tiny plots, often on pergolas, as is traditional in the area. The cool, rainy Atlantic climate and granitic soils give Salnés Albariño its characteristic acidity and aromatic profile.

Its best-known wine is Martín Códax Albariño, a young white with wide international distribution. The range also includes Burgáns, another young, approachable Albariño, lees-aged wines, a barrel-fermented Albariño and special cuvées.

The group has expanded with projects in other Galician appellations and beyond Galicia. Martín Códax’s style focuses on fresh, aromatic Albariños meant to be enjoyed young, which have helped make the variety known in many markets.

Visits & wine tourism

It offers guided tours of the Cambados winery with a tasting, by prior booking.
