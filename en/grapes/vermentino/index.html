White grape

Vermentino

A Mediterranean white with citrus and herbal aromas and a saline finish, emblematic of Sardinia, Liguria and Corsica, and known as Rolle in Provence and the Languedoc.

Origin

Vermentino is an old variety of the western Mediterranean coast, grown for centuries in Liguria, Sardinia, Corsica and coastal Tuscany. Its exact origin is unknown. DNA analysis has shown that Pigato from Liguria, Favorita from Piedmont and Rolle from southern France are the same variety, although each region keeps its own biotypes and names.

Viticulture

It is a vigorous vine of medium to late ripening that tolerates heat, drought and sea winds well and adapts to poor soils such as the granites of Gallura in Sardinia or the schists of Corsica. It keeps reasonable acidity even in warm climates, especially in high-altitude vineyards or those exposed to sea breezes. It is susceptible to some fungal diseases, such as downy mildew, in wet years.

In the glass

It makes straw-yellow whites with green glints, medium-bodied, with fresh acidity and a characteristic slightly bitter, saline finish. Its aromas recall lemon, grapefruit, green apple, white peach, white flowers and Mediterranean herbs such as fennel or rosemary. It is usually fermented in tank and drunk young, although the best wines from Gallura and Corsica can take a few years in bottle. In Provence it adds freshness and aroma to many rosés.

Where it is grown

In Sardinia it is the most important white grape, with Vermentino di Gallura DOCG in the north-east of the island and Vermentino di Sardegna DOC. In Liguria the wines of Colli di Luni and the Riviera Ligure di Ponente, where Pigato is also made, stand out. In Tuscany it is grown along the coast, in Bolgheri and the Maremma. In France, as Vermentinu, it is the great white grape of Corsica, and as Rolle it is found throughout Provence and Languedoc-Roussillon. Beyond Europe there are plantings in Australia and California.

At the table

It is a natural partner for Mediterranean cooking: grilled fish, shellfish, pasta with pesto, grilled vegetables and fresh cheeses. Serve at 8–11 °C.
