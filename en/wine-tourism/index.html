Travelling through wine

Wine tourism: wine routes and cellars

A guide to visiting wine country: the cellars of Catalonia, the great wine routes of Spain and the world, what to see in each area, when to go and which wineries in the encyclopedia open their doors.

Home › Wine tourism

Wine tourism brings together wine and the landscape, architecture, history and cooking of each place. Visiting a winery lets you see the vineyard up close, understand how wine is made and taste it where it is born. In this section you will find travel guides to the main wine areas, starting with the twelve appellations of Catalonia, with the villages worth knowing, the places of interest, the best time to go and the local food.

Each guide links to the region and to the wineries already in the encyclopedia. Visiting conditions change often: before you go, always check on each winery's website whether you need to book, and the opening times and prices.
