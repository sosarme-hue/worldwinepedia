D.O. Somontano · at the foot of the Pyrenees

Somontano

Barbastro, Alquézar and the Guara canyons: a route that combines modern wineries, art and outdoor sports in Upper Aragon.

Somontano means “at the foot of the mountain”: the appellation spreads over the rolling Huesca countryside that opens south of the Sierra de Guara and the Pyrenees. Alongside local varieties such as Moristel and Parraleta, many international varieties were planted from the 1980s, giving the area a modern profile.

Barbastro

Barbastro, the main town, has a Renaissance cathedral and is home to the Espacio del Vino, the appellation's interpretation centre. From here most wineries are a few minutes' drive away, several with contemporary architecture and art collections.

Alquézar and the Sierra de Guara

Alquézar, hanging over the Vero river canyon with its collegiate church and castle, is one of the most beautiful villages in Aragon. The Vero walkways and the Guara gorges attract hikers and canyoning fans, and the Vero valley has rock shelters with prehistoric paintings, part of the World Heritage listing of the Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin.

Towns and villages

Barbastro, Alquézar, Salas Altas, Salas Bajas, Huerta de Vero, Colungo, Naval.

What to see

  • Espacio del Vino in Barbastro.
  • Alquézar and the Vero walkways.
  • Vero rock paintings.
  • Naval salt pans.

When to go

Spring to autumn; in August Barbastro hosts the Vino Somontano Festival.

Food

Aragon lamb, longaniza sausage, migas and Barbastro pink tomatoes in summer.

Tip

Keep a day for Guara: wine tastes better after a good walk.
