D.O. Montsant · around Priorat

Montsant

An appellation that rings Priorat, with Falset as its capital, modernista cooperatives and characterful Grenache and Carignan.

The Montsant D.O. was created in 2001 from the former Falset sub-zone of the Tarragona D.O. and almost completely surrounds Priorat, at the foot of the range that gives it its name. Its soils are more varied than its neighbour's (granite, clay, limestone and some slate) and its wines, mostly reds from Grenache and Carignan, tend to be somewhat more approachable.

Falset

Falset, capital of the Priorat district, is the most practical base for exploring both appellations. Its cooperative, by the architect Cèsar Martinell, is one of the modernista wine cathedrals (see the dedicated guide). The castle houses a local museum, and the arcaded square keeps the feel of a market town.

Capçanes, Cornudella and beyond

In Capçanes, the local cooperative became known, among other things, for making kosher wines. Cornudella de Montsant also has a Martinell cooperative and is the gateway to Siurana and its reservoir. Marçà, Els Guiamets, La Figuera and the villages along the Ebro complete a route of very varied landscapes, from mountain to river.

Towns and villages

Falset, Capçanes, Cornudella de Montsant, Marçà, Els Guiamets, La Figuera, Masroig.

What to see

  • Falset's modernista cooperative.
  • Falset castle.
  • Siurana and its reservoir.
  • Footpaths in the Serra de Montsant.

When to go

Spring, autumn and the September–October harvest.

Food

Olive oil, hazelnuts, calçots in winter and mountain cooking.

Tip

Combine Montsant and Priorat in the same trip: from Falset most villages are less than half an hour away.
