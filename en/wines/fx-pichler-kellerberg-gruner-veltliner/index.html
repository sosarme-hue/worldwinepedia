Grüner Veltliner Smaragd Kellerberg

WhiteSmaragd (Vinea Wachau)DAC (Districtus Austriae Controllatus) Wachau

Grüner Veltliner Smaragd from the Dürnsteiner Kellerberg, a gneiss slope above the Danube, highly concentrated and age-worthy.

The Kellerberg is a south-facing terraced slope rising above the Danube west of Dürnstein. Its poor, stony gneiss soils and the influence of cold air currents descending at night from the Waldviertel, combined with daytime warmth, allow long ripening that produces wines that are both concentrated and fresh.

F.X. Pichler makes a Smaragd-category wine from this vineyard’s Grüner Veltliner, the category Vinea Wachau reserves for its ripest dry wines. The grapes are picked by hand and the wine ferments in tanks and casks, without seeking new-oak influence. It is a full-bodied Grüner Veltliner, with the variety’s characteristic peppery note, good acidity and remarkable ageing potential.

It is best not served too cold and left to breathe in the glass, as its aromas open up with air; with a few years in bottle it usually gains complexity.

Tasting profile

Aromas of ripe yellow fruit, citrus, white pepper and herbs, over a mineral background. Dry, broad and concentrated on the palate, with firm acidity and a long, spicy finish.

Food pairing

Wiener Schnitzel, vegetables, asparagus, freshwater fish, Asian food

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
