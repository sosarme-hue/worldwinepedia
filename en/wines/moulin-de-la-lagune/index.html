Château La Lagune

Moulin de La Lagune

RedHaut-Médoc, second wineBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The second wine of Château La Lagune, a fruity, approachable Haut-Médoc that reflects the fine style of the Ludon estate.

Moulin de La Lagune is the second wine of Château La Lagune, a third growth in the 1855 classification, located in Ludon-Médoc at the southern tip of the Médoc. As at other great Bordeaux estates, the second wine allows only the best lots to be kept for the grand vin while offering a wine with the house’s identity.

It comes mainly from younger vines and fruitier lots from the estate’s vineyard, on the gravel and sandy soils of the Haut-Médoc appellation. It is made from Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, often completed with some Petit Verdot; Merlot usually carries more weight than in the grand vin.

Barrel ageing is shorter and uses less new oak. The result is a medium-bodied red with red and black fruit, spicy notes and soft tannins, which can be enjoyed after just a few years and offers a good introduction to the elegant style of La Lagune.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of cherry, blackcurrant, spice and a hint of vanilla. Round, fruity palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, roast poultry, cured meats, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, Bordeaux glass
