Biondi-Santi

Biondi-Santi Brunello di Montalcino

RedBrunello di Montalcino DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Brunello di Montalcino

The Brunello from Tenuta Greppo, the house traditionally credited with the birth of the appellation, a classically styled, long-lived Sangiovese.

Biondi-Santi’s Brunello di Montalcino, often called “Annata” to distinguish it from the Riserva, comes from the vineyards of Tenuta Greppo, on the hills just outside the town of Montalcino, at a relatively high altitude for the area.

It is made only from Sangiovese, from the selection of vines that the family has preserved and propagated for generations. Ageing takes place in large Slavonian oak casks, following the house’s traditional method, and the wine then rests in bottle before release.

It is a classically styled Brunello that favours acidity, finesse and tension over concentration. When young it can seem austere, and it is designed to evolve slowly in the cellar over many years.

Tasting profile

Bright, medium-depth garnet. Aromas of sour cherry, red berries, dried herbs and earthy, spicy notes. Taut and elegant on the palate, with pronounced acidity and fine, firm tannins.

Food pairing

Roast red meat, game, dishes with truffle or mushrooms, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; benefits from decanting
