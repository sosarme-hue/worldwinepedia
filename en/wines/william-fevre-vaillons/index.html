Domaine William Fèvre

Domaine William Fèvre Chablis 1er Cru Vaillons

WhitePremier CruAOC (Petit Chablis, Chablis, Chablis Premier Cru and Chablis Grand Cru) Chablis

Chablis premier cru from Domaine William Fèvre, from the Vaillons vineyard on the left bank of the Serein, fine and mineral in profile.

Vaillons is one of the best-known premiers crus of Chablis. It lies on the left bank of the Serein river, south-west of the town, on slopes facing mainly south-east, on the Kimmeridgian marl and limestone that characterise the area’s best vineyards. Domaine William Fèvre owns its own plots there.

The wine is made from hand-picked Chardonnay from these vines. Following the house method for its premiers crus, part of the must is fermented and aged in used barrels, with no new oak, and part in stainless steel tanks; the two are blended before bottling. The aim is to add texture without letting the oak dominate the expression of the terroir.

It is a Chablis of great finesse, with white and citrus fruit, floral notes and marked minerality, somewhat broader than a village Chablis. It can be enjoyed a few years after the harvest and develops well for several more years.

Tasting profile

Bright pale yellow. Aromas of lemon, pear, white flowers and notes of oyster shell and chalk. Fresh, fine palate with good tension, a delicate texture and a saline finish.

Food pairing

Oysters, shellfish, grilled fish, sushi, goat’s cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
