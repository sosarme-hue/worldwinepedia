Gusbourne Brut Reserve

SparklingBrutWine region (English Wine PDO) Kent

English sparkling wine from Gusbourne made by the traditional method from Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier from its vineyards in Kent and West Sussex.

Brut Reserve is the benchmark blended sparkling wine of Gusbourne, a producer based at Appledore, in the south of Kent. Like the house’s other wines, it is made solely from the grapes of its own vineyards, in Kent and West Sussex.

It is made from the three classic traditional-method varieties, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier, in proportions that vary with the vintage. The clay and sand soils of Kent and the chalk of West Sussex give different nuances to the base wines, which are vinified separately before blending. After the second fermentation in bottle, the wine stays on its lees for an extended period before disgorgement, which gives it aromatic complexity, pastry notes and fine bubbles.

The cool climate of southern England allows slow ripening that preserves high acidity and precise aromas, characteristic features of English sparkling wine. It can be drunk on release or kept for a few years.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with fine bubbles. Aromas of green apple, lemon, white flowers, brioche and toasted almond. Fresh, taut palate with lively acidity, creamy texture and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, oysters, fried fish, shellfish, soft cheeses

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine or tulip glass
