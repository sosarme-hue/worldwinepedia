Château Langoa Barton

RedThird Growth (1855 classification)AOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Saint-Julien

Saint-Julien third growth, owned by the Barton family since 1821 and made at the same château as Léoville Barton.

Château Langoa Barton is a third growth of Saint-Julien in the 1855 classification. The Barton family, of Irish origin and established in the Bordeaux wine trade since the 18th century, bought it in 1821, a few years before acquiring part of neighbouring Léoville. Langoa’s elegant 18th-century building is the family residence and houses the winery where both wines are made, although each vineyard is vinified separately.

The vines grow on the gravel typical of Saint-Julien. The wine is made mostly from Cabernet Sauvignon, completed by Merlot and a small proportion of Cabernet Franc, and is aged in oak barrels.

It shares the house’s classic style, untouched by fashion: dark fruit, notes of cedar and tobacco, good structure and firm tannins. It is usually somewhat more approachable than Léoville Barton, but also has notable ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Deep red. Aromas of blackcurrant, cedar, tobacco and spice. Classic, structured palate with firm tannins.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled red meat, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
