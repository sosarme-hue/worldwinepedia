Harveys

Harveys Bristol Cream

FortifiedCreamDesignation of Origin (DO) Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

Sweet cream sherry from Harveys, one of the world's best-selling sherry brands, a blend of dry aged wines and Pedro Ximénez.

Harveys Bristol Cream is probably the best-known sweet sherry in the world. It was born in the nineteenth century in Bristol, the English port where the Harvey family traded in wine, as a richer version of the traditional Bristol Milk; according to the house's history, its name arose from the remark of a lady visitor who considered it “the cream” compared with “the milk”. Its characteristic blue bottle is instantly recognisable.

It is a blended wine, as befits the cream category of the Jerez D.O.: it combines dry Palomino wines aged in Jerez —finos, amontillados and olorosos— with sweet Pedro Ximénez wine, in proportions the house does not disclose. The blend is adjusted to maintain a consistent style, and its component wines are aged by the criaderas and solera system. The result is a sweet yet balanced sherry, mahogany in colour, with aromas of nuts and raisins. It can be served chilled or over ice, as an aperitif or with dessert.

Tasting profile

Bright mahogany. Aromas of raisins, dried figs, walnut, caramel and wood notes. Sweet, silky and round on the palate, with a nutty finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif over ice, foie gras, blue cheeses, tarts, nuts

Serving

10-12 °C or over ice, white wine glass
