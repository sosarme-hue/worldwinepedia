Tommasi Ripasso Valpolicella Classico Superiore

RedValpolicella Classico Superiore Ripasso DOCDOC and DOCG (Amarone and Recioto) Valpolicella

Tommasi Ripasso, a Valpolicella Classico Superiore refermented on Amarone pomace, halfway between Valpolicella and Amarone.

Tommasi’s Ripasso comes from the family’s vineyards in Valpolicella Classica and is made from Corvina, Corvinone and Rondinella. The base wine is a Valpolicella made from fresh grapes after the autumn harvest.

Later, when the dried grapes for Amarone are pressed, the Valpolicella is put in contact with the resulting pomace, still rich in sugars, aromas and colour. A second fermentation then begins, giving the wine more body, alcohol and complexity. It is then aged in large oak casks, in the house’s traditional manner.

It is a medium- to full-bodied red with aromas of ripe fruit and spice and good freshness. Its balance between structure and drinkability has made it one of Tommasi’s best-selling wines, well suited to everyday cooking.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of cherry, morello cherry, plum, spice and a touch of raisin. Smooth, savoury palate with gentle tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Pasta with ragù, roast meat, stews, roast chicken, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
