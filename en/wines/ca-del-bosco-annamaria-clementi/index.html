Ca' del Bosco

Ca’ del Bosco Annamaria Clementi

SparklingFranciacorta Riserva DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Franciacorta

Ca’ del Bosco Franciacorta Riserva dedicated to the founder’s mother, the house’s top sparkling wine, with very long ageing on the lees.

Annamaria Clementi bears the name of the mother of Maurizio Zanella, founder of Ca’ del Bosco, and is the most ambitious wine of the Erbusco house. It is made only in vintages the winery considers suitable and is released with the Riserva designation, which in Franciacorta requires extended ageing on the lees.

It is made from Chardonnay, Pinot Bianco and Pinot Noir from a selection of the estate’s vineyards; the proportions vary with the vintage. The base wines ferment in small oak barrels, adding complexity and texture. After blending, the wine undergoes its second fermentation in bottle by the traditional method and remains in contact with the lees for many years before disgorgement.

It is a sparkling wine of great complexity, with notes of ripe fruit, pastry and nuts, very fine bubbles and a structure that allows it to accompany food and to evolve in bottle.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow, very fine and persistent bubbles. Aromas of ripe fruit, brioche, hazelnut, honey and candied citrus. Broad, creamy, fresh palate with a long finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, baked fish, risotto, white meat, aged cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, large white wine glass
