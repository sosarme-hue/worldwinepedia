Domaine Vacheron

Vacheron Sancerre Blanc

WhiteSancerreAOC Sancerre

White Sancerre from Domaine Vacheron, biodynamically farmed Sauvignon Blanc from limestone and flint soils, fresh and mineral.

The white Sancerre is Domaine Vacheron’s most widely distributed wine and the best introduction to its style. It comes from vines spread over different sectors of the appellation, around the town of Sancerre, with limestone soils — the caillottes and the terres blanches — and flint.

It is made exclusively from Sauvignon Blanc grown in biodynamically farmed vineyards. The grapes are hand-picked, and fermentation, with indigenous yeasts, takes place mainly in tank in order to preserve freshness and aromatic purity. The wine rests for a time on its lees before bottling.

Unlike some very aromatic Sauvignon Blancs from other regions, this Sancerre emphasises tension and mineral notes, with restrained citrus fruit. It is a white to drink in its first few years, although good vintages hold well for somewhat longer.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of lemon, grapefruit, white flowers and chalky, flinty notes. Fresh, taut palate with lively acidity and a saline finish.

Food pairing

Grilled fish, shellfish, salads, Crottin de Chavignol

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
