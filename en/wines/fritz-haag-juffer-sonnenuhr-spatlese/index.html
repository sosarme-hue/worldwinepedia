Brauneberger Juffer-Sonnenuhr Riesling Spätlese

WhiteSpätleseAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Mosel

Riesling Spätlese from Fritz Haag from the Brauneberger Juffer-Sonnenuhr vineyard, with delicate sweetness, lively acidity and great ageing potential.

The Juffer-Sonnenuhr is the central and most highly regarded part of the Juffer vineyard in Brauneberg, a very steep south-facing slope of Devonian slate opposite the village, across the Mosel. Its name comes from the sundial that stands among the vines. For centuries its wines have been among the most prized of the Mosel.

Spätlese, or “late harvest”, is the Prädikat category for grapes picked riper than those for Kabinett. Fritz Haag makes it from Riesling picked by hand, often in several passes, and fermented at low temperature until it stops with residual sweetness balanced by the Mosel’s natural acidity. Alcohol is moderate.

It is a wine of great finesse, in which the sweetness feels delicate thanks to the acidity. It can be enjoyed young for its fruit, but it stands out above all for its ability to age for decades, when the sweetness integrates and more complex notes appear.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of peach, apricot, citrus, flowers and slate notes. Delicate palate, with sweetness and acidity in tension and a long finish.

Food pairing

Thai cuisine, Peking duck, foie gras, blue cheeses, fruit tarts

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
