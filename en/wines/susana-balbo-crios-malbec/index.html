Críos Malbec

RedWine region Valle de Uco

Malbec from Susana Balbo’s Críos line, a fruity Mendoza red expressing the style of Argentina’s flagship variety.

Críos is the varietal line of Susana Balbo Wines, the winery founded in 1999 in Agrelo (Luján de Cuyo) by winemaker Susana Balbo. Its name, meaning ‘offspring’ or ‘children’ in Argentine Spanish, is the founder’s tribute to her children, who now take part in the family business. The range seeks expressive, approachable wines true to their variety and origin.

Malbec, originally from south-west France, found its great adopted home in Mendoza and has become Argentina’s flagship variety. In the high-altitude vineyards of the Uco Valley, strong sunshine and the wide temperature range between day and night give grapes with deep colour, ripe fruit and good acidity.

Críos Malbec is made to show that fruit directly. Oak ageing, where used, is brief and discreet, so the spotlight falls on the black fruit aromas and floral notes typical of the variety.

It is a juicy, medium-bodied red with soft tannins, intended to be enjoyed in its first few years and very versatile at the table.

Tasting profile

Plum, blackberry, black cherry and violet, with a spicy touch. Juicy palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Asado, grilled red meat, empanadas, pasta with meat sauce, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
