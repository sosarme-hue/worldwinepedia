Barón de Chirel

RedReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Top red from Marqués de Riscal, created in the mid-1980s from old-vine grapes, mainly Tempranillo, and aged in French oak.

Barón de Chirel is one of the most ambitious wines from Marqués de Riscal, the Elciego winery founded in 1858. It was born in the mid-1980s, when the house wanted to make a red in a more modern style than its classic Reserva, with greater concentration and ageing in French oak instead of the traditional American oak.

It is made from low-yielding old vineyards selected by the winery in Rioja Alavesa and other parts of the appellation. The main variety is Tempranillo, and the house, which has a long history with Cabernet Sauvignon in Rioja, has included other varieties in small proportions in some vintages. It is made only in harvests considered suitable.

The wine is aged in French oak barrels and rests in bottle before release, with the Reserva designation. The result is a concentrated red with ripe black fruit and firm structure, capable of long ageing.

Tasting profile

Intense deep cherry colour. Ripe black fruit, spice, cocoa and toasty French oak notes. Concentrated on the palate, with firm, ripe tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled red meat, roast milk-fed lamb, large game, aged sheep’s cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
