Kellerei Kaltern – Cantina Kaltern

Kellerei Kaltern Alto Adige Gewürztraminer

WhiteAlto Adige DOCWine region Trentino-Alto Adige

An Alto Adige Gewürztraminer from the Caldaro cooperative, highly aromatic, with notes of rose and spice.

Gewürztraminer has a special link with Alto Adige: the variety takes its name from the village of Tramin (Termeno), a neighbour of Caldaro, believed to be the home of the old Traminer from which this aromatic, pink-skinned form descends. Today it is one of the region’s white specialities.

This Gewürztraminer from Kellerei Kaltern is part of the classic varietal line of the Caldaro cooperative and comes from its members’ vineyards in the lake area. It is made to preserve the aromatic intensity characteristic of the variety, in a dry or off-dry style with an unctuous texture on the palate.

It is an expressive white, medium- to full-bodied with moderate acidity, enjoyed in its first few years, and it pairs especially well with spicy cuisines and strongly flavoured cheeses.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of rose petals, lychee, tropical fruit and spice. Unctuous, aromatic palate with a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Asian cuisine, curry, foie gras, washed-rind cheeses

Serving

9-11 °C, white wine glass
