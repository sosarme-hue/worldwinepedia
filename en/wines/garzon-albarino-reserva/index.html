Bodega Garzón

Garzón Albariño Reserva

WhiteReservaWine department Maldonado

Albariño from the Atlantic hills of Maldonado, one of the best-known whites from Bodega Garzón and from Uruguay.

Albariño, native to Galicia and northern Portugal, has found conditions in south-eastern Uruguay similar to those of its homeland: a cool, humid and windy climate with strong Atlantic influence. Bodega Garzón has been one of the wineries that has done most to make Uruguayan Albariño known, and its Reserva version has become one of its most widely available wines.

The grapes come from hillside plots in the winery’s vineyards near the village of Garzón, on shallow soils of decomposed granite. Harvesting aims to preserve acidity and aromatic freshness, and winemaking is geared to keeping the expression of the fruit, with a period of lees contact that adds texture. The result is a dry, fresh white of marked saline character, designed to be drunk in its first few years, although it can gain complexity with some time in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Aromas of white peach, citrus and flowers, with a saline touch. Fresh on the palate, with lively acidity and a mineral finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, grilled fish, ceviche, sushi

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
