Château de Beaucastel

Coudoulet de Beaucastel Rouge

RedCôtes du RhôneRegional AOC of the Rhône valley (Côtes du Rhône and Côtes du Rhône Villages) Côtes du Rhône

Red Côtes du Rhône from the Perrin family, from vineyards neighbouring Château de Beaucastel and often described as the château’s younger sibling.

Coudoulet de Beaucastel comes from a vineyard just east of Château de Beaucastel, separated from it by the A7 motorway and lying outside the boundaries of the Châteauneuf-du-Pape appellation, so it is sold as Côtes du Rhône. Its soils nevertheless share features with those of the neighbouring estate, with rounded pebbles over clay and molasse deposits.

The red blends Grenache, Mourvèdre, Syrah and Cinsault, in proportions that vary with the vintage. The Perrin family makes it according to the same principles as Beaucastel, with farming free of synthetic chemicals, vinification by variety and ageing in large oak casks.

It has more structure and depth than most Côtes du Rhône and can age well for several years, though it is approachable earlier than the house’s Châteauneuf-du-Pape.

Tasting profile

Garnet red. Aromas of black fruit, cherry, spice, garrigue and slightly gamey notes. Structured palate with firm tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, duck, Provençal stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, large glass
