Tyrrell's

Tyrrell's Vat 47 Hunter Chardonnay

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Hunter Valley

Hunter Valley Chardonnay released by Tyrrell’s in the early 1970s, regarded as one of the first varietal Chardonnays sold in Australia.

When Tyrrell’s released Vat 47 in the early 1970s, Chardonnay was an almost unknown variety in Australia, where white vineyards were dominated by Semillon and other grapes. Murray Tyrrell, convinced of its potential after getting to know the white wines of Burgundy, planted the variety in the Hunter and made one of the country’s first varietal Chardonnays, which helped trigger the subsequent boom of this grape in Australia.

The wine comes from the family’s vineyards in the Hunter. Unlike the house Semillon, it is fermented and aged in French oak barrels, and the winery seeks a balance between the richness provided by the valley’s warm climate and freshness. It is a medium- to full-bodied white with ripe fruit and well-integrated oak notes, which can develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Peach, nectarine and citrus, with notes of hazelnut, vanilla and toast. Broad, creamy palate with good acidity.

Food pairing

Roast chicken, fish in butter sauce, scallops, risotto

Serving

11-13 °C, large white wine glass
