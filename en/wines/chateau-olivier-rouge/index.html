Château Olivier

Château Olivier Rouge

RedCru Classé de GravesAOC Pessac-Léognan

The red of Château Olivier, a Cru Classé de Graves in Léognan, based on Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot.

The red Château Olivier is the main wine of this Léognan estate in the Pessac-Léognan appellation, classified as a Cru Classé de Graves. It comes from the property’s gravel ridges, surrounded by woodland, which give evenly ripening grapes.

It is made from a blend of Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, completed by a small proportion of Petit Verdot. The plots are vinified separately according to the estate’s soil study, and the wine is aged in oak barrels before blending.

Its style favours freshness and finesse over power: dark fruit, graphite and spice notes, and polished tannins. It is a red that gains complexity with a few years in bottle and reflects well the character of the gravel wines of Pessac-Léognan.

Tasting profile

Deep red. Aromas of blackcurrant, cherry, graphite and spice. Fresh, elegant palate with polished tannins.

Food pairing

Lamb, duck, roast red meat, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
