Domaine de Bila-Haut L'Esquerda

RedCôtes du Roussillon Villages LesquerdeWine region Languedoc-Roussillon

A red from Chapoutier’s Domaine de Bila-Haut, from the area of Lesquerde, one of the named villages of Roussillon.

The Côtes du Roussillon Villages appellation, in the northern half of Roussillon, allows certain villages to add their name to the label: Caramany, Latour-de-France, Lesquerde, Tautavel and Les Aspres. Lesquerde, in the Agly Valley, stands out for its soils of granitic origin, which differ from the schist and limestone that dominate other parts of the valley.

L’Esquerda is the red that Domaine de Bila-Haut, the Chapoutier house’s estate in Latour-de-France, devotes to this area; its name is the Catalan form of the place name. Like the other Villages wines, it is made from a blend of Mediterranean varieties, and the style seeks to show the freshness and tension usually associated with granite soils.

It is a medium- to full-bodied red with black fruit, spicy notes and fine tannins, which gains complexity with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Dark ruby. Aromas of black fruit, violet, pepper and dried herbs. Fresh, taut palate with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Lamb, grilled red meat, small game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
