Familia Torres

Salmos

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Priorat

Familia Torres red from the Priorat D.O.Ca., a blend of Garnacha, Carignan and Syrah from vineyards on llicorella slate.

Salmos is the wine with which Familia Torres is represented in Priorat. Its name (Psalms) evokes the district's monastic past, linked since the Middle Ages to the charterhouse of Scala Dei, whose monks promoted vine growing in the area.

It is made from Garnacha, Carignan and Syrah from the family's vineyards and other plots in the district, grown on the characteristic llicorella. The grapes are vinified separately and the wine is aged in oak barrels before the final blend. It is a ripe, spicy Priorat with the concentration typical of the designation, suited both to drinking in its first years and to medium-term cellaring.

Tasting profile

Ripe black fruit, notes of liquorice, spice and a mineral background. Broad palate with ripe tannins and a warm finish.

Food pairing

Red meats, game, hearty mountain stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
