Pur Sang

WhitePouilly-FuméWine region Loire

Pouilly-Fumé from Domaine Didier Dagueneau made from Sauvignon Blanc in a pure, taut style, one of the domaine’s classic wines alongside Silex.

Pur Sang is one of the classic Pouilly-Fumés of Domaine Didier Dagueneau. Its name, “thoroughbred”, reflects the character of the wine as Didier Dagueneau conceived it: a precise, nervy Sauvignon Blanc without artifice, which seeks to express terroir rather than aromatic exuberance.

It is made from Sauvignon Blanc grown on parcels around Saint-Andelain, with soils combining clay and flint. Like the house’s other wines, it comes from vines farmed for low yields and is fermented and aged in barrels of various sizes, so that the oak brings texture without marking the aromatic profile.

It is a white of great purity, with marked acidity and mineral notes, which can be enjoyed relatively young but gains complexity with a few years in bottle. Like Silex, it is produced in limited quantities.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green glints. Aromas of citrus, white currant, fresh herbs and stony notes. Vibrant palate with marked acidity and a mineral finish.

Food pairing

Grilled fish, shellfish, sushi, goat’s cheeses

Serving

12-13 °C, large white wine glass
