Belleruche Côtes du Rhône Rouge

RedCôtes du RhôneRegional AOC of the Rhône valley (Côtes du Rhône and Côtes du Rhône Villages) Côtes du Rhône

Red Côtes du Rhône from M. Chapoutier, based on Grenache and Syrah, one of the house’s most widely distributed wines, with its characteristic Braille label.

Belleruche is M. Chapoutier’s Côtes du Rhône range, and its red is one of the house’s wines easiest to find in shops. Like the rest of Chapoutier’s labels, it carries the Braille inscription the house has used since the 1990s.

It comes from vineyards in the southern Rhône, where Grenache is the main variety. This brings ripe red fruit and roundness, while Syrah adds colour, freshness and spicy notes; the proportions vary with the vintage. Winemaking takes place in tank, with no oak ageing, to preserve the expression of the fruit.

It is a fruity, easy-drinking red, designed to be enjoyed young. It reflects the generous style of the wines of the southern valley, with a lighter profile than the house’s great wines from Hermitage or Châteauneuf-du-Pape.

Tasting profile

Ruby red. Aromas of strawberry, cherry, liquorice and gentle pepper. Fruity, round palate with soft tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Pizza, pasta, grilled white meats, charcuterie

Serving

15-16 °C, standard red wine glass
