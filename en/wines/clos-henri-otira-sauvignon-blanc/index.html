Clos Henri Otira Single Vineyard Sauvignon Blanc

WhiteMarlboroughGeographical Indication (GI) Marlborough

Single-vineyard Sauvignon Blanc from Clos Henri, from the part of the estate called Otira, in the Wairau Valley, Marlborough.

Otira is the single-vineyard Sauvignon Blanc of Clos Henri, the Marlborough estate founded by the Bourgeois family of Sancerre. It comes from a specific part of the property that is vinified separately to show the influence of the soil, following the Loire tradition in which the family was trained.

Sauvignon Blanc is the variety that made Marlborough famous, with its sunny climate, cool nights and long ripening season. On stony alluvial soils, which are common in the Wairau Valley, the vines tend to give taut wines with marked acidity and a mineral feel rather than tropical aromatic exuberance.

Winemaking, with partial fermentation in wood or lees ageing, aims to add texture and complexity. The result is a food-oriented Sauvignon, closer to the Sancerre style than to many wines of the region, which can gain complexity with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of grapefruit, lemon, gooseberry and wet-stone notes. Taut, textured palate with a long, mineral finish.

Food pairing

Oysters, white fish, goat’s cheese, dishes with fresh herbs

Serving

9-11 °C, white wine glass
