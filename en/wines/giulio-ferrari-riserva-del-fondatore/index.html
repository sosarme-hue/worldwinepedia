Ferrari Trento

Giulio Ferrari Riserva del Fondatore

SparklingTrento DOC RiservaWine region Trentino-Alto Adige

Ferrari’s prestige cuvée, dedicated to its founder: a single-vineyard vintage Chardonnay with very long lees ageing.

The Riserva del Fondatore bears the name of Giulio Ferrari, who founded the house in 1902, and is the winery’s most ambitious sparkling wine. It is made only in the best vintages, from Chardonnay grown at Maso Pianizza, a high-altitude vineyard owned by the Lunelli family on the slopes of Trentino.

It is made by the traditional method and stands out for its lees ageing, much longer than that of most sparkling wines: the wine spends years in bottle on the yeasts before disgorgement, which gives it remarkable aromatic complexity and a very creamy texture.

It is a sparkling wine of great finesse, with evolved aromas of nuts, honey and pastry, supported by the lively acidity of mountain Chardonnay. It can continue to evolve in bottle after release.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow with very fine bubbles. Complex aromas of nuts, honey, candied citrus and toasted pastry. Creamy, deep and fresh on the palate, with a very long finish.

Food pairing

Fine shellfish, noble fish, risotto, poultry in sauce, aged cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, large white wine glass
