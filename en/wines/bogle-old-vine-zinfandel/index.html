Bogle Vineyards

Bogle Old Vine Zinfandel

Red

Californian Zinfandel from Bogle made from old-vine grapes, fruity, generous and spicy in style, and affordably priced.

Old Vine Zinfandel is one of Bogle’s best-known wines. Zinfandel, genetically identical to Italy’s Primitivo, is one of California’s historic varieties, and many old vineyards survive in the state, a good number of them head-trained without trellising. Bogle makes this wine from grapes from old vines in different parts of inland California, notably the Lodi area, and for this reason it is sold under the general California designation.

Old vines give low yields and small bunches, which produce wines with concentrated fruit. The wine is aged in oak barrels, mainly American, which contribute sweet and spicy notes. The result is a medium- to full-bodied red, very fruity with soft tannins, intended to be drunk in its first few years with flavourful dishes, barbecues and casual food.

Tasting profile

Purple-tinged ruby. Aromas of blackberry, ripe raspberry, black pepper and vanilla. Juicy, broad palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Barbecue, pork ribs, burgers, pizza, chili con carne

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
