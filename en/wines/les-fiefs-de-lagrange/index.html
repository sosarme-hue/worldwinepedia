Château Lagrange

Les Fiefs de Lagrange

RedSecond wineAOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Saint-Julien

Second wine of Château Lagrange, a third-growth Saint-Julien, more approachable in its youth than the grand vin.

Les Fiefs de Lagrange is the second wine of Château Lagrange in Saint-Julien. It is made from the lots that, after plot selection, are not used for the grand vin, often from younger vines or plots with a fruitier profile. It shares the appellation and the gravel vineyard in the inland part of the commune with the grand vin.

The blend is based on Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, in proportions that vary with the vintage, with shorter barrel ageing and less new oak than the grand vin.

It is a medium-bodied red with black and red fruit, spicy notes and softer tannins, which allows the Saint-Julien style to be appreciated a few years after the vintage, though it can also be kept for some time.

Tasting profile

Aromas of blackcurrant, black cherry, spice and gentle toasty notes. Round palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Red meat, roast poultry, mushrooms, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass
