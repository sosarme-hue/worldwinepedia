Marchesi Antinori

Guado al Tasso

RedBolgheri DOC SuperioreControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Bolgheri

Bolgheri Superiore from the estate the Antinori family owns on the Tuscan coast, made from Bordeaux varieties.

Guado al Tasso, “the badger’s ford”, is the name of the Antinori estate in Bolgheri, on the coast of the province of Livorno. The property came to the family through Piero Antinori’s maternal line, connected to the Della Gherardesca counts, the historic lords of this area. It lies on a plain surrounded by hills, near the sea, with a Mediterranean climate tempered by sea breezes.

The estate’s main wine bears the same name and is sold as Bolgheri Superiore. It is made from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, in proportions that vary with the vintage. The varieties are vinified separately and the wine is aged in oak barriques before blending.

It is a red of great structure and ripe fruit, representative of the Bolgheri style, able to evolve for many years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Very deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, plum, Mediterranean herbs, tobacco and spice. Broad palate with dense, velvety tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled red meat, roast lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
