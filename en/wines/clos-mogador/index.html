Clos Mogador

Clos Mogador

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Priorat

The flagship red of the Barbier family in Gratallops, one of the wines that marked the revival of Priorat, sourced from the family estate.

Clos Mogador is the wine that gives René Barbier's winery its name and one of the reds that put Priorat back on the map at the end of the 20th century. It comes from the vineyards the family farms on the llicorella slopes of Gratallops, on low-yielding terraces and hillsides.

It is a blend in which Garnacha usually takes the lead, accompanied by Carignan, Syrah and Cabernet Sauvignon, in proportions that vary with the vintage. After fermentation the wine is aged in oak for an extended period. It is a structured, concentrated red designed to develop in bottle over many years, expressing the warm, spicy and mineral character of the area.

Tasting profile

Ripe black fruit, balsamic notes, liquorice, spice and slate. Broad and structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast red meats, game, braises, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decant when young
