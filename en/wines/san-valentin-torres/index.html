San Valentín

WhiteSemi-sweetDesignation of Origin (DO) Catalunya

Semi-sweet white from Familia Torres made from Parellada, with a light, fruity profile.

San Valentín is Familia Torres' semi-sweet white, a long-standing wine of the house that has kept its popularity among those who prefer wines with a touch of sweetness. It is covered by the Catalunya D.O.

It is made from Parellada, the traditional white variety of the Penedès, valued for its aromatic delicacy and fresh acidity. To achieve its characteristic style, fermentation is stopped before the yeasts convert all the sugar in the must, so that the wine retains some natural residual sugar from the grape and a moderate alcohol level. Winemaking takes place in stainless steel tanks at low temperatures, without oak, to preserve the fruity and floral aromas. The acidity of Parellada balances the sweetness and keeps the wine from feeling heavy. It is a light white, intended to be drunk young and well chilled.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of white fruit, peach, flowers and light honey. Soft on the palate, with moderate sweetness balanced by fresh acidity.

Food pairing

Asian cuisine, spicy dishes, foie gras, fruit, lightly sweet desserts

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
