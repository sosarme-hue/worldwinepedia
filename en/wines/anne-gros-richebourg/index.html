Domaine Anne Gros

Richebourg Grand Cru

RedGrand CruBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Nuits

Pinot Noir from the Richebourg grand cru in Vosne-Romanée, made by Domaine Anne Gros.

Domaine Anne Gros, in Vosne-Romanée, is known for Pinot Noirs of great aromatic purity and silky texture, aged in barrel with oak that supports without dominating.

Richebourg is one of the most highly regarded grands crus of Vosne-Romanée. Located in the northern part of the village’s group of grands crus, above the road and next to Romanée-Saint-Vivant, it lies on clay-limestone soils that give wines of particular richness and depth. Anne Gros’s Pinot Noir from this climat is aged in barrel and combines concentration and elegance: dark fruit, spicy and floral notes and fine but firm tannins. It is a wine for long ageing, which needs many years to show its full complexity.

As with the rest of the estate’s range, the grapes are hand-harvested and carefully sorted before vinification.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of black cherry, raspberry, violet, spice and undergrowth. Rich, silky palate with fine tannins and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Game birds, duck, roast veal, mushrooms, washed-rind cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, Burgundy glass
