Recanati Winery

Recanati Reserve Wild Carignan

RedReserve

Recanati red made from old, dry-farmed bush vine Carignan, one of the wines that have revived the reputation of this variety in Israel.

Carignan was for decades one of the most widely planted varieties in Israel, but it was almost always used for simple or high-volume wines. Recanati was one of the wineries that set out to revive the old vines of this variety, bush-trained and dry-farmed, which give low yields and concentrated grapes.

The Wild Carignan from the Reserve range was born of that search. Its name refers to the almost wild appearance of these old vines, which grow without trellising or irrigation. The grapes are hand-picked and, after fermentation, the wine is aged in oak barrels, partly used, so as to respect the fruit and freshness of the variety. The result is a red with a Mediterranean character, with ripe fruit, spicy notes and lively acidity that gives it tension, certified kosher like the rest of the winery's range.

Tasting profile

Aromas of black cherry, raspberry and plum, with notes of Mediterranean herbs, pepper and earth. Juicy and lively, with firm tannins and fresh acidity.

Food pairing

Chargrilled lamb, roast aubergine, spiced meats, aged sheep's cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
