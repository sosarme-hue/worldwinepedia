Château Marquis d'Alesme

Château Marquis d'Alesme

RedThird Growth (1855 classification)AOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Margaux

The grand vin of Château Marquis d’Alesme, a Margaux third growth, from Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot.

Château Marquis d’Alesme is the main wine of this Margaux estate, classified as a third growth in 1855 and owned by the Perrodo family since 2006. For many years it was sold as Marquis d’Alesme Becker, a name that can still be found on some bottles.

It is made from a blend of Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, to which small amounts of Petit Verdot and Cabernet Franc may be added, from gravel, clay and limestone plots in the commune. The plots are vinified separately in the winery built during the estate’s renovation, and the wine is aged in oak barrels before blending.

It follows the classic Margaux style: aromas of dark fruit and violet, fine texture and elegant tannins, with a structure that allows it to develop for many years in bottle, gaining notes of cedar and tobacco.

Tasting profile

Deep red. Aromas of blackcurrant, violet, cedar and spice. Fine, elegant palate with silky tannins and a lingering finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled red meat, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
