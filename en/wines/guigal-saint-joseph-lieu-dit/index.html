E. Guigal

Saint-Joseph Lieu-dit

RedSaint-JosephAOC (Northern Rhône cru) Saint-Joseph

Red Saint-Joseph from E. Guigal, sourced from hillside vineyards in the historic heart of the appellation, made from Syrah and aged in barrel.

The Saint-Joseph Lieu-dit belongs to the range of site-specific wines that Guigal developed on the right bank of the Rhône after acquiring vineyards in Saint-Joseph in the early 2000s. The name refers to the place that gave the appellation its name: the granite slopes near Tournon and Mauves, home to the old vineyard known as “Saint-Joseph”.

It is made exclusively from Syrah, the only red variety of the northern Rhône, grown on steep terraces over granite soils. Ageing takes place in oak barrels, partly new, over an extended period, in keeping with the house style, which favours concentrated wines with ageing potential.

Compared with the winery’s classic Saint-Joseph, this version offers more depth and structure, with the dark fruit and spicy notes typical of northern Syrah framed by the oak. It gains in complexity after a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep purple. Aromas of blackberry, blackcurrant, black pepper, olive and toasty notes. Dense and structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, game, duck, spiced stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
