Inglenook

Inglenook Rubicon

RedAVA Napa Valley

Inglenook’s flagship wine, a Bordeaux-style blend based on Cabernet Sauvignon from the estate vineyards in Rutherford, Napa Valley.

Rubicon is the flagship wine of Inglenook, the historic Rutherford estate in the Napa Valley owned by Francis Ford Coppola. It was born in the late 1970s, shortly after Coppola bought Gustave Niebaum’s former residence, with the idea of making a great Bordeaux-inspired red from the estate’s historic vineyards.

It is made from the estate’s own grapes, on the Rutherford Bench. The base is Cabernet Sauvignon, completed depending on the vintage with Cabernet Franc, Merlot and Petit Verdot. After fermentation, the wine is aged in French oak barrels and then rests in bottle before release.

It is a red of great structure and concentration, with aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, cedar, cocoa and spice, and firm tannins that soften with time. It is designed for long cellaring and gains complexity over many years in bottle, revealing notes of tobacco and earth.

Tasting profile

Very dark garnet. Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, cedar, cocoa and spice. Broad, concentrated palate with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled rib steak, roast lamb, braised short ribs, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass; decant
