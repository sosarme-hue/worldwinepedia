Bodegas Terras Gauda

Terras Gauda O Rosal

WhiteDesignation of Origin (DO) Rías Baixas

White from the O Rosal subzone of Rías Baixas, made from Albariño, Loureira and Caíño Blanco in proportions that vary with the vintage.

Terras Gauda O Rosal is the best-known wine from Bodegas Terras Gauda and an example of the traditional style of the O Rosal subzone, in the south of the Rías Baixas Denominación de Origen, in the Miño valley. There, unlike in the Val do Salnés, it is customary to blend Albariño with other local white varieties.

Its basis is Albariño, which brings fruit and aroma, completed by Loureira, floral in profile and high in acidity, and by Caíño Blanco, a late-ripening variety the winery helped to revive, which adds tension and structure. The proportions vary with the vintage. Each variety is vinified separately, in tank at controlled temperature, before blending, without oak.

The result is an aromatic, fresh white with notes of fruit, flowers and herbs, good acidity and a complexity that sets it apart from single-varietal Albariños. It is enjoyed young but can develop for one or two years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow with green tints. Aromas of stone fruit, citrus, white flowers and fresh herbs. Fresh, broad and lively in acidity on the palate, with a long finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, grilled fish, Galician-style octopus, sushi

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
