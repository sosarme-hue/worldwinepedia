Zenato

Zenato Ripassa

RedValpolicella Ripasso DOC SuperioreDOC and DOCG (Amarone and Recioto) Valpolicella

Zenato’s Valpolicella Ripasso, refermented on Amarone pomace, one of the reds that did most to popularise this style.

Ripassa is one of Zenato’s best-known wines and one of the most internationally widespread Valpolicella Ripassos. Its name refers to the ripasso technique, in which a Valpolicella made from fresh grapes is passed, in spring, over the pomace left after the fermentation of Amarone, which is still rich in sugars and aromas.

This second fermentation increases the wine’s alcohol, body and complexity, giving it some of Amarone’s traits without losing Valpolicella’s freshness. It is made from Corvina, Corvinone and Rondinella from the family’s Valpolicella vineyards and aged in oak before bottling.

It is a medium- to full-bodied red with ripe fruit and spicy notes, offering a more affordable and versatile alternative to Amarone. It can be enjoyed young or kept for a few years.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of ripe cherry, plum, black fruit, spice and vanilla. Rounded, broad palate with soft tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Pasta with ragù, roast meat, stews, roast chicken, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
