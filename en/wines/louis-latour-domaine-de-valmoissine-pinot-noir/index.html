Maison Louis Latour

Louis Latour Domaine de Valmoissine Pinot Noir

RedWine region Provence

Pinot Noir from Domaine de Valmoissine, Louis Latour’s estate in the inland Var, in northern Provence.

Domaine de Valmoissine is the estate owned by Louis Latour in the Haut-Var, the inland and higher part of the Var department in Provence. There, at some altitude, the Beaune house sought a climate cool enough to grow Pinot Noir, an unusual variety in a region dominated by Mediterranean grapes and rosé wines.

The wine is sold under the Var protected geographical indication. It is made to express the fruity side of the variety, in an approachable style without dominant oak ageing.

The cool nights of the higher areas of the Var help to preserve acidity, while the Mediterranean light brings ripe fruit. The result is a light- to medium-bodied red with red fruit and soft tannins, meant to be enjoyed young and as an affordable alternative to Burgundian Pinot Noir.

Tasting profile

Light ruby. Aromas of cherry, strawberry and a touch of spice. Light, fresh palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, poultry, Provençal cooking, oily fish

Serving

14-16 °C, Burgundy glass
