M. Chapoutier Crozes-Hermitage Petite Ruche

RedCrozes-HermitageRhône valley subregion Northern Rhône

A red Crozes-Hermitage from Chapoutier, a fruity, spicy Syrah to be enjoyed young.

Crozes-Hermitage is the largest appellation in the northern Rhône. It surrounds the hill of Hermitage and stretches across alluvial terraces and rounded pebbles in the south and granite soils in the north. Its reds are made from Syrah, which may be completed with a small proportion of the white grapes Marsanne and Roussanne.

Petite Ruche, “little beehive”, is one of the classic ranges of the Chapoutier house in this appellation, made in both red and white versions. The red is conceived as a direct expression of northern Syrah: black fruit, notes of violet and pepper, and soft tannins, without dominant oak ageing.

It is a medium-bodied, juicy red that is easy to drink in its first years. It can be served slightly cool and goes well with simple dishes of meat and charcuterie.

Tasting profile

Purplish ruby. Aromas of blackberry, blackcurrant, violet and pepper. Juicy palate with soft tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, burgers, grilled meat, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, red wine glass
