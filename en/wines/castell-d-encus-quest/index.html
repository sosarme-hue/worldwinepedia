Castell d'Encús

Castell D'Encús Quest

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Costers del Segre

Bordeaux-style blend from Castell d’Encús, Raül Bobet’s mountain winery in the Pallars Jussà, in the Costers del Segre DO.

Castell d’Encús is the project that Raül Bobet launched at the start of this century in Talarn, in the Pallars Jussà, in the Pre-Pyrenees of Lleida. The vineyards lie at high altitude, roughly between 800 and 1,000 metres, in a cool mountain climate with sharp temperature swings, which allows varieties of very different cycles and profiles to be grown. The estate preserves ancient fermentation vats carved into the rock, of medieval origin, which the winery has brought back into use for part of its wines.

The wines are made under the Costers del Segre DO, an appellation made up of scattered sub-zones across inland Catalonia.

Quest is the house’s Bordeaux-inspired red, blending Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Franc, Merlot and Petit Verdot. The cool high-altitude climate lets these varieties ripen slowly, keeping acidity and fresh aromas. After fermentation it is aged in wood and reaches 14% ABV. It is a structured red with good ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Intense deep cherry red. Aromas of blackcurrant, cherry, fine herbal notes, graphite and spice. Fresh and structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Lamb, chargrilled red meat, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
