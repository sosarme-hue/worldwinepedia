Juvé & Camps

Juvé & Camps Reserva de la Familia

SparklingGran Reserva Brut NatureDesignation of Origin (DO) Cava

Gran Reserva Brut Nature Cava from Juvé & Camps, the house's emblematic wine, made from estate-grown Penedès grapes with long ageing on the lees.

Reserva de la Familia is Juvé & Camps' best-known Cava and one of the most widely available Gran Reservas of the appellation. It is made from the three traditional Cava varieties —Macabeo, Xarel·lo and Parellada— largely from the family's own vineyards in the Alt Penedès; the proportions may vary with the vintage.

It follows the traditional method, with a second fermentation in bottle, and the Gran Reserva category requires a minimum of thirty months' ageing on the lees, which the house usually extends. It is a Brut Nature, meaning that no sugar, or only a minimal amount, is added in the dosage, which clearly reveals the character of the base wine and of the ageing. It is a vintage Cava, so each harvest shows its own nuances. Its dry style and fine mousse make it versatile at the table, well beyond the celebratory toast.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow with fine bubbles. Aromas of white fruit, bakery notes, nuts and a citrus background. Dry, creamy and lively on the palate, with a long finish.

Food pairing

Aperitifs, shellfish, fish, rice dishes, Iberian ham

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
