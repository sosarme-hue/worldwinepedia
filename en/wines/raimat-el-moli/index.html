Raimat

Raimat El Molí

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Costers del Segre

Raimat El Molí is a red wine from Raimat, made under the DO Costers del Segre appellation in Spain.

It is aged in wood. It has 14% ABV.

Large wine estate near Lleida, acquired in 1914 by Manuel Raventós of the Codorníu family, and a pioneer of the Costers del Segre D.O.

Costers del Segre is a designation of origin in inland Catalonia, mainly in the province of Lleida, made up of several scattered subzones along the Segre river and its tributaries. Its dry continental climate gives rise to a wide variety of reds, whites and rosés.

Food pairing
