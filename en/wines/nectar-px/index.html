Néctar Pedro Ximénez

FortifiedPedro XiménezDesignation of Origin (DO) Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

Sweet Pedro Ximénez from González Byass, made from sun-dried grapes and aged in solera, one of the easiest PX wines to find.

Néctar is the Pedro Ximénez in González Byass's range. It is made from grapes of the Pedro Ximénez variety which, after harvest, are laid out in the sun to raisin and concentrate their sugar. The resulting very dense, sweet must is fortified with grape spirit to stop fermentation.

The wine then ages in oak casks under the criaderas and solera system, in an oxidative ageing that darkens its colour to mahogany and adds complexity to its dried-fruit aromas. It is a very sweet, unctuous wine, served in small quantities, and one of the most popular PX wines thanks to its availability and reasonable price.

Thanks to its oxidative ageing and high concentration, it keeps well for weeks once the bottle is opened, provided it is tightly closed and stored somewhere cool.

Tasting profile

Raisins, dried figs, dates, molasses and notes of coffee and cocoa. Very sweet, dense, velvety palate.

Food pairing

Vanilla ice cream, chocolate desserts, blue cheeses, nuts

Serving

12-14 °C, small dessert wine glass
