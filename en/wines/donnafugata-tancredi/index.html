Donnafugata

Tancredi

RedIGT Terre SicilianeWine region Sicily

Sicilian red from Donnafugata combining Nero d'Avola and Cabernet Sauvignon, named after a character from The Leopard.

Tancredi bears the name of the Prince of Salina's nephew in The Leopard, the novel by Giuseppe Tomasi di Lampedusa with which Donnafugata shares its own name, that of the Salina family's country residence. Like other wines of the house, its label draws on that literary world. Some editions come in a special design resulting from Donnafugata's collaboration with the fashion house Dolce&Gabbana.

It is made at the Contessa Entellina estate, inland in western Sicily, from a blend based on Nero d'Avola, the island's great red grape, and Cabernet Sauvignon, sometimes completed with other varieties. Nero d'Avola brings fruit and freshness; Cabernet, structure and black fruit notes. Ageing takes place in barrel.

It is an intense, balanced red with firm yet approachable tannins, able to develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of black cherry, plum, spice, liquorice and Mediterranean herbs. Broad, structured palate with a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast red meats, lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
