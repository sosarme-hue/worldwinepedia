Grosset

Grosset Springvale Riesling

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Clare Valley

Dry Riesling from Watervale, in the Clare Valley, from Grosset’s Springvale vineyard on terra rossa and limestone.

Watervale is one of the Clare Valley’s most highly regarded sub-areas for Riesling. Its terra rossa soils, reddish earth over a limestone subsoil, differ from the slates of Polish Hill River, and that difference is the basis of the comparison between Grosset’s two Rieslings.

Springvale comes from a Watervale vineyard farmed organically by the winery. Like the house’s other Rieslings, it is fermented in stainless steel at low temperature, without oak, and bottled under screwcap. The style is dry and fresh, but compared with Polish Hill it tends to be somewhat more open, floral and generous in its youth, with equally firm acidity. It can be drunk young or cellared for years, during which it develops toasty and honeyed notes.

Tasting profile

Lime, lemon, orange blossom and green apple, with a touch of spice. Dry, juicy palate with lively acidity and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Grilled fish, spiced pork, Thai dishes, salads

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
