Domaine du Tariquet

Tariquet Classic

WhiteIGP Côtes de GascogneAOC (South West France) Madiran

Domaine du Tariquet’s best-known dry white, a fresh and fruity blend of Ugni Blanc and Colombard with Sauvignon Blanc and Gros Manseng.

Classic is the flagship wine of Domaine du Tariquet and one of the whites that did most to make the IGP Côtes de Gascogne known. It comes from the Grassa family’s vineyards in Bas-Armagnac, in the Gers department.

It is based on two historic Gascon varieties, Ugni Blanc and Colombard, which for centuries were grown mainly for distilling Armagnac. They are complemented by Sauvignon Blanc and Gros Manseng, which add aroma and a little more body.

The grapes are picked at their freshest and fermented at low temperature in tank, without oak, to preserve fruit and acidity. The result is a light, dry white of moderate alcohol, lively and aromatic, intended to be drunk young, within the year after the harvest.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Aromas of grapefruit, lemon, passion fruit and white flowers. Light, lively and refreshing palate.

Food pairing

Aperitif, shellfish, salads, grilled fish, mild Asian cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
