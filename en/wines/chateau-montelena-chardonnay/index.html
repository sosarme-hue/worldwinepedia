Chateau Montelena

Chateau Montelena Chardonnay

WhiteAVA Napa Valley

Napa Valley Chardonnay from Chateau Montelena, heir to the famous 1973 of the Paris tasting, classic in style with good acidity.

Chardonnay is the wine that brought Chateau Montelena international fame: its 1973 vintage was one of the Californian whites presented at the 1976 Paris tasting. Today the winery still makes it from grapes grown in different parts of the Napa Valley, largely from the cooler southern areas of the valley such as Oak Knoll and Los Carneros, whose climate, moderated by San Pablo Bay, favours acidity.

The house has kept to a classic, restrained style. Traditionally the wine does not go through malolactic fermentation, which preserves its freshness and natural acidity, and it is aged in French oak with a moderate use of new wood. The result is a Chardonnay with a taut, precise profile, less opulent than many of its neighbours, which can be drunk young but can also develop positively in bottle for several years.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of apple, lemon, pear and mineral and almond notes. Taut, fresh palate with marked acidity and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Grilled fish or fish in sauce, shellfish, poultry, soft cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
