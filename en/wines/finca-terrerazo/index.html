Finca Terrerazo

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Utiel-Requena

Bobal from Bodega Mustiguillo, the flagship wine of the El Terrerazo Vino de Pago on the Utiel-Requena plateau.

Finca Terrerazo is the wine that best sums up Mustiguillo's project: to show that Bobal can produce fine, age-worthy reds. It comes from the El Terrerazo estate, in the Utiel-Requena district, recognised as a Vino de Pago, so it is labelled not under the Utiel-Requena D.O. but under its own designation.

The grapes come from vineyards at about 800 metres, on stony limestone soils, in a continental climate with wide temperature swings. Bobal, with its large bunches and late ripening, brings deep colour, good acidity and marked tannins. The wine is aged in oak with an approach that seeks to polish the tannins and preserve the fruit, giving a structured yet fresh red.

Tasting profile

Black and red fruit, notes of violet, wild herbs and spice. Structured palate with lively acidity, firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Red meats, Requena charcuterie, lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decant when young
