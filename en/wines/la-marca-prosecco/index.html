La Marca Vini e Spumanti

La Marca Prosecco

SparklingProsecco DOCDOC and DOCG Prosecco

La Marca Prosecco DOC, recognisable by its light blue label, one of the world’s best-selling Italian sparkling wines.

La Marca Prosecco is the best-known product of La Marca Vini e Spumanti, the group of cooperatives in the province of Treviso. Its bottle with a light blue label has become a familiar sight in supermarkets and wine shops, especially in the United States.

It is made from Glera from the vineyards of member growers within the Prosecco DOC production area in north-eastern Italy. After a first fermentation, the base wine goes into large closed stainless steel tanks where it undergoes the second fermentation that creates the bubbles, using the Charmat or Martinotti method. This system preserves the grape’s fresh aromas and gives a light, fruity sparkling wine.

Its style, with a touch of sweetness, makes it easy to drink. It is enjoyed young, as an aperitif or in cocktails such as the spritz.

Tasting profile

Pale straw, fine and abundant bubbles. Aromas of green apple, peach, lemon and flowers. Light, fresh, slightly sweet palate.

Food pairing

Aperitif, spritz, mild cured meats, salads, fruit desserts

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass or flute
