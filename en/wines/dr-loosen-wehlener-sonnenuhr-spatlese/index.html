Wehlener Sonnenuhr Riesling Spätlese

WhitePrädikatswein SpätleseAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Mosel

Riesling Spätlese from the blue-slate Wehlener Sonnenuhr vineyard, made by Dr. Loosen with natural sweetness balanced by Mosel acidity.

The Wehlener Sonnenuhr is one of the Mosel's most famous vineyards. It stretches across a very steep, south-facing slope opposite the village of Wehlen, covered with Devonian blue slate; its name comes from the large sundial set among the vines. Weingut Dr. Loosen, based in Bernkastel, owns old vines there, many of them ungrafted.

In the German Prädikatswein system, the Spätlese (“late harvest”) category corresponds to grapes picked riper than those for Kabinett. In the traditional Mosel style followed by the estate, fermentation is stopped leaving noticeable natural sweetness, balanced by Riesling's lively acidity and low alcohol.

The result is a white of great purity, with ripe fruit and a characteristic mineral note, able to develop for many years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Bright pale yellow. Aromas of peach, apricot, citrus and flowers, with a mineral background. Sweet, juicy palate held up by vibrant acidity.

Food pairing

Spiced Asian cuisine, pork, foie gras, blue cheeses, fruit tarts

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
