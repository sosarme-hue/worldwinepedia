Parallèle 45 Rouge

RedCôtes du Rhône

Red Côtes du Rhône from Paul Jaboulet Aîné, based on Grenache and Syrah, named after the 45th parallel, which crosses the Rhône valley near the house’s headquarters.

Parallèle 45 is Paul Jaboulet Aîné’s best-known entry-level wine and one of the Côtes du Rhône most often seen in shops and restaurants. Its name refers to the 45th parallel, the line halfway between the equator and the North Pole, which crosses the Rhône valley not far from Tain-l’Hermitage.

Unlike the house’s estate wines, it comes mainly from grapes grown in the southern Rhône, where most of the Côtes du Rhône vineyard area is concentrated. It is based on Grenache, which brings ripe fruit and warmth, and Syrah, which adds colour, spicy notes and structure; the proportions vary with the vintage. Ageing is short and aims to preserve the fruit.

It is a red meant to be drunk young, versatile at the table and representative of the fruity, spicy style of southern Rhône wines. The Parallèle 45 range also includes a white and a rosé.

Tasting profile

Ruby red. Aromas of ripe red berries, plum and gentle spice, with a touch of dried herbs. Round, fruity palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Pizza, pasta with ragù, burgers, charcuterie, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

15-16 °C, standard red wine glass
