Marchesi Antinori

Il Bruciato

RedBolgheri DOCControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Bolgheri

The entry-level Bolgheri from Tenuta Guado al Tasso, Antinori’s estate on the Tuscan coast, made from Bordeaux varieties and Syrah.

Il Bruciato comes from Tenuta Guado al Tasso, the Antinori family’s estate in Bolgheri, a large property on the coastal plain and hills between the Tyrrhenian Sea and the village of Bolgheri. The area, with a Mediterranean climate tempered by sea breezes, has become known for its reds from Bordeaux varieties.

The wine is made from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Syrah, in proportions that vary with the vintage. Each variety is vinified separately, and the wine is aged for a few months in barriques before blending and bottling.

It is the estate’s second wine, after Guado al Tasso, and is designed to show the Bolgheri style in an approachable way: ripe fruit, medium body and soft tannins that make it enjoyable young.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby red. Aromas of ripe black fruit, plum and spice, with balsamic notes and a light toastiness. Round on the palate, with soft tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled meat, burgers, pasta with meat sauces, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
