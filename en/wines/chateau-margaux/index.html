Château Margaux

Château Margaux

RedPremier Grand Cru Classé (1855)Bordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The grand vin of Château Margaux, a Premier Grand Cru Classé in the Margaux appellation, based on Cabernet Sauvignon.

The estate’s grand vin comes from a selection of the best plots in the vineyard, on the gravels of the Margaux appellation. Cabernet Sauvignon is the dominant variety, often in very high proportions, completed depending on the vintage with Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot.

Each plot is vinified separately and the final blend is decided after rigorous tasting, so only part of the production goes into the grand vin; the rest is kept for Pavillon Rouge and other wines. Ageing takes place in new French oak barrels, many made in the estate’s own cooperage.

Château Margaux is known for its elegance and aromatic finesse. It needs years in bottle to show its complexity and can age for several decades.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, violet, cedar and fine spice, with tobacco notes as it ages. Elegant and deep on the palate, with fine, very persistent tannins.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, beef fillet, game birds, mushrooms

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass; decanting recommended
