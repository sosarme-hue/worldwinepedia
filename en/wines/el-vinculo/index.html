Tinto Pesquera (Familia Fernández Rivera)

El Vínculo

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) La Mancha

Tempranillo red from La Mancha made by the Fernández Rivera family, of Tinto Pesquera, at their winery in Campo de Criptana.

El Vínculo is the La Mancha project of the family of Alejandro Fernández, creator of Tinto Pesquera in Ribera del Duero. The winery is in Campo de Criptana, the La Mancha town known for its windmills, and its name, meaning “the bond”, alludes to the family's ties to the land and to winegrowing tradition.

The wine is made exclusively from Tempranillo, the same variety the family works with in Ribera, here called Cencibel. The grapes come from old dry-farmed bush vines on the La Mancha plateau, at considerable altitude, with an extreme continental climate of very hot summers, cold winters and scant rain, and calcareous soils. In keeping with the house style, the wine is aged in American oak barrels, which give it spicy and vanilla notes. The result is a red with ripe fruit, a full body and firm tannins, in a classic style reminiscent of the family's Duero reds.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red with a garnet rim. Aromas of ripe black fruit, liquorice, vanilla, coconut and spice. Broad on the palate, with firm tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled meats, Manchego cheese, game stews

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
