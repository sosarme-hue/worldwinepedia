La Rioja Alta, S.A.

Viña Ardanza Reserva

RedReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Classic reserva from La Rioja Alta, made from Tempranillo with Grenache, aged in American oak and produced only in selected vintages.

Viña Ardanza is one of La Rioja Alta’s historic wines and one of the most recognised labels of classic Rioja. It is made from a blend that is mostly Tempranillo with a proportion of Grenache, largely sourced from the winery’s own vineyards in the eastern part of the appellation.

The varieties are aged separately in American oak barrels made in the winery’s cooperage, and racked by hand following the traditional method. After blending, the wine rests in bottle before release. The winery does not make it every year, only in vintages it considers suitable.

Its style combines the ripe fruit of Grenache with the structure of Tempranillo and the spicy notes of American oak ageing, in a balanced whole with good cellaring potential.

Tasting profile

Ruby with a garnet rim. Aromas of ripe red fruit, vanilla, coconut, sweet spice and balsamic notes. Silky and balanced on the palate, with fresh acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, red meat, meat stews, mushrooms, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
