Domaine du Vieux Télégraphe

Vieux Télégraphe La Crau Rouge

RedChâteauneuf-du-PapeAOC Châteauneuf-du-Pape

The main red of Domaine du Vieux Télégraphe, from the pebble-covered La Crau plateau and based on Grenache.

Vieux Télégraphe La Crau is one of the benchmark red Châteauneuf-du-Papes. It comes from the vines the Brunier family farms on the La Crau plateau in Bédarrides, high ground covered with large rounded pebbles over red clay, where some of the estate’s oldest vines grow.

The blend is based on Grenache, completed by Syrah, Mourvèdre and Cinsault and, in small amounts, other authorised varieties; the proportions vary with the vintage. Grapes are picked by hand and, after fermentation, the wine is aged for a long period in large oak casks, a traditional vessel that respects the fruit and the character of the terroir.

It is a generous, spicy red, yet with the freshness and structure to age for many years. In its youth it is fruity and somewhat firm; with time, notes of leather, truffle and dried herbs appear.

Tasting profile

Garnet red. Aromas of black cherry, ripe raspberry, liquorice, garrigue and spice. Broad, warm palate with firm but polished tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Lamb with herbs, beef stew, game, mushrooms

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
