Feudi di San Gregorio Rubrato Aglianico

RedWine region Campania

An Irpinian Aglianico from Feudi di San Gregorio in a young, fruity style, a more approachable introduction than Taurasi to Campania’s great red grape.

Aglianico is the great red variety of southern Italy, and in Irpinia it gives its best-known expression, Taurasi. It is a late-ripening grape, rich in tannin and acidity, which in its most ambitious form needs many years of ageing.

Rubrato is Feudi di San Gregorio’s younger, more direct take on Aglianico. It is made from grapes grown in Irpinia vineyards and vinified to preserve fruit and soften the variety’s tannic structure. Ageing is relatively short, so that the wine reaches the market with its fruit intact.

The result is a medium-bodied, fruity, spicy red, with Aglianico’s characteristic acidity and tannin in a softer form, meant to be drunk in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby with purple glints. Aromas of black cherry, plum, blackberry and pepper, with a touch of violet. Juicy and fresh on the palate, with present yet polished tannins.

Food pairing

Pasta with Neapolitan ragù, pizza, grilled meat, cured meats, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, medium-sized glass
