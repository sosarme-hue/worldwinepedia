Vinoterra Saperavi

RedWine region (with several designations of origin) Kakheti

A Saperavi red from Schuchmann Wines’ Vinoterra range, made by the traditional qvevri method in Kakheti.

Vinoterra is the traditional wine range of Schuchmann Wines, the winery in Kisiskhevi near Telavi. This red is made from Saperavi from vineyards in the Alazani valley in Kakheti, the variety’s home region.

Following the Kakhetian method, the must ferments in buried qvevri in contact with the skins, and the wine stays in the vessels for an extended maceration before being racked. This process extracts the intense colour typical of a variety with coloured flesh and a notable tannic structure, which Saperavi’s natural acidity keeps in balance. The wine may then rest for a time before bottling. The result is a dry, dark red with black fruit and firm tannins, which illustrates the classic style of Kakheti’s qvevri reds and develops favourably with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Very intense purplish red. Aromas of blackberry, blueberry, plum, liquorice and spice, with earthy notes. Dry, structured and fresh on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

grilled lamb and pork, mtsvadi (Georgian skewers), spiced stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
