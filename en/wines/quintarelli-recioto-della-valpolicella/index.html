Quintarelli Recioto della Valpolicella Classico

SweetRecioto della Valpolicella Classico DOCGDOC and DOCG (Amarone and Recioto) Valpolicella

Sweet red from dried grapes of the Valpolicella Classica, made by Giuseppe Quintarelli only in suitable vintages.

Recioto della Valpolicella is the historic wine of the Valpolicella, older than Amarone: a sweet red made from grapes left to dry for months before pressing. Unlike Amarone, fermentation stops before all the sugar has turned into alcohol, so the wine keeps a natural sweetness.

At the Quintarelli winery in Negrar, in the Valpolicella Classica, it is made from Corvina, Corvinone and Rondinella grapes, together with other varieties from the estate, selected and set to dry in the lofts. It is produced only in vintages when the grapes reach the necessary quality, and ageing in large oak casks is long, as with all the house’s wines.

It is a sweet wine of great concentration, with aromas of cherries in liqueur, prunes, chocolate and spice, and an acidity and tannins that balance the sugar. It has long ageing potential and remains one of the rarest wines in the Quintarelli range.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet. Aromas of cherries in liqueur, prunes, chocolate and spice. Sweet, concentrated palate balanced by acidity and tannins.

Food pairing

Dark chocolate desserts, pastries with nuts, blue cheeses, long-aged cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, medium-sized glass
