Edoardo Miroglio

Edoardo Miroglio Mavrud

RedWine region (Thracian Lowlands PGI) Thracian Valley

A varietal Mavrud red from the Edoardo Miroglio winery in Elenovo. It shows the character of Bulgaria’s most emblematic native grape.

The Italian-owned Edoardo Miroglio winery planted both international varieties and Bulgarian grapes on its Elenovo estate, in the eastern part of the Thracian plain. Among the latter is Mavrud, the most representative native red grape of the south of the country, from which the winery makes this varietal wine.

Mavrud is a late-ripening variety that needs the long, warm summers of the Thracian Valley to complete its cycle and gives wines with intense colour, marked acidity and abundant tannins. After fermentation, the wine may spend time in barrel, which helps polish its structure, and then rests in bottle. The result is a medium- to full-bodied red with black fruit and spicy notes, which retains the variety’s freshness and can improve with a few years of cellaring. It is a good example of Bulgarian wineries’ renewed interest in their native grapes.

Tasting profile

Very intense cherry red. Aromas of blackberry, plum, black cherry and violet, with notes of pepper, tobacco and toast. Structured on the palate, with lively acidity and firm tannins.

Food pairing

grilled meat, lamb, Balkan stews with paprika, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
