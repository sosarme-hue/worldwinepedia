Azienda Agricola Sottimano

Azienda Agricola Sottimano Barbaresco Cottà

RedControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Barbaresco

Barbaresco from the Sottimano family of Neive, from the Cottà vineyard and made from Nebbiolo.

Sottimano is a family winery in Neive, one of the municipalities of the Barbaresco DOCG in Piedmont’s Langhe. The family farms its own vineyards in several of the area’s additional geographical mentions (MGA) and is known for vinifying each cru separately, in a style that seeks the transparency of Nebbiolo rather than extraction.

Cottà is one of those vineyards. It lies within the municipality of Neive, near the winery, and is one of the family’s historic crus. Barbaresco is made solely from Nebbiolo, a late-ripening variety high in acidity and tannin which, on the calcareous hills of the right bank of the Tanaro, gives wines that are pale in colour but highly structured.

Ageing in wood, compulsory in the appellation, softens the texture without masking the fruit. It has 14.5% ABV. It is a red that needs time: when young it can be tannic and closed, and with age it develops notes of dried rose, tar and forest floor.

Tasting profile

Medium-intensity garnet red. Aromas of cherry, raspberry, rose, spice and earthy notes. Fresh and tannic on the palate, with good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Tajarin with truffle, braised red meat, brasato al Barolo, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
