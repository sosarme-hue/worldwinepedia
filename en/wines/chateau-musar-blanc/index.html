Château Musar

Château Musar Blanc

WhiteWine region Bekaa Valley

A white for ageing from Château Musar made from the native Lebanese varieties Obaideh and Merwah, grown in high-altitude vineyards. It is a wine of oxidative style and great longevity.

The white Château Musar is one of the wines that have done most to make Lebanon’s native white varieties known. It is made from Obaideh and Merwah, two traditional grapes grown in high-altitude vineyards, often from old vines, in proportions that vary with the vintage.

The wine is fermented and aged in oak barrels and, like the house red, spends several years in the cellars at Ghazir before release. Winemaking with minimal intervention produces a white of oxidative style, broad texture and great complexity, very different from young, aromatic whites. Each vintage has its own character, and the wine can evolve for decades in bottle, developing ever more complex notes over time. It is best served not too cold, so that its complexity can be fully appreciated, and it can benefit from some time in the glass.

Tasting profile

Golden in colour. Aromas of nuts, honey, candied citrus, wax and notes from ageing. Broad and unctuous on the palate, with acidity that holds it together and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, roast poultry, Lebanese mezze, dishes with nuts, aged cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, large glass
