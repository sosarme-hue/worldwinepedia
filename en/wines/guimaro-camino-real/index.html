Guímaro

Guímaro Camiño Real

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Ribeira Sacra

Selection red from Guímaro in Ribeira Sacra, made mainly from old-vine Mencía from the Amandi subzone.

Camiño Real is one of the most ambitious reds from Guímaro, the Rodríguez family winery in Sober, in the Amandi subzone of Ribeira Sacra. It sits above the young Guímaro and below the house’s single-plot wines, and comes from old vines spread across different sites on the slopes of the Sil.

It is made mainly from Mencía, accompanied in the old vineyards by small amounts of other traditional Galician varieties, whose proportions vary with the vintage. The vineyards are farmed on terraces on slate soils, by hand. Winemaking seeks gentle extraction, in some cases with a portion of whole bunches, and the wine is aged in used wooden vessels, so that the oak does not dominate the character of the fruit.

The result is a medium-bodied red, deeper than the young Guímaro, with red and black fruit, notes of herbs and spice and a marked mineral character. It can develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Medium-intensity deep cherry red. Red and black fruit, wild herbs, spice and slate notes. Fresh and savoury on the palate, with fine tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, Galician empanada, white meat, mushrooms, cow’s milk cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
