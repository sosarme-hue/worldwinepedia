Bodegas Los Bermejos

Los Bermejos Malvasía Naturalmente Dulce

SweetDesignation of Origin (DO) Lanzarote

Naturally sweet volcanic Malvasía from Lanzarote, heir to the ancient tradition of Canary Malvasía wines.

The sweet Malvasía wines of the Canaries enjoyed great fame in Europe, especially in England, during the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, when they were known as Canary or Malmsey. Los Bermejos' Malvasía Naturalmente Dulce belongs to that tradition, applied to the volcanic vineyards of Lanzarote.

It is made from volcanic Malvasía from ungrafted vines planted in pits dug into the picón. To obtain a sweet wine without adding alcohol, harvesting is delayed so that the grapes reach very high ripeness and concentrate their sugars, often with some raisining on the vine. During fermentation, the high sugar content causes the yeasts to stop naturally, leaving substantial residual sweetness. The result is a sweet wine of moderate alcohol that retains the acidity and aromas of the variety. It is usually sold in half-litre bottles.

Tasting profile

Deep gold. Aromas of dried apricot, honey, flowers, candied orange peel and a mineral touch. Sweet and unctuous on the palate, balanced by lively acidity.

Food pairing

Blue cheeses, foie gras, almond desserts, nuts

Serving

8-10 °C, small glass
