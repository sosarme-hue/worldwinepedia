Domaine Leflaive

Domaine Leflaive Puligny-Montrachet

WhiteVillageBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Beaune

Chardonnay white from the Puligny-Montrachet village appellation, from several of Domaine Leflaive’s biodynamically farmed plots.

Domaine Leflaive’s Puligny-Montrachet village comes from several of the estate’s plots within the commune, outside the premiers and grands crus, generally on the lower part of the slope, where the soils are somewhat deeper. Blending different plots makes it possible to produce a wine representative of the village’s character.

It is made from biodynamically farmed Chardonnay, picked by hand. The must ferments in oak barrels, with a moderate proportion of new wood, and the wine is aged for about a year in barrel and then for a few months in tank before bottling.

It is a classic Puligny white, with white fruit, floral notes and a fine texture supported by lively acidity and a mineral character. It can be enjoyed a few years after the harvest, although it gains complexity with moderate cellaring. It is the gateway to the domaine’s wines.

Tasting profile

Bright pale yellow. Aromas of pear, lemon, white flowers and hazelnut, with a mineral touch. Fresh, fine palate with good tension and a clean, persistent finish.

Food pairing

White fish in butter sauce, scallops, poultry in cream, soft cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, large white wine glass
