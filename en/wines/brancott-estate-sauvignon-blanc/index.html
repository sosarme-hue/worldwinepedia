Brancott Estate

Brancott Estate Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Marlborough

Sauvignon Blanc from Brancott Estate, successor to Montana, the pioneering company that planted Marlborough's first commercial vineyards.

Few labels are as closely tied to the history of New Zealand Sauvignon Blanc as this one. The company now called Brancott Estate, known for decades as Montana, planted the first commercial vineyards in Marlborough in the Brancott Valley in 1973 and made the region's first Sauvignon Blancs there at the end of that decade.

Today's wine is made from grapes from several Marlborough vineyards, both its own and those of partner growers, which bring complementary nuances: ripe tropical fruit, citrus notes and a characteristic herbaceous touch. Winemaking takes place in stainless steel tanks at low temperatures, and the wine is bottled early, without oak, to retain its freshness. It is an aromatic, lively white with marked acidity that sums up well the style that brought Marlborough international fame, and it is best enjoyed in its youth.

Tasting profile

Aromas of passion fruit, grapefruit and gooseberry, with notes of green pepper and fresh grass. Vibrant, with lively acidity and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Oysters, grilled fish, goat's cheese salad, Vietnamese dishes

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
