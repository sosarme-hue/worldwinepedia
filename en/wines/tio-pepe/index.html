González Byass

Tío Pepe

FortifiedFinoDesignation of Origin (DO) Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

The best-known fino from Jerez, made by González Byass from Palomino and aged under flor in the criaderas and solera system.

Tío Pepe is González Byass's flagship wine, named in honour of José Ángel de la Peña, the founder's uncle. It is a fino, the dry, biologically aged style characteristic of Jerez de la Frontera, made from Palomino grapes from vineyards on albariza soils.

After fermentation the wine is lightly fortified with grape spirit and goes into the casks of the criaderas and solera system. There, over several years, a veil of flor grows on its surface: a layer of yeast that protects it from oxygen and gives its characteristic aromas. The result is a very dry, pale, pungent wine, best served chilled and drunk within a reasonable time once the bottle is opened. The house also releases en rama versions with minimal filtration.

Tasting profile

Notes of almond, yeast, bread dough and saline, herbal hints. Very dry, light, pungent palate with a saline finish.

Food pairing

Iberian ham, olives, almonds, fried fish, shellfish

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
