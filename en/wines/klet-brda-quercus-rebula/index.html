Quercus Rebula

WhiteWine district with protected designation of origin (ZGP) Goriška Brda

A young, fresh Rebula from the Quercus line of the Klet Brda cooperative, an accessible introduction to the great white grape of Goriška Brda.

The Quercus line is one of the most widely available from Klet Brda, the Goriška Brda cooperative based in Dobrovo. Its varietal wines aim to show the fruit and freshness of the area’s grapes, and this Rebula is a good example of the lighter, more direct side of the region’s emblematic variety.

The grapes come from members’ vineyards spread across the hills of Brda on the local flysch soils. Unlike the macerated whites that have made the border area famous, this wine is made as a conventional white: pressing of the grapes, temperature-controlled fermentation in tank and brief ageing before bottling, to preserve the primary aromas and natural acidity of the variety. It is a dry, light and fresh white, designed to be drunk young, within a year or two of the harvest.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of citrus, green apple, pear and white flowers. Light, fresh, savoury palate with a clean finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, grilled fish, salads, aperitifs

Serving

8-10 °C, white-wine glass
