Faustino V Reserva

RedReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Reserva from Bodegas Faustino, made mainly from Tempranillo and aged for at least three years between barrel and bottle.

Faustino V Reserva occupies the middle step of Bodegas Faustino’s classic range, between the young Faustino VII and the Faustino I Gran Reserva. It is therefore a wine of intermediate ageing within Rioja’s classic ladder. It is made mainly from Tempranillo from vineyards in Rioja Alavesa and Rioja Alta, sometimes with small additions of other traditional varieties, depending on the vintage.

In accordance with the rules of the Reserva category in the Rioja Denominación de Origen Calificada, the wine ages for at least three years before release, with a minimum of one year in oak barrels. The wood ageing brings notes of spice and vanilla, while bottle rest in the Oyón cellars rounds out the whole.

It is a classic-style Rioja, lighter than the Gran Reserva, combining the ripe fruit of Tempranillo with the complexity of ageing.

Tasting profile

Ruby red with a brick rim. Ripe red fruit, vanilla, spice and a toasty touch. Smooth and balanced on the palate, with gentle tannins.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled lamb chops, Iberian cured meats, semi-cured cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
