Blandy’s

Blandy’s Duke of Clarence

FortifiedRich (sweet)Protected Designation of Origin (PDO) Madeira

Sweet Madeira from Blandy’s in the “rich” style, named after the legend of the Duke of Clarence, drowned in a butt of Malmsey in the fifteenth century.

Duke of Clarence is one of the best-known Madeiras from Blandy’s, the Blandy family house in Funchal. Its name refers to a famous English story: according to tradition, George, Duke of Clarence, sentenced to death in 1478, chose to be drowned in a butt of Malmsey, the sweet wine later associated with Madeira.

The wine is a young Madeira in the rich style, that is, sweet, made mainly from Tinta Negra, the most widely planted variety on the island. As with all Madeiras, fermentation is stopped with grape spirit, and the wine then undergoes ageing in warm conditions that encourage oxidative development and bring out the characteristic aromas of caramel, nuts and candied fruit. It is then aged for some time in oak casks before blending and bottling.

Like all Madeiras, it is highly resistant to oxidation and keeps well for months once opened.

Tasting profile

Dark amber. Aromas of caramel, raisins, walnut, dried fig and spice. Sweet, smooth palate balanced by Madeira’s typical acidity, with a nutty finish.

Food pairing

Nut tart, honey cake, dark chocolate, aged cheeses, on its own with coffee

Serving

14-16 °C, fortified or white wine glass
