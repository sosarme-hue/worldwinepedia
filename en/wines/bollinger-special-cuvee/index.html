Champagne Bollinger

Bollinger Special Cuvée

SparklingBrutAOC (sparkling wine) Champagne

Bollinger’s non-vintage brut, led by Pinot Noir, with some wines fermented in barrel and reserve wines kept in magnum.

Special Cuvée is Bollinger’s benchmark champagne and the one that best sums up the house style. Pinot Noir is the main variety in the blend, completed with Chardonnay and a smaller proportion of Pinot Meunier, with grapes largely from the house’s own vineyards.

Some of the base wines are fermented in used oak barrels, and the blend includes a significant proportion of reserve wines, many of them kept in magnums. After the second fermentation in bottle, the wine rests on its lees for longer than the minimum required for non-vintage champagnes.

The result is a vinous-style champagne, with body and complexity, particularly well suited to accompanying meals.

Tasting profile

Golden with fine bubbles. Aromas of baked apple, pear, spice and notes of walnut and brioche. Structured and broad on the palate, with firm acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast poultry, fish in sauce, mushrooms, aged cheeses, cured ham

Serving

9-11 °C, white wine glass
