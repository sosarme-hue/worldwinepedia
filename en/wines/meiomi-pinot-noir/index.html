Meiomi

Meiomi Pinot Noir

Red

Californian Pinot Noir made from grapes grown in several coastal areas, fruity and smooth in style, and one of the best-selling in the United States.

Meiomi Pinot Noir is the wine with which the brand was born, in the mid-2000s. Its approach consists of blending grapes from different parts of the California coast, mainly from Monterey, Santa Barbara and Sonoma counties, to obtain a wine with a consistent profile from one vintage to the next. For that reason it carries the general designation of California on the label.

Each area contributes different traits: ripe, rounded fruit from some, freshness and acidity from those most exposed to the influence of the Pacific. The wine is partly aged in oak, which gives it toasty and vanilla notes, and it is bottled in a generous, very fruity style with soft tannins. That combination of fruit, smoothness and easy drinking explains its great popularity in supermarkets and shops in the United States. It is a Pinot Noir intended to be drunk young.

Tasting profile

Bright ruby. Aromas of ripe strawberry, cherry, blackberry and notes of vanilla and mocha. Smooth, rounded palate with light tannins.

Food pairing

Seared salmon, duck, roast poultry, mushroom dishes

Serving

14-16 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
