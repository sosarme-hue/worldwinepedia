Elena Walch

Elena Walch Gewürztraminer

WhiteAlto Adige DOCWine region Trentino-Alto Adige

Alto Adige Gewürztraminer made by Elena Walch in Tramin, the village traditionally associated with the origin of this aromatic variety’s name.

Gewürztraminer is an aromatic, pink-skinned variety belonging to the Traminer family, whose name is traditionally linked to Tramin (Termeno) in southern Alto Adige. Elena Walch is based there, and the variety holds a central place in its production.

The wine is made from grapes from vineyards around Tramin and other slopes in southern Alto Adige, where the sunny climate and cool nights allow full ripening without loss of freshness. Fermentation takes place at controlled temperature in stainless steel and the wine rests for some time on its fine lees before bottling, without oak ageing, to preserve its aromatic intensity.

It is a very perfumed white, dry or off-dry, medium- to full-bodied. The house also makes a single-vineyard version from the Kastelaz estate, on the slope above the village.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Intense aromas of rose, lychee, tropical fruit, orange blossom and spice. Broad, soft palate with an aromatic, lightly spicy finish.

Food pairing

Asian cuisine, mild curry, aromatic cheeses, foie gras, spicy dishes

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
