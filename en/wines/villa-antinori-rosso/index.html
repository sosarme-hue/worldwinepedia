Marchesi Antinori

Villa Antinori Rosso

RedToscana IGTWine region Tuscany

Antinori Tuscan red combining Sangiovese with international varieties, one of the house’s historic and most widely available labels.

Villa Antinori is one of the Antinori family’s oldest brands. It was born in the first third of the twentieth century as a Chianti and for decades was one of the Tuscan wines most present on international markets. Over time its composition evolved and today it is sold under the Toscana IGT geographical indication, which allows greater freedom in the choice of varieties.

It is made mainly from Sangiovese, combined with Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and, depending on the vintage, other varieties; the proportions vary every year. The grapes come from different family vineyards in Tuscany. After fermentation the wine is aged in oak for a few months before bottling.

It is a medium-bodied, balanced red that is easy to pair with food, blending the red fruit and acidity of Sangiovese with the roundness of international varieties.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of cherry, plum, black fruit and notes of spice and vanilla. Smooth, balanced palate with rounded tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Pasta with ragù, grilled meat, bistecca alla fiorentina, Tuscan cured meats, pecorino

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
