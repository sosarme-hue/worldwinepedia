Viña Concha y Toro

Casillero del Diablo Cabernet Sauvignon

RedReservaDesignation of Origin (wine region grouping Maipo, Rapel, Curicó and Maule) Central Valley

Chilean Cabernet Sauvignon from Concha y Toro’s Casillero del Diablo brand, one of Chile’s best-selling wines worldwide.

Casillero del Diablo is Concha y Toro’s best-known brand and one of the best-selling Chilean wine brands in the world. Its name, meaning “the Devil’s Cellar”, comes from a house legend: Melchor de Concha y Toro, the winery’s founder, is said to have spread the rumour that the devil lived in the part of the Pirque cellar where he kept his finest wines, in order to deter those who tried to take them.

Cabernet Sauvignon is the brand’s historic wine. It is made from grapes grown in different valleys of central Chile, such as the Maipo, Rapel or Maule, and therefore carries the Central Valley designation. The Mediterranean climate, with dry, sunny summers and cool nights, favours even ripening. Part of the wine is aged in oak, which contributes toasty notes. It is a fruity, medium-bodied red, intended to be drunk in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, black cherry, plum and toasty notes. Fruity, medium-bodied palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, roast lamb, beef stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
