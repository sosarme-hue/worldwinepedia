Marchesi Mazzei

Philip

RedIGT ToscanaWine region Tuscany

Tuscan Cabernet Sauvignon from Castello di Fonterutoli, dedicated to Filippo Mazzei, the family ancestor linked to the history of the United States.

Philip pays tribute to Filippo Mazzei, a member of the family who emigrated to Virginia in the 18th century, befriended Thomas Jefferson and tried to introduce vine growing there. The Mazzei family, owners of Castello di Fonterutoli in Castellina in Chianti, dedicate this red to him under the English form of his name.

It is made from Cabernet Sauvignon grown in Tuscany and sold as IGT Toscana, since the variety is not the basis of the Chianti Classico appellation. On the Tuscan hills, Cabernet Sauvignon ripens well and gives wines of deep colour, firm tannins and black fruit aromas, which barrel ageing rounds out.

It is a structured, concentrated red, international in style but with the freshness typical of the area, which can develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Dark purple-red. Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, roasted pepper, cedar and spice. Dense palate with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Grilled rib steak, lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
