CVNE (Compañía Vinícola del Norte de España)

Cune Rosado

RoséQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Young Tempranillo rosé from CVNE, fresh and fruity, made to be drunk in the year after the harvest.

Cune Rosado is the rosé in the young range of the Compañía Vinícola del Norte de España (CVNE), the Haro winery founded in 1879. Although the house is best known for its aged reds, Rioja also has a long tradition of rosés and claretes, everyday wines very present on the region’s tables.

It is made from Tempranillo from vineyards in Rioja Alta. To obtain its colour, the red grapes are kept in contact with their skins for a short time, until the must takes on the desired hue; it is then separated and fermented at low temperature, like a white wine, to preserve the fruit aromas. The wine does not go into barrel and is bottled shortly after the harvest.

The result is a raspberry-coloured rosé, aromatic and fresh, with red fruit and good acidity, designed to be drunk young, as an aperitif or with light meals.

Tasting profile

Bright raspberry pink. Aromas of strawberry, raspberry and fruit drops, with a floral touch. Fresh, light and lively in acidity on the palate.

Food pairing

Aperitifs, salads, rice dishes, pasta, light Mediterranean cooking

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
