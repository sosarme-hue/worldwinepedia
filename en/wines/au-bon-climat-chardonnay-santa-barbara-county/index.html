Au Bon Climat

Chardonnay Santa Barbara County

WhiteCounty with several AVAs Santa Barbara

Cool-climate Au Bon Climat Chardonnay, barrel-fermented and aged yet with lively acidity and a restrained style.

Au Bon Climat’s Chardonnay Santa Barbara County is made from grapes from cool vineyards in the county, in particular the Santa Maria Valley, where the marine influence allows the variety to ripen slowly and retain high acidity.

Jim Clendenen took his inspiration from the whites of the Côte de Beaune: the wine is fermented in French oak barrels, rests for a time on its lees and is aged in wood, but with a moderate proportion of new barrels and a harvest that avoids overripeness. The result is a Chardonnay with texture and notes from ageing, but more taut and less opulent than the Californian style most common when the winery was founded. It can be enjoyed young, although it gains complexity with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow. Aromas of lemon, apple, pear and hazelnut with a toasty touch. Fresh and taut on the palate, with a creamy texture and a mineral finish.

Food pairing

Lobster, scallops, chicken in cream sauce, baked fish, risotto

Serving

10-12 °C, large glass
