Crozes-Hermitage Domaine de Thalabert

RedCrozes-HermitageRhône valley subregion Northern Rhône

Red Crozes-Hermitage from Syrah grown at Domaine de Thalabert, a historic Paul Jaboulet Aîné estate on the pebble-strewn terraces of the Les Chassis plain.

Domaine de Thalabert is one of the best-known estates in the Crozes-Hermitage appellation, the largest in the northern Rhône, which surrounds the hill of Hermitage. Paul Jaboulet Aîné has farmed it for decades, and for a long time its wine was one of those that showed that Crozes could produce reds capable of ageing.

The vines lie in the Les Chassis area, south of Tain-l’Hermitage, on alluvial terraces covered with rounded pebbles that store heat and help Syrah ripen well. The wine is made solely from this variety and aged in barrel for several months, some of them new or lightly used.

It is a red that is more approachable in its youth than the house’s Hermitages, but with enough structure to develop well for some years in bottle, when the fruit gives way to spicier and earthier notes.

Tasting profile

Purple-red. Aromas of blackberry, black cherry, pepper and black olive. Medium-bodied, juicy palate with firm tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Grilled meats, duck, charcuterie, stews with olives

Serving

16-17 °C, large glass
