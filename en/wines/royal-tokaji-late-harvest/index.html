Royal Tokaji

Royal Tokaji Late Harvest

SweetLate HarvestProtected designation of origin (OEM) Tokaj

Sweet Tokaj wine made by Royal Tokaji from late-harvested grapes, lighter and fresher than an Aszú, offering an accessible introduction to the region’s sweet wines.

Tokaj is famous for Aszú, made from berries affected by noble rot that are macerated in a must or base wine. Alongside this traditional category, the region’s producers also make late-harvest wines, in which the bunches are left on the vine well into autumn, when the grapes have concentrated their sugar through overripening, shrivelling and, in part, the action of Botrytis cinerea.

Royal Tokaji Late Harvest is made from the region’s main varieties, Furmint and Hárslevelű, possibly joined by Sárgamuskotály (Muscat Blanc à Petits Grains); the proportions vary with the vintage. Unlike Aszú, the grapes are pressed directly, without the maceration of botrytised berries, and the must ferments until the wine retains a notable amount of residual sugar. Ageing is short, to preserve fruit and freshness.

The result is a medium-bodied sweet wine in which Furmint’s characteristic acidity balances the sweetness. It is drunk young, although it can keep for a few years.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of apricot, peach, orange peel, honey and flowers. Sweet, fresh palate with lively acidity balancing the sugar and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses, fruit tart, apricot desserts, spicy cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, small white wine glass
