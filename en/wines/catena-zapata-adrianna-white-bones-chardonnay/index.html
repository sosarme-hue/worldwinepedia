Bodega Catena Zapata

Adrianna Vineyard White Bones Chardonnay

WhiteWine region Valle de Uco

Single-plot Chardonnay from the Adrianna Vineyard in Gualtallary, made by Catena Zapata from a high-altitude plot with calcareous soils.

White Bones is one of the single-plot wines that Catena Zapata makes in the Adrianna Vineyard, in Gualtallary, in the Uco Valley. The vineyard’s altitude, with cold nights and a wide temperature range between day and night, is especially favourable for Chardonnay, which retains high acidity and great aromatic precision.

The name refers to the soil of the plot, in which, beneath a surface layer, there are whitish calcareous deposits of alluvial origin where fossil remains have been found. The wine is fermented in French oak barrels and aged on its lees, in some cases under a veil of yeast, a technique uncommon among Argentine whites that contributes saline and nutty notes. It is a Chardonnay with a taut, mineral and very fresh profile, with the capacity to develop in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with greenish hints. Aromas of lemon, pear, white flowers, nuts and saline notes. Taut palate with vibrant acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Baked white fish, shellfish, poultry with mild sauces, soft cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
