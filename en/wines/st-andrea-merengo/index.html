St. Andrea

St. Andrea Merengő

RedEgri BikavérProtected designation of origin (OEM) Eger

Egri Bikavér from the St. Andrea winery, a blend of several red varieties from Eger with Kékfrankos as its usual base, fresh and elegant in style.

Egri Bikavér, “bull’s blood of Eger”, is the best-known red of northern Hungary. According to tradition, its name goes back to the Ottoman siege of Eger in 1552, when the defenders are said to have drunk a red wine so dark that the attackers took it for bull’s blood. Today it is a designation that requires a blend of several varieties, and producers such as St. Andrea have helped to restore its prestige after decades of mass production.

Merengő is one of the winery’s Bikavér wines. It is made from a blend of red varieties grown in Eger, in which Kékfrankos (Blaufränkisch) is usually the base, together with others such as Merlot, Cabernet Franc, Pinot Noir or Kadarka; the composition and proportions vary with the vintage. The varieties are vinified separately and aged in oak barrels and casks before final blending.

The style seeks the freshness and elegance typical of Eger’s relatively cool climate, with lively acidity and fine tannins, rather than power.

Tasting profile

Medium-intensity ruby. Aromas of cherry, red and black fruit, spice and herbal notes. Medium-bodied, fresh, juicy palate with fine tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Goulash, roast duck, grilled meat, spiced charcuterie, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
