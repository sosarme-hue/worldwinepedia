Château Tronquoy

Château Tronquoy

RedAOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Saint-Estèphe

Saint-Estèphe red, formerly called Château Tronquoy-Lalande, with a blend in which Merlot and Petit Verdot play a prominent role.

The wine of Château Tronquoy was sold for decades as Château Tronquoy-Lalande; recent vintages carry the shorter name, but it is the same wine from the same Saint-Estèphe estate, owned by the proprietors of Château Montrose.

Its blend departs from the dominant Médoc model: Merlot plays a very important role, accompanied by Cabernet Sauvignon and a higher-than-usual proportion of Petit Verdot, which brings colour, spice and freshness. The proportions vary with the vintage. The gravel and clay soils give the wine the structure typical of Saint-Estèphe.

Ageing takes place in oak barrels. The result is a red with generous fruit and rounded tannins, supported by the freshness of the appellation, which is approachable fairly early and gains complexity over several years of cellaring.

Tasting profile

Deep red. Aromas of plum, blackberry, violet and spice. Fleshy palate with rounded tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Red meat, lamb, duck, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass
