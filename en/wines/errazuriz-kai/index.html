Viña Errázuriz

Kai

RedDesignation of Origin (region and subregion) Aconcagua Valley

Top-end Carmenère-based red from Viña Errázuriz, made from grapes from its vineyards in the Aconcagua Valley.

Kai is Viña Errázuriz’s statement on Carmenère, the variety that has become one of the hallmarks of Chilean wine since it was identified in the country in the 1990s. It is made from grapes from the house’s vineyards in the inland Aconcagua Valley, on the slopes near Panquehue.

Carmenère is a very late-ripening variety that needs a warm climate and a long growing season to lose the vegetal notes it shows when not fully ripe. The abundant sunshine of inland Aconcagua encourages this full ripening, while the stony hillside soils limit vigour. The grapes are hand-picked and vinified in small lots, and the wine, based on Carmenère, may include small proportions of other varieties depending on the vintage. It is aged in French oak barrels. The result is a very deeply coloured red, with ripe black fruit, spice and a characteristic touch of pepper and herbs, with a silky texture.

Tasting profile

Very deep purple. Blackberry, plum, black pepper, sweet paprika and herbs. Ample, silky palate with ripe tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Roast red meat, lamb with herbs, spiced dishes, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
