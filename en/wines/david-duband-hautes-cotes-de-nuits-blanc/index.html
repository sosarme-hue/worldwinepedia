David Duband

David Duband Hautes Côtes de Nuits Blanc

WhiteWine region Burgundy

Hautes-Côtes de Nuits Chardonnay made by Domaine David Duband, a fresh white from the hills above the Côte de Nuits.

Domaine David Duband is based in Chevannes, in the Hautes-Côtes de Nuits, the hilly sector above and behind the Côte de Nuits. The property was created by Pierre Duband and his son David took it over in the 1990s, extending its vineyards to several Côte de Nuits villages. The house is known for vinifying part of the harvest as whole bunches, with gentle extraction and barrel ageing with little new oak, aiming for aromatic, fine wines.

Although most of the house’s output is red, it also makes whites, such as this Chardonnay from the Hautes-Côtes de Nuits. The Côte de Nuits is given over almost entirely to Pinot Noir, and the area’s whites come mainly from these high hills, with limestone soils and a cooler climate that favours wines with lively acidity and clean aromas.

The wine is fermented and aged in barrel, with a discreet proportion of new oak, which adds texture without reducing freshness. At 12.5% ABV, it is a light, taut white, closer in profile to cool-climate whites than to the great whites of the Côte de Beaune, intended to be drunk in its first years.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of lemon, green apple, white flowers and a slight touch of hazelnut. Fresh and taut on the palate, with lively acidity and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Gougères, grilled fish, shellfish, goat’s cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
