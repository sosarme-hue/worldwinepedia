Bodegas Muga

Muga Rosado

RoséQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Pale-coloured rosé from Bodegas Muga, based on Grenache, fresh and meant to be drunk young.

Muga Rosado is made mainly from Grenache, together with some Viura (Macabeo) and Tempranillo. The grapes come from vineyards around Haro and are harvested with the aim of preserving freshness.

To obtain its characteristic pale colour, the must is separated from the skins after brief contact. The wine does not aim for ageing aromas but for a clean expression of fruit.

It is a delicate rosé, moving away from the deeply coloured traditional rosés of the area towards a lighter, more aromatic profile. It is best enjoyed in its first year.

Rosés have a long tradition in Rioja, where they were historically made from Grenache and known in some areas as “claretes”. Muga Rosado belongs to the current trend towards paler, more delicate rosés, designed to accompany meals rather than serve only as an aperitif.

Tasting profile

Pale pink. Aromas of strawberry, stone fruit and floral notes. Dry, light and fresh on the palate, with lively acidity.

Food pairing

Appetisers, salads, rice dishes, grilled fish, Mediterranean cooking

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
