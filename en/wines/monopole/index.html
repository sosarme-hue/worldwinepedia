CVNE (Compañía Vinícola del Norte de España)

Monopole

WhiteQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Young Viura white from CVNE, a white Rioja brand with a long history.

Monopole is one of CVNE’s historic brands and one of the oldest in white Rioja. In its most widely available current version it is a young white made from Viura, as Macabeo is called in La Rioja, from Rioja Alta vineyards.

Winemaking aims to preserve fruit and freshness: the grapes are picked at their optimum acidity and the wine ferments at controlled temperature in tank, without barrel ageing. It is bottled early to retain its aromas.

The winery has also revived longer-aged versions under the same brand, inspired by the old style of white Rioja. The young Monopole is a dry, light and versatile white, meant to be drunk in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of white fruit, apple, citrus and floral notes. Dry, light and fresh on the palate, with balanced acidity.

Food pairing

Appetisers, salads, white fish, shellfish, vegetables

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
