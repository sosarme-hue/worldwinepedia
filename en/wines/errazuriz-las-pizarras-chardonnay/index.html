Viña Errázuriz

Las Pizarras Chardonnay

WhiteDesignation of Origin (region and subregion) Aconcagua Valley

Chardonnay from Viña Errázuriz sourced from Aconcagua Costa, whose name refers to the slate soils of its vineyards near the sea.

Las Pizarras is a Viña Errázuriz line devoted to cool-climate Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, and is among the wines that have revealed the potential of Aconcagua Costa, the western strip of the Aconcagua Valley planted in the first decades of the twenty-first century a few kilometres from the Pacific Ocean.

Its name comes from the slate and schist soils of some vineyards in the area, poor and well drained, which limit the vines’ yields. Maritime influence, with morning fogs and cool breezes, prolongs ripening and preserves acidity. The grapes are hand-picked and the wine is fermented and aged in French oak barrels, with careful use of oak so as not to mask the vineyard’s expression. The result is a taut, precise Chardonnay with citrus fruit, flinty notes and a marked saline character, able to develop for several years in bottle. It is a good example of Chile’s top-end coastal whites.

Tasting profile

Bright pale yellow. Lemon, grapefruit, green apple, notes of flint and hazelnut. Taut, saline palate with lively acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Grilled fish, shellfish, ceviche, poultry in creamy sauce, soft cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
