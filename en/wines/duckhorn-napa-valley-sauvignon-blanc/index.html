Duckhorn Vineyards

Napa Valley Sauvignon Blanc

WhiteAVA Napa Valley

A fresh, aromatic Duckhorn Sauvignon Blanc, with a small proportion of Semillon and partial barrel ageing.

Although Duckhorn is known above all for its reds, its Napa Valley Sauvignon Blanc is one of its most widely available wines. It is made from grapes from different Napa Valley vineyards, from cool areas in the south of the valley as well as warmer parcels, which makes it possible to balance freshness with ripeness of fruit.

The Sauvignon Blanc is often completed with a small proportion of Semillon, following the model of white Bordeaux, which adds texture and roundness. Most of the wine is fermented in stainless steel tanks to preserve its aromas, while a portion is fermented and aged in oak barrels, mostly used, to gain complexity without marking the wine with wood. It is a white to enjoy young, which works well both on its own and at the table.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Aromas of grapefruit, melon, passion fruit and fresh grass. Lively and flavourful on the palate, with a slightly creamy texture.

Food pairing

Oysters, ceviche, grilled fish, salads, fresh goat’s cheese

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
