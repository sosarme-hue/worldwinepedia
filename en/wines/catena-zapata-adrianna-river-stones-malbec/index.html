Bodega Catena Zapata

Adrianna Vineyard River Stones Malbec

RedWine region Valle de Uco

Single-plot Malbec from the Adrianna Vineyard in Gualtallary (Tupungato), one of Catena Zapata’s top wines, from soils rich in river stones.

The Adrianna Vineyard, planted by the Catena family in Gualtallary, in the department of Tupungato, lies at high altitude in the Uco Valley, at the foot of the Andes. At the time it was one of the highest vineyards in Mendoza, and detailed study of it allowed the winery to identify plots with very different soils. Some of these give single-plot wines sold under their own names.

River Stones comes from a plot whose alluvial soil contains a large proportion of rounded stones deposited by ancient riverbeds. These poor, stony, free-draining soils limit the vigour of the vine and give small, concentrated grapes. The wine is fermented and aged in French oak barrels. It is a Malbec with deep colour and firm structure, in which the freshness of altitude balances the concentration, and it is intended for long ageing.

Tasting profile

Very deep purple. Aromas of blackberry, plum, violet, spice and mineral notes. Concentrated, taut palate with firm, fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Patagonian lamb, grilled rib-eye, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; may benefit from decanting
