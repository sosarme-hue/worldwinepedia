Achaval-Ferrer

Achaval-Ferrer Malbec

RedWine-producing province Mendoza

Mendoza Malbec from Achaval-Ferrer, made from grapes from several areas of the province. It is the entry point to the winery’s range.

Whereas Achaval-Ferrer’s single-estate wines express specific vineyards, its Mendoza Malbec aims to show the general character of the variety in the province. To this end the winery blends grapes from different origins, typically from Luján de Cuyo and the Uco Valley, which contribute ripeness and freshness respectively.

Winemaking follows the house principles: hand harvesting, grape selection and fermentation with careful control of extraction. The wine is aged for a few months in French oak barrels, with the aim that the oak should not mask the fruit. It is a direct, fruity Malbec with good acidity, enjoyable young but able to keep for a few years.

Thanks to its balance of fruit and freshness, it is a versatile wine at the table, suited to grilled meat as well as pasta with meat sauces or roasted vegetables.

Tasting profile

Violet-red. Black cherry, plum and violet, with a spicy background. Fresh, juicy palate with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Argentine asado, grilled red meat, beef empanadas, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
