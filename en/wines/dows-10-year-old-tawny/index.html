Dow’s 10 Year Old Tawny Port

Fortified10 Year Old TawnyControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Port

10-year-old tawny from Dow’s, a Symington family house known for a drier style of Port than other houses, with ripe fruit and nutty notes.

Dow’s is one of the historic Port houses belonging to the Symington family. It is traditionally credited with a somewhat drier style than other houses, the result of stopping fermentation a little later and therefore retaining less residual sugar. Its benchmark vineyards are Quinta do Bomfim, near Pinhão, and Quinta da Senhora da Ribeira, in the Douro Superior.

The 10 Year Old Tawny is a blend of wines from several harvests whose average age and character correspond to the indication on the label, approved by the regulatory body. It is made from traditional Douro varieties such as Touriga Nacional, Touriga Franca and Tinta Roriz (Tempranillo), among others. After fermentation, stopped with grape spirit, the wines age in oak casks, where slow contact with oxygen softens the tannins, lightens the colour and develops notes of nuts and spice.

It is bottled filtered and ready to drink, and once opened it keeps well for a few weeks.

Tasting profile

Tawny with amber glints. Aromas of ripe and dried red fruit, almond, walnut and a touch of spice and wood. Balanced palate of restrained sweetness, with a clean, relatively dry finish.

Food pairing

Nuts, almond tart, crème brûlée, aged cheeses, foie gras

Serving

12-14 °C, lightly chilled, Port or white wine glass
