Prunotto

Prunotto Barolo

RedBarolo DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Barolo

Prunotto’s core Barolo, a Nebbiolo combining grapes from several municipalities of the appellation in a balanced, classic style.

Prunotto’s Barolo is made from Nebbiolo from vineyards in different municipalities of the appellation, following the tradition of blending grapes from complementary areas. Alongside it, the winery produces Barolo Bussia, from its vineyard in the cru of the same name in Monforte d’Alba.

After fermentation with maceration, the wine is aged in oak for an extended period and then rests in bottle before release, as the appellation requires.

It is a balanced, classically styled Barolo in which Nebbiolo’s floral and red-fruit aromas combine with spicy notes and a firm tannic structure. It can be enjoyed a few years after the vintage and evolves well in bottle.

Tasting profile

Medium-depth garnet. Aromas of rose, cherry, raspberry and spice, with notes of liquorice. Dry and firm on the palate, with present tannins, good acidity and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Braised red meat, agnolotti del plin, game, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
