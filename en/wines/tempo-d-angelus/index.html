Château Angélus

Tempo d’Angélus

RedBordeauxWine region (AOC Bordeaux and Bordeaux Supérieur) Bordeaux

AOC Bordeaux red from the family that owns Château Angélus, Merlot-dominated and fruity, supple in style and meant to be drunk young.

The de Boüard de Laforest family, owners of Château Angélus in Saint-Émilion, complements the grand vin and its second wine, Carillon d’Angélus, with Tempo d’Angélus, a more accessible label under the generic Bordeaux appellation. As its name suggests, it shares the world of the house, whose name refers to the Angelus bells that could be heard from the vineyards.

Being an AOC Bordeaux, the grapes may come from different parts of the region, outside the Saint-Émilion estate itself. The wine is based on Merlot, the dominant variety on the Right Bank, and is made with fruit, softness and balance in mind, with moderate ageing.

The result is a medium-bodied red with aromas of plum, cherry and gentle spicy notes, rounded tannins and freshness. It is a wine to enjoy in its first few years, offering a simple introduction to the house style.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby red. Aromas of plum, cherry, blackberry and gentle spice. Round, fresh palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled meat, duck, charcuterie, pasta with meat sauce, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, medium-sized glass
