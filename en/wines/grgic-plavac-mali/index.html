Grgić Vina

Plavac Mali

RedWine region Dalmatia

A Plavac Mali red from the Pelješac peninsula made by Grgić Vina, the Croatian winery of Mike Grgich.

This is the flagship red of Grgić Vina, the winery founded in 1996 by Miljenko “Mike” Grgich in Trstenik, on the Pelješac peninsula. It is made from Plavac Mali, Dalmatia’s main red variety, from vineyards on the peninsula grown on stony limestone slopes facing the sea, where intense sunlight encourages great ripeness.

The winemaking combines Dalmatian tradition with the experience Grgich gained in California: grape selection, controlled fermentation and ageing in oak barrels, which polishes the variety’s naturally firm tannins. The result is a full-bodied, warm and spicy red with the characteristic power of coastal Plavac Mali and a structure that allows it to develop in bottle for some years. It is one of the wines that have done most to make this variety known outside Croatia, particularly in the United States.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackberry, ripe plum, dried fig, pepper and Mediterranean herbs. Warm, powerful palate with firm tannins.

Food pairing

Lamb, grilled meats, pašticada, aged sheep’s cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
