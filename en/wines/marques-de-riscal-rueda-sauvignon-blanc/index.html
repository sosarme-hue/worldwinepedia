Herederos del Marqués de Riscal

Marqués de Riscal Sauvignon Blanc

WhiteDesignation of Origin (DO) Rueda

Sauvignon Blanc from the Rueda Denominación de Origen made by Marqués de Riscal, one of the first wineries to grow this variety in the area.

Marqués de Riscal, the historic Rioja winery from Elciego, began making white wines in Rueda in the early 1970s, convinced that the Castilian plateau offered better conditions for whites than Rioja. Its arrival helped to modernise the area, which until then had mainly produced fortified-style wines, and the winery was one of the first to plant Sauvignon Blanc there.

This wine is made from Sauvignon Blanc from vineyards in the Rueda Denominación de Origen, on stony, sandy soils, with a continental climate of hot summers and cool nights that help to preserve acidity and aroma. The grapes are taken quickly to the winery to preserve their freshness and ferment in tank at low temperature, without oak.

The result is a young, intensely aromatic and fresh white, with the herbaceous and fruity character typical of the variety, designed to be drunk in the year after the harvest.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Intense aromas of tropical fruit, citrus, freshly cut grass and boxwood. Fresh and lively on the palate, with marked acidity.

Food pairing

Aperitifs, salads, grilled fish, vegetables, mild Asian cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
