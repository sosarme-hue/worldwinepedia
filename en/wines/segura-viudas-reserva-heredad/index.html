Segura Viudas

Segura Viudas Reserva Heredad

SparklingReserva BrutDesignation of Origin (DO) Cava

Top-of-the-range Cava from Segura Viudas, presented in a bottle decorated with metal pieces, made from a selection of estate grapes with extended ageing.

Reserva Heredad is Segura Viudas' most representative Cava and takes its name from the Heredad, the old estate in Torrelavit where the winery is based. It is easily recognised by its dark bottle adorned with a crest and a metal base, a presentation intended for special occasions.

It is made mainly from Macabeo and Parellada, from a selection of Alt Penedès grapes; the proportions may vary with the vintage. After the second fermentation in bottle by the traditional method, it rests on its lees for longer than the house's young Cavas, which gives it greater complexity and a more integrated mousse. Its dosage is Brut. Macabeo provides body and ripe fruit notes, while Parellada brings finesse and freshness. It is an elegant, somewhat creamier Cava, suitable both as an aperitif and to accompany a full meal.

Tasting profile

Pale golden yellow with fine bubbles. Aromas of ripe white fruit, flowers, nuts and toasty ageing notes. Creamy, balanced and persistent on the palate.

Food pairing

Shellfish, fish in sauce, poultry, rice dishes, semi-cured cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
