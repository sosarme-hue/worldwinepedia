Cortes de Cima

Cortes de Cima Palhete 1 Litro

RoséControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) and Alentejano GI Alentejo

Cortes de Cima Palhete 1 Litro is a rosé wine from Cortes de Cima, made under the Vinho Regional Alentejo appellation in Portugal.

It is aged in clay vessels such as amphorae. It has 10.5% ABV.

The Alentejo, the great warm plain of southern Portugal, produces mainly ripe, fruity reds and broad whites, and keeps alive the ancient tradition of fermenting wine in clay amphorae.

Food pairing

Aperitif, salads, rice dishes, fish

8-10 °C, white wine glass
