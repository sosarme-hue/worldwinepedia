Château Guiraud

Château Guiraud

Sweet1er Grand Cru ClasséAOC (sweet wine) Sauternes

First-growth Sauternes from Château Guiraud, a sweet wine from botrytised Sémillon with Sauvignon Blanc, rich and long-lived.

Château Guiraud is the main wine of this estate in the village of Sauternes, classified as a first growth in 1855 and known for its certified organic farming.

The sweet wines of Sauternes and Barsac are made from grapes affected by Botrytis cinerea, the noble rot which, encouraged by morning mists from the Ciron and the Garonne, dehydrates the berries and concentrates sugars, acidity and aromas. Harvesting is done by hand, in several passes through the vineyard.

It is made mainly from Sémillon, which brings richness and texture, completed by Sauvignon Blanc, which adds freshness and aroma. The must ferments and ages in oak barrels for an extended period. It is a sweet, unctuous and concentrated wine, with acidity that balances the sugar. Young, it offers exotic fruit and candied citrus; with time it evolves towards notes of honey, saffron and nuts, and it can be kept for decades.

Tasting profile

Deep gold. Aromas of apricot, mango, candied orange peel, honey and vanilla. Rich, unctuous palate balanced by acidity, with a very long finish.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses, fruit desserts, spiced Asian cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, small white wine glass
