Condrieu Les Cassines

WhiteAOC (Northern Rhône cru) Condrieu

Condrieu from Paul Jaboulet Aîné, a northern Rhône Viognier, aromatic and unctuous, from the appellation that is the variety’s birthplace.

Condrieu is an appellation on the right bank of the Rhône, south of Côte-Rôtie, devoted exclusively to white wines from Viognier. Its vineyards occupy very steep granite slopes, often terraced, and for much of the twentieth century the variety came close to disappearing before regaining its prestige.

Paul Jaboulet Aîné, a Rhône house founded in 1834, makes Les Cassines from grapes grown in this appellation. Vinification aims to preserve the aromatic intensity typical of Viognier, and part of the wine may be aged in oak to gain texture without losing fruit expression.

Condrieu wines are highly perfumed whites, with aromas of apricot, peach and flowers and an unctuous palate with moderate acidity. They are best enjoyed young, when the fruit is at its peak, although the finest keep their interest for a few years.

Tasting profile

Pale gold. Intense aromas of apricot, peach, violet and acacia blossom. Broad, unctuous palate with an aromatic finish.

Food pairing

Spiced Asian cuisine, shellfish, poultry in sauce, foie gras, goat’s cheese

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
