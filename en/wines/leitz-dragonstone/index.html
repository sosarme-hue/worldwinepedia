Weingut Leitz

Leitz Dragonstone Riesling

WhiteAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Rheingau

Riesling from Weingut Leitz from the Drachenstein vineyard in Rüdesheim, with light sweetness balanced by acidity.

Dragonstone takes its name from the Drachenstein vineyard, “dragon stone”, in the upper part of the slopes of Rüdesheim, in the Rheingau. It is one of Weingut Leitz’s most widely distributed wines and, together with Eins-Zwei-Dry, one of those that have made the estate known in international markets.

It is made exclusively from Riesling. The vines lie in a relatively high and cool part of Rüdesheim. Fermentation, at low temperature in stainless-steel tanks, is stopped leaving moderate residual sweetness, balanced by the lively acidity of the variety, so that the wine is fruity rather than cloying.

It is a low-alcohol Riesling, aromatic and refreshing, very versatile with spiced cuisines. It is designed to be drunk young, though it can keep for a few years in good condition.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of peach, apricot, apple and citrus. Light palate with moderate sweetness and refreshing acidity.

Food pairing

Thai and Indian cuisine, sushi, sweet-and-sour pork, mild cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
