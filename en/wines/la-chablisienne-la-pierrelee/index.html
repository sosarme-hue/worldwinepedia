La Chablisienne

La Chablisienne Chablis La Pierrelée

WhiteAOC (Petit Chablis, Chablis, Chablis Premier Cru and Chablis Grand Cru) Chablis

Chablis from the La Chablisienne cooperative, a blend of members’ plots from different parts of the appellation, fresh and mineral in style.

La Pierrelée is one of La Chablisienne’s most widely distributed Chablis. Its name evokes the stony character of the local soils. It is made from grapes from the plots of many members spread across the Chablis appellation, which makes it possible to compose a wine representative of the general style of village Chablis.

It is made solely from Chardonnay. The different lots are vinified separately in tank, and the wine is aged on fine lees for several months, which adds some texture without reducing its freshness. The cooperative does not use new oak for this wine, in order to keep the direct expression of fruit and the minerality of the limestone soils.

It is a dry Chablis, light to medium in body, with citrus and floral aromas and lively acidity. It is meant to be drunk in the first years after the harvest and is a common choice in restaurants and shops thanks to its consistency.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green hints. Aromas of lemon, apple, white flowers and a mineral touch. Dry, fresh, light palate with lively acidity and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Oysters, shellfish, grilled fish, sushi, goat’s cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
