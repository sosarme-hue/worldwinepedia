Masciarelli Montepulciano d'Abruzzo

RedMontepulciano d’Abruzzo DOCWine region Abruzzo

The Montepulciano d’Abruzzo from Masciarelli’s Classica line, a fruity, deeply coloured red with soft tannins that is easy to find.

Montepulciano is the main red grape of Abruzzo, on the Adriatic coast of central Italy. It should not be confused with Tuscany’s Vino Nobile di Montepulciano, which takes its name from a town and is made from Sangiovese. The variety gives deeply coloured wines with dark fruit and plentiful but soft tannins.

The Montepulciano d’Abruzzo from the Classica range is Masciarelli’s most widely available red. It is made from grapes from the winery’s vineyards in different parts of the region, between the Maiella massif and the sea. Winemaking aims to preserve the fruit, with brief ageing.

It is a medium-bodied, juicy, versatile red that conveys the generous character of the variety well. It is best enjoyed in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Dark ruby with purple glints. Aromas of black cherry, plum, blackberry and a touch of liquorice and spice. Juicy and round on the palate, with soft tannins and good freshness.

Food pairing

Arrosticini, pasta with ragù, pizza, grilled meat, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, medium-sized glass
