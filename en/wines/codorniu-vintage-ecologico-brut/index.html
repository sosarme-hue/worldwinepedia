Codorníu

Codorníu Cava Vintage Ecológico Brut

SparklingBrutDesignation of Origin (DO) Cava

Vintage brut Cava from Codorníu, made from organically grown grapes using the traditional method.

Codorníu, in Sant Sadurní d’Anoia, is regarded as one of the pioneering Cava houses: in the 1870s Josep Raventós made the first traditional-method sparkling wines there from Penedès varieties. Its modernist cellars, designed by Josep Puig i Cadafalch, contain kilometres of underground galleries where the bottles rest.

This Cava comes from organically farmed vineyards, that is, without synthetic herbicides, pesticides or fertilisers, and carries a vintage date, so all the grapes come from a single harvest. Like all Cava, it is made by the traditional method, with a second fermentation in bottle and ageing on the lees before disgorgement.

The brut dosage gives it a dry, fresh profile. It is a finely sparkling wine with white fruit and notes from lees ageing, suitable both as an aperitif and with a light meal.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow, fine bubbles. Aromas of apple, citrus, white flowers and a background of toast. Dry, fresh and light palate.

Food pairing

Aperitif, shellfish, fried fish, rice dishes, tapas

Serving

8-10 °C, tulip glass
