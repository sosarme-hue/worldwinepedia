Château Valandraud

Château Valandraud

RedAOC Saint-Émilion 1er Grand Cru Classé

A great Saint-Émilion created in the early 1990s by Jean-Luc Thunevin and Murielle Andraud, emblem of the "garage wines" and a Premier Grand Cru Classé since 2012.

Château Valandraud was born in the early 1990s as a tiny project of Jean-Luc Thunevin and Murielle Andraud, who made their first vintages in a small building in the town of Saint-Émilion. Their approach, with very low yields, very ripe harvests and intensive ageing in new oak, made Valandraud the symbol of the so-called "garage wines", a movement that shook up the Bordeaux scene in that decade.

Over time the estate brought together higher-quality vineyards, particularly on the limestone slopes of Saint-Étienne-de-Lisse, in the east of the appellation. Merlot dominates the blend, with Cabernet Franc and small proportions of other Bordeaux varieties.

In the 2012 classification Valandraud was promoted to Premier Grand Cru Classé, the culmination of an ascent unusual in the region. The wine, aged in French oak barrels, is a concentrated, deep red with ripe fruit and dense texture, which over the years has gained freshness and precision and needs time in bottle.

Tasting profile

Very dark cherry red. Aromas of blackberry, plum, cocoa, liquorice and toasted oak. Concentrated, enveloping palate with dense tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Grilled beef, large game, duck, stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large Bordeaux glass; decanting recommended
