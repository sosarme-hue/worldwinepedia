Les Vins Georges Duboeuf

Georges Duboeuf Beaujolais Nouveau

RedPrimeur wine (nouveau)Wine region (AOC Beaujolais, Beaujolais-Villages and ten crus) Beaujolais

Georges Duboeuf’s Beaujolais Nouveau, the new-vintage wine released on the third Thursday of November, just weeks after the harvest.

Beaujolais Nouveau is a primeur wine: it is made and put on sale very shortly after the harvest, and regulations set its release on the third Thursday of November. Georges Duboeuf was one of the great promoters of this tradition outside France, and his Nouveau, with a label that changes every year, is one of the most widely available.

It is made from Gamay, generally by carbonic or semi-carbonic maceration: whole bunches are placed in the tank and part of the fermentation takes place inside the berries, producing brightly coloured, very fruity wines with little tannin.

It is a light, cheerful red, designed to be drunk in the months following its release, slightly chilled and without needing a special occasion.

Tasting profile

Bright purple. Intense aromas of strawberry, raspberry, cherry and banana, typical of carbonic maceration. Light and juicy on the palate, with very soft tannins.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, pâté, young cheeses, Lyonnaise cooking, tapas

Serving

12-14 °C, medium red wine glass
