Osborne

Amontillado Coquinero

FortifiedAmontilladoDesignation of Origin (DO) Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

Dry amontillado from Osborne, with biological and oxidative ageing in El Puerto de Santa María.

Coquinero is Osborne's classic amontillado and one of the most traditional dry sherries of the El Puerto de Santa María house. Its style reflects the dual ageing that defines amontillados, halfway between fino and oloroso.

It is made from Palomino from albariza vineyards. The wine begins its ageing as a fino, under flor, in Osborne's bodegas in El Puerto, where the sea's humidity favours the life of the yeast. After several years, when the flor disappears, the wine is fortified and continues ageing oxidatively in the criaderas and solera system. This second stage gives it its amber colour, more body and aromas of nuts and wood, while it keeps the pungent character and dryness inherited from its biological phase. It is a dry, complex wine that keeps well for several weeks after opening and pairs successfully with many dishes.

Tasting profile

Amber. Aromas of hazelnut, toasted almond, wood and pungent notes. Dry, savoury and long on the palate.

Food pairing

Consommés, artichokes, mushrooms, white meats, aged cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, white wine glass
