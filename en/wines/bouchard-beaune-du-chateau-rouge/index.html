Bouchard Père & Fils

Bouchard Père & Fils Beaune du Château 1er Cru Rouge

RedPremier CruBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Beaune

Red Beaune premier cru from Bouchard Père & Fils, a blend of several of the house’s premier cru plots in Beaune, named after its historic headquarters.

Beaune du Château is a wine that Bouchard Père & Fils has made for decades, named after the Château de Beaune, the fortress that houses the company’s cellars. Unlike single-plot premiers crus, it is a blend of several Beaune premiers crus owned by the house, which makes it possible to combine the structure of some vineyards with the finesse of others. It exists in both red and white versions.

The red is made from Pinot Noir picked by hand in the various plots, which are vinified separately and aged in French oak barrels before final blending. The composition varies with the vintage.

It is a Beaune premier cru with a balanced, approachable profile, ripe red fruit, spicy notes and soft tannins. It can be enjoyed relatively young and improves with a few years of cellaring.

Tasting profile

Medium-intensity ruby. Aromas of ripe cherry, redcurrant and sweet spice over an earthy background. Round, fresh palate with soft tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Roast duck, veal, boeuf bourguignon, mushrooms, cheeses such as Époisses

Serving

15-17 °C, large Burgundy glass
