Casanova di Neri

Casanova di Neri Brunello di Montalcino Tenuta Nuova

RedBrunello di Montalcino DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Brunello di Montalcino

Casanova di Neri Brunello from the Tenuta Nuova estate, in the southern part of the Montalcino territory, one of the house’s best-known wines.

Tenuta Nuova is an estate acquired by the Neri family in the southern part of Montalcino, in an area warmer than that of its original base on the eastern side. Its vineyards give a Brunello of ripe fruit and generous structure, which has become one of Casanova di Neri’s most widely distributed wines.

It is made solely from Sangiovese, as the appellation requires. The house vinifies the plots separately and ages the wine in oak, with a more modern approach than traditional producers, including the use of medium-sized casks. After ageing the wine rests in bottle and is not released before the fifth year after the harvest.

It is a concentrated, expressive Brunello that may be somewhat more approachable in its youth than others from the area, though it has good ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby-garnet. Aromas of black cherry, plum, wild berries, spice and toasty notes. Broad, concentrated palate with dense tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast red meat, game, pappardelle with wild boar, mushrooms, aged pecorino

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
