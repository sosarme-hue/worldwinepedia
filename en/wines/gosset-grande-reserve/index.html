Champagne Gosset

Gosset Grande Réserve Brut

SparklingBrutAOC (sparkling wine) Champagne

Gosset’s non-vintage brut champagne, made without malolactic fermentation and with long lees ageing, vinous and fresh in style.

Grande Réserve is Gosset’s non-vintage brut and the wine that best represents the house style. It is made from Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and a small share of Pinot Meunier, largely from grand cru and premier cru villages. The proportions vary from blend to blend, which includes wines from several harvests.

Like most of the house’s wines, it is generally made without malolactic fermentation, which preserves lively acidity. To balance it, the champagne stays on its lees for a long period, several years, before disgorgement. It is presented in Gosset’s characteristic antique-shaped bottle.

It is a medium- to full-bodied champagne, with aromas of ripe fruit, citrus, nuts and brioche, and a palate that is both fresh and vinous. Its structure makes it particularly suitable with meals, although it also works as an aperitif.

Tasting profile

Gold with fine bubbles. Aromas of ripe fruit, quince, lemon, hazelnut and brioche. Vinous, fresh palate with lively acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, poultry, veal, aged cheeses such as Comté or Parmesan

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass rather than a flute
