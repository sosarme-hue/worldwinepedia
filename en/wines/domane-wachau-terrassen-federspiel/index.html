Domäne Wachau

Grüner Veltliner Federspiel Terrassen

WhiteFederspiel (Vinea Wachau)DAC (Districtus Austriae Controllatus) Wachau

Dry Federspiel-category Grüner Veltliner made by Domäne Wachau from terraced vineyards across the region, fresh and medium-bodied.

This wine is made from Grüner Veltliner grown on the dry-stone terraces farmed by the cooperative’s members along the Wachau, hence its name. The vines grow on primary-rock and loess soils, in a climate where the warmth of the Pannonian plain alternates with cool nights arriving from the Waldviertel.

It belongs to Vinea Wachau’s Federspiel category, which covers medium-bodied dry wines with an alcohol content between 11.5% and 12.5%. Winemaking aims to preserve the fruit and freshness of Grüner Veltliner, with its characteristic white-pepper note. It is a straightforward, balanced wine that offers a clear introduction to the character of the Wachau.

It is a wine designed to be enjoyed young, while it keeps all its freshness, and it is very versatile at the table, with simple dishes as well as vegetable and fish cookery.

Tasting profile

Aromas of green apple, pear, citrus and white pepper. Dry, fresh and medium-bodied on the palate, with lively acidity and a lightly spicy finish.

Food pairing

Wiener Schnitzel, vegetables, asparagus, freshwater fish, Asian food

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
