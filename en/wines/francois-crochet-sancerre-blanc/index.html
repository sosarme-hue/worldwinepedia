François Crochet

François Crochet Sancerre Blanc

WhiteAOC Sancerre

François Crochet Sancerre Blanc is a white wine from François Crochet, made under the AOC Sancerre appellation in France.

It is made from Sauvignon Blanc. It is aged in wood. It has 13% ABV.

Sancerre, on the left bank of the Loire in the Cher département, is one of the benchmark appellations for Sauvignon Blanc. It also produces reds and rosés from Pinot Noir.

Food pairing

Fish, shellfish, rice dishes, fresh cheeses

Serving

8-11 °C, white wine glass
