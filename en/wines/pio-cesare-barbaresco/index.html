Pio Cesare

Pio Cesare Barbaresco

RedBarbaresco DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Barbaresco

Pio Cesare’s Barbaresco, a Nebbiolo from the Barbaresco area made in the classic style of the Alba house.

Alongside its Barolo, Pio Cesare has for generations made a Barbaresco from Nebbiolo grown in the Barbaresco area, north-east of Alba. The family owns vineyards there, which it supplements with grapes from local growers.

Barbaresco is a smaller appellation than Barolo, and its wines are often described as somewhat finer and approachable at a younger age, although they share the same variety and a great capacity for ageing. The regulations require a slightly shorter minimum ageing period than for Barolo.

After fermentation the wine is aged in oak casks and then in bottle. The result is an aromatic, elegant Nebbiolo with firm yet polished tannins that gains complexity over the years.

Tasting profile

Medium-depth garnet. Aromas of red berries, rose, violet and sweet spice, with a liquorice background. Elegant and firm on the palate, with good acidity and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Truffle risotto, roast red meat, game birds, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
