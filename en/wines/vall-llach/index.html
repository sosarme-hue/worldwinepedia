Celler Vall Llach

Vall Llach

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Priorat

The flagship red of Celler Vall Llach, from the oldest Cariñena vines in Porrera.

The wine that bears the winery's name is the most ambitious expression of Celler Vall Llach and one of the benchmarks for Cariñena in the Priorat. It comes from very old vines in the municipality of Porrera, some of them recovered by the winery when they were on the point of being abandoned.

Cariñena is the leading variety, accompanied by Garnacha and, depending on the vintage, small amounts of other varieties; the proportions change from year to year. The vines grow on very steep slopes of llicorella, in a valley somewhat cooler than other parts of the area, which helps preserve acidity. Yields are very low and harvesting is done by hand. The wine is aged in French oak barrels for an extended period. It is a deeply coloured, highly concentrated red with black fruit, mineral and balsamic notes, and an acidity that gives it longevity.

Tasting profile

Almost opaque deep cherry red. Aromas of blackberry, black plum, liquorice, balsamic herbs and graphite. Powerful and taut on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, game, grilled meats, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
