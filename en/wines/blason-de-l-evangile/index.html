Château L'Évangile

Blason de l'Évangile

RedSecond wineAOC Pomerol

The second wine of Château L’Évangile, a Pomerol of Merlot and Cabernet Franc that is more approachable in youth.

Blason de l’Évangile is the second wine of Château L’Évangile, a Pomerol estate located between Pétrus and Cheval Blanc and owned by Domaines Barons de Rothschild (Lafite). It is made from estate grapes not selected for the grand vin, often from younger vines or from plots with a fruitier character.

Like the main wine, it is based on Merlot with a share of Cabernet Franc, and comes from the property’s clay, gravel and sand soils. It is vinified with the same care and aged in oak barrels, though with a lower proportion of new wood and for a somewhat shorter time.

The result is a Pomerol of generous fruit, soft texture and rounded tannins, which keeps the house’s rich character but can be enjoyed well before the grand vin, while retaining some ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Deep red. Aromas of plum, black cherry, spice and a touch of cocoa. Soft, fruity palate with rounded tannins.

Food pairing

Red meat, duck, roast poultry, mushrooms, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
