El Enemigo

El Enemigo Bonarda

RedWine-producing province Mendoza

Mendoza Bonarda from El Enemigo, one of the wines that helped restore the reputation of this variety, Argentina’s second most planted red.

Argentine Bonarda, identified in the country with the variety known in France as Douce Noir or Corbeau, was long a grape destined for simple table wines, despite being one of the most planted in the country. El Enemigo was one of the projects that showed it could also produce quality reds, and its Bonarda is one of the house’s best-known wines.

The grapes come mainly from old vineyards in eastern Mendoza, a warmer, lower-lying area, especially around the town of Junín, and the wine usually includes a small proportion of Cabernet Franc, in amounts that vary with the vintage. It is aged in French oak. The result is a deeply coloured red with juicy fruit, soft tannins and a freshness that makes it very enjoyable at the table, with good ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Intense purple. Aromas of black cherry, plum, raspberry, pepper and herbs. Juicy palate with soft tannins and good acidity.

Food pairing

Pasta with ragù, cured meats, roast meats, pizza

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
