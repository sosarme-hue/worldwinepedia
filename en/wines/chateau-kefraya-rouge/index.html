Château Kefraya

Château Kefraya Rouge

RedWine region Bekaa Valley

The grand vin red of Château Kefraya in the western Bekaa, a barrel-aged blend of Bordeaux and Mediterranean varieties.

The red that bears the estate's name, Château Kefraya, is the winery's benchmark wine, alongside its Comte de M selection. It comes from the vineyards the de Bustros family planted in the village of Kefraya, in the western part of the Bekaa Valley, on the slopes rising towards Mount Barouk.

It is made from a blend of Bordeaux and Mediterranean varieties, with Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah usually playing the leading roles, sometimes joined by others such as Mourvèdre or Grenache; the proportions vary with the vintage. The altitude of the vineyards brings cool nights that preserve acidity, while dry summers allow full ripening. The wine is aged for an extended period in French oak barrels. The result is a structured, spicy red with ripe fruit and good ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Aromas of blackberry, plum and cassis, with notes of spice, cedar, tobacco and dried herbs. Broad and structured, with firm tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, kibbeh, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
