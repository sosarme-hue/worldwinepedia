Alambre Moscatel de Setúbal

SweetMoscatel de Setúbal (DOC)Setúbal DOC, Palmela DOC and Península de Setúbal GI Setúbal

Moscatel de Setúbal from José Maria da Fonseca, a sweet fortified Muscat wine with long skin maceration and cask ageing.

Alambre is the Moscatel de Setúbal brand of José Maria da Fonseca, a house closely linked to the history of this wine since the nineteenth century. It is made from Muscat of Alexandria grown on the Setúbal peninsula, at the foot of the Arrábida hills.

In keeping with the appellation’s tradition, fermentation is stopped with grape spirit, and the wine then stays in contact with the grape skins for several months. This long maceration extracts Muscat’s intense aromas and gives the wine its character. It is then aged in oak casks in the house’s cellars in Azeitão, where slow oxidation adds notes of nuts, honey and caramel. The house also makes older versions under the same brand. It is a sweet, intense wine with good acidity, enjoyed in small measures with dessert or after a meal.

Tasting profile

Golden amber colour. Intense aromas of raisin, orange blossom, candied orange peel, honey and nuts. Sweet and unctuous on the palate, with acidity that adds freshness.

Food pairing

Egg-based sweets, nut tarts, blue cheeses, foie gras

Serving

10-12 °C, small dessert wine glass
