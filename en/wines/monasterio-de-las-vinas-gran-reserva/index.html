Grandes Vinos y Viñedos

Monasterio de las Viñas Gran Reserva

RedGran ReservaDesignation of Origin (DO) Cariñena

Long-aged red from the Cariñena D.O. made by Grandes Vinos y Viñedos from Garnacha, Tempranillo and Cariñena, in the traditional style.

Monasterio de las Viñas is one of the historic brands of Grandes Vinos y Viñedos and the one that best represents the traditional ageing style of the Cariñena D.O. Its Gran Reserva version spends the longest time in oak and bottle of the range and is usually found at a modest price for its category.

It is made from the area's traditional varieties: Garnacha, which brings ripe fruit and flesh; Tempranillo, which adds colour and structure; and Cariñena, the grape that bears the name of the area, which contributes acidity and freshness. The proportions vary with the vintage. The Gran Reserva category requires long ageing, with at least eighteen months in oak barrels and an additional period in bottle before release. This ageing gives the wine evolved notes —leather, tobacco, spice— over a background of ripe fruit, and tannins softened by time.

Tasting profile

Garnet red with a brick rim. Aromas of ripe fruit, leather, tobacco, vanilla and spice. Smooth and round on the palate, with integrated tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Roasts, braised meats, small game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
