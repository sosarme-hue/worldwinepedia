Clos Mogador

Manyetes

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Priorat

Red from Clos Mogador centred on Cariñena, from a plot of the same name in Gratallops.

Manyetes is the single-plot wine of Clos Mogador, the René Barbier family winery in Gratallops. It takes its name from the vineyard it comes from and has become one of the best-known expressions of Cariñena in the Priorat, a variety long regarded as rustic and now prized for its freshness and its ability to convey the soil.

The vineyard lies on llicorella and clay, with Cariñena vines of some age, which may be joined by a small share of other local varieties depending on the vintage. Late-ripening and high in acidity, Cariñena finds in the warm, dry climate of the Priorat the conditions to ripen fully without losing tension. Winemaking seeks to respect the fruit, with oak ageing that accompanies rather than dominates. It is a deeply coloured red, with black fruit, herbal notes and a marked mineral character.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red. Aromas of blackberry, plum, wild herbs, liquorice and graphite notes. Taut and fresh on the palate, with firm tannins and a mineral finish.

Food pairing

Game, lamb, braised meats, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
