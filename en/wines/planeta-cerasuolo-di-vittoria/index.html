Planeta Cerasuolo di Vittoria

RedCerasuolo di Vittoria DOCGWine region Sicily

A red from south-eastern Sicily blending Nero d’Avola and Frappato, a fruity, fresh wine from the island’s only DOCG.

Cerasuolo di Vittoria is produced in south-eastern Sicily, around the town of Vittoria in the province of Ragusa, and is the island’s only DOCG. Its name refers to the wine’s cherry colour (“cerasa”). The regulations require a blend of two native varieties: Nero d’Avola, which brings colour, body and dark fruit, and Frappato, which brings floral aromas, red fruit and freshness.

Planeta makes this wine at its Dorilli winery, in the Vittoria area, from vineyards on the reddish sandy soils typical of the area. Winemaking aims to preserve the grapes’ fruity character, without lengthy ageing.

The result is a medium-bodied, aromatic, easy-drinking red, less powerful than many varietal Nero d’Avolas. It is enjoyed young and can be served slightly chilled.

Tasting profile

Bright, medium-depth ruby. Aromas of cherry, strawberry, pomegranate and flowers, with a spicy touch. Fresh and juicy on the palate, with soft tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Pasta with tomato, pizza, grilled tuna, fish couscous, cured meats

Serving

14-16 °C, medium-sized glass
