Rodero

Carmelo Rodero TSM

RedReservaDesignation of Origin (DO) Ribera del Duero

Top-of-the-range red from Bodegas Rodero in Pedrosa de Duero, made from a selection of its vineyards and given long barrel ageing.

Bodegas Rodero is a family winery in Pedrosa de Duero, in the Burgos part of Ribera del Duero. Carmelo Rodero’s family had been growers for generations, selling their grapes to other wineries, until in the early 1990s they decided to make and bottle their own wines. The winery works only with its own vineyards around the village, on clay and limestone soils at around 800 metres above sea level, with cold winters, hot summers and wide differences between day and night temperatures.

TSM sits at the top of the winery’s range. It comes from a selection of grapes from its best plots, with Tinto Fino as the base. It carries the Reserva designation, which means at least three years of ageing between barrel and bottle, with a minimum of twelve months in oak.

It is a red conceived for cellaring, with more concentration and structure than the rest of the range, at 14% ABV. Barrel ageing brings toasty, spicy notes that integrate over time in bottle. When drunk young, it benefits from lengthy decanting.

Tasting profile

Opaque deep cherry red. Aromas of ripe black fruit, spice, liquorice, toast and mineral notes. Concentrated and powerful on the palate, with firm but ripe tannins and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled red meat, large game, roast lamb, well-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
