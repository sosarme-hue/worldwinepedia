Villa Maria

Villa Maria Reserve Wairau Valley Sauvignon Blanc

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Marlborough

Sauvignon Blanc from Villa Maria’s Reserve range, sourced from selected vineyards in Marlborough’s Wairau valley.

Within Villa Maria’s range, the Reserve wines come from a stricter selection of vineyards and aim to express the character of a specific area more precisely. This Sauvignon Blanc comes from the Wairau valley, the historic heart of Marlborough’s vineyards, whose stony alluvial soils and sunny, dry climate give ripe, highly aromatic grapes.

The plots are harvested and vinified separately, allowing the team to assemble the final blend from lots with different profiles. Most of the wine is fermented in stainless steel at low temperature, although in some vintages a small portion may be fermented in barrel or with indigenous yeasts to add texture. Compared with the Private Bin, it is a wine of greater concentration and length, retaining the freshness and aromatic intensity typical of the region, and it can be kept for a few years.

Tasting profile

Passion fruit, guava, pink grapefruit and lime, with herbal notes and a mineral background. Concentrated palate with firm acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, white fish, asparagus, fresh goat’s cheese

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
