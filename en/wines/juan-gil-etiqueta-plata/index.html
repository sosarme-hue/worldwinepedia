Bodegas Juan Gil

Juan Gil Etiqueta Plata

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Jumilla

Barrel-aged Monastrell from Bodegas Juan Gil, the house's best-known wine and a benchmark for the Jumilla D.O.

Juan Gil Etiqueta Plata —known on international markets as Silver Label— is Bodegas Juan Gil's most widely distributed red. It is a single-variety Monastrell, Jumilla's traditional grape, from old bush vines on the limestone, stony soils of the high plateau.

Late-ripening Monastrell copes well with the area's heat and drought and gives deeply coloured wines with ripe black fruit and plenty of tannin. In this wine the grapes are vinified for concentration and then aged for around a year in oak barrels, which round out the tannins and add spicy, toasty notes. It is a powerful, generous red that has helped shape Jumilla's modern image abroad.

Tasting profile

Blackberry, ripe black plum, liquorice, spice and toasty oak. Fleshy, full-bodied palate with ripe tannins and a warm finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled meats, lamb, stews, rice with rabbit, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
