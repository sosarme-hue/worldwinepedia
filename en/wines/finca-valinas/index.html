Mar de Frades

Mar de Frades Finca Valiñas

WhiteDesignation of Origin (DO) Rías Baixas

Single-vineyard Albariño from Mar de Frades, from Finca Valiñas in the Val do Salnés, with extended ageing on lees.

Finca Valiñas is Mar de Frades’s most ambitious wine and comes from the vineyard of the same name, located next to the winery in the Val do Salnés, in the Rías Baixas Denominación de Origen. Unlike the house’s main Albariño, made from grapes from different sources, this wine focuses on a single estate.

It is made exclusively from Albariño grown on granitic soils in a humid, mild Atlantic setting. After fermentation the wine remains for an extended period in contact with its fine lees, a practice that adds body, texture and aromatic complexity without reducing the variety’s freshness.

The result is an Albariño broader and more structured than the area’s young whites, with ripe fruit, herbal notes and a saline, mineral background. It can be enjoyed young, but its structure allows it to develop for several years in bottle, a quality Albariño shows when it comes from good vineyards.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow with golden tints. Ripe white fruit, citrus, herbs and lees notes. Broad and fresh on the palate, with a creamy texture and a saline finish.

Food pairing

Baked fish, seafood rice dishes, poultry in sauce, soft cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large glass
