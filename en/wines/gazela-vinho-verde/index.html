Gazela Vinho Verde

WhiteControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Vinho Verde

White Vinho Verde from Sogrape, young, light and gently spritzy, one of the most popular brands of the designation.

Gazela is one of Sogrape’s Vinho Verde brands and represents the best-known style of the designation: a young white of moderate alcohol, fresh and slightly spritzy. The Vinho Verde region, in north-western Portugal between the rivers Minho and Douro, has a rainy, mild Atlantic climate and mostly granitic soils, conditions that give wines of lively acidity.

It is made from native varieties of the region, such as Loureiro and Trajadura (Treixadura), among others, in proportions that vary with the vintage. The grapes are vinified in stainless steel tanks with temperature-controlled fermentation to preserve fruity and floral aromas. Before bottling a small amount of carbon dioxide is retained or added, giving the slightly prickly sensation typical of many popular Vinho Verde wines.

It has no ageing and is bottled early to be drunk young. Its fresh profile and moderate alcohol make it suitable as an aperitif and for light meals.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with greenish glints. Aromas of lemon, green apple, pear and white flowers. Light, fresh, lively palate with a subtle effervescence and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, grilled sardines, salads, fried fish, appetisers

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
