La Petite Villa

RedMinervois La LivinièreWine region Languedoc-Roussillon

Red from Domaine Gayda from the Minervois La Livinière appellation, the most highly regarded cru of the Minervois.

La Petite Villa is Domaine Gayda’s red from Minervois La Livinière, a recognised cru within the Minervois that groups several villages at the foot of the Montagne Noire. It is an area of well-exposed slopes with stony limestone and marl soils, a Mediterranean climate and the influence of dry northerly winds.

The appellation requires blends of Rhône and Languedoc varieties, with a significant share of Syrah, Grenache and Mourvèdre, and a minimum ageing period before release. The winery, based in Brugairolles near Limoux, thus complements its Pays d'Oc range with an appellation wine tied to a specific terroir.

The result is a well-structured red, with dark fruit, spice and the scrubland character typical of the area, intended to improve with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Ripe black fruit, liquorice, spice and scrubland. Full palate, firm tannins and a long, spicy finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, game, braised meats, cassoulet, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
