Villa Maria

Villa Maria Single Vineyard Taylors Pass Sauvignon Blanc

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Marlborough

A single-vineyard Sauvignon Blanc from Villa Maria, from the Taylors Pass vineyard in Marlborough.

Villa Maria, founded in Auckland in 1961 by George Fistonich, is one of New Zealand’s best-known wineries and played an important part in spreading Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc internationally. Its range runs from the Private Bin wines up to single-vineyard selections.

This wine belongs to the Single Vineyard series, which aims to show the differences between specific sites. It comes from the Taylors Pass vineyard in Marlborough, the large region in the north of the South Island where the sunny, cool climate with cold nights allows the variety to retain lively acidity and intense aromatic expression.

Compared with the more exuberant style of entry-level Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc, a single-site wine like this usually offers more concentration, texture and a somewhat more mineral and herbal profile. It can be enjoyed young, though it will keep for a short time.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green hints. Aromas of grapefruit, lime, passion fruit, fresh grass and a mineral touch. Intense, fresh palate with good texture and a long finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, oysters, grilled fish, salads with goat’s cheese

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
