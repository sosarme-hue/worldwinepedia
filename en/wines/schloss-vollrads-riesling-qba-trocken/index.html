Schloss Vollrads

Schloss Vollrads Riesling Trocken

WhiteQualitätswein trockenAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Rheingau

Dry Riesling from Schloss Vollrads, the estate’s base wine, fresh and fruity, representative of the dry Rheingau style.

The Riesling trocken is the base wine of Schloss Vollrads and a good introduction to the estate’s style. In Germany, the term trocken denotes a dry wine with low residual sugar, and in recent decades this style has gained considerable ground in regions such as the Rheingau.

It is made exclusively from Riesling from the estate’s vineyards on the slopes above Winkel. The grapes are picked at full ripeness and fermented at low temperature in stainless-steel tanks, to preserve the expression of the fruit and the variety’s natural acidity. The wine is bottled young.

It is a dry Riesling, light to medium in body, with fresh fruit and mineral notes, versatile at the table. It shows the character of the Rheingau, somewhat broader and riper than the Mosel, without losing the freshness typical of the variety.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of green apple, lemon, white peach and a herbal touch. Dry, fresh and taut palate with a clean finish.

Food pairing

Grilled fish, shellfish, salads, Asian cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
