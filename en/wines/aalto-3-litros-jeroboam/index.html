Aalto 3 Litros (Jéroboam)

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Ribera del Duero

Aalto, the Tinto Fino red from Mariano García and Javier Zaccagnini's winery in Ribera del Duero, presented in a 3-litre jeroboam.

Aalto is the winery founded in 1999 in Ribera del Duero by Mariano García, for decades the winemaker at Vega Sicilia, and Javier Zaccagnini, a former director of the appellation's Regulatory Council. Its winery is in Quintanilla de Arriba (Valladolid) and its approach is based on selecting grapes from old vineyards spread across different villages of the Ribera, combining a variety of soils and altitudes.

Aalto is the house's main wine. It is made from Tinto Fino, the local variant of Tempranillo, from numerous plots in different parts of the appellation, which are vinified separately before blending. After fermentation it is aged in oak barrels for an extended period. It is an intense, concentrated red of around 14% ABV, with black fruit, good structure and ageing potential.

Large formats affect how a wine develops: the lower ratio of oxygen to volume means it ages more slowly and gradually than in a 75 cl bottle, so these formats are especially valued for long cellaring and for serving at gatherings with many guests.

Tasting profile

Very deep cherry red. Aromas of ripe black fruit, liquorice, spice, mineral notes and toasty oak. Concentrated, structured palate with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast milk-fed lamb, chargrilled red meat, large game, aged sheep's cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
