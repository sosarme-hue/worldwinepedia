La Chablisienne

Chablis Cuvée 1923

WhiteAOC (Petit Chablis, Chablis, Chablis Premier Cru and Chablis Grand Cru) Chablis

Chablis from La Chablisienne, a fresh, mineral Chardonnay from the Chablis appellation.

The Chablis appellation, in northern Burgundy, is the largest in the area and lies largely on Kimmeridgian marl and limestone, rich in fossils of small oysters. This soil and the cool climate give a Chardonnay with lively acidity and a mineral profile, very different from the broader whites of the Côte de Beaune. Cuvée 1923, whose name recalls the year the cooperative was founded, is a Chablis intended to show that classic style.

La Chablisienne is the Chablis cooperative, founded in 1923 by local growers. Its members farm vineyards spread across the whole area, which allows it to make wines from every tier of the appellation as well as other Auxerrois appellations, in a style that favours freshness and the expression of terroir. The cooperative makes, ages and bottles the wines at its own facilities in Chablis.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green glints. Aromas of lemon, green apple and white flowers over a mineral background. Light, fresh and direct on the palate.

Food pairing

Oysters, shellfish, grilled fish, sushi, goat’s cheese

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
