Wynns Coonawarra Estate

Wynns Coonawarra Estate John Riddoch Cabernet Sauvignon

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Coonawarra

Wynns’ top Cabernet Sauvignon, made from a selection of the best Coonawarra plots since the 1982 vintage and dedicated to the founder of the wine region.

John Riddoch Cabernet Sauvignon bears the name of the Scottish entrepreneur who founded the Coonawarra colony in 1890 and built the winery that now belongs to Wynns. It was first made with the 1982 vintage as the estate’s most ambitious expression of Cabernet Sauvignon, and it is produced only in years the team considers suitable.

The grapes come from a selection of plots on Coonawarra’s terra rossa, often old vines, which are vinified separately before the final blend is assembled. Ageing takes place in French oak barrels, a good proportion of them new, for an extended period. Compared with Black Label it is more concentrated and structured, with greater intensity of fruit and a long life ahead of it. It is best to wait several years before opening it and to decant it so that it can express itself fully.

Tasting profile

Concentrated cassis, blackberry and plum, with notes of cedar, graphite, mint and spice. Dense palate with fine but firm tannins and great length.

Food pairing

Roast spring lamb, red meat, large game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
