Bodega Garzón

Balasto

RedWine department Maldonado

Tannat-based red blend, Bodega Garzón’s most ambitious wine, whose name refers to the decomposed granite soil of its vineyards.

In Uruguay the mixture of decomposed granite and rock fragments that forms the subsoil of many Maldonado slopes is called balasto, and Bodega Garzón chose this word to name its most prestigious wine. Balasto is a blend in which Tannat is the main variety, accompanied by others such as Cabernet Franc, in proportions that change with the vintage.

The grapes come from selected plots in the winery’s vineyards, on hills close to the Atlantic Ocean, where poor, well-drained soils limit vine vigour and sea breezes moderate temperatures. Each plot is vinified separately and the wine is aged in wood before blending and bottling. The result is a red that combines the tannic structure typical of Tannat with the freshness of Maldonado’s maritime climate, designed to develop over years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Very deep purplish red. Aromas of blackberry, plum and black cherry, with notes of spice, graphite and fine oak. Firm and taut on the palate, with abundant, fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
