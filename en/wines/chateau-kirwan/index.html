Château Kirwan

Château Kirwan

Red3rd Grand Cru Classé (1855)AOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Margaux

The grand vin of Château Kirwan, a Margaux third growth, an elegant red with a generous structure.

The grand vin of Château Kirwan comes from the estate's vineyards in Cantenac, within the Margaux appellation. It is a third growth of the 1855 classification, owned by the Schÿler family.

It is made from a blend of Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, completed with Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, in proportions that vary with the vintage. The gravel soils, with sandier or more clayey sectors, make it possible to combine the structure of Cabernet with the roundness of Merlot, while Petit Verdot adds colour and spice.

Ageing takes place in oak barrels, part of them new. The result is an elegant, perfumed Margaux, with black and red fruit, floral and spicy notes and firm but fine tannins. It is a wine for cellaring that shows best after a few years in bottle and develops towards hints of cedar and forest floor.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, black cherry, violet, spice and cedar. Elegant palate with firm, fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, beef fillet, duck, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass
