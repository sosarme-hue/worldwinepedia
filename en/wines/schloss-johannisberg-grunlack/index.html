Schloss Johannisberg

Schloss Johannisberg Riesling Grünlack

WhitePrädikatswein SpätleseAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Rheingau

Sweet-style Riesling Spätlese from Schloss Johannisberg, identified by its green seal and heir to the late-harvest tradition associated with the estate.

According to tradition, the value of late harvesting was discovered at Schloss Johannisberg in 1775, when a delay in the permission to harvest meant that the grapes were picked very ripe and affected by noble rot. The green-sealed Grünlack is the estate’s Spätlese and is directly linked to that story.

It is made from Riesling from the vineyard surrounding the palace, on a south-facing slope above the Rhine, hand-picked at higher ripeness than the house’s entry-level wines. It retains noticeable residual sugar, balanced by the firm acidity characteristic of the Rheingau. It can be enjoyed young for its fruit, but also develops well for years in bottle, acquiring notes of honey and ripe fruit.

Its sweetness makes it particularly suitable for dishes with a touch of heat or a sweet-and-sour element, and it is best served well chilled to bring out its liveliness.

Tasting profile

Aromas of peach, apricot, candied citrus and flowers. Juicy and medium-sweet on the palate, with firm acidity that adds freshness and a long finish.

Food pairing

Thai or Vietnamese food, roast duck, pork with sweet-and-sour sauces, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
