Colomé Torrontés

WhiteWine-producing province Salta

Torrontés from Bodega Colomé made from grapes grown in very high-altitude vineyards in the Calchaquí Valleys of Salta.

Colomé Torrontés is a characteristic example of Argentina’s great white grape in its homeland. The grapes come from the winery’s vineyards in the Calchaquí Valleys, located at very high altitudes, where intense sunlight encourages highly expressive aromas and cold nights preserve acidity.

The variety used is Torrontés Riojano, the most widely planted and most aromatic of the varieties called Torrontés in Argentina. The grapes are hand-picked and the must ferments at controlled temperature in stainless steel tanks. The wine sees no oak, so its floral and fruity aromas remain intact. The result is a dry white with an intense perfume and a palate fresher and lighter than its nose might suggest. It is a wine to drink young, in its first few years, when its aromatic character is at its liveliest. It goes well with the food of northern Argentina and with spicy dishes.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Intense aromas of rose, jasmine, orange blossom, lychee and citrus. Dry, fresh, aromatic palate with a slightly bitter finish.

Food pairing

Salta-style empanadas, ceviche, mildly spiced Thai or Indian dishes, fresh goat’s cheese

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
