Veramonte Sauvignon Blanc Reserva

WhiteReservaDesignation of Origin (subregion) Casablanca Valley

Sauvignon Blanc from Veramonte made from Casablanca Valley grapes, a fresh, aromatic cool-climate white.

Sauvignon Blanc is one of Veramonte’s most representative wines. It comes from the Casablanca Valley, the coastal area between Santiago and Valparaíso that from the 1990s became a benchmark for cool-climate whites in Chile.

The influence of the Pacific Ocean, with morning fogs and cool breezes, prolongs ripening and preserves the acidity and aromas of Sauvignon Blanc. After harvest, the must ferments at low temperature in stainless steel tanks, without oak ageing. In Chile the term Reserva is regulated in general terms and is used for wines with characteristics above those of the basic range. The result is a dry, lively and aromatic white with notes of citrus, fresh grass and tropical fruit, ideal for drinking young, as an aperitif or with fish and shellfish.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Grapefruit, lime, passion fruit, freshly cut grass and green pepper. Dry, fresh, vibrant palate with a citrus finish.

Food pairing

Ceviche, oysters and shellfish, grilled fish, salads, fresh goat’s cheese

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
