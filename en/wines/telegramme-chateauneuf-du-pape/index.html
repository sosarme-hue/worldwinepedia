Domaine du Vieux Télégraphe

Télégramme

RedChâteauneuf-du-PapeAOC Châteauneuf-du-Pape

The second wine of Domaine du Vieux Télégraphe: a red Châteauneuf-du-Pape from younger vines, designed to be enjoyed sooner than the grand vin from La Crau.

Télégramme is the second wine of Domaine du Vieux Télégraphe. The Brunier family makes it from the estate’s younger vines, and its name plays on that of the main wine and on the history of the optical telegraph that gave the estate its name.

Like Vieux Télégraphe, it is based on Grenache, accompanied by Syrah and Mourvèdre in proportions that vary with the vintage. Winemaking is similar, but ageing is shorter, with the aim of producing a wine that is more open and fruity on release.

It offers a good introduction to the style of the house and of the appellation: ripe red fruit, Mediterranean herb notes and a lighter structure than the grand vin. It can be drunk young, though it also develops well for a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Ruby red. Aromas of raspberry, cherry, thyme and gentle pepper. Juicy, round palate with soft tannins and a lightly spicy finish.

Food pairing

Roast chicken, lamb, ratatouille, aged goat’s cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, large glass
