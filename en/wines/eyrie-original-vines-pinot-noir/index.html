The Eyrie Vineyards

Original Vines Pinot Noir

RedAVA Willamette Valley

Pinot Noir from the vines David Lett planted in the 1960s in the Dundee Hills, the first of the variety in the Willamette Valley.

The Original Vines Pinot Noir comes from the vineyard that David and Diana Lett planted in the mid-1960s in the Dundee Hills, the first Pinot Noir vineyard in the Willamette Valley. The vines, dry-farmed and on their own roots, are among the oldest of the variety in Oregon, and with age they give low yields and highly concentrated grapes.

Winemaking follows the philosophy defined by David Lett and maintained by his son Jason: minimal intervention, fermentation with native yeasts, very limited use of new oak and no aggressive fining. The result is a Pinot Noir light in colour and apparently delicate, with lively acidity, fine red-fruit aromas and floral and earthy notes. It is a wine that usually needs time in bottle to show its full complexity, and it can evolve for many years.

Tasting profile

Light ruby red. Aromas of sour cherry, raspberry, rose petals and damp earth. Light yet intense on the palate, with lively acidity and very fine tannins.

Food pairing

Salmon, duck, roast poultry, mushrooms, soft cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
