Dominio de Pingus

Pingus

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Ribera del Duero

Red by Peter Sisseck made since 1995 from very old Tinto Fino vines in La Horra, in very limited quantities; it is one of the Spanish wines most sought after by collectors.

Pingus was born in 1995, when Danish winemaker Peter Sisseck first made a wine from the grapes of a few small, very old bush-trained Tinto Fino vineyards around La Horra, on the Burgos side of Ribera del Duero. The name comes from his childhood nickname. That first vintage immediately caught the attention of international critics and placed Pingus among Spain’s most prized reds.

The source vineyards are farmed following biodynamic principles and by hand, on clay and limestone soils at high altitude. Yields from these old vines are very low, so production is very small. In the cellar, Sisseck seeks careful extraction and ageing in French oak that accompanies the fruit without dominating it.

Pingus is a red of great concentration and depth, but also of notable finesse, designed for very long ageing. Because of its scarcity, it is seldom found outside specialist shops.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry colour. Very pure black fruit, violets, fine spice, mineral notes and well-integrated oak. Dense yet fresh on the palate, with fine tannins and a very persistent finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled red meat, roast milk-fed lamb, large game, aged sheep’s cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
