Alvear Fino C.B.

FortifiedFinoDesignation of Origin (DO) Montilla-Moriles

Classic Alvear fino from the Montilla-Moriles D.O., made from Pedro Ximénez and aged under flor.

Fino C.B. is one of Alvear's best-known wines and a good example of Montilla-Moriles fino. Unlike Jerez fino, it is made from Pedro Ximénez, a grape that in the warm climate of southern Córdoba ripens enough to reach the required strength naturally, so these finos do not usually need fortification.

After fermentation, traditionally carried out in large tinajas (earthenware or concrete vats), the wine goes into the criaderas and solera system, where it ages under flor. The result is a pale, dry, aromatic fino, somewhat broader and softer on the palate than many Jerez finos. It should be served chilled and drunk within a reasonable time once opened.

Tasting profile

Almond, yeast, bready notes and a herbal touch. Dry, smooth, fresh palate with a slightly bitter finish.

Food pairing

Salmorejo, olives, ham, fried fish, aubergines with honey

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
