Schloss Gobelsburg

Grüner Veltliner Ried Lamm

WhiteKamptal DACDAC (Districtus Austriae Controllatus) Kamptal

Single-vineyard Grüner Veltliner from Schloss Gobelsburg from the Lamm, a loess slope near Langenlois, broad and age-worthy.

The Lamm is one of the most highly regarded vineyards in the Kamptal for Grüner Veltliner. It lies at the foot of the Heiligenstein, near Langenlois, in a sheltered, warm spot, with deep loess and calcareous clay soils that produce full-bodied wines. The Österreichische Traditionsweingüter association regards it as one of the top-tier vineyards of the area.

Schloss Gobelsburg makes this wine exclusively from Grüner Veltliner from its parcels in the Lamm. The grapes are hand-picked at full ripeness, and the wine ferments and ages in large oak casks, which bring texture without marking the aromatic profile. It is sold under the Kamptal DAC appellation with the vineyard name.

It is a dry Grüner Veltliner of great concentration and creamy texture, with ripe fruit, the spicy white-pepper note typical of the variety and good acidity. It can age for many years, gaining complexity.

Tasting profile

Pale gold. Aromas of ripe pear, quince, white pepper, herbs and honey notes. Dry, broad and creamy palate with a long, spicy finish.

Food pairing

Wiener schnitzel, veal, vegetables, river fish, Asian cuisine

Serving

11-13 °C, large white wine glass
