Descendientes de J. Palacios

Corullón

RedCrianzaDesignation of Origin (DO) Bierzo

Village wine from Descendientes de J. Palacios made from old-vine Mencía grown on the slate slopes of Corullón, in Bierzo.

Descendientes de J. Palacios is the project that Álvaro Palacios and his nephew Ricardo Pérez Palacios launched in 1999 in Corullón, west of Villafranca del Bierzo. Their work was decisive in the rediscovery of hillside Mencía and in the idea of classifying Bierzo wines by origin, much like the Burgundian hierarchy: regional wine, village wine and single-site wines.

Corullón is the house’s village wine. It comes from many small plots of old Mencía vines spread across the steep slopes around the village, on slate soils, with a few vines of other traditional varieties interplanted. The vineyards are farmed biodynamically, often with the help of horses or mules because of the steep gradient.

The wine ferments with native yeasts and is aged in oak barrels and vats. It is a 14% ABV red showing the finest, most mineral side of Mencía, with good potential to develop in bottle.

Tasting profile

Medium cherry red. Aromas of red and black fruit, violet, mountain herbs and mineral notes. Fresh and fine on the palate, with silky tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Botillo and charcuterie, roast meats, mushrooms, game birds, aged cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
