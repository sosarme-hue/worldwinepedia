Wirra Wirra

Wirra Wirra Church Block

RedGeographical Indication (GI) McLaren Vale

Blend of Cabernet Sauvignon, Shiraz and Merlot from McLaren Vale, one of Wirra Wirra's first wines after its re-establishment in 1969.

Church Block takes its name from a vineyard block beside a small church near the Wirra Wirra winery in McLaren Vale. It is one of the wines with which Greg and Roger Trott relaunched the estate in the 1970s, and it has since become one of the house's best-known and most enduring labels.

It is a blend of Cabernet Sauvignon, Shiraz and Merlot, a very Australian combination bringing together the structure of Cabernet, the flesh of Shiraz and the softness of Merlot; the proportions vary with the vintage. The grapes come from vineyards in McLaren Vale, where the proximity of the sea tempers the heat. The wine is aged in oak barrels, largely used, in order to preserve the fruit. It is a balanced, versatile red, enjoyable young and able to develop for a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Aromas of blackcurrant, plum and cherry, with notes of herbs, chocolate and gentle spice. Medium-bodied, juicy and balanced.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled meats, baked pasta, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
