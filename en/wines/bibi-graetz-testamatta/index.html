Bibi Graetz

Bibi Graetz Testamatta

RedIGT ToscanaWine region Tuscany

Bibi Graetz’s signature wine, an old-vine Tuscan Sangiovese, fresh, elegant and age-worthy.

Sangiovese is Italy’s most widely planted red variety and the basis of the great Tuscan reds, from Chianti Classico to Brunello di Montalcino. It gives wines with marked acidity, firm tannins and aromas of cherry, violet and herbs, which gain complexity with age.

Testamatta, which in Italian means something like “crazy head”, is the wine with which Bibi Graetz made his name and which gives his project its identity. It is made from old-vine Sangiovese from various plots across Tuscany, under the IGT Toscana indication, which frees it from the rules of the appellations. Winemaking aims for gentle extraction, and ageing is geared towards preserving fruit and freshness.

It is a medium-bodied red, focused more on elegance than power, with cherry, red berries, floral and spicy notes and fine tannins. It can be enjoyed young after aeration but improves with several years of ageing.

Tasting profile

Bright ruby. Aromas of cherry, raspberry, violet, herbs and spice. Fresh, elegant palate with fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Bistecca alla fiorentina, pappardelle with wild boar, game birds, pecorino

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
