Scharzhofberger Riesling Kabinett

WhitePrädikatswein KabinettAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Mosel

Riesling Kabinett from the Scharzhofberg vineyard on the Saar, light, delicately sweet and very lively in acidity.

This wine comes from the Scharzhofberg, the slate slope surrounding the Müller family estate near Wiltingen in the Saar valley. Kabinett is the lightest Prädikat category and, in the Saar tradition, it is made with moderate residual sugar that balances very high acidity. The result is a wine low in alcohol, light on the palate and full of tension.

The grapes are picked by hand and the wine ferments slowly in tanks and traditional wooden casks, without seeking any influence from new oak. Although enjoyable when young, thanks to the balance between sugar and acidity it can age for many years, during which the sweetness becomes integrated and more complex notes appear. It is one of the most celebrated examples of the classic style of Mosel Riesling Kabinett.

With its low alcohol and its balance between sweetness and acidity, it is a very versatile wine at the table, pairing well with lightly spiced dishes, and it can also be served as an aperitif.

Tasting profile

Aromas of green apple, citrus, white flowers and mineral slate notes. Light on the palate, with subtle sweetness offset by cutting acidity and a long, clean finish.

Food pairing

Mildly spicy Asian food, trout, salads with fruit, as an aperitif

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
