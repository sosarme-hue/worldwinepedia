Duque de Viseu Tinto

RedControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Dão

Dão red from Sogrape, one of the region’s most widely available wines, made from Touriga Nacional and other traditional varieties.

Duque de Viseu is Sogrape’s best-known Dão brand; the company’s estate in the region is Quinta dos Carvalhais. Its name evokes Viseu, the main city of the Dão.

The red is made from the region’s traditional varieties: Touriga Nacional, thought to originate in this area, Tinta Roriz (Tempranillo), Alfrocheiro and Jaen (Mencía). The grapes come from vineyards on the granite soils of the Dão plateau, where cool nights help to retain acidity. The winemaking aims to round out the structure without masking the fruit. The result is a medium-bodied, fresh and balanced red that reflects the elegant character of Dão wines and can be drunk young or after a few years in bottle.

Thanks to its wide distribution, for many drinkers it is a first introduction to the reds of this region in central Portugal.

Tasting profile

Bright ruby. Aromas of red berries, floral notes and a touch of spice and pine resin. Medium-bodied on the palate, with fine tannins and fresh acidity.

Food pairing

Roast meats, kid, baked salt cod, sheep’s cheeses such as Serra da Estrela

Serving

16-17 °C, large glass
