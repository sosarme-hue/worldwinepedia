Monte Velho Tinto

RedControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) and Alentejano GI Alentejo

Young red from Esporão under the Vinho Regional Alentejano designation, ripe-fruited and easy to drink, and one of the most widely available Portuguese wines.

Monte Velho is Esporão’s most widely distributed brand and a familiar wine on Portuguese tables. It is sold as Vinho Regional Alentejano, a geographical indication that covers the whole region and allows greater freedom in the choice of varieties than the DOC.

It is made from grapes from the Herdade do Esporão and other Alentejo vineyards, using varieties such as Aragonez (Tempranillo), Trincadeira, Touriga Nacional and Syrah, in proportions that may vary with the vintage. The winemaking aims for a red with ripe fruit and soft tannins, without prolonged ageing in wood, that expresses the warm, sunny character of the Alentejo. It is meant to be drunk young, in the first years after the harvest, and to accompany everyday meals.

The Monte Velho range also includes a white and a rosé, made with the same approach of fresh, approachable wines.

Tasting profile

Purple-tinged ruby. Aromas of ripe red and black fruit, plum and a touch of spice. Smooth, fruity and rounded on the palate.

Food pairing

Grilled meat, pasta, pizza, charcuterie

Serving

15-16 °C, red wine glass
