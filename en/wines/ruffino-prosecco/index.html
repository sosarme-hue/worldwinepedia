Ruffino

Ruffino Prosecco

SparklingProsecco DOCDOC and DOCG Prosecco

Ruffino Prosecco DOC, a light, fruity Glera sparkling wine that has become one of the house’s best-selling products.

Although Ruffino is a Tuscan house, its range includes a Prosecco made within the production area of this appellation, in north-eastern Italy, between Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia. It is one of the Proseccos most widely found in shops and supermarkets in many countries.

It is made from Glera, the main Prosecco variety. As is usual in this appellation, the second fermentation takes place in large closed stainless steel tanks, using the method known as Charmat or Martinotti. This system preserves the grapes’ fruity and floral aromas and produces fine, light bubbles.

It is a young, fresh sparkling wine, in a brut or extra dry style depending on the market, intended to be drunk early. It is enjoyed above all as an aperitif and is a common base for cocktails such as the spritz.

Tasting profile

Pale straw, fine bubbles. Aromas of green apple, pear, citrus and white flowers. Light, fresh palate with a clean finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, spritz, mild cured meats, fried food, sushi

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass or flute
