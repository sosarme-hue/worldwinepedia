Beringer Vineyards

Beringer White Zinfandel

Rosé

Californian Zinfandel rosé from Beringer, light, fruity and off-dry, one of the best-known examples of the White Zinfandel style.

White Zinfandel is a style of rosé born in California in the second half of the twentieth century, made from Zinfandel, a red variety, with only very brief contact between the juice and the skins. The result is a pale pink wine with light sweetness and low alcohol, which achieved enormous popularity in the United States. Beringer, a historic Napa Valley winery, is one of its best-known producers.

The grapes come from different parts of California, mainly inland, and the wine is sold under the general California designation. Fermentation takes place at low temperature in stainless steel tanks and is stopped before all the sugar has turned into alcohol, so the wine retains residual sweetness. It is a fresh, fruity rosé, intended to be drunk very young and well chilled, as an aperitif or with light dishes.

Tasting profile

Pale pink. Aromas of strawberry, raspberry, watermelon and fruit candy. Light, fresh palate with gentle sweetness.

Food pairing

Aperitifs, mildly spicy Asian food, fruit salads, red-berry desserts

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
