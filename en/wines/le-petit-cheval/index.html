Château Cheval Blanc

Le Petit Cheval

RedSecond wineAOC (Saint-Émilion and Saint-Émilion Grand Cru) Saint-Émilion

Second wine of Château Cheval Blanc, in Saint-Émilion, made from Merlot and Cabernet Franc from the estate.

Le Petit Cheval is the second wine of Château Cheval Blanc, an estate at the north-western tip of Saint-Émilion, on the border with Pomerol, on an unusual combination of gravel and clay. The estate stands out for the importance it gives to Cabernet Franc.

It comes from the same vineyards as the grand vin, from plots or lots not selected for it, often from younger vines. As at Cheval Blanc, the blend is based on Merlot and Cabernet Franc, in proportions that vary with the vintage, and the wine is aged in oak barrels.

Le Petit Cheval shares the house style, which combines ripe fruit, freshness and aromatic finesse, in a profile somewhat more approachable when young than the grand vin. Even so, it has good ageing potential and gains complexity over the years.

Tasting profile

Dark ruby. Aromas of ripe red and black fruit, violet, spice and oak notes. Smooth, fresh palate with fine tannins and good length.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, veal, duck, mushrooms, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large Bordeaux glass; best decanted
