Château Rayas

Château Rayas Châteauneuf-du-Pape Rouge

RedChâteauneuf-du-PapeAOC Châteauneuf-du-Pape

Red Châteauneuf-du-Pape made solely from old-vine Grenache on sandy soils, one of the most singular and sought-after wines of the southern Rhône.

Château Rayas red is a case apart in Châteauneuf-du-Pape. While most producers blend several of the authorised varieties, Rayas makes it solely from Grenache, from old vines planted on sandy soils in cool parcels surrounded by woodland.

Yields are very low and winemaking is traditional, with no search for excessive extraction. The wine is aged in used barrels and large casks, so that oak does not dominate. The Reynaud family selects the vats that are bottled as Rayas; the rest may go into the second wine, Pignan.

The result is usually a red lighter in colour than many of its neighbours, aromatic and silky in texture, with great ageing potential. Because of its style it is often compared with the great wines of Burgundy rather than with powerful Châteauneuf-du-Pape. Production is small and distribution very limited.

Tasting profile

Medium-intensity red. Aromas of ripe strawberry, raspberry, dried flowers, sweet spice and garrigue notes. Silky palate with fine tannins and a long, aromatic finish.

Food pairing

Squab, partridge, roast lamb, truffle dishes, pressed cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
