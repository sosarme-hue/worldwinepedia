Les Vins Georges Duboeuf

Georges Duboeuf Morgon

RedBeaujolais cruWine region (AOC Beaujolais, Beaujolais-Villages and ten crus) Beaujolais

A Morgon from Georges Duboeuf, Gamay from one of the ten Beaujolais crus, known for producing fuller-bodied reds with ageing potential.

Morgon is one of the ten Beaujolais crus, the communal appellations in the north of the region that represent its top quality tier. Its soils of decomposed granite and schist, especially on the famous Côte du Py, produce Gamay reds that are more structured than those of other crus and which with age can recall some Burgundy reds.

Georges Duboeuf sells several Morgons, both from its floral-label range and from named individual estates. They are made from Gamay with a vinification aimed at preserving the fruit, with short ageing before bottling.

It is a fuller-bodied red than a Beaujolais-Villages, with ripe fruit and present but soft tannins, which can be drunk young or kept for a few years.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of ripe cherry, plum, black fruit and floral and spicy notes. Medium-bodied and juicy on the palate, with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Coq au vin, sausages, roast pork, mushrooms, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, Burgundy glass
