Henriques & Henriques

Henriques & Henriques 10 Year Old Malvasia

Fortified10 Year Old, MalmseyProtected Designation of Origin (PDO) Madeira

Ten-year-old sweet Madeira from Henriques & Henriques made from Malmsey, the noble variety that gives the island’s richest style.

Malmsey (Malvasia) is the sweetest of Madeira’s four noble varieties. To retain its natural sweetness, fermentation is stopped early with grape spirit, and the high acidity the grape keeps in the island’s Atlantic climate then balances that sugar. By regulation, a Madeira that names the variety must be made predominantly from it.

Henriques & Henriques, founded in 1850 in Câmara de Lobos, makes this wine from grapes from the island’s south coast, partly from its own vineyards. After fermentation, the wine ages in oak casks in the warmest parts of the lodge, so oxidative development is slow and gradual. The ten-year statement indicates the average age and character of the blend, approved by the regulatory body.

Like all Madeiras, it withstands time very well once opened, and is best served in small measures.

Tasting profile

Dark amber with mahogany glints. Aromas of raisins, date, caramel, coffee, walnut and spice. Sweet, dense, rich palate with vibrant acidity and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Nut tart, honey cake, dark chocolate, aged cheeses, on its own with coffee

Serving

14-16 °C, fortified or white wine glass
