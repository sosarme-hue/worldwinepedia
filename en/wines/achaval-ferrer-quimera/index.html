Achaval-Ferrer Quimera

RedWine-producing province Mendoza

Red blend from Achaval-Ferrer combining Malbec with Bordeaux varieties from different Mendoza vineyards.

Quimera is Achaval-Ferrer’s answer to the blending tradition. In contrast to its single-estate Malbecs, which seek to express a single place, this wine pursues complexity by combining varieties and origins. Its name, which refers to a mythological creature made up of parts of different animals, reflects this idea of a whole composed of diverse elements.

The base is Malbec, accompanied by Bordeaux varieties such as Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, whose proportions change every year depending on the harvest. The grapes come from vineyards in Luján de Cuyo and the Uco Valley and are vinified separately. After ageing in French oak barrels, the winery decides the final blend through successive tastings. The result is an elegant, complex red with good structure and ageing potential, moving away from varietal Malbec to offer more nuance.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby red. Black and red fruit, gentle herbal notes, spice and tobacco. Balanced, elegant palate with fine tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Roast red meat, duck, game stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
