Celler Vall Llach

Embruix de Vall Llach

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Priorat

Entry-level red from Celler Vall Llach, a blend of Garnacha, Cariñena and other varieties from vineyards in and around Porrera.

Embruix, Catalan for “spell”, is the most approachable red from Celler Vall Llach, the winery founded by Lluís Llach in Porrera. Designed to show the character of the Priorat without the need for long ageing, it is the house wine most easily found in shops.

It is a blend in which Garnacha and Cariñena play the main role, completed with varieties such as Syrah, Merlot or Cabernet Sauvignon; the proportions vary with the vintage. The grapes come from vineyards in Porrera and nearby villages, on llicorella soils, and include both young vines and some older ones. The varieties are vinified separately and the wine is aged for a few months in oak barrels before blending. The result is a Mediterranean red with ripe fruit, spicy notes and the freshness brought by local Cariñena, well suited to drinking in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red. Aromas of ripe black fruit, spice, Mediterranean herbs and a toasty touch. Smooth and savoury on the palate, with ripe tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled meats, charcuterie, mountain rice dishes, stews

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
