Schloss Vollrads

Schloss Vollrads Riesling Kabinett

WhiteKabinettAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Rheingau

Riesling Kabinett from Schloss Vollrads in the Rheingau, light and fruity, with moderate sweetness balanced by acidity.

The Kabinett is one of the most widely distributed wines of Schloss Vollrads, the historic Rheingau estate devoted exclusively to Riesling. In the German classification of Prädikat wines, Kabinett is the first level and corresponds to ripe but not overripe grapes, producing light, low-alcohol wines.

The grapes come from the estate’s vineyards on the slopes above Winkel, facing south towards the Rhine, with loess, marl and quartzite soils. Fermentation takes place at controlled temperature and is stopped, depending on the version, leaving some residual sugar, which in the Rheingau tends to come with a little more body than in the Mosel. The estate also makes drier versions.

It is an aromatic Riesling with fresh fruit and lively acidity, showing the combination of ripeness and freshness typical of the Rheingau. It can be drunk young, though it develops well for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of peach, apricot, apple and citrus. Light, juicy palate with moderate sweetness and refreshing acidity.

Food pairing

Asian cuisine, pork, poultry, mild blue cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
