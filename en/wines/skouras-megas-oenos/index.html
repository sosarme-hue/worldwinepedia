Domaine Skouras

Skouras Megas Oenos

Red

Domaine Skouras red blending Agiorgitiko and Cabernet Sauvignon, one of the wines that marked the renewal of Greek wine in the late twentieth century.

Megas Oenos, “great wine” in Greek, is the best-known red of Domaine Skouras. When it appeared, it was one of the first Greek wines to combine a native variety with an international one in a blend designed for barrel ageing and cellaring, and it became a benchmark for the country’s new generation of quality wines.

It is made from Agiorgitiko, the red variety of Nemea, which brings ripe red fruit, colour and softness, and Cabernet Sauvignon, which adds structure, firm tannins and blackcurrant aromas; the proportion varies with the vintage, with Agiorgitiko usually predominating. The grapes come from vineyards in the Peloponnese, partly from higher-altitude sites, and the wine is sold under a Protected Geographical Indication, since the blend does not meet the rules of the Nemea designation.

After fermentation, the wine is aged in French oak barrels for an extended period. It is a structured red, able to develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet. Aromas of blackcurrant, black cherry, plum, spice, cedar and toasty notes. Structured, broad palate with firm tannins, good acidity and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled red meat, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
