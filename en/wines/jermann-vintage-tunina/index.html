Jermann Vintage Tunina

WhiteVenezia Giulia IGTWine region Friuli-Venezia Giulia

Jermann’s white blend combining international and native Friulian varieties, one of Italy’s best-known whites since the 1970s.

Vintage Tunina is Silvio Jermann’s most emblematic wine and one of Italy’s first great white blends. It was born in the 1970s, when most Friulian whites were sold as single-variety wines, and showed that combining different grapes could give wines of great complexity.

It is made mainly from Sauvignon Blanc and Chardonnay, joined by local varieties such as Ribolla Gialla, Malvasia and Picolit; the proportions vary with the vintage. Part of the fruit is picked at an advanced stage of ripeness. Depending on the year, the varieties are vinified together or separately, and the wine rests on its lees before bottling.

It is a broad, aromatic white, with notes of ripe fruit, flowers and honey, combining richness and freshness. It has good ageing potential and gains complexity with a few years in bottle. It is sold as Venezia Giulia IGT.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of ripe yellow fruit, flowers, honey, citrus and gentle spice. Broad, unctuous palate with freshness and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fish, shellfish, white meat, risotto, medium-aged cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
