Viña De Martino

De Martino Viejas Tinajas Cinsault

RedDesignation of Origin (subregion) Itata Valley

Cinsault from old dry-farmed vines in the Itata Valley, made by Viña De Martino in old clay tinajas, without oak.

The Viejas Tinajas line is one of the projects through which Viña De Martino has sought to revive the traditional viticulture of southern Chile. Its Cinsault comes from old vines in the Itata Valley, grown as bush vines without irrigation by smallholders on the granitic hills of the Coastal Range.

The name refers to how it is made: the wine ferments and ages in old clay tinajas, traditional vessels of the area that the winery has recovered, without the use of barrels. The aim is simple, minimal-intervention winemaking, with indigenous yeasts and gentle extraction, to express the fruit of the variety and the character of the place. Cinsault, widely planted in Itata, gives here a light, aromatic red, pale in colour, with fresh red fruit, floral notes and lively acidity. It is a wine to enjoy young and slightly chilled, and a good example of the revival of the dry-farmed wines of southern Chile.

Tasting profile

Bright, light ruby. Strawberry, raspberry, cherry, floral and herbal notes. Light, juicy, fresh palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, empanadas, oily fish, poultry, pulse dishes

Serving

13-15 °C, red wine glass
