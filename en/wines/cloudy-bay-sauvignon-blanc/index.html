Cloudy Bay

Cloudy Bay Sauvignon Blanc

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Marlborough

The Sauvignon Blanc that brought Marlborough international fame from its first vintage, 1985. It is an aromatic, lively white with marked acidity.

Few wines have had as great an influence on a country’s image as Cloudy Bay Sauvignon Blanc. From its first vintage in 1985, its intensely aromatic style and its label showing the outline of the mountains became synonymous with Marlborough Sauvignon in markets such as Britain and Australia, and helped open the way for the whole region.

The grapes come from the winery’s own vineyards and from partner growers in the Wairau valley and other parts of Marlborough, on alluvial soils of various kinds. Most of the wine is fermented in temperature-controlled stainless steel tanks to preserve the fruit, and a small proportion may be fermented in used barrels to add texture. It is bottled early and intended to be drunk young, when it shows all its aromatic freshness, although it keeps well for a few years.

Tasting profile

Passion fruit, grapefruit, lime and gooseberry, with notes of fresh grass and tomato leaf. Juicy palate with lively acidity and a refreshing finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, goat’s cheese salads, grilled fish, dishes with fresh herbs

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
