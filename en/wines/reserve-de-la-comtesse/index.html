Château Pichon Longueville Comtesse de Lalande

Réserve de la Comtesse

RedSecond wineBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The second wine of Château Pichon Longueville Comtesse de Lalande, soft and aromatic in style, in which Merlot usually plays a prominent role alongside Cabernet Sauvignon.

Réserve de la Comtesse is the second wine of Pichon Comtesse. It is made from the estate’s grapes in Pauillac, generally from younger vines or from lots that, during each year’s selection tasting, are not used for the grand vin.

The blend combines Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, with a possible contribution from Cabernet Franc; the proportions vary with the vintage, and Merlot is usually more present than in the grand vin, which contributes to a rounded texture. It is vinified in the same facilities as the grand vin and aged in French oak barrels, with less new wood.

It shares the house’s characteristic elegance with the grand vin, showing ripe fruit, cedar notes and supple tannins. It is a relatively affordable way to approach the property’s style and can be enjoyed young, although it can also be cellared for a few years.

Tasting profile

Intense ruby. Aromas of ripe cherry, blackcurrant and sweet spice. Round, silky palate with soft tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, roast duck, meat stews, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, Bordeaux glass
