Bodegas El Grifo

El Grifo Listán Negro

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Lanzarote

Listán Negro red from El Grifo, from ungrafted vines on volcanic ash on Lanzarote, light and fresh in profile.

Listán Negro is the most widely planted red variety in the Canary Islands, and on Lanzarote it is grown, like Malvasía, in pits and trenches dug into the volcanic ash, ungrafted thanks to the absence of phylloxera on the island. El Grifo uses it to make a red that reflects the Atlantic, volcanic character of its vineyards.

The grapes are hand-harvested and the wine is made to preserve the variety's fruit and freshness, with moderate extraction and discreet or no use of oak depending on the version. The result is a light- to medium-bodied red, aromatic and with an earthy, spicy background, very different from mainland reds from continental climates. It is best enjoyed young and slightly chilled.

Tasting profile

Fresh red fruit, notes of pepper, herbs and an earthy volcanic background. Light palate with lively acidity and soft tannins.

Food pairing

Goat meat, rabbit in Canarian salmorejo marinade, seared tuna, semi-cured cheeses

Serving

13-15 °C, red wine glass
