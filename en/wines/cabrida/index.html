Celler de Capçanes

Cabrida

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Montsant

Very old-vine Garnacha from Celler de Capçanes, the most ambitious wine of this Montsant cooperative.

Cabrida is one of the most ambitious wines of Celler de Capçanes and one of the benchmark Garnachas of the Montsant D.O. Its existence illustrates the transformation of the Capçanes cooperative, which moved from selling bulk wine to selecting its best plots to make wines of origin.

It is a single-varietal Garnacha from very old bush vines planted in small mountain plots in the municipality of Capçanes, on poor soils dominated by decomposed granite and sand. These vines give very low yields and highly concentrated grapes. The wine is aged for more than a year in French oak barrels. Old-vine Garnacha gives a red of great aromatic intensity here, with ripe red and black fruit, notes of Mediterranean herbs and a broad texture, balanced by the freshness brought by altitude and soil. It is a wine with ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Intense cherry red. Aromas of very ripe red and black fruit, wild herbs, spice and toasty notes. Broad and silky on the palate, with ripe tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, game, grilled meats, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
