Château Montrose

Château Montrose

RedSecond Growth (1855 Classification)AOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Saint-Estèphe

Grand vin of Château Montrose, a Saint-Estèphe second growth: a powerful, long-lived red dominated by Cabernet Sauvignon.

Château Montrose is the grand vin of the estate of the same name, classified as a second growth in 1855 and located in Saint-Estèphe, facing the Gironde estuary. It comes from a single-block vineyard on a gravel rise with a clay subsoil.

The blend is dominated by Cabernet Sauvignon, which brings structure, colour and aromas of blackcurrant and cedar, completed by Merlot and small proportions of Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, depending on the vintage. The estuary moderates temperatures and protects the vineyard from frost. The wine is aged in French oak barrels for an extended period.

It is a deep, powerful red with dense tannins, which can be austere in its youth. It needs many years in bottle to develop its complexity, with notes of tobacco, truffle and spice, and ranks among the longest-lived wines of the Médoc.

Tasting profile

Very dark purple-red. Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, cedar, graphite and spice. Powerful, dense palate with firm tannins and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, rib steak, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
