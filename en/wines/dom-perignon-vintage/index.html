Dom Pérignon

Dom Pérignon Vintage

SparklingVintage BrutAOC (sparkling wine) Champagne

Dom Pérignon’s vintage champagne, a blend of Chardonnay and Pinot Noir from a single harvest, aged for years on the lees before release.

White Dom Pérignon is always a vintage champagne, made from grapes from a single harvest and only in years the house considers suitable. It is based on two varieties, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, usually present in relatively balanced proportions, although these vary with the vintage. The grapes come from classified Champagne villages and from the historic plot of the abbey of Hautvillers.

After the first fermentation and blending, the wine is bottled for the second fermentation by the traditional method and remains in the cellars on its lees for many years, generally well beyond the minimum required for vintage champagnes, before disgorgement and release.

It is a champagne of great complexity, with notes of white fruit, flowers, brioche and toast, a fine mousse and a creamy texture. It can be enjoyed on release, but many vintages develop for a long time.

Tasting profile

Pale gold with very fine bubbles. Aromas of white flowers, citrus, stone fruit, brioche and toasty, smoky notes. Creamy, precise palate of great breadth, with a long, saline finish.

Food pairing

Lobster, caviar, turbot, poultry with truffle, aged cheeses such as Comté

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass rather than a flute
