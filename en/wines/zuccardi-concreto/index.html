Familia Zuccardi

Zuccardi Concreto

RedWine region Valle de Uco

Malbec from Paraje Altamira made by Familia Zuccardi entirely in uncoated concrete vessels, without any oak.

Concreto is one of the wines that best sums up Familia Zuccardi’s change of style under Sebastián Zuccardi. It comes from Malbec vineyards in Paraje Altamira, in San Carlos, in the southern Uco Valley, an area of alluvial soils with abundant boulders coated with calcium carbonate.

Its name refers to how it is made: the wine ferments and ages in uncoated concrete vessels, without barrels, so that nothing stands between the grape and the expression of place. Fermentation takes place with indigenous yeasts and gentle extraction. The result is a Malbec with a fresher, tauter profile than many traditional Malbecs, with precise fruit, floral notes and a character that the winery associates with the calcareous soils of the area. It can be enjoyed young, though it is able to develop for several years in bottle. It is a good example of the new trends in Argentine wine.

Tasting profile

Bright violet-red. Fresh red and black fruit, violet, mountain herbs and a mineral background. Taut palate with fine, grainy tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Lamb, chargrilled meat, mushroom dishes, aged goat’s cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
