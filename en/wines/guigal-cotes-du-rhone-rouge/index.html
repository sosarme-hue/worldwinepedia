E. Guigal

Guigal Côtes du Rhône Rouge

RedCôtes du RhôneRegional AOC of the Rhône valley (Côtes du Rhône and Côtes du Rhône Villages) Côtes du Rhône

Red Côtes du Rhône from E. Guigal, one of the region’s most widely distributed wines, with an unusually high proportion of Syrah for the appellation.

Guigal’s red Côtes du Rhône is one of the best-known labels of the Rhône valley and one of the easiest to find in shops around the world. Although the house is based in Ampuis, in the north of the valley, this wine comes mainly from southern Rhône grapes and wines selected from local growers.

Its particularity is the weight of Syrah, which is usually the majority variety, something unusual in an appellation dominated by Grenache; Grenache itself and Mourvèdre complete the blend, in proportions that vary with the vintage. The house ages it for a relatively long period in large oak casks before bottling.

The result is a red of good colour and structure, with black fruit and spicy notes, presented as a gateway to the Guigal style. It can be drunk young, though it holds well for a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Dark red. Aromas of blackberry, black cherry, pepper and sweet spice. Medium-bodied palate with firm tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Roast meats, beef stews, pasta with ragù, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

15-16 °C, standard red wine glass
