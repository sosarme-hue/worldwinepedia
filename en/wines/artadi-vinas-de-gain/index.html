Artadi

Artadi Viñas de Gain

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Tempranillo red from Artadi, from vineyards in Laguardia in Rioja Alavesa, which serves as the winery’s village wine and entry to its range.

Viñas de Gain is Artadi’s entry-level wine and the one that best sums up the philosophy of the winery, which advocates classifying wines by origin —village and vineyard— rather than by ageing time. It is made from Tempranillo from the winery’s vineyards in the municipality of Laguardia, in Rioja Alavesa, at the foot of the Sierra de Cantabria, on clay-limestone soils.

The wine is fermented and aged in oak barrels for a moderate period, with the intention that the wood should accompany rather than dominate the fruit and the expression of the limestone soil. It carries no ageing designation on the label, in keeping with the winery’s approach.

Since the mid-2010s Artadi has made its wines outside the Rioja Denominación de Origen Calificada, although it still uses grapes from the same Álava vineyards. Viñas de Gain is a medium-bodied, fresh and precise red that can be drunk young or kept for a few years.

Tasting profile

Medium-intensity cherry red. Fresh red and black fruit, floral notes and a chalky background. Fresh, precise and fine-tannined on the palate.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled lamb chops, Iberian cured meats, semi-cured cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
