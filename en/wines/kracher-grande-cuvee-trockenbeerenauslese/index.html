Kracher Grande Cuvée Trockenbeerenauslese

SweetTrockenbeerenausleseWine-growing region (Weinbaugebiet), with several DACs Burgenland

Kracher Trockenbeerenauslese blending botrytised Chardonnay and Welschriesling, aged in barrel and released each year with a number within the house series.

The Grande Cuvée is one of the emblematic Trockenbeerenauslese wines of Kracher, the house in Illmitz, in the Seewinkel, by Lake Neusiedl. Like the estate's other TBAs, it is released within a numbered series that is set anew with each harvest, so its number (No. 3, No. 5, etc.) varies from one vintage to another.

It belongs to the Nouvelle Vague line, whose wines are aged in oak barrels. It blends Chardonnay, which brings body and texture, with Welschriesling, which brings freshness and vivacity. The grapes, heavily concentrated by Botrytis cinerea encouraged by the lake's autumn mists, are picked by hand in successive passes, berry by berry.

The result is a very sweet, concentrated wine in which lively acidity prevents heaviness. It has great ageing potential and develops over decades.

Tasting profile

Deep gold to amber. Aromas of candied apricot, pineapple, honey, vanilla and spice. Unctuous, very sweet palate balanced by lively acidity.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses, tarte Tatin, fruit desserts

Serving

8-10 °C, small dessert wine glass
