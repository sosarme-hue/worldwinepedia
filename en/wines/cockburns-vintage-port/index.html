Cockburn’s

Cockburn’s Vintage Port

FortifiedVintage PortControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Port

Cockburn’s Vintage Port, based on fruit from Quinta dos Canais in the Douro Superior, firm and relatively dry in style and intended for long cellaring.

Cockburn’s has a long tradition of making Vintage Port, the single-harvest wine bottled unfiltered after about two years in wood, which evolves for decades in bottle. As at other houses, a Vintage is “declared” only in years considered exceptional.

The backbone of the wine comes from Quinta dos Canais, in the Douro Superior, one of the hottest and driest parts of the region, with vineyards on schist slopes above the river. It is made from traditional varieties such as Touriga Nacional and Touriga Franca, along with others, in proportions that vary with the vintage. Under the ownership of the Symington family, the house has sought to restore the historic style of its Vintage Ports, with firm structure, less apparent sweetness and great ageing potential.

As it is not filtered, it throws a sediment in the bottle over time, so it should be decanted before serving.

Tasting profile

When young, very dark in colour, with aromas of black fruit, violet, rockrose and spice, and a powerful palate with firm tannins and a dry finish. With age it gains notes of nuts, cocoa and tobacco.

Food pairing

Blue cheeses such as Stilton, dark chocolate, nuts, on its own after a meal

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted off its sediment, Port or red wine glass
