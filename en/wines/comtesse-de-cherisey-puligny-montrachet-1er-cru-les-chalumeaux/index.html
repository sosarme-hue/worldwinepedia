Comtesse de Chérisey

Comtesse de Chérisey Puligny-Montrachet 1er Cru Les Chalumeaux

WhiteAOC Puligny-Montrachet 1er Cru / Côte-d'Or

Puligny-Montrachet premier cru from the Les Chalumeaux vineyard, on the slope rising towards Blagny, made by Domaine Comtesse de Chérisey and aged in barrel.

Domaine Comtesse de Chérisey is a small family property based in Blagny, a hamlet high up in the Côte de Beaune between Meursault and Puligny-Montrachet. The family farms vineyards in both villages, many of them on the upper slopes around the hamlet, where the greater altitude and exposure give wines of a taut, mineral profile.

Les Chalumeaux is a Puligny-Montrachet premier cru in the upper part of the commune, on the border with Meursault and on the slope that climbs towards Blagny. Its stony, shallow limestone soils and high position give wines of a fresher, sharper profile than the premiers crus in the lower part of Puligny, known for their richness.

The wine is made from Chardonnay and is fermented and aged in oak barrels, with some new wood, following traditional Côte de Beaune methods. The Puligny style is characterised by finesse, tension and aromatic precision, traits accentuated in this vineyard by altitude. At 13% ABV, it is a white that gains complexity with a few years of ageing.

Tasting profile

Bright pale yellow. Aromas of citrus, white flowers, stone fruit and notes of hazelnut and wet stone. Taut and precise on the palate, with vibrant acidity and a long, mineral finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, shellfish, farmyard poultry, veal in cream sauce, soft cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
