Masciarelli Villa Gemma Montepulciano d’Abruzzo Riserva

RedMontepulciano d’Abruzzo DOC RiservaWine region Abruzzo

Masciarelli’s Montepulciano d’Abruzzo Riserva, the house’s top red, aged for an extended period in oak and bottle.

Villa Gemma is the most ambitious line from Masciarelli, the winery founded by Gianni Masciarelli in San Martino sulla Marrucina, in the province of Chieti. Its red is a Montepulciano d’Abruzzo Riserva that has helped show the variety’s potential for age-worthy wines.

It is made solely from Montepulciano from selected family vineyards, farmed for low yields in the hills between the Maiella massif and the Adriatic. After fermentation with maceration, the wine is aged for an extended period in oak barriques and then rests in bottle before release.

Montepulciano is a variety rich in colour and tannin, and in this version it gives a red of great concentration, with ripe black fruit, spice and balsamic notes. It is a wine for long cellaring that needs time to show its full complexity.

Tasting profile

Almost opaque ruby. Aromas of blackberry, plum, black cherry, spice, liquorice, cocoa and balsamic notes. Powerful, dense palate with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled red meat, roast lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
