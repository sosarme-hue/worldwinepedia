Elena Walch

Elena Walch Beyond the Clouds

WhiteAlto Adige DOC BiancoWine region Trentino-Alto Adige

Elena Walch’s Chardonnay-based white blend, fermented and aged in wood, the house’s most ambitious white wine.

Beyond the Clouds is Elena Walch’s most ambitious white. Its name evokes the high vineyards of Alto Adige, where grapes ripen under intense sunshine but with cool nights that preserve acidity.

It is made mainly from Chardonnay, accompanied by a small proportion of other white varieties from the estate, a composition the house does not fix rigidly and which varies with the vintage. The grapes come from a selection of plots. Fermentation and ageing take place in oak barrels and casks, in contact with the lees, which adds texture and complexity.

It is a full-bodied white with ripe fruit, notes from oak ageing and an acidity that keeps it balanced. Unlike most whites from the area, it has good capacity to evolve in bottle and can be kept for several years.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of ripe yellow fruit, citrus, flowers, butter and toasty notes. Broad, creamy palate with good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fish, shellfish, white meat, risotto, medium-aged cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
