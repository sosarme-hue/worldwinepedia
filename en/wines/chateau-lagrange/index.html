Château Lagrange

Château Lagrange

Red3rd Classified GrowthAOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Saint-Julien

The grand vin of Château Lagrange, a third-growth Saint-Julien, based on Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot.

The grand vin of Château Lagrange comes from the best plots of this large Saint-Julien estate, located in the inland part of the appellation on two gravel hillocks. The blend is based on Cabernet Sauvignon, generally the majority, and Merlot, with a small contribution of Petit Verdot.

Since Suntory’s arrival in 1983, the estate has renovated its vineyard and winery, with plot-by-plot vinification and strict selection for the grand vin, while the remaining lots go to the second wine, Les Fiefs de Lagrange. Ageing takes place in French oak barrels, a proportion of them new.

It is a classic Saint-Julien, with black fruit, notes of cedar and spice, firm structure and fine tannins. It needs several years in bottle and can age for decades.

Tasting profile

Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, cedar, graphite and spice. Structured palate with fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Pauillac lamb, entrecôte bordelaise, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass
