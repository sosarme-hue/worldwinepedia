Villa Maria

Villa Maria Private Bin East Coast Chardonnay

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Hawke's Bay

Chardonnay from Villa Maria’s Private Bin range, made from grapes grown on the east coast of New Zealand’s North Island.

The Private Bin East Coast Chardonnay belongs to the Private Bin range of Villa Maria, the most widely distributed line of varietal wines from this New Zealand winery founded in 1961 in Auckland. While the house is best known for Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc, this Chardonnay comes from the east coast of the North Island, particularly from regions such as Hawke’s Bay and Gisborne, which are warmer and sunnier and where the variety ripens easily.

It is made from Chardonnay in a style that aims to highlight the fruit. Part of the wine may be fermented or aged in contact with oak and on its lees to gain texture, without the wood dominating. Like the rest of the range, it is sealed with a screwcap, a practice Villa Maria helped to make widespread in New Zealand.

It is a dry, rounded white with ripe fruit, notes of peach and citrus, a soft texture and balanced acidity, designed to be drunk in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Pale golden yellow. Aromas of peach, nectarine, citrus and a touch of vanilla. Round, fresh palate.

Food pairing

Roast chicken, fish in sauce, creamy pasta, mild cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white-wine glass
