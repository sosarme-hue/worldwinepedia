Taylor’s (Taylor, Fladgate & Yeatman)

Taylor’s 10 Year Old Tawny Port

Fortified10 Year Old TawnyControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Port

Taylor’s 10-year-old tawny, a blend of wines from several harvests aged in oak casks, balancing ripe fruit with the aromas of oxidative ageing.

Taylor’s 10 Year Old Tawny belongs to the family of age-dated tawnies, blends of wines from several harvests whose average age matches the indication on the label and whose character is certified by the regulatory body. The house also makes 20, 30 and 40-year-old versions.

The grapes come from the Douro, including the house’s own quintas, and from traditional varieties such as Touriga Nacional, Touriga Franca and Tinta Roriz (Tempranillo). After fermentation, stopped with grape spirit, the wines age in oak casks in the lodges of Vila Nova de Gaia. The wood allows slow contact with oxygen that softens the wine, lightens its colour and develops notes of nuts and spice. It is bottled filtered, ready to drink, and once opened it keeps well for a few weeks.

Tasting profile

Reddish amber colour. Aromas of ripe and dried fruit, toasted almond, toffee and sweet spice. Silky and sweet on the palate, with a long, fresh finish.

Food pairing

Nut tarts, crème caramel, aged cheeses, foie gras

Serving

10-14 °C, lightly chilled, Port glass
