Yalumba

Yalumba The Signature Cabernet Sauvignon Shiraz

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Barossa Valley

Classic Australian blend of Barossa Cabernet Sauvignon and Shiraz, made by Yalumba since 1962 and dedicated each vintage to a person connected with the company.

The Signature is one of Yalumba’s emblematic wines and an example of the Cabernet Sauvignon–Shiraz blend, a combination deeply rooted in Australian tradition and unusual in Europe. It has been made since the 1962 vintage. Each vintage carries on its label the signature of a person who has contributed to the history of the company, whether a family member, a winemaker, a grower or another collaborator, hence its name.

The grapes come from Barossa vineyards, and Cabernet Sauvignon is usually the majority, contributing structure and cassis notes, while Shiraz adds flesh and ripe fruit. The wine is aged in barrels, some of them made in Yalumba’s own cooperage. It is a full-bodied red with good ageing potential, which develops notes of cedar, tobacco and spice over the years.

Tasting profile

Cassis, blackberry and plum, with notes of cedar, mint and spice. Structured palate with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Grilled rib steak, lamb, braises, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
