Château Margaux

Pavillon Rouge du Château Margaux

RedSecond wineBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The second wine of Château Margaux, made from grapes from the same vineyard and aged with the same care as the grand vin.

Pavillon Rouge du Château Margaux is the estate’s second wine. It is made from the plots and lots that, after selection, do not go into the grand vin, often from younger vines or plots whose profile suits a wine that develops more quickly. Like the grand vin, it is based on Cabernet Sauvignon, with Merlot and small proportions of Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot.

It is aged in French oak barrels, generally with a lower proportion of new wood than the grand vin. Selection has become stricter over the years, so it now accounts for a small part of total production.

It offers the elegant Margaux style with more immediate fruit and can be enjoyed earlier than the grand vin, although it also ages well.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, cherry, violet and notes of cedar and toast. Medium-bodied, silky and balanced on the palate, with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Lamb, duck breast, grilled red meat, mild aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass
