Boekenhoutskloof

Boekenhoutskloof Cabernet Sauvignon Franschhoek

RedWine of Origin (WO) Franschhoek

Cabernet Sauvignon from the Franschhoek valley in South Africa, one of Boekenhoutskloof’s signature reds, aged in barrel.

Boekenhoutskloof is a historic estate in the Franschhoek valley, in South Africa’s Cape, whose origins as a farm go back to the 18th century. Since the late 1990s, under the direction of Marc Kent, it has become one of the country’s most influential wineries, with a range that runs from accessible wines to highly ambitious single-site reds and whites.

This Cabernet Sauvignon comes from vineyards in the Franschhoek valley itself, a mountain-ringed area east of Stellenbosch and Paarl, with a warm, sunny climate tempered by altitude and the influence of the ranges, and soils of granitic and sandy origin at the foot of the slopes. From the early years it was one of the wines that made the winery’s name.

It is made with controlled fermentation and extended ageing in oak barrels, partly new, which rounds out the variety’s tannin. Its high alcohol reflects the full ripeness of the grapes. It is a classically styled red, concentrated and structured, with good ageing potential, best appreciated after a few years in bottle or with prior decanting.

Tasting profile

Blackcurrant, blackberry, cedar, tobacco and toasty notes. Dense and firm on the palate, with marked but ripe tannins and a long, spicy finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled red meat, roast lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
