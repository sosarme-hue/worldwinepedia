Domaine Huet

Huet Le Mont Sec

WhiteVouvrayWine region Loire

Dry Vouvray from Domaine Huet, from Le Mont, one of the house’s three historic vineyards, made from biodynamically farmed Chenin Blanc.

Le Mont is, together with Le Haut-Lieu and the Clos du Bourg, one of the three vineyards that form the core of Domaine Huet in Vouvray. Gaston Huet added it to the property in the mid-20th century. It lies on the first line of slopes above the Loire, with shallow clay and flint soils over the limestone tuffeau typical of the area, which produce taut wines with a marked mineral character.

Like all the house’s wines, it is made from biodynamically farmed Chenin Blanc. Depending on the vintage, Huet may make dry, off-dry or sweet wines from each vineyard; the sec version is the driest of them. Fermentation is slow, with indigenous yeasts, in used casks and barrels and in tank, without adding oak notes.

It is a white of great purity and acidity, which can seem austere in its youth and develops remarkable complexity over the years. Huet’s Vouvrays are renowned for ageing for decades.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of apple, pear, quince, white flowers and chalky, flinty notes. Dry, taut and precise palate with a long, mineral finish.

Food pairing

River fish, scallops, pork with apple, Touraine goat’s cheeses

Serving

12-13 °C, large white wine glass
