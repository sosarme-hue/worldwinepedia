Quintarelli Amarone della Valpolicella Classico

RedAmarone della Valpolicella Classico DOCGDOC and DOCG (Amarone and Recioto) Valpolicella

Giuseppe Quintarelli’s Amarone, made from grapes dried for months and aged for many years in large casks, one of Italy’s cult wines.

Quintarelli’s Amarone is made at the small family winery in Negrar, in Valpolicella Classica, following the methods established by Giuseppe Quintarelli. The grapes, mainly Corvina, Corvinone and Rondinella together with other estate varieties, are dried for several months in the winery’s lofts before pressing, a process that concentrates sugars, aromas and extract.

Fermentation is slow and the wine is then aged for many years in large oak casks, a period considerably longer than the minimum required by the appellation. It is made only in vintages considered suitable; in the others, the grapes go into other house wines, such as Rosso del Bepi.

It is a red of great richness and complexity, dry yet opulent, combining the power typical of Amarone with remarkable elegance. It can evolve for decades.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet. Complex aromas of cherry liqueur, raisins, dried figs, spice, chocolate and balsamic notes. Broad, velvety palate of great length.

Food pairing

Braised red meat, game, risotto all’Amarone, aged cheeses such as Monte Veronese or Parmigiano

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
