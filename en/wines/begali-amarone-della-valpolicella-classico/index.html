Begali Lorenzo

Begali Amarone della Valpolicella Classico

RedDOCG Amarone della Valpolicella Classico

Amarone della Valpolicella Classico from the small Begali family winery in San Pietro in Cariano, made from dried grapes and aged in wood.

Begali Lorenzo is a small family winery in San Pietro in Cariano, in the heart of Valpolicella Classica, the historic hill zone north-west of Verona. The family farms its own vineyards on the area’s slopes and makes the appellation’s classic range, from Valpolicella to Amarone, in a careful, traditional style.

Amarone della Valpolicella is made mainly from Corvina, Corvinone and Rondinella. After harvest, the bunches are left to dry for several months in ventilated lofts, the process known as appassimento, which concentrates sugars, aromas and tannins. The resulting must ferments until practically dry, which explains its high alcohol, and the wine then undergoes long ageing in wood.

The Classico designation indicates that it comes from the appellation’s historic zone. It has 16% ABV. It is a powerful, enveloping red that balances the richness of dried grapes with Corvina’s acidity, and it develops for many years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet red. Aromas of cherry liqueur, prune, spice, cocoa and balsamic notes. Broad, warm and velvety on the palate, with ripe tannins and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Braised beef, game, risotto all’Amarone, long-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
