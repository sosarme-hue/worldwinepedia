Bellavista

Bellavista Satèn

SparklingFranciacorta Satèn DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Franciacorta

Bellavista’s Franciacorta Satèn, a white-grape sparkling wine with lower pressure and especially soft bubbles.

Satèn is a category unique to Franciacorta, with no exact equivalent elsewhere. The rules require it to be made only from white grapes, Chardonnay and possibly Pinot Bianco, and to have a lower bottle pressure than other Franciacorta, which gives a more delicate bubble and a silky texture, the origin of its name. It is also always released in the brut category.

Bellavista’s Satèn is made from Chardonnay from several plots on the estate. Part of the base wines ferments in small oak barrels. The wine then undergoes its second fermentation in bottle by the traditional method and stays on its lees for an extended period before disgorgement.

It is a soft, creamy sparkling wine with a delicate profile and notes of white fruit, flowers and nuts. Its texture makes it particularly pleasant with fish dishes and delicate cooking.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow with golden hints, very fine bubbles. Aromas of white peach, flowers, almond and pastry. Soft, silky, fresh palate with a delicate finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, shellfish, raw fish, risotto, fried food, soft cheeses

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass or flute
