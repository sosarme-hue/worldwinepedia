Château Lafon-Rochet

Les Pèlerins de Lafon-Rochet

RedSecond wineAOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Saint-Estèphe

Second wine of Château Lafon-Rochet, a fruitier, more approachable Saint-Estèphe than the grand vin.

Les Pèlerins de Lafon-Rochet is the second wine of Château Lafon-Rochet, a fourth growth in Saint-Estèphe. It shares its vineyard, team and winemaking philosophy with the grand vin.

It comes from the estate's gravel and clay vineyards, with a selection that usually includes younger vines and lots not retained for the grand vin. The blend combines Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, the latter often with a greater share than in the first wine, bringing roundness and fruit.

With shorter ageing and gentler extraction, it offers the character of Saint-Estèphe — black fruit, freshness and structure — in an earlier-drinking version. It can be enjoyed in its first few years, without the long wait required by the grand vin. It is also a good way to approach the style of the estate at a more accessible level.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, plum and spicy notes. Fruity palate with rounded tannins and good freshness.

Food pairing

Grilled meats, duck, charcuterie, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, Bordeaux glass
