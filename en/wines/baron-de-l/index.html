Baron de L

WhitePouilly-FuméWine region Loire

The prestige cuvée of the de Ladoucette house: a Pouilly-Fumé made from a selection of the best parcels and made in smaller quantities.

Baron de L is the prestige wine of the de Ladoucette house, created by Baron Patrick de Ladoucette as the ultimate expression of Pouilly-Fumé from the Château du Nozet. It is made from a selection of the best-sited parcels and of the ripest, healthiest grapes of each harvest.

Like all Pouilly-Fumé, it is a Sauvignon Blanc white. The selection of parcels and grapes, stricter than for the main wine, seeks greater concentration and depth while keeping the freshness typical of the appellation. Winemaking favours purity of fruit and lees ageing.

It is a broader and more complex white than the house’s classic Pouilly-Fumé, with greater ageing potential. With a few years in bottle it can develop deeper notes while retaining an acidity that gives it length.

Tasting profile

Pale gold. Aromas of ripe white fruit, citrus, flowers and stony notes. Broad, fresh and persistent palate.

Food pairing

Turbot, scallops, lobster, poultry in cream sauce

Serving

12-13 °C, large white wine glass
