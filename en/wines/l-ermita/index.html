L'Ermita

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Priorat

Single-vineyard wine from Álvaro Palacios from a slope of old Garnacha in Gratallops, one of Spain's most prestigious reds.

L'Ermita is Álvaro Palacios' most famous wine and one of the reds that symbolise the revival of the Priorat. It comes from a small hillside vineyard in the municipality of Gratallops that takes its name from a nearby hermitage. In the Priorat D.O.Ca. classification by levels of origin, it is recognised as a Gran Vinya Classificada.

The vineyard is planted mainly with very old Garnacha vines, with a small presence of other varieties, and faces north, an exposure that helps retain freshness in the warm Priorat. It grows on llicorella, on slopes that require work by hand or with mules. Yields are very low. Over the years, winemaking has sought a more transparent expression of the site, with more discreet oak ageing. It is a wine made in very limited quantities and with great ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby red. Aromas of red and black fruit, dried flowers, wild herbs, spice and a marked mineral background. Fine and deep on the palate, with silky tannins and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Game birds, milk-fed lamb, truffle and mushroom dishes, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
