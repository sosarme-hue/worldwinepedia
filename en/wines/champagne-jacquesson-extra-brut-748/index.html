Champagne Jacquesson

Champagne Jacquesson Extra Brut 748

SparklingExtra Brut

Numbered cuvée from Jacquesson, the Dizy house, built on the 2020 harvest and bottled with very low dosage in an extra brut style.

Jacquesson is a Champagne house founded in 1798 and based in Dizy, next to Aÿ, in the Vallée de la Marne. Owned by the Chiquet family since the 1970s, it is known for having replaced its classic non-vintage brut with the numbered 700 series of cuvées, and for its single-vineyard Champagnes, made from estate grapes in grand cru and premier cru villages.

The 700 series began with cuvée 728, based on the 2000 harvest, and each new edition receives a consecutive number. Rather than aiming for an identical style every year, as traditional non-vintage bruts do, each cuvée starts from a specific harvest, completed with reserve wines, and reflects the character of that year. Cuvée 748 is thus built on the 2020 harvest.

It is made from Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier, aged for a long time on the lees and given a very low dosage, so that the wine comes across as dry, precise and vinous, with fruit and origin taking precedence over sugar.

Tasting profile

Pale gold with fine bubbles. Aromas of apple, pear, citrus, nuts and toasted bread. Vinous and dry on the palate, with firm acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, shellfish, fish in sauce, poultry, hard aged cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
