Gérard Bertrand Gris Blanc

RoséIGP Pays d’OcWine region Languedoc-Roussillon

A very pale rosé from Gérard Bertrand made from Grenache Gris, in a style close to that of a white wine.

Gris Blanc takes its name from Grenache Gris, a colour mutation of Grenache with greyish-pink skins, traditional in the south of France. It is sold under the Pays d’Oc protected geographical indication, which covers the whole of Languedoc-Roussillon.

The grapes come from vineyards near the sea, where the breeze helps moderate the heat. To obtain its very pale colour, the must has hardly any contact with the skins: it is pressed directly, like a white wine, and fermented at low temperatures.

The result is a dry, light, fresh wine, almost white in colour with pink glints, best enjoyed young and well chilled, as an aperitif or with light dishes.

Tasting profile

Very pale pink, almost white. Aromas of grapefruit, white peach, light red fruit and flowers. Dry, light and lively on the palate.

Food pairing

Aperitif, shellfish, ceviche, salads, sushi

Serving

7-9 °C, white wine glass
