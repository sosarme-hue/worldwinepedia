Henschke Hill of Grace

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Eden Valley

Henschke single-vineyard Shiraz from a plot with vines planted in the nineteenth century opposite a Lutheran church in the Eden Valley. It is one of Australia’s most prestigious reds.

The Hill of Grace vineyard lies near Keyneton, in the Eden Valley, opposite the Gnadenberg Lutheran church, whose German name means “hill of grace” and has given the wine its name. The oldest vines, planted in the nineteenth century on their own roots, are known in the family as the “grandfathers”. Cyril Henschke first bottled this vineyard separately in the 1950s.

The wine is made exclusively from Shiraz from this plot and is aged in barrel for an extended period. Stephen and Prue Henschke have spent decades caring for these old vines, with organic and biodynamic practices and by selecting planting material from the best vines for replanting. The style combines concentration and finesse, with a spicy, elegant profile that sets Eden Valley Shiraz apart from that of warmer areas. It is a wine for long ageing.

Tasting profile

Ripe black and red fruit, pepper, exotic spice and floral and herbal notes. Concentrated yet fine palate, with silky tannins, good acidity and great length.

Food pairing

Herb-roasted lamb, duck, game, mushrooms

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
