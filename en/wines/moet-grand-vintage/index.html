Moët & Chandon

Moët & Chandon Grand Vintage

SparklingVintage BrutAOC (sparkling wine) Champagne

Moët & Chandon’s vintage champagne, made from grapes from a single harvest that the house considers worthy of its own wine.

Grand Vintage is Moët & Chandon’s vintage champagne. Unlike Moët Impérial, which seeks a consistent style from year to year thanks to reserve wines, each Grand Vintage is made from grapes from a single harvest and aims to express the character of that year. It is produced only in vintages the house considers suitable.

It is made from the three Champagne varieties, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier, in proportions that change considerably from one vintage to another according to the quality of each variety that year. After the second fermentation in bottle, the wine is aged for several years on its lees in the Épernay cellars, well beyond the minimum required for vintage champagnes.

Its profile varies with the harvest, but it usually shows more complexity and ageing notes than Impérial, with aromas of ripe fruit, nuts and brioche and a broad, fresh palate.

Tasting profile

Gold with fine bubbles. Aromas of ripe fruit, citrus, toasted almond and brioche. Broad, fresh palate with good complexity and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Lobster, caviar, turbot, poultry with truffle, aged cheeses such as Comté

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass rather than a flute
