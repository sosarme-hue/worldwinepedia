Masseto

RedIGT ToscanaControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Bolgheri

Merlot red from the Bolgheri area, born within the Ornellaia orbit and regarded as one of the world’s great Merlots.

Masseto takes its name from a hill in the Bolgheri area on the Tuscan coast, whose compact, cool blue Pliocene clay soils proved particularly suited to Merlot. The wine was born in the 1980s within the Tenuta dell’Ornellaia and soon became one of Italy’s most renowned reds.

Over time, Masseto came to be run as an independent estate within the same group, with its own winery. It is made almost exclusively from Merlot, sometimes with a small proportion of Cabernet Franc, and aged in French oak barrels. It is sold under the IGT Toscana designation.

It is a red of great concentration and texture, with ripe black fruit, spicy notes and a dense yet polished tannic structure. It is designed for very long cellaring.

Tasting profile

Aromas of black plum, blackberry, cocoa, spice and balsamic notes. Dense, velvety palate with fine tannins and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Large game, fillet steak, roast lamb, long-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decanting is advisable
