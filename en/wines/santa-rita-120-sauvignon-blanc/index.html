Viña Santa Rita

Santa Rita 120 Sauvignon Blanc

WhiteDesignation of Origin (wine region grouping Maipo, Rapel, Curicó and Maule) Central Valley

Chilean Sauvignon Blanc from Santa Rita’s 120 line, a fresh, aromatic and affordable white.

The 120 line is the range of affordable wines from Viña Santa Rita, one of Chile’s historic wineries, based in Alto Jahuel in the Maipo Valley. Its name recalls an episode from Chilean independence: in 1814, after the Battle of Rancagua, 120 patriot soldiers took refuge in the cellars of the Santa Rita estate, sheltered by its owner, Paula Jaraquemada.

The line’s Sauvignon Blanc is made from grapes grown in central Chile, with the Central Valley designation. Fermentation takes place at low temperature in stainless steel tanks, with no oak, to preserve the variety’s citrus and herbaceous aromas. It is a light, dry and refreshing white, intended to be drunk within a year or so of the harvest. Its lively, direct profile makes it a common choice as an aperitif and as a partner for the fish and shellfish dishes of the Chilean coast.

Tasting profile

Pale greenish yellow. Aromas of lemon, grapefruit, fresh grass and tropical fruit. Light, lively and dry palate.

Food pairing

Salads, ceviche, shellfish, fresh goat’s cheese

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
