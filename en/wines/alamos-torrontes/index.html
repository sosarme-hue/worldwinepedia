Alamos Torrontés

WhiteWine-producing province Salta

Torrontés from the Alamos brand of the Catena family: an aromatic, dry and fresh white made to be drunk young.

Alamos is the Catena family brand devoted to accessible varietal wines, and it has become one of the most widely exported Argentine labels. Within the range, the Torrontés represents the country’s most characteristic white grape, Torrontés Riojano, whose reference area is the high valleys of northern Argentina, above all in Salta.

It is made without oak and fermented at low temperature to preserve the variety’s floral and fruity aromas. It is a dry, light and easy-drinking white, best enjoyed young while it keeps all its freshness.

Torrontés stands out for an aromatic profile reminiscent of Muscat, yet in its dry version it is far more versatile at the table than its aroma suggests. It pairs well with spiced dishes and with the cooking of northern Argentina, and also works as an aperitif.

Tasting profile

Aromas of white flowers, rose, peach and citrus. Dry, light palate with fresh acidity and an aromatic finish.

Food pairing

Asian cuisine, ceviche, salads, empanadas, as an aperitif

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
