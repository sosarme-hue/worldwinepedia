Maison Louis Jadot

Louis Jadot Beaujolais-Villages Combe aux Jacques

RedBeaujolais-VillagesWine region (AOC Beaujolais, Beaujolais-Villages and ten crus) Beaujolais

Louis Jadot’s Beaujolais-Villages, a fruity, fresh Gamay that is easy to find and designed to be drunk young.

Combe aux Jacques is the Beaujolais-Villages of Louis Jadot, a house with a long presence in Beaujolais, where it owns Château des Jacques in Moulin-à-Vent. The Beaujolais-Villages appellation groups a number of communes in the north of the region, often on granitic soils, considered of higher quality than generic Beaujolais.

The wine is made exclusively from Gamay, with a vinification aimed at preserving the variety’s fruit and freshness, and it is bottled young, without extended ageing.

The result is a light, juicy and very fruity red with good acidity, ideal for drinking in its early years and slightly chilled.

Gamay, a thin-skinned, early-ripening variety, finds one of its preferred terrains in the granitic soils of northern Beaujolais.

Tasting profile

Purplish ruby. Aromas of cherry, raspberry, redcurrant and floral notes. Light, juicy and fresh on the palate, with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, pâté, roast chicken, bistro cooking, pizza

Serving

13-15 °C, medium red wine glass
