Maison Joseph Drouhin

Laforêt Bourgogne Pinot Noir

RedWine region Burgundy

Red Bourgogne from Joseph Drouhin, a Pinot Noir from the Laforêt range, light and fruity, intended to be drunk young.

Within the Laforêt range, which Joseph Drouhin devotes to Burgundy’s regional wines, this red represents the most accessible face of Pinot Noir. The house, founded in Beaune in 1880, is both a vineyard owner and a négociant, and buys grapes and must from growers in different parts of the region to supply this range.

The regional Bourgogne appellation allows grapes of very varied origins within Burgundy to be blended, which gives a wine of consistent style from year to year. Vinification seeks above all to preserve the fresh fruit and lightness typical of the variety, with little or no oak influence.

It is a light- to medium-bodied red, with soft tannins and good acidity, offering a first introduction to the style of Burgundian Pinot Noir. It is best enjoyed in its first few years and can be served slightly cool, which makes it very versatile at the table.

Tasting profile

Light ruby. Aromas of strawberry, cherry and raspberry. Light, juicy palate with soft tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, pizza, roast poultry, grilled salmon, mild cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, red wine glass
