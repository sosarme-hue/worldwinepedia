Graeme & Julie Bott

Bott Condrieu Magnum

WhiteAOC (Northern Rhône cru) Condrieu

Condrieu from Graeme and Julie Bott in magnum, a northern Rhône Viognier white aged in wood.

Graeme and Julie Bott are a winegrowing couple based in Ampuis, in the northern Rhône valley, who set up their own domaine in the second half of the 2010s after working at wineries in the area. They farm small plots on the slopes of this part of the valley and make wines in small quantities, with careful intervention and a style that seeks freshness and definition rather than power.

The Condrieu appellation, on the right bank of the Rhône south of Côte-Rôtie, is devoted exclusively to whites from Viognier, the variety that was saved here from near abandonment in the mid-20th century. Its vineyards occupy very steep slopes, in terraces held up by stone walls, on decomposed granite soils known locally as arzelle.

This Condrieu is made from Viognier, with fermentation and ageing in wood that add volume and texture. The magnum format favours slower, more even development of the wine in bottle.

Tasting profile

Aromas of apricot, peach, white flowers and violet, with a touch of spice. Unctuous yet fresh on the palate, with good texture and a slightly bitter finish.

Food pairing

River fish, prawns, poultry in cream sauce, gently spiced dishes, goat’s cheeses

Serving

11-13 °C, large white wine glass
