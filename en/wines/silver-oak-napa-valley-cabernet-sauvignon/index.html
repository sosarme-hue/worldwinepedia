Silver Oak Cellars

Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon

RedAVA Napa Valley

Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon aged exclusively in American oak and released after long ageing in barrel and bottle.

The Silver Oak Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon is one of the two wines the winery makes. The grapes come from vineyards in different parts of Napa Valley, and the wine is based on Cabernet Sauvignon, to which small proportions of other Bordeaux varieties may be added.

Its defining feature is the ageing: it takes place exclusively in American oak barrels, which contribute aromas of vanilla, coconut and sweet spice, and lasts a considerable time before an additional period of rest in bottle. The winery thus aims to offer a wine that can be enjoyed on release, with its tannins already softened. Compared with the Alexander Valley wine, the Napa wine usually shows more structure and greater ageing potential.

Its consistent style makes it a good reference point for understanding the effect of American oak on Cabernet Sauvignon.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby red. Aromas of black cherry, blackcurrant, vanilla, coconut and sweet spice. Broad and structured on the palate, with firm yet rounded tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, roast lamb, rib steak, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; may be decanted
