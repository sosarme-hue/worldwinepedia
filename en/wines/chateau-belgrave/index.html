Château Belgrave

Red5th Classified GrowthBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

Fifth classified growth of 1855 in the Haut-Médoc appellation, owned by Dourthe, made from Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot.

Château Belgrave is located in Saint-Laurent-Médoc, in the Haut-Médoc appellation, close to the boundary with Saint-Julien. It is listed as a fifth growth in the 1855 classification and belongs to Dourthe, one of the leading Bordeaux houses, which acquired it in the late 1970s and has since carried out a major renovation of the vineyard and cellar.

The vineyard, on Médoc gravel, is planted mainly with Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, with a small share of other Bordeaux varieties. The wine is aged in oak barrels for several months, including a share of new wood, and the proportions of the blend vary with the vintage.

It is a red in classic Médoc style, with dark fruit, cedar notes and a firm tannic structure. It gains complexity with some years of bottle age, as the tannins integrate and hints of tobacco and forest floor emerge.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet. Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, cedar and toasty spice. Structured palate with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, rib-eye steak, duck breast, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large Bordeaux glass
