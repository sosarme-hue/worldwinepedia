Codorníu

Anna de Codorníu Blanc de Blancs

SparklingBrutDesignation of Origin (DO) Cava

Codorníu cava combining Chardonnay with the traditional Penedès varieties, one of the most popular and widely available cavas.

The name of this cava pays tribute to Anna de Codorníu, the family heiress who in the 17th century joined her line to that of the Raventós family. Codorníu was among the first houses to introduce Chardonnay into cava, and this label became the symbol of that choice.

It is a blanc de blancs, that is, made only from white grapes: Chardonnay together with Parellada, Xarel·lo and Macabeo. It follows the traditional method, with a second fermentation in bottle and ageing on the lees in the cellars of Sant Sadurní d'Anoia. The result is a fresh, fruity sparkling wine with fine bubbles and a touch of creaminess from the Chardonnay, very versatile as an aperitif and at the table.

Tasting profile

White and gentle tropical fruit, citrus and a light hint of pastry. Fresh palate, fine bubbles and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Appetisers, canapés, sushi, light fish dishes, salads

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine or tulip glass
