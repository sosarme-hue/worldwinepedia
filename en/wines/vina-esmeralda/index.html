Viña Esmeralda

White

Aromatic white from Familia Torres made from Moscatel and Gewürztraminer, one of the most popular Spanish whites for aperitif drinking.

Viña Esmeralda is one of Familia Torres' best-selling whites and owes its name to the emerald-green colour of its bottle. It belongs to the Catalunya D.O. and was created as a fresh, aromatic wine for drinking young, at a time when Spanish whites tended to be more neutral.

It is made from Muscat of Alexandria and Gewürztraminer, two highly aromatic varieties; the proportions may vary with the vintage. Muscat contributes notes of fresh grapes and flowers, and Gewürztraminer adds roses and spice. Winemaking takes place at controlled temperatures in stainless steel tanks, without oak, to preserve the primary aromas. It is a light white with moderate alcohol and a touch of roundness that balances its acidity, making it very easy to drink. It is best enjoyed in the year after the harvest.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with greenish hints. Intense aromas of Muscat grape, rose petals, lychee and citrus. Light, fresh and slightly mellow on the palate.

Food pairing

Aperitifs, spicy Asian cuisine, salads with fruit, shellfish

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
