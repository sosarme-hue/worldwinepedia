Delas Frères

Cornas Chante-Perdrix

RedAOC (Northern Rhône cru) Cornas

Cornas from Delas Frères, a Syrah red from the granite slopes of Cornas on the right bank of the northern Rhône.

Chante-Perdrix is Delas Frères’ Cornas. The Cornas appellation, on the right bank of the Rhône, south of Saint-Joseph and opposite Valence, allows only red wines made exclusively from Syrah. Its vines grow on steep, sheltered slopes, often terraced, on decomposed granite soils.

This setting, warmer than other parts of the northern valley, traditionally produces deeply coloured wines with firm tannins and a rustic character in youth. Delas aims for a more precise expression of Syrah, with careful winemaking and barrel ageing that softens the structure without masking the fruit.

It is a red built for cellaring. Young, it shows black fruit, violet and spicy notes; after a few years in bottle it gains complexity, with aromas of black olive, leather and forest floor typical of Cornas Syrah.

Tasting profile

Deep purple. Aromas of blackberry, blackcurrant, violet, pepper and smoky notes. Concentrated palate with firm tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Game, grilled red meat, braised dishes, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
