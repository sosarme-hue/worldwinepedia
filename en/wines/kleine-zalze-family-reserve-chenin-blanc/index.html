Kleine Zalze

Kleine Zalze Family Reserve Chenin Blanc

WhiteWine of Origin (WO) Stellenbosch

Top-tier Chenin Blanc from Kleine Zalze, from old Stellenbosch bush vines, fermented and aged in barrel.

The Family Reserve is the top tier of the Kleine Zalze range, and its Chenin Blanc is the wine that best sums up the house speciality. Compared with the fresh, straightforward style of the Cellar Selection, this wine aims to show the depth South African Chenin Blanc can reach when it comes from old vines and is barrel-aged.

The grapes come from plots of old, dry-farmed bush vines in the Stellenbosch area, which give low yields and concentrated grapes. The juice is fermented in French oak barrels, partly with indigenous yeasts, and the wine is aged for several months on its lees with periodic stirring, which gives it texture and complexity. The result is a broad, structured white with ripe fruit, toasty notes and an acidity that allows it to develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Aromas of quince, yellow peach and pineapple, with notes of honey, vanilla and toasted nuts. Broad and creamy, with firm acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Lobster, roast pork, chicken in cream sauce, ripe soft cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
