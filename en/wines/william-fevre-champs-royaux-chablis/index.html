Domaine William Fèvre

William Fèvre Champs Royaux Chablis

WhiteAOC (Petit Chablis, Chablis, Chablis Premier Cru and Chablis Grand Cru) Chablis

Chablis from William Fèvre’s négociant range, made from Chardonnay supplied by growers in the appellation and aged in tank to preserve its freshness.

Champs Royaux is the name under which William Fèvre sells its négociant Chablis, made from grapes or musts bought from growers in the appellation with whom the house works regularly. Unlike the domaine wines, it does not come from estate vineyards, but it is vinified at the house’s facilities in Chablis and follows its style.

It is made solely from Chardonnay from plots with limestone and marl soils in the Chablis appellation. Fermentation and ageing take place in stainless steel tanks, without oak, to preserve the purity of the fruit and the acidity characteristic of the area, which gives Chablis wines their taut, saline profile.

It is a classic Chablis, dry and fresh, with notes of citrus and green apple and a mineral touch. It is meant to be drunk young, in the first years after the harvest, and is one of the most widely available Chablis in shops.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green hints. Aromas of lemon, green apple and white flowers, with a touch of wet stone. Dry, light, taut palate with lively acidity and a saline finish.

Food pairing

Oysters, shellfish, grilled fish, sushi, goat’s cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
