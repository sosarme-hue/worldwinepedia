Golan Heights Winery

Yarden HeightsWine

SweetWine region Golan Heights

Sweet Gewürztraminer wine from Golan Heights Winery, made by freezing the grapes to concentrate sugars and aromas, in the style of an ice wine.

On the Golan Heights, winters are not cold enough to make a true ice wine, in which the grapes freeze naturally on the vine. Golan Heights Winery therefore turned to another technique for its HeightsWine, sold under the Yarden brand: after harvest, very ripe Gewürztraminer grapes are frozen in the winery and pressed while still frozen.

As with traditional ice wine, the water remains in the form of crystals, and the juice obtained is scant and highly concentrated in sugars, acids and aromas. Fermentation is stopped before all the sugar is consumed, giving a sweet wine of moderate alcohol. Gewürztraminer brings its characteristic aromatic intensity, with notes of lychee, rose and spice, balanced by the acidity retained by grapes grown at altitude. It is an aromatic, unctuous dessert wine.

Tasting profile

Intense aromas of lychee, rose petals, apricot and honey, with a touch of ginger. Sweet and unctuous, balanced by fresh acidity.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses, fruit tarts, nut desserts

Serving

8-10 °C, small glass
