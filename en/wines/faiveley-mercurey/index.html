Domaine Faiveley

Domaine Faiveley Mercurey Rouge

RedWine region Burgundy

Pinot Noir red from the Mercurey appellation, in the Côte Chalonnaise, made by Domaine Faiveley, one of the leading vineyard owners in the village.

Mercurey is the largest appellation of the Côte Chalonnaise, the area south of the Côte d’Or, and produces mainly Pinot Noir reds. The Faiveley family, based in Nuits-Saint-Georges, has long been one of the leading vineyard owners in the village, where it holds both village appellation plots and premiers crus.

This wine is made from Pinot Noir from the vines the domaine farms in Mercurey, on clay and limestone soils. The grapes are picked by hand and sorted and, after fermentation, the wine is aged in French oak barrels with a moderate proportion of new wood.

It is a medium-bodied red with red and black fruit, spicy notes and a slightly firmer structure than a regional Bourgogne, offering a good balance between character and price within the region. It can be enjoyed a few years after the harvest and develops well for some years.

Tasting profile

Intense ruby. Aromas of cherry, blackcurrant and spice, with an earthy touch. Medium-bodied palate with firm but supple tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Roast duck, veal, boeuf bourguignon, mushrooms, cheeses such as Époisses

Serving

14-16 °C, Burgundy glass
