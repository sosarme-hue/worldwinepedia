Hamilton Russell Vineyards

Hamilton Russell Vineyards Pinot Noir

RedThree wards (WO) in the Walker Bay district Hemel-en-Aarde

Pinot Noir from the pioneering estate in the Hemel-en-Aarde Valley, one of South Africa's most recognised Burgundy-inspired reds.

Hamilton Russell Vineyards makes a single Pinot Noir, sourced exclusively from its vineyards in the Hemel-en-Aarde Valley, near Hermanus, on the Cape's south coast. The estate was one of the first to commit to this variety in South Africa, in a cool area where Atlantic winds temper summer temperatures.

The vineyards grow on stony, poor, clay-rich soils derived from shale, which limit the vigour of the vine and produce small, concentrated grapes. Harvesting is done by hand and the winemaking follows a Burgundy-inspired approach: fermentation in open-top vessels and ageing in French oak barrels with a moderate proportion of new oak. The result is a medium-bodied Pinot Noir focused more on texture, tension and earthy notes than on fruit exuberance, with good ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Aromas of cherry, raspberry and red plum, with notes of forest floor, spice and damp earth. Fine and taut, with silky tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Duck, squab, mushrooms, roast salmon

Serving

14-16 °C, Burgundy glass
