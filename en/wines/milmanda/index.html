Familia Torres

Milmanda

WhiteDesignation of Origin (DO) Conca de Barberà

Estate Chardonnay from Familia Torres from the Conca de Barberà, beside Milmanda castle near the monastery of Poblet.

Milmanda is Familia Torres' great white and one of Spain's benchmark Chardonnays. It comes from the estate surrounding Milmanda castle, a medieval tower in the Conca de Barberà D.O., in Tarragona, very close to the Cistercian monastery of Poblet, whose monks grew vines in the area for centuries.

The Conca de Barberà is a basin surrounded by hills, with a Mediterranean climate of continental influence and cool nights that help the grapes retain acidity. The Chardonnay vineyards grow on calcareous and clay soils. The wine is made in the style of white Burgundy: the must ferments in French oak barrels and the wine is aged in them on its lees for several months. The result is a broad-textured, highly complex white with the freshness brought by the local climate, able to develop for years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Bright golden yellow. Aromas of white and stone fruit, ripe citrus, nuts, butter and toasty notes. Broad and unctuous on the palate, with fresh acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fine fish, lobster, poultry in sauce, mushrooms, creamy cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
