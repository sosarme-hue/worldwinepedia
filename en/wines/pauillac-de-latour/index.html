Château Latour

Pauillac de Latour

RedBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The third wine of Château Latour, made from the cuvées that go into neither the grand vin nor Les Forts de Latour, lighter in profile and meant to be drunk earlier.

Pauillac de Latour is the third wine of Château Latour. It results from the selection the estate makes every year: the cuvées not considered suitable for the grand vin or for Les Forts de Latour, often from young vines or secondary plots of the property within the Pauillac appellation, are brought together in this blend.

Like the house’s other wines, it is based on Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, in proportions that change with the vintage, and is aged in barrel, though with less new oak. Its existence allows the selection for the first two wines to be stricter.

It is a classic Pauillac red in profile, with black fruit and structure, but lighter and more supple than its older siblings, so it can usually be enjoyed after a few years in bottle, even though it is not meant for such long ageing.

Tasting profile

Intense ruby. Aromas of black fruit, cedar and a hint of smoke. Medium-bodied palate with firm but already integrated tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, roast duck, meat stews, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, Bordeaux glass
