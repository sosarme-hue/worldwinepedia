Disznókő

Disznókö Tokaji Aszú 6 Puttonyos 50 cl.

Sweet6 PuttonyosTokai

A Tokaji Aszú from the Disznókő estate at Mezőzombor, made from botrytised berries of Furmint and other Tokaj varieties; the 6 Puttonyos mention denotes one of the richest levels of the category.

Disznókő is a historic estate in the Tokaj region of north-eastern Hungary, located at Mezőzombor at the southern tip of the Zemplén hills. Since 1992 it has belonged to AXA Millésimes, which restored it after the socialist era. Its name, "boar's stone", refers to a large volcanic rock rising among its vineyards.

Tokaji Aszú is Hungary's most famous sweet wine. It is made from aszú berries, grapes affected by noble rot (Botrytis cinerea) and shrivelled on the vine, which are picked berry by berry and macerated in must or base wine before a slow fermentation. The main varieties are Furmint, which brings very high acidity, and the more aromatic Hárslevelű, along with Zéta and Sárgamuskotály.

The 6 Puttonyos mention, derived from the traditional baskets once used to measure the proportion of aszú berries, corresponds to the most concentrated Aszú wines. After barrel ageing in the region's underground cellars, the result is a very sweet wine, balanced by Furmint's lively acidity and capable of great longevity.

Tasting profile

Golden amber. Aromas of dried apricot, candied orange, honey, quince and spice. Very sweet, concentrated palate supported by vibrant acidity, with a very long finish.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses, apricot tart, nut-based desserts

Serving

8-10 °C, small dessert wine glass
