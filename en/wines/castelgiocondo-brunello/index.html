Castelgiocondo Brunello di Montalcino

RedBrunello di Montalcino DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Brunello di Montalcino

The Brunello di Montalcino from the Castelgiocondo estate, owned by the Frescobaldi family in south-western Montalcino, a long-aged Sangiovese.

Castelgiocondo is an estate in the south-western part of the Montalcino municipality, around an old castle, with hillside vineyards at different altitudes. It belongs to the Frescobaldi family, who have made it their production centre for Brunello.

As the appellation requires, the wine is made exclusively from Sangiovese, the variety traditionally known in Montalcino as Brunello. After fermentation it is aged for an extended period in oak and then in bottle; under the regulations, Brunello cannot be released until five years after the harvest.

The result is a structured red with firm tannins and good acidity, and the ripe fruit typical of southern Tuscany. It can be enjoyed on release, but its structure allows it to evolve for years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Medium-depth garnet. Aromas of ripe cherry, plum and dried fruit, with notes of spice, leather and tobacco. Broad and firm on the palate, with pronounced tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast and braised red meat, game, mushrooms, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; best opened in advance
