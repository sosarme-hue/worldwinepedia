Schloss Johannisberg

Schloss Johannisberg Riesling Silberlack

WhiteGrosses Gewächs (VDP), dryAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Rheingau

Dry Grosses Gewächs Riesling from Schloss Johannisberg, identified by its silver seal and grown in the vineyard surrounding the palace.

Schloss Johannisberg distinguishes its wines by the colour of their seal or capsule, a tradition going back to the nineteenth century. The silver Silberlack denotes the top-category dry Riesling, the Grosses Gewächs, from the vineyard surrounding the palace, on a south-facing slope above the Rhine with quartzite and loess soils.

The grapes are picked by hand and selected from the best plots in the vineyard. The wine is fermented and aged in the historic cellars beneath the palace, with winemaking that seeks to preserve purity of fruit and the expression of the vineyard. It is a dry Riesling of medium to full body, with the structure typical of the Rheingau, and benefits from a few years’ bottle ageing to express its full complexity.

It is best not served too cold and left to breathe in the glass, as its aromas open up with air; with a few years in bottle it usually gains complexity.

Tasting profile

Aromas of peach, ripe citrus, herbs and spicy, mineral notes. Dry and structured on the palate, with firm acidity, good concentration and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, poultry, veal, freshwater fish, asparagus

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
