Hatzidakis

Hatzidakis Santorini Assyrtiko

WhiteProtected designation of origin (PDO) Santorini

Santorini designation Assyrtiko made by Hatzidakis from organically farmed grapes, with very lively acidity and marked mineral character.

This wine is Hatzidakis’ core bottling in the Santorini designation, showing the character of Assyrtiko in the island’s volcanic setting. The winery, founded in 1997 in Pyrgos, works with organic viticulture and minimal-intervention winemaking.

The grapes come from old vines grown on soils of volcanic ash, pumice and lava, poor in organic matter and very free-draining. On Santorini the vines are traditionally trained into baskets at ground level to protect them from wind and sun, and phylloxera never established itself on the island, so many vineyards keep very old root systems. Yields are very low. The wine ferments with native yeasts and rests on its lees before bottling, without new-oak ageing.

On the island Assyrtiko keeps very high acidity despite the summer heat, giving taut, saline whites of firm body. It can be drunk young but develops well in bottle for several years.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of lemon, grapefruit, wet stone, saline notes and a touch of herbs. Taut, firm palate with very marked acidity, a strong mineral sensation and a long finish.

Food pairing

Grilled fish, shellfish, octopus, Mediterranean dishes with lemon and olive oil, fresh cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
