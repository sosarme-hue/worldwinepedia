Saxenburg

Saxenburg Private Collection Cabernet Sauvignon

RedWine of Origin (WO) Stellenbosch

Saxenburg Private Collection Cabernet Sauvignon is a red wine from Saxenburg, made under the WO Stellenbosch appellation in South Africa.

It is made from Cabernet Sauvignon. It is aged in wood. It has 13.5% ABV.

Stellenbosch, east of Cape Town, is South Africa’s best-known wine district and the centre of its wine education and research. It stands out for Cabernet Sauvignon, Bordeaux blends, Pinotage and Chenin Blanc.

Food pairing

Red meat, roasts, stews, aged cheeses

Serving

15-18 °C, large glass
