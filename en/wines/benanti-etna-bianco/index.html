Benanti

Benanti Etna Bianco

WhiteEtna DOC BiancoControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Etna

Benanti’s Etna Bianco made from Carricante, the native white grape of the volcano’s slopes, with a fresh, mineral profile.

Carricante is the most characteristic white grape of Etna, where it has been grown for centuries, especially on the volcano’s eastern side, exposed to the influence of the Ionian Sea. It gives wines of high acidity, citrus aromas and notable capacity to evolve, which many compare with the great cool-climate whites.

Benanti, the Viagrande house that contributed to the modern revival of Etna wines, makes its Etna Bianco from vineyards at altitude on the volcano’s slopes, on volcanic sand and rock. The grapes are vinified in stainless steel at controlled temperature, and the wine rests on its fine lees before bottling, without oak ageing.

It is a white that is light in body but intense, with lively acidity and a savoury finish, which drinks well young but can also evolve in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale straw with green hints. Aromas of lemon, green apple, white flowers, aniseed and mineral notes. Taut, fresh, savoury palate.

Food pairing

Baked fish, shellfish, pasta with clams, grilled vegetables, fresh cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
