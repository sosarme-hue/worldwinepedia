Le Clos des Fées

De battre mon cœur s'est arrêté

RedCôtes du Roussillon VillagesWine region Languedoc-Roussillon

Top-tier cuvée from Le Clos des Fées, named after a well-known French film, from a selection of plots around Vingrau.

De battre mon cœur s’est arrêté is one of the most ambitious cuvées of Le Clos des Fées, the estate founded by Hervé Bizeul in Vingrau. Its name, the same as that of a well-known French film, illustrates the house’s taste for evocative, literary labels.

It is made from a selection of the estate’s plots on the limestone and stony slopes of northern Roussillon, an area with a dry, very sunny Mediterranean climate exposed to the tramontane wind. The low-yielding vines are picked by hand and the grapes are rigorously sorted. The varietal make-up reflects the Mediterranean red varieties grown on the estate.

It belongs to the Côtes du Roussillon Villages appellation and is aged in barrel for an extended period. Like the house’s other great cuvées, it is a concentrated red with firm structure, able to develop for years in bottle; when young it benefits from decanting.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet colour. Aromas of black fruit, spice, smoky notes and garrigue. Dense, structured palate with fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Large game, roast lamb, red meat, mature cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, large glass; decant
