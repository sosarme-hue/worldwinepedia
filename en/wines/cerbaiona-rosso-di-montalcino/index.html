Cerbaiona

Cerbaiona Rosso di Montalcino

RedDOC Rosso di Montalcino

Sangiovese Rosso di Montalcino from Cerbaiona, the earlier-drinking expression of Sangiovese from this small Montalcino estate.

Cerbaiona is a small estate on the eastern slope of the hill of Montalcino, a short distance from the town, with vineyards at good altitude on stony soils. For decades it was associated with Diego Molinari, who bought it in the 1970s and made it one of the benchmarks of classic-style Brunello, and in the mid-2010s it passed to new owners, who have kept its traditional approach.

The winery makes few wines, with long fermentations and ageing in large oak casks, seeking the transparency of Sangiovese rather than power or the imprint of oak.

Rosso di Montalcino is made, like Brunello, exclusively from Sangiovese from the municipality of Montalcino, but under much shorter ageing rules that allow it to be released from the year after the harvest. It is usually the freshest, most approachable expression of each estate’s Sangiovese.

Cerbaiona’s version is aged in wood and has 14% ABV. It keeps the house’s classic style, with good acidity and fine tannins, and can be enjoyed earlier than the Brunello, although it also develops well for a few years.

Tasting profile

Ruby red. Aromas of cherry, red berries, violet, aromatic herbs and a spicy touch. Fresh and juicy on the palate, with lively acidity, fine tannins and a savoury finish.

Food pairing

Pasta with ragù, grilled meat, Tuscan charcuterie, pecorino

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
