Fendant La Liaudisaz

WhiteAOC Valais Valais

A Fendant (Chasselas) from Fully made by Marie-Thérèse Chappaz from biodynamically farmed vines.

Fendant is the name given to Chasselas in the Valais, and it is the canton’s traditional white wine. Marie-Thérèse Chappaz makes it from vines in Fully, on the right bank of the Rhône, farmed according to biodynamic principles like the rest of her estate. La Liaudisaz is the name of a site in the area.

Far from the image of a simple wine that sometimes clings to Fendant, this white aims to show Chasselas’s ability to convey the character of its soil. The winemaking is low-intervention and respects the finesse of the variety, with ageing on the lees that adds texture. The result is a dry white with light to medium body, moderate acidity and a delicate aromatic profile. It can be drunk young, although it also develops well for a few years in bottle, gaining more complex notes.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of lime blossom, white flowers, pear and mineral notes. Fine, smooth palate with moderate acidity and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Raclette, fondue, lake fish, aperitifs

Serving

8-10 °C, white-wine glass
