Château d’Esclans

Château d’Esclans Whispering Angel

RoséCôtes de ProvenceWine region Provence

Château d’Esclans’ best-known rosé, a pale, dry Côtes de Provence that became a symbol of the international boom in Provence rosé.

Whispering Angel was born shortly after Sacha Lichine acquired Château d’Esclans in 2006 and became one of the best-selling and most recognisable rosés in the world.

It is made from grapes from the estate’s vineyards and local growers, mainly Grenache, Cinsault and Rolle (Vermentino), with other Mediterranean varieties in smaller proportions. The grapes are gently pressed and the must ferments at low temperatures in stainless-steel tanks, giving a very pale wine with fresh aromas.

It is a dry, light and fruity rosé, designed to be drunk young, as an aperitif or with Mediterranean food.

Côtes de Provence is the largest appellation in Provence, and most of its production is rosé. Its sunny, windy Mediterranean climate yields healthy grapes, and producers often harvest early or at night to retain freshness.

Tasting profile

Very pale pink. Aromas of strawberry, raspberry, grapefruit and white peach, with floral notes. Dry, fresh and smooth on the palate, with a clean finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, salads, shellfish, salmon, Mediterranean cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
