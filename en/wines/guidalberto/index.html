Guidalberto

RedToscana IGTWine region Tuscany

Tenuta San Guido’s second red, from Bolgheri Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, more approachable and meant to be drunk earlier than Sassicaia.

Guidalberto was created in the early 2000s to offer a red from the estate that would be more approachable than Sassicaia, in both style and price.

It is made from Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot from Tenuta San Guido’s vineyards in Bolgheri, in proportions that vary with the vintage. The Merlot brings roundness and fruit and sets it apart from Sassicaia, which uses only the two Cabernets. After fermentation the wine is aged for some months in barriques.

Although it is produced in Bolgheri, it is labelled as Toscana IGT. It is a red with generous fruit and gentle tannins, pleasant when young, that can also be cellared for a few years.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of ripe black and red fruit, with notes of aromatic herbs, spice and a light touch of vanilla. Round and balanced on the palate, with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled meat, lamb, pasta with ragù, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
