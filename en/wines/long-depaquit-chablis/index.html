Domaine Long-Depaquit Chablis

WhiteAOC (Petit Chablis, Chablis, Chablis Premier Cru and Chablis Grand Cru) Chablis

Chablis from Domaine Long-Depaquit, owned by the Albert Bichot house, made from Chardonnay from its own vineyards and aged mainly in tank.

Domaine Long-Depaquit is one of the historic estates of Chablis and belongs to the Albert Bichot house. It is based in the town of Chablis and owns vineyards in the various categories of the appellation, including La Moutonne, a grand cru plot lying between Vaudésir and Les Preuses.

Its Chablis is made from Chardonnay from its own plots in the village appellation, on the marl and limestone soils characteristic of the area. Fermentation and ageing take place mainly in stainless steel tanks, with a period on fine lees that adds texture, and, depending on the vintage, a small part may spend time in barrel.

It is a Chablis with a classic profile, dry and taut, with citrus and mineral notes and good length for its category. It can be enjoyed young and develops well for a few years.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green hints. Aromas of lemon, grapefruit, white flowers and chalky notes. Dry, fresh, taut palate with lively acidity and a mineral finish.

Food pairing

Oysters, shellfish, grilled fish, sushi, goat’s cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
