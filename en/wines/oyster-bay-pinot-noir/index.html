Oyster Bay

Oyster Bay Pinot Noir

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Marlborough

Marlborough Pinot Noir from Oyster Bay, a light, fruity red in an approachable style.

Alongside its well-known Sauvignon Blanc, Oyster Bay makes a Marlborough Pinot Noir that reflects the growing importance of this variety in New Zealand, where it has become the leading red grape. The region's cool climate, with abundant light and cold nights, favours wines with clear-cut fruit and lively acidity.

The grapes come from Marlborough vineyards, often from the slopes and valleys in the south of the region, where soils with more clay bring a little more structure. After a fermentation that aims to extract colour and fruit gently, the wine is aged for a short period, partly with French oak, to add nuance without hiding the character of the variety. It is a light- to medium-bodied Pinot Noir, soft and aromatic, intended to be enjoyed in its first few years and versatile at the table.

Tasting profile

Aromas of cherry, strawberry and red plum, with a touch of gentle spice. Light and silky, with fine tannins and fresh acidity.

Food pairing

Salmon, duck, pasta with mushrooms, roast poultry

Serving

14-16 °C, Burgundy glass
