Domaine Zind-Humbrecht

Pinot Gris Clos Windsbuhl

WhiteAlsaceWine region (AOC Alsace, Alsace Grand Cru and Crémant d'Alsace) Alsace

Zind-Humbrecht Pinot Gris from the Clos Windsbuhl in Hunawihr, a cool vineyard with limestone soils on the heights above the village.

The Clos Windsbuhl lies on the heights of Hunawihr, near Ribeauvillé, in an elevated and relatively cool area with limestone soils. Although it is not classified as a grand cru, it is one of the most highly regarded clos in Alsace, and Zind-Humbrecht makes Riesling, Gewurztraminer and Pinot Gris there, all farmed biodynamically.

The Clos Windsbuhl Pinot Gris is made from grapes of this variety which, thanks to the site’s cooler climate, ripen slowly and retain good acidity. Fermentation is carried out with indigenous yeasts in old casks and can last for months. The level of residual sugar varies with the vintage, and the house indicates it on the label through its sweetness index.

The result is a Pinot Gris of broad texture and great complexity, in which the variety’s natural richness is balanced by the freshness and tension brought by the limestone. It has remarkable ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of ripe pear, peach, honey, smoky and spicy notes. Broad, unctuous palate balanced by fresh acidity, with a long finish.

Food pairing

Foie gras, roast poultry, mushrooms, washed-rind cheeses

Serving

12-13 °C, large white wine glass
