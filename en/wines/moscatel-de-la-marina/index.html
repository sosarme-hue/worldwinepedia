Moscatel de la Marina

SweetDesignation of Origin (DO) Alicante

Sweet Muscat of Alexandria wine from Enrique Mendoza, from the coastal La Marina sub-zone of the Alicante D.O.

The La Marina district, on Alicante's northern coast, has a long tradition of growing Muscat of Alexandria, both for table grapes and raisins and for sweet wines. Enrique Mendoza's Moscatel de la Marina captures that tradition in a fresh, aromatic style.

The grapes come from vineyards near the sea, in a mild, humid Mediterranean climate. They are harvested ripe and the wine is made to retain a significant share of the grapes' natural sugar and the variety's aromatic intensity, without oak ageing. It is a light-style sweet wine, fresh rather than dense, drunk young and very pleasant with fruit desserts or simply after a meal.

Tasting profile

Fresh Muscat grape, orange blossom, peach, apricot and honey. Sweet, light and fresh on the palate.

Food pairing

Fruit desserts, turrón, almond pastries, mild blue cheeses

Serving

6-8 °C, small dessert wine glass
