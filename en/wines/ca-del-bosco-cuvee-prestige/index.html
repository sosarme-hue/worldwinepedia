Ca' del Bosco

Ca' del Bosco Cuvée Prestige

SparklingFranciacorta DOCG Extra BrutControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Franciacorta

Ca’ del Bosco’s most widely available Franciacorta, a traditional-method sparkling wine from Chardonnay with Pinot Noir and Pinot Blanc, blending wines from several vintages.

Cuvée Prestige is Ca’ del Bosco’s non-vintage Franciacorta and its benchmark wine. It is made mostly from Chardonnay, with smaller proportions of Pinot Noir and Pinot Blanc, from numerous Franciacorta plots that are vinified separately.

Like all Franciacorta, it is made by the traditional method: after the base wines are fermented and blended, including reserve wines from earlier vintages, the wine undergoes a second fermentation in the bottle and rests on its lees for an extended period before disgorgement.

It is a dry sparkling wine with fine bubbles, combining fresh fruit with bakery notes from lees ageing. Its low dosage makes it versatile as an aperitif and with food.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow with fine, persistent bubbles. Aromas of apple, citrus and white flowers, with notes of toast and pastry. Fresh, creamy and dry on the palate, with good acidity.

Food pairing

Aperitif, shellfish, raw fish, fried dishes, risotto

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass or tulip
