Arrogant Frog Merlot - Cabernet Sauvignon

RedIGP Pays d’OcWine region Languedoc-Roussillon

IGP Pays d’Oc red from the Arrogant Frog brand by Domaines Paul Mas, blending Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon in a fruity style.

Arrogant Frog is one of the Domaines Paul Mas brands with the widest international presence. With a light-hearted label featuring a frog, the brand plays on clichés about the French and is designed to bring the wines of southern France to a broad audience, with familiar varieties named on the label.

This red blends Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon, two varieties of Bordeaux origin well suited to the Languedoc, under the IGP Pays d’Oc geographical indication. Merlot brings roundness and ripe fruit; Cabernet Sauvignon adds structure and blackcurrant notes. Winemaking aims to preserve the fruit, with only a short period of ageing.

It is a medium-bodied, fruity red with soft tannins, the sunny character of the Mediterranean and an easy-drinking profile. It is meant to be enjoyed young, as an everyday wine, with simple meat dishes, pasta or charcuterie, and served slightly cool in warm weather.

Tasting profile

Ruby. Aromas of plum, blackcurrant and red fruit, with a spicy touch. Round, fruity palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Burgers, grilled meats, pasta, charcuterie, mild cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, red wine glass
