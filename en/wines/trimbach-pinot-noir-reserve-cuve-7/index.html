Trimbach Pinot Noir Réserve Cuve 7

RedRéserveWine region (AOC Alsace, Alsace Grand Cru and Crémant d'Alsace) Alsace

Pinot Noir red from Trimbach’s Réserve range in Ribeauvillé: a light, fruity Alsace red in a fresh style.

Pinot Noir is the only red variety permitted in the Alsace appellation and occupies a small share of the vineyard in a region dominated by whites. Traditionally it gave very light reds and even rosés, although in recent decades many houses have made versions with more colour and structure.

The house of Trimbach, founded in the seventeenth century and based in Ribeauvillé, includes a Pinot Noir in its Réserve range, which sits between the house’s classic wines and its great selections.

It reflects the Trimbach style, which favours freshness, purity and precision: it is a light- to medium-bodied red with clear red fruit, fine tannins and lively acidity. It is best enjoyed in its first years and pairs well with Alsatian cuisine and lighter dishes.

Tasting profile

Light ruby. Aromas of cherry, raspberry, strawberry and a touch of spice. Light, fresh and fruity palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, tarte flambée, poultry, freshwater fish, mild cheeses

Serving

13-15 °C, Burgundy-style glass
