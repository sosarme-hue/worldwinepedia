Cayuse Vineyards

Cayuse Armada Syrah

RedAVA (shared by Washington and Oregon, within Columbia Valley) Walla Walla Valley

Single-vineyard Syrah from Cayuse Vineyards, Christophe Baron’s biodynamic winery in the cobblestone area of Walla Walla.

Cayuse Vineyards is the winery founded in the late 1990s in the Walla Walla Valley by the Frenchman Christophe Baron, who comes from a family of Champagne growers. Baron planted his vineyards in the Oregon part of the valley, near Milton-Freewater, on an ancient riverbed covered with basalt cobblestones, a soil reminiscent of the galets of Châteauneuf-du-Pape. The area was later recognised as an AVA in its own right, The Rocks District of Milton-Freewater.

The vineyards are farmed on biodynamic principles, with dense plantings, and the winery makes single-vineyard wines, above all from Syrah, which have become benchmarks for the variety in the United States. The stones store heat during the day and release it at night, giving very ripe reds with highly distinctive earthy, savoury notes, often described as “Rocks funk”.

Armada is the Syrah from the vineyard of the same name, one of the estate’s sites in The Rocks District. It has 13.5% ABV, and expresses the particular character of its plot within the house style.

Tasting profile

Intense purple-red. Aromas of blackberry, black olive, smoked meat, pepper, violet and earthy notes. Fleshy and savoury on the palate, with fine tannins and a long, saline finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled lamb, smoked ribs, game, mushrooms, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
