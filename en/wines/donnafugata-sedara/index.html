Donnafugata

Donnafugata Sedàra

RedSicilia DOCWine region Sicily

A Nero d’Avola-based Sicilian red from Donnafugata, fruity and easy to drink, whose name comes from a character in The Leopard.

Sedàra is one of Donnafugata’s most widely available reds. Like other wines from the house, its name is taken from the novel The Leopard, in this case from the Sedàra family, to which Angelica, one of the main characters, belongs.

It is made mainly from Nero d’Avola, Sicily’s principal red grape, with which other varieties may be blended in smaller proportions. The grapes come mostly from the Contessa Entellina vineyards, in the hills of inland western Sicily. Winemaking aims for a fruity red: after fermentation the wine is aged mainly in tank, and part may spend time in wood.

The result is a medium-bodied red with the ripe, smooth fruit typical of Nero d’Avola, meant to be drunk in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby with purple glints. Aromas of plum, black cherry and blackberry, with notes of spice and Mediterranean herbs. Smooth and fruity on the palate, with gentle tannins.

Food pairing

Pasta with meat or tomato sauce, pizza, grilled meat, aubergine parmigiana

Serving

15-17 °C, medium-sized glass
