Pinot Noir Dundee Hills

RedAVA Willamette Valley

Estate Pinot Noir from Domaine Drouhin Oregon in the Dundee Hills, made along Burgundian lines on reddish volcanic soils.

The Pinot Noir Dundee Hills is Domaine Drouhin Oregon’s central wine and the one that best reflects the idea with which Robert Drouhin came to Oregon in 1987: to see how Pinot Noir expresses itself in a cool New World climate when worked with Burgundian methods.

The grapes come from the estate vineyards, planted on slopes over the reddish volcanic soils of the Dundee Hills, at a planting density high for the region. Véronique Drouhin-Boss directs the winemaking, in a winery designed to move grapes and wine by gravity. The wine is aged in French oak barrels, with a limited proportion of new wood, so as not to mask the fruit. The result is a medium-bodied, fresh and elegant red in which red fruit is accompanied by spicy and floral notes.

Tasting profile

Bright ruby red. Aromas of cherry, raspberry and violet with notes of gentle spice. Fresh and silky on the palate, medium-bodied with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Salmon, duck, roast poultry, mushrooms, soft cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
