Château Ksara

Château Ksara

RedWine region Bekaa Valley

An aged red bearing the house name, a Bordeaux-style blend from Bekaa vineyards. It is one of the most ambitious wines from the oldest winery of modern Lebanon.

The red Château Ksara sits at the top of the winery’s range. It is a Bordeaux-style blend of Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Petit Verdot, in proportions that vary with the vintage, from Bekaa vineyards at around a thousand metres above sea level.

After fermentation the wine is aged in oak barrels and completes its maturation in the winery’s underground galleries, whose stable temperature and humidity encourage slow development. The result is a red that combines the ripe fruit of the Bekaa’s dry, sunny summers with the structure of the Bordeaux varieties and the freshness brought by cool high-altitude nights. It can be drunk after a few years in bottle, though it will also age for a long time. It is a good example of the style of the Bekaa’s aged reds, and a decanting before serving can help it open up.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red. Aromas of ripe black fruit, cedar, spice and toasty notes from ageing. Structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, lamb, spiced stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
