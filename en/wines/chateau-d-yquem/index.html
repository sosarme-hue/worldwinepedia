Château d’Yquem

Château d’Yquem

SweetPremier Cru Supérieur (1855)AOC (sweet wine) Sauternes

The most famous sweet wine of Sauternes, made from Sémillon and Sauvignon Blanc affected by noble rot.

The wine of Château d’Yquem is made from Sémillon, which makes up most of the blend, and Sauvignon Blanc. The grapes are picked by hand in several successive passes through the vineyard over a period of weeks, gathering only the bunches or berries that have reached the right degree of concentration through the action of Botrytis cinerea.

The very sugar-rich must ferments in new oak barrels and the wine is aged in barrel for a long period before bottling. Yields per vine are very low, and in vintages where quality is not judged sufficient the estate forgoes releasing the wine.

Yquem combines great sugar richness with acidity that gives it balance. It is famous for its longevity: it can age for many decades, gaining complexity and darker hues.

Tasting profile

Deep gold, turning amber with age. Aromas of apricot, candied orange peel, honey, pineapple, saffron and vanilla. Unctuous and very long on the palate, with acidity balancing the sweetness.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses such as Roquefort, yellow-fruit desserts, spiced dishes

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
