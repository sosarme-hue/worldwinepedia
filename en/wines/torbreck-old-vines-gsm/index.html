Torbreck

Old Vines GSM

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Barossa Valley

Barossa Valley red from Torbreck, a blend of old-vine Grenache, Shiraz and Mataro, in the style of the southern Rhône.

Torbreck is a Barossa Valley winery in South Australia, founded in the 1990s and focused from the start on Rhône varieties and on making the most of the old vines, many of them bush-trained and dry-farmed, that are plentiful in the region.

In Australia the initials GSM stand for the blend of Grenache, Shiraz and Mataro, the local name for Mourvèdre, the classic combination of the southern Rhône. As its name says, this wine comes from old-vine vineyards, which give concentrated, low-yielding grapes.

The Barossa’s warm, dry climate favours full ripening. The result is a generous, spicy red in which Grenache brings red fruit and roundness, Shiraz colour and black fruit, and Mataro structure and earthy notes. It can be enjoyed young and develops well for a few years.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of raspberry, plum, blackberry, pepper and dried herbs. Broad, warm palate with soft tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Barbecue, spiced lamb, ribs, stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
