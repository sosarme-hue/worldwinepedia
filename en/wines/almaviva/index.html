Viña Almaviva

Almaviva

RedDesignation of Origin (subregion) Maipo Valley

Grand vin from Puente Alto born of the partnership between Concha y Toro and Baron Philippe de Rothschild. It is a Cabernet Sauvignon-based blend whose first vintage was 1996.

Almaviva is the sole grand vin of the winery of the same name, the fruit of an alliance between Chile’s Concha y Toro and Bordeaux’s Baron Philippe de Rothschild. The first vintage was 1996, and it has since become one of Chile’s most internationally acclaimed reds. The name comes from Count Almaviva in The Marriage of Figaro.

The grapes come from the Puente Alto vineyards in Alto Maipo, on an alluvial terrace of the Maipo River with stony, well-drained soils. The base of the blend is Cabernet Sauvignon, accompanied by Carmenère, Cabernet Franc, Petit Verdot and, in some vintages, Merlot, in proportions that change every year. Winemaking is inspired by that of the great Bordeaux châteaux: plot-by-plot vinification, rigorous selection and ageing in French oak barrels. The result is a deep, elegant red with fine tannins and great ageing potential, combining the ripe fruit of the Maipo with a Bordeaux-inspired structure.

Tasting profile

Very deep ruby red. Cassis, blackberry, black cherry, notes of graphite, tobacco, cocoa and spice. Ample, elegant palate with fine tannins and a very persistent finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, beef tenderloin, large game, mushrooms, hard cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
