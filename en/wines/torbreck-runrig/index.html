Torbreck

Torbreck RunRig

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Barossa Valley

Torbreck's flagship red: Shiraz from very old Barossa vines co-fermented with a small proportion of Viognier.

RunRig is Torbreck's most emblematic wine. Its name comes from the run rig, an old Scottish system of dividing farmland into strips, a nod to the winery's Scottish origins and to the agreements David Powell made with Barossa growers to farm their old vines.

The wine is made from Shiraz from very old, dry-grown vines in various plots across the Barossa, with a small proportion of Viognier fermented together with the red, in the manner of Côte-Rôtie in the northern Rhône. This practice adds perfume and helps to stabilise colour. The wine is aged for a long period in French oak barrels, a large share of them new. It is a red of great concentration and depth, intended to develop for many years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Very deep colour. Intense aromas of blackberry, plum and candied dark fruit, with floral notes, spice, chocolate and liquorice. Dense, velvety and very long.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, braised beef cheek, large game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
