Clos des Papes

Clos des Papes Châteauneuf-du-Pape Rouge

RedChâteauneuf-du-PapeAOC Châteauneuf-du-Pape

Red Châteauneuf-du-Pape from the Avril family, a blend of Grenache, Mourvèdre and Syrah from parcels spread across the appellation.

The Clos des Papes red is the domaine’s main wine and one of the most respected Châteauneuf-du-Papes. It is made from grapes grown on the many parcels the Avril family owns in different sectors of the appellation, which makes it possible to combine the power of the pebbly soils with the freshness of sand and limestone.

Grenache forms the basis of the blend, accompanied by a substantial share of Mourvèdre, by Syrah and by small amounts of other authorised varieties; the proportions vary with the vintage. The grapes are hand-picked at low yields, and the wine is aged in oak casks, with no prominent role for new oak.

It is a wine of firm structure and great ageing potential. Young, it can be somewhat closed, and it usually gains complexity after several years in bottle, with notes of black fruit, spice, garrigue and leather.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet. Aromas of blackberry, black cherry, spice, garrigue and earthy notes. Structured palate with firm tannins, freshness and a long finish.

Food pairing

Furred game, roast milk-fed lamb, braised cheek, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
