Wehlener Sonnenuhr Riesling Spätlese

WhitePrädikatswein SpätleseAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Mosel

Riesling Spätlese from J.J. Prüm from the Wehlener Sonnenuhr, richer than the Kabinett and renowned for its ability to age for decades.

The Spätlese from the Wehlener Sonnenuhr is one of J.J. Prüm’s most emblematic wines. It is made from grapes riper than those for the Kabinett, from the estate’s vines on this south-facing blue-slate slope opposite Wehlen, whose exposure and soil favour slow, complete ripening of Riesling.

The wine ferments slowly in tanks and casks and retains clearly perceptible residual sugar, balanced by acidity and low alcohol. When young it can seem somewhat closed and show the reductive notes typical of the house style. Over time the sweetness becomes integrated and notes of honey, ripe fruit and mineral and spicy nuances emerge. J.J. Prüm’s Spätlesen are among the German Rieslings most highly prized for cellaring.

Its sweetness makes it particularly suitable for dishes with a touch of heat or a sweet-and-sour element, and it is best served well chilled to bring out its liveliness.

Tasting profile

Aromas of peach, apricot, citrus and white flowers, with mineral and, when young, reductive notes. Denser than the Kabinett on the palate, with elegant sweetness and acidity that gives great length.

Food pairing

Thai or Vietnamese food, roast duck, pork with sweet-and-sour sauces, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
