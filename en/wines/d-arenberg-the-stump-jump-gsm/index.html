d'Arenberg

d'Arenberg The Stump Jump GSM

RedGeographical Indication (GI) McLaren Vale

Grenache, Shiraz and Mourvèdre blend from McLaren Vale, d'Arenberg's most approachable red, named after a plough invented in South Australia.

The stump-jump plough was a plough invented in South Australia in the nineteenth century, able to «jump» over the stumps and roots left in newly cleared land. d'Arenberg, a family winery in McLaren Vale known for its original wine names, chose it to name its most approachable range.

The best-known wine in the series is this blend of Grenache, Shiraz and Mourvèdre, the Rhône-inspired trio of varieties known in Australia by their initials, GSM. Grenache brings red fruit and warmth, Shiraz colour and body, and Mourvèdre spicy notes and structure; the proportions vary with the vintage. The grapes come from McLaren Vale vineyards, and the wine is made with little use of new oak in order to preserve its fruity character. It is a juicy, easy-drinking red intended to be enjoyed young.

Tasting profile

Aromas of raspberry, cherry and plum, with notes of pepper, dried herbs and spice. Juicy and soft, medium-bodied, with friendly tannins.

Food pairing

Pizza, spiced lamb, grilled sausages, Mediterranean dishes

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
