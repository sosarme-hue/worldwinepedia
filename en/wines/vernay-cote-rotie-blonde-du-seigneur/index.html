Georges Vernay Côte-Rôtie Blonde du Seigneur

RedCôte-RôtieAOC (Northern Rhône cru) Côte-Rôtie

Côte-Rôtie from Domaine Georges Vernay, a Syrah from the granite slopes of the northern Rhône, fine, perfumed and spicy.

Blonde du Seigneur is one of the Côte-Rôties from Domaine Georges Vernay, the Condrieu family estate run by Christine Vernay. Its name evokes the local legend in which a lord of Ampuis left his two daughters, one blonde and one dark-haired, the two slopes that gave their names to the Côte Blonde and the Côte Brune.

The Côte-Rôtie appellation, the northernmost in the Rhône, covers very steep, terraced slopes on the right bank of the river, with schist and gneiss soils that receive intense sunshine. It is Syrah country, to which the rules allow a small proportion of Viognier to be added.

True to the house style, this wine seeks finesse and perfume before power: a red with precise fruit, the notes of violet, black olive, pepper and smoke typical of the appellation, and fine tannins. It is aged in wood and gains complexity with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of raspberry, blackberry, violet, pepper and smoky notes. Fine, fresh palate with silky tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Lamb, squab, grilled red meats, game birds

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
