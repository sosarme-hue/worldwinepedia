Ciacci Piccolomini D'Aragona

Ciacci Piccolomini Brunello di Montalcino

RedControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Brunello di Montalcino

A Brunello di Montalcino from Ciacci Piccolomini d'Aragona, an estate in Castelnuovo dell'Abate on the south-eastern side of the appellation.

Ciacci Piccolomini d'Aragona is an estate in Castelnuovo dell'Abate, a hamlet in the commune of Montalcino near the abbey of Sant'Antimo. Its name comes from the noble family that owned the 17th-century palazzo which remains the heart of the estate; since the late 20th century it has been run by the Bianchini family, who inherited the property and developed its wine production.

The vineyards lie on the south-eastern side of the appellation, in the Orcia river valley, an area warmer and more sheltered than northern Montalcino, which favours good ripening of Sangiovese. As the DOCG requires, Brunello di Montalcino is made exclusively from this variety.

The wine is aged for an extended period in large oak casks, in keeping with local tradition, and cannot be released until several years after the harvest. The result is a generous, structured Brunello with ripe fruit and spicy notes, along with the acidity and firm tannins that allow it to age for a long time.

Tasting profile

Garnet red. Aromas of ripe cherry, plum, violet, spice, leather and balsamic notes. Warm, structured palate with lively acidity, firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Bistecca alla fiorentina, wild boar, stews, pici with ragù, aged pecorino

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decanting recommended
