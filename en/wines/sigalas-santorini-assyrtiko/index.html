Domaine Sigalas

Domaine Sigalas Santorini Assyrtiko

WhiteSantorini PDOProtected designation of origin (PDO) Santorini

A dry Assyrtiko white from Domaine Sigalas, the most representative wine of the Oia winery. It shows the classic style of Santorini whites.

This wine is made from Assyrtiko from vineyards spread across different parts of Santorini, many of them old ungrafted vines grown in the island’s volcanic soils and trained in a basket shape. Blending grapes from several origins makes it possible to compose a wine that reflects the general character of the island rather than that of a single plot.

Fermentation takes place in stainless steel tanks, and the wine may spend some time on its lees before bottling, which gives it a little more texture without reducing its freshness. It is not aged in oak. It is a dry white with high acidity and the citrus, mineral and saline profile characteristic of Santorini Assyrtiko. It is expressive when young, but it also evolves well for several years in bottle, when more complex notes appear.

Tasting profile

Bright pale yellow. Aromas of lemon, grapefruit, stone, sea salt and a floral touch. Dry and vibrant on the palate, medium-bodied, with very marked acidity and a long, saline finish.

Food pairing

grilled fish, shellfish, oysters, octopus, Greek dishes with olive oil and lemon

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
