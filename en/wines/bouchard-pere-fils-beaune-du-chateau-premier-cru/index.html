Bouchard Père & Fils

Bouchard Père & Fils Beaune du Château Premier Cru

RedPremier CruAOC Beaune 1er Cru / Côte-d'Or

Beaune Premier Cru from Bouchard Père & Fils, a blend of several of the house’s Beaune premiers crus, named after the château where it has its headquarters.

Bouchard Père & Fils is one of Burgundy’s historic houses, founded in the 18th century and based at the Château de Beaune, a former fortress in the town whose cellars are used to age its wines. In addition to its négociant business, it is one of the major vineyard owners of the Côte d’Or, with many plots in premiers and grands crus.

The Beaune appellation has a large area of premiers crus on the slope west of the town, on clay-limestone soils. The red Beaune du Château Premier Cru is a blend of Pinot Noir from several of those climats owned by the house. Blending plots with different exposures and soils aims for a balanced wine representative of the appellation as a whole, rather than the expression of a single site.

It is aged in oak barrels in the château’s cellars. It is a red with red fruit, medium body and soft tannins, which can be enjoyed in its first years and develops well for a while in bottle.

Tasting profile

Cherry, raspberry, redcurrant and gentle spicy, toasty notes. Medium-bodied, fresh and rounded, with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Roast poultry, veal, ham, mushrooms, soft cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, Burgundy glass
