Dominus Estate

Dominus

RedAVA Napa Valley

The grand vin of Dominus Estate, Christian Moueix's red from the Napa Valley, based on Cabernet Sauvignon from the Napanook vineyard.

Dominus is the grand vin of Dominus Estate, the property that Christian Moueix, a key figure in Pomerol, runs in Yountville in the Napa Valley. It comes from the historic Napanook vineyard at the foot of the Mayacamas Mountains.

It is made from a blend dominated by Cabernet Sauvignon, completed with Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, in proportions that vary with the vintage. Grapes from the best plots are selected for the grand vin, and ageing takes place in French oak barrels.

Its style combines the ripeness of Californian fruit with a Bordeaux-inspired structure and restraint: firm but fine tannins, noticeable acidity and a search for balance over power. It is a wine for long ageing, closed in its youth, which develops complexity over the years with notes of cedar, tobacco, graphite and ripe black fruit.

Tasting profile

Very deep red. Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, graphite, cedar and spice. Structured palate with firm, fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Rack of lamb, roast beef, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass; decant when young
