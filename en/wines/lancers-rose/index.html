Lancers Rosé

Rosé

Slightly sparkling rosé from José Maria da Fonseca, created in the 1940s for the American market and known for its opaque, ceramic-looking bottle.

Lancers was created by José Maria da Fonseca, the historic Azeitão house, in the 1940s, with the idea of making a Portuguese rosé aimed at the United States market. Its opaque bottle, imitating an earthenware jar, and its fresh, slightly sweet and gently sparkling style made it, together with other Portuguese rosés of the time, an export phenomenon during the second half of the century.

The wine is made from Portuguese red varieties, whose composition is not fixed and varies with the vintage. After brief contact between the must and the skins to obtain the colour, fermentation takes place at low temperature, which preserves fruity aromas. The wine keeps a slight sweetness and a gentle effervescence provided by a small amount of carbon dioxide.

Today the brand also includes other versions, but the rosé remains its best-known wine. It is a young wine, without ageing, meant to be drunk well chilled and within the year.

Tasting profile

Pale, bright pink. Aromas of strawberry, raspberry and fresh red fruit. Light, fresh, slightly sweet palate with a gentle effervescence.

Food pairing

Aperitif, salads, Asian cuisine, light summer dishes

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
