Castello Banfi

Castello Banfi Rosso di Montalcino

RedRosso di Montalcino DOCWine region Tuscany

Castello Banfi’s Rosso di Montalcino, a Sangiovese from the same area as Brunello but with shorter ageing and a younger style.

Rosso di Montalcino is the appellation that lets Montalcino producers make a Sangiovese red from the same area as Brunello but with much shorter ageing, so that it can be released soon after the harvest. It is often described as Brunello’s “little brother”.

Castello Banfi’s version is made exclusively from Sangiovese from the estate’s vineyards in southern Montalcino. After fermentation the wine spends a few months in oak before bottling, just enough to round it out without overshadowing the fruit.

It is a more approachable and affordable red than Brunello that keeps the character of Montalcino Sangiovese: red fruit, lively acidity and tannins that are present but gentle. It is meant to be drunk in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Bright ruby. Aromas of cherry, raspberry and a floral, spicy touch. Medium-bodied on the palate, fresh and balanced, with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Pasta with tomato or meat sauce, pizza, roast poultry, cured meats

Serving

15-17 °C, medium-sized glass
