Château des Jacques Moulin-à-Vent

RedMoulin-à-VentWine region (AOC Beaujolais, Beaujolais-Villages and ten crus) Beaujolais

Moulin-à-Vent from Château des Jacques, Louis Jadot’s estate in Romanèche-Thorins, made from Gamay in the style of Burgundy reds.

Château des Jacques is an old property in Romanèche-Thorins, in the Moulin-à-Vent cru, which the Burgundy house Louis Jadot acquired in the 1990s. Moulin-à-Vent, named after an old windmill that overlooks the vineyard, is known for producing the most structured and long-lived Beaujolais, thanks to its pink granite soils veined with manganese.

The wine is made from Gamay and, unlike most Beaujolais, is not vinified by carbonic maceration but according to traditional Burgundian methods: the grapes are destemmed, maceration is longer and the wine is aged for a time in oak barrels. The estate also makes wines from various climats within the cru.

The result is a red of good structure and ageing potential, closer in spirit to a Burgundy than to a light Beaujolais. Over the years it develops spicy and earthy notes.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of black cherry, raspberry, violet and spice, with lightly toasty notes. Structured palate with firm tannins and good freshness.

Food pairing

Grilled red meats, duck, game birds, pressed cheeses

Serving

14-15 °C, Burgundy-style glass
