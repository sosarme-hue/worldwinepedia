San Marzano Timo Vermentino

WhiteIGT Salento

San Marzano Timo Vermentino is a white wine from Cantine San Marzano, made under the IGT Salento appellation in Italy.

It is aged in stainless steel tanks. It has 12.5% ABV.

Salento cooperative founded in 1962 in San Marzano di San Giuseppe, near Taranto, known for its Sessantanni Primitivo di Manduria.

Food pairing

Fish, shellfish, rice dishes, fresh cheeses

Serving

8-11 °C, white wine glass
