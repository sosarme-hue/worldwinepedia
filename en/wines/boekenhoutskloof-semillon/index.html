Boekenhoutskloof

Boekenhoutskloof Semillon

WhiteWine of Origin (WO) Franschhoek

Old-vine Semillon from the Franschhoek valley made by Boekenhoutskloof, one of South Africa’s benchmark whites from this variety.

In the nineteenth century Semillon was the dominant variety in Cape vineyards, to the point that it was known simply as “green grape” because of the colour of its leaves. Over time it lost ground to other varieties, but Franschhoek still has some of the oldest Semillon vines in the country, and Boekenhoutskloof was one of the first modern wineries to devote a wine of its own to them.

The grapes come from old vineyards in the valley, grown on sandy and granite soils. The wine is fermented in French oak barrels, some of them new, and aged on its lees for several months, which gives it texture and complexity. It may include a small proportion of other white varieties depending on the vintage. It is a medium- to full-bodied white with notes of citrus, wax and honey, which gains depth with a few years of cellaring.

Tasting profile

Lemon, orange peel and baked apple, with notes of wax, honey, lanolin and nuts. Unctuous palate with good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, poultry, roast pork, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
