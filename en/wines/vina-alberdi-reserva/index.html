La Rioja Alta, S.A.

Viña Alberdi Reserva

RedReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Tempranillo Reserva from La Rioja Alta, S.A., in Haro, aged in American oak barrels made in the winery’s own cooperage.

Viña Alberdi is the youngest wine in the classic range of La Rioja Alta, S.A., the winery founded in 1890 in Haro’s Station District. For years it was sold as a Crianza, and it is now released as a Reserva, in keeping with the house’s long-aged style.

It is made exclusively from Tempranillo from its own vineyards in Rioja Alta. The wine is aged in American oak barrels that the winery builds in its own cooperage, and during ageing it is periodically racked in the traditional way, by candlelight, to separate the wine from its sediment. It then rests in bottle before release.

The result is a classic medium-bodied Rioja, with ripe red fruit, notes of vanilla, coconut and spice and fresh acidity, offering a good introduction to the traditional Haro style.

Tasting profile

Ruby red with a brick rim. Ripe red and black fruit, sweet spice, soft leather and toasty notes. Broad on the palate, with polished tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled lamb chops, Iberian cured meats, semi-cured cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
