Château Minuty

Minuty Prestige Rosé

RoséCôtes de ProvenceWine region Provence

Côtes de Provence rosé from Château Minuty made from Grenache and Tibouren from the estate’s vineyards on the Saint-Tropez peninsula.

Minuty Prestige sits on an intermediate step in Château Minuty’s rosé range, above M de Minuty. It comes mainly from the estate’s vineyards and from nearby parcels on the Saint-Tropez peninsula, with schist soils and under the influence of the sea.

The blend is based on Grenache, accompanied by Tibouren, a traditional variety of the Provençal coast, little grown outside this area, which brings fine aromatic notes and a slightly spicy character; the proportions vary with the vintage. The grapes are picked in the coolest hours and pressed directly to obtain a pale colour.

Low-temperature fermentation and brief ageing on lees aim to preserve the fruit and add a little more texture than in the entry-level rosé. It is a dry, elegant rosé of good persistence, suited both to the aperitif and to the table.

Tasting profile

Pale pink with onion-skin hues. Aromas of grapefruit, peach, white flowers and a spicy touch. Fresh, silky and persistent palate.

Food pairing

Grilled fish, sushi, bouillabaisse, salade niçoise

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
