Apóstoles Palo Cortado VORS

FortifiedPalo Cortado VORSDesignation of Origin (DO) Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

Very old palo cortado from González Byass, certified as VORS, with a small proportion of Pedro Ximénez.

Apóstoles belongs to González Byass' collection of very old wines with VORS certification, which guarantees an average age of more than thirty years. Like the rest of the collection, it comes from historic soleras the house keeps in its bodegas in the centre of Jerez.

Palo cortado is one of the most singular sherry styles: a wine that begins its ageing under flor, like a fino, but soon shows a finer, more aromatic character, so it is set aside to continue ageing oxidatively. The result combines the aromatic delicacy of amontillado with the body of oloroso. Apóstoles is made from Palomino with a small proportion of Pedro Ximénez that rounds out its palate without turning it into a sweet wine. After decades of ageing in criaderas and solera, it is a very concentrated, complex wine, to be enjoyed in small quantities.

Tasting profile

Mahogany with amber hints. Aromas of walnut, hazelnut, fine wood, spice, leather and a slight touch of raisins. Broad, almost dry, intense and very long on the palate.

Food pairing

Red meats, game, stews, well-aged cheeses, nuts

Serving

13-15 °C, white wine glass
