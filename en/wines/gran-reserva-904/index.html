La Rioja Alta, S.A.

Gran Reserva 904

RedGran ReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Gran reserva from La Rioja Alta, made from Tempranillo with Graciano, long aged in American oak and produced only in great vintages.

Gran Reserva 904 is one of La Rioja Alta’s top wines. Its name refers to the year 1904, an important date in the winery’s history. It is made from a majority of Tempranillo and a small proportion of Graciano from the winery’s own vineyards in Rioja Alta.

The wine is aged for an extended period in American oak barrels from the house cooperage, with traditional manual racking, and then rests for several years in bottle before release. It is made only in vintages the winery considers exceptional.

It is a representative example of classic Rioja gran reserva: a long-aged, complex and elegant red in which Graciano brings freshness and Tempranillo structure. It has great ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Ruby with a brick rim. A complex nose of ripe red fruit, spice, leather, tobacco, vanilla and balsamic notes. Fine and silky on the palate, with fresh acidity and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Roasts, game, traditional stews, dishes with mushrooms or truffle, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decant if there is sediment
