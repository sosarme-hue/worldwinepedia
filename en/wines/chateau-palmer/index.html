Château Palmer

Château Palmer

RedThird Grand Cru Classé (1855)Bordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The grand vin of Château Palmer, a Margaux third growth with a very high proportion of Merlot for the Médoc and an aromatic, silky style.

The grand vin of Château Palmer comes from the estate’s best plots, on the gravels of the Margaux plateau, in the communes of Cantenac and Margaux. Unlike most of the great Médoc wines, its blend gives a very important role to Merlot, often in a proportion similar to that of Cabernet Sauvignon, with a small contribution of Petit Verdot. The proportions vary with the vintage.

The vineyard is farmed according to biodynamic principles, and the wine is aged in French oak barrels, with a significant proportion of new wood. Selection is rigorous, and the lots not retained go into Alter Ego.

Its style combines the floral fragrance and finesse associated with Margaux with a fleshy, velvety texture. It can be seductive fairly early, yet it has great ageing potential.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet. Aromas of plum, blackberry, violet and spice, with notes of truffle and leather over the years. Velvety, broad palate with fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled rib steak, game, hard aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large Bordeaux glass
