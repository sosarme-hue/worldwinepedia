Finca Antigua

Finca Antigua Syrah

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) La Mancha

Single-variety Syrah from Finca Antigua in the La Mancha D.O., from the estate's own high-altitude vineyards in Los Hinojosos.

Finca Antigua is one of the La Mancha wineries that have chosen to make varietal wines from their own vineyards. Its Syrah comes from the Los Hinojosos estate, in the province of Cuenca, which lies at a high altitude for the plateau, bringing cool nights and slower ripening.

Syrah has adapted well to La Mancha's dry continental climate, where it reaches good ripeness and colour. The wine is aged in barrel for a moderate period, which adds spicy notes and rounds out the tannins without hiding the fruit. It is a modern-style red, fruity and spicy, whose value for money illustrates the recent evolution of bottled La Mancha wines.

It is best served slightly below room temperature, so that its fruit and freshness show clearly, and preferably enjoyed within its first few years after the harvest.

Tasting profile

Blackberry, blueberry, violet and black pepper, with a toasty touch. Medium-bodied palate with ripe tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled meats, lamb, Manchego cheese, game stews

Serving

16-17 °C, large glass
