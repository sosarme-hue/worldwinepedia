Kiedrich Gräfenberg Riesling Grosses Gewächs

WhiteGrosses Gewächs (VDP), dryAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Rheingau

Dry Riesling from the Kiedricher Gräfenberg vineyard, Robert Weil’s most emblematic site, with great structure and ageing potential.

The Gräfenberg is a steep south-west-facing slope rising above the village of Kiedrich at the foot of the Taunus. Its shallow, stony soils of phyllite and slate drain well and warm up easily, while altitude and the nearby forest keep nights cool. Robert Weil is the vineyard’s main owner.

The Grosses Gewächs is the top-category dry wine from this vineyard under the VDP classification. It is made from hand-picked, selected grapes and released after a period of ageing in the cellar. It is a Riesling of medium to full body, with notable concentration and firm acidity, which can seem somewhat reserved when young and gains complexity with age.

It is best not served too cold and left to breathe in the glass, as its aromas open up with air; with a few years in bottle it usually gains complexity.

Tasting profile

Aromas of peach, citrus, herbs and mineral, spicy notes. Dry, concentrated and taut on the palate, with firm acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, poultry, veal, freshwater fish, asparagus

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
