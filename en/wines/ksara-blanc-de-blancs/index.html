Château Ksara

Blanc de Blancs

WhiteWine region Bekaa Valley

A dry white from Château Ksara made from Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon from the Bekaa, with part of the wine fermented in barrel. It combines freshness and texture.

Blanc de Blancs is one of Château Ksara’s best-known whites. It is made from Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon from vineyards in the Bekaa, in proportions that may vary from one vintage to another. The altitude of the vineyards and the cool summer nights help the grapes keep their acidity despite the intense summer sunshine.

In winemaking, part of the wine, generally the Chardonnay, is fermented and aged for a time in barrel, while the rest is made in tank to preserve the fruit and aromatic freshness of the Sauvignon Blanc. The blend gives a dry white with a balanced profile, combining the liveliness of young whites with a somewhat broader texture from the oak. It can be drunk young or after a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with golden glints. Aromas of white fruit, citrus and floral notes, with a light toasty background. Fresh and medium-bodied on the palate, with a clean finish.

Food pairing

Mezze, grilled fish, salads, poultry, fresh cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
