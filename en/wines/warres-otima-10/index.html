Warre’s Otima 10

Fortified10 Year Old TawnyControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Port

Warre’s 10-year-old tawny presented in a clear glass bottle, light and fresh in style, designed to be served lightly chilled.

Otima 10 is the 10-year-old tawny from Warre’s, a house founded in 1670 that is regarded as the first British firm devoted to Port and now belongs to the Symington family. Its name comes from the Portuguese ótima, “excellent”, and it stands out for its presentation in a clear glass bottle that shows off the colour of the wine, unusual in the category.

It is a blend of wines from several harvests whose average age and character correspond to the ten-year statement, approved by the regulatory body. The grapes come from the Douro, from quintas such as Quinta da Cavadinha, in the valley of the river Pinhão, and from traditional varieties such as Touriga Nacional, Touriga Franca and Tinta Roriz (Tempranillo). After fermentation, stopped with grape spirit, the wines age in oak casks, where they acquire their amber colour and nutty aromas.

The style aims for lightness and freshness, so that it is pleasant served slightly chilled, either as an aperitif or with dessert.

Tasting profile

Pale amber. Aromas of ripe red fruit, toasted almond, caramel and a touch of orange peel. Light, smooth palate with freshness and a delicate finish.

Food pairing

Nuts, almond tart, crème brûlée, aged cheeses, foie gras

Serving

10-12 °C, lightly chilled, white wine glass
