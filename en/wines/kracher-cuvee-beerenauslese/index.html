Kracher Cuvée Beerenauslese

SweetPrädikatswein BeerenausleseWine-growing region (Weinbaugebiet), with several DACs Burgenland

Nobly sweet wine from Kracher, made in the Seewinkel by Lake Neusiedl from a blend of varieties that usually includes Welschriesling and Chardonnay.

The Cuvée Beerenauslese is the entry-level sweet wine in the range of Kracher, the Illmitz estate that has brought international fame to the sweet wines of Lake Neusiedl. It is made from grapes grown in the Seewinkel, where autumn mists created by the lake and nearby ponds, followed by sunny afternoons, favour the development of noble rot.

Beerenauslese requires overripe grapes, largely affected by Botrytis cinerea, which concentrates sugars and acidity and contributes characteristic aromas. The blend of varieties can vary with the vintage; it usually includes Welschriesling, which brings freshness, and Chardonnay, which adds body. The wine is made to preserve the balance between high sweetness and lively acidity. It is often sold in half bottles and can be enjoyed young or after several years of ageing.

It can accompany desserts that are not sweeter than the wine itself, as well as blue cheeses or foie gras, though it is also enjoyable on its own, in small quantities, at the end of a meal.

Tasting profile

Aromas of apricot, peach in syrup, pineapple, honey and candied citrus. Sweet and unctuous on the palate, with fresh acidity balancing the sugar and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fruit desserts, apricot or apple tarts, blue cheeses, foie gras

Serving

8-10 °C, small dessert wine glass
