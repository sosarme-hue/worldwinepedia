Muros Antigos Loureiro

WhiteControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Vinho Verde

Vinho Verde from Anselmo Mendes made from Loureiro, a white grape of the Minho with a floral profile and great freshness.

Anselmo Mendes is a Portuguese winemaker closely linked to the Vinho Verde region, and in particular to the Monção e Melgaço subregion on the banks of the Minho, where he has devoted himself to exploring the potential of the native white varieties.

Within his unoaked Muros Antigos range, this white is made solely from Loureiro, the grape known in Galicia as Loureira. Widely planted in the southern and coastal parts of Vinho Verde, particularly in the Lima valley, it stands out for an aromatic profile led by flowers, such as bay laurel or orange blossom, and citrus.

The region’s Atlantic climate and granite soils favour wines with lively acidity and moderate alcohol. This Loureiro is a dry, light and very aromatic white, intended to be enjoyed young, while it keeps all its freshness. It is particularly suited to aperitifs, shellfish and simple fish dishes.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Aromas of orange blossom, bay leaf, lime and green apple. Light, fresh and vibrant palate.

Food pairing

Shellfish, grilled fish, salt cod, salads, light Asian cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
