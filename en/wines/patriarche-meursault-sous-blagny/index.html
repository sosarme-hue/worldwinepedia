Meursault 1er Cru Sous Blagny

WhitePremier CruBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Beaune

Chardonnay white from the Sous Blagny premier cru in Meursault, beside the hamlet of Blagny.

Meursault is the village with the largest area of white-wine vineyards in the Côte de Beaune and one of the great names of Chardonnay in Burgundy. Its best-known premiers crus lie in the southern part of the commune, towards Puligny-Montrachet. Sous Blagny, as its name suggests, sits below the hamlet of Blagny, high on the slope and on the border with Puligny. Because of its altitude and exposure, wines from this sector usually combine Meursault’s typical breadth with notable freshness and tension. It is a white with ageing potential that develops nutty notes over the years.

Like the rest of the Maison Patriarche range, a négociant house founded in Beaune in 1780, this wine is made from grapes or wines selected within the appellation and aged in the house’s cellars, in a classic style that aims to faithfully reflect the character of its origin.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of white fruit, citrus, hazelnut and toasty notes. Broad yet taut palate with a long finish.

Food pairing

Lobster, turbot, poultry in cream sauce, hard cheeses

Serving

11-13 °C, large white-wine glass
