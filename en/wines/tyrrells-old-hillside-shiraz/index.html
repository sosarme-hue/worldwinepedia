Tyrrell’s Old Hillside Shiraz

RedHunter ValleyGeographical Indication (GI) Hunter Valley

Hunter Valley Shiraz from Tyrrell’s, from old hillside vines, in the medium-weight, fresh style typical of the region’s reds.

The Hunter Valley, north of Sydney, is one of Australia’s oldest wine regions. Although it is famous for its Semillon, Shiraz —the Australian name for Syrah— is its main red variety and gives very different wines there from the powerful reds of the country’s south: medium-weight, with fresh acidity, fine tannins and earthy notes that become more pronounced with age.

Tyrrell’s, a family winery founded at Pokolbin in the nineteenth century, maintains some of the region’s oldest vines and makes several single-vineyard Shirazes. Old Hillside, as its name indicates, comes from old vines planted on a slope, whose low yields bring concentration.

Like other reds from the house, it is made with moderate oak influence, seeking to preserve the expression of fruit and site. One can expect a medium-bodied red with red and dark fruit, spice and an earthy background, which gains complexity with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Medium-deep ruby. Aromas of cherry, plum, red berries, spice and earthy notes. Medium-weight, fresh palate with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled meat, duck, mushrooms, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
