Château Miraval

Miraval Côtes de Provence Rosé

RoséCôtes de ProvenceWine region Provence

Côtes de Provence rosé from Château Miraval, made with the Perrin family, pale, fresh and delicately fruity.

The Château Miraval rosé was launched with the 2012 vintage and quickly became one of the best-known Provence rosés in the world, driven both by its style and by the fame of its owners at the time. Its production is in the hands of the Perrin family of Château de Beaucastel.

It is made from Cinsault, Grenache, Syrah and Rolle (Vermentino), in proportions that vary with the vintage, with grapes from the Correns estate and from growers in the Côtes de Provence appellation. The grapes are pressed directly to obtain a pale colour. Most of the wine is fermented in tank, while a small proportion of Rolle is aged in barrel to add texture.

It is a dry, fresh and elegant rosé, with delicate fruit and good acidity, designed to be drunk young, as an aperitif or with Mediterranean dishes.

Tasting profile

Pale pink. Aromas of strawberry, raspberry, citrus and white flowers. Fresh, balanced palate with a slightly silky texture and a saline finish.

Food pairing

Salads, shellfish, grilled fish, Mediterranean cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
