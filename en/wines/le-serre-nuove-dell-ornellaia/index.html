Le Serre Nuove dell’Ornellaia

RedBolgheri DOC RossoControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Bolgheri

Second wine of Tenuta dell’Ornellaia, a Bolgheri Rosso from Bordeaux varieties made with the same care as the estate’s grand vin.

Le Serre Nuove dell’Ornellaia was created in the late 1990s as the estate’s second wine, following a common practice at the great Bordeaux châteaux. It comes from Ornellaia’s vineyards in Bolgheri, on the Tuscan coast, particularly from younger plots or from lots not destined for Ornellaia.

It is made from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, in proportions that vary with the vintage. Varieties and plots are vinified separately, and the wine is aged in oak barriques before blending and a period of rest in bottle.

It is a red of Mediterranean profile, with ripe fruit and rounded tannins, more approachable in its youth than Ornellaia but able to evolve for several years. It offers an introduction to the estate’s style.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, cherry, plum, Mediterranean herbs and spice. Rounded, balanced palate with soft tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled red meat, roast lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
