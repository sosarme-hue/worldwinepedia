Château Branaire-Ducru

Duluc de Branaire-Ducru

RedSecond wineAOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Saint-Julien

Second wine of Château Branaire-Ducru, a Saint-Julien more approachable in youth than the grand vin.

Like most of the Médoc’s classified growths, Château Branaire-Ducru makes a second wine from plots and lots not selected for the grand vin, often from younger vines. Its name, Duluc, recalls the Du Luc family, one of the estate’s former owners and the origin of the second part of the château’s name.

The wine comes from the property’s vineyards in Saint-Julien, on gravel soils, and blends Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, possibly with some Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. It is aged in barrel, with a lower proportion of new oak than the grand vin. The result is a red that keeps the elegance and fruit typical of Saint-Julien but with softer tannins, so that it can be enjoyed earlier, although it can also be kept for several years.

Tasting profile

Dark ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, black cherry, cedar and gentle spice. Round, elegant palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, red meats, game birds, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass
