Compañía de Vinos Telmo Rodríguez

MR Mountain Wine

SweetDesignation of Origin (DO) Málaga

Sweet Moscatel wine from the mountains of Málaga made by Telmo Rodríguez, reviving the historic tradition of Málaga “Mountain”.

MR is the most accessible version of the sweet Moscatel wines that Telmo Rodríguez makes in the Axarquía, the mountainous area in the east of Málaga province, where he also produces Molino Real. Its description as a Mountain Wine recalls the name by which English merchants knew the sweet wines of Málaga for centuries, when they were highly prized in northern Europe.

It is made from Muscat of Alexandria from old vines on very steep slate slopes, where all work is done by hand. Unlike traditional fortified Málaga wines, MR is naturally sweet: the grapes are picked very ripe and allowed to concentrate, and the must ferments until its own high sugar content stops the yeasts, with no added alcohol. This preserves the aromatic freshness of the variety. The result is a sweet wine of moderate alcohol and lively acidity, sold in half-litre bottles.

Tasting profile

Bright gold. Aromas of Muscat grape, orange blossom, apricot, honey and citrus. Sweet, fresh and delicate on the palate, with an aromatic finish.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses, fruit desserts, almonds

Serving

8-10 °C, small glass
