Honoro Vera Garnacha

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Calatayud

Young, affordable Garnacha from Bodegas Ateca in the Calatayud D.O., fruit-driven and designed for everyday drinking.

Honoro Vera is a line of young wines from the Gil Family Estates group, and its Garnacha version comes from the Calatayud D.O., where it is made by Bodegas Ateca. The grapes come from high-altitude vineyards in the area, in a continental climate that brings freshness.

The wine is made to highlight the variety's fruit: temperature-controlled fermentation and early bottling, without barrel ageing or with only very brief contact with oak. It is a simple, fruity red at a very affordable price, widely distributed on the international market as an introduction to Aragonese Garnacha.

It is best served slightly below room temperature, so that its fruit and freshness show clearly, and preferably enjoyed within its first few years after the harvest.

Tasting profile

Ripe red fruit, raspberry and cherry, with floral notes and a touch of spice. Soft, juicy palate.

Food pairing

Pasta, pizza, tapas, white meats, charcuterie

Serving

14-16 °C, red wine glass
