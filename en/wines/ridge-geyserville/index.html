Ridge Vineyards

Geyserville

RedCounty with several AVAs Sonoma

A field blend from old vines in Alexander Valley, Sonoma, based on Zinfandel, which Ridge has made since the 1960s.

Geyserville comes from vineyards near the town of the same name, in the Alexander Valley AVA in the north of Sonoma County. Some of the vines were planted in the late nineteenth century, and, as in many historic Californian vineyards, Zinfandel grows alongside Carignan, Petite Sirah and Mataro (Mourvèdre). Ridge has made this wine since the 1960s, making it one of the house’s reds with the longest history.

The stony, well-drained soils and warm climate of the area give ripe, concentrated grapes. Zinfandel dominates the blend, while Carignan contributes acidity and Petite Sirah colour and structure. The wine is fermented with native yeasts and aged in American oak barrels. It usually shows spicier and with a somewhat fresher profile than other Zinfandels from the region.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby red. Aromas of black cherry, raspberry, pepper and sweet spice with a touch of oak. Juicy and balanced on the palate, with good acidity and ripe tannins.

Food pairing

Barbecued ribs, spiced lamb, charcuterie, pizza, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; may be decanted
