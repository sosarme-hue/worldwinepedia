La Cañada Pedro Ximénez

FortifiedPedro XiménezDesignation of Origin (DO) Montilla-Moriles

Very long-aged Pedro Ximénez from Pérez Barquero, from one of the house's oldest soleras.

La Cañada is Pérez Barquero's oldest and most concentrated Pedro Ximénez and one of the benchmark sweet wines of Montilla-Moriles. It comes from a very old solera preserved by the winery, from which only small quantities are drawn.

Like all Pedro Ximénez, it is made from sun-dried Pedro Ximénez grapes, whose dense must is fortified and then aged in oak casks. The difference lies in the length of oxidative ageing: over decades the wine loses water through evaporation through the wood, concentrating sugar, acidity and aromas. Over time it takes on an almost black colour, a very dense texture and an aromatic complexity that goes beyond dried fruit, with notes of coffee, cocoa, spice and fine wood. The acidity concentrated by long ageing balances the sweetness and gives it remarkable length. It is served in small quantities.

Tasting profile

Jet black and very dense. Aromas of raisins, figs, coffee, cocoa, liquorice and spice. Extremely sweet and concentrated on the palate, with lively acidity and an endless finish.

Food pairing

Vanilla ice cream, dark chocolate, blue cheeses, nut desserts

Serving

12-14 °C, small glass
