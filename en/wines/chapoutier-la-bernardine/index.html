M. Chapoutier

M. Chapoutier Châteauneuf-du-Pape La Bernardine

RedChâteauneuf-du-PapeAOC Châteauneuf-du-Pape

Chapoutier’s red Châteauneuf-du-Pape, based on Grenache, from the southern Rhône Valley.

Although Chapoutier is based in the northern Rhône, the house owns vineyards in Châteauneuf-du-Pape, the best-known appellation in the south of the valley. La Bernardine is its most widely available red from this area.

Unlike many Châteauneuf-du-Papes, which blend numerous varieties, Grenache plays a clearly dominant role in La Bernardine, consistent with the house’s idea of expressing terroir through the variety best suited to it. The vineyards are farmed biodynamically.

It is a generous, warm and spicy red, with the roundness characteristic of southern Rhône Grenache.

The Châteauneuf-du-Pape appellation, between Avignon and Orange, owes its name to the summer residence the Avignon popes built there in the 14th century. Many of its vineyards are covered with large rounded pebbles that store the day’s heat and release it at night, helping Grenache to ripen fully.

Tasting profile

Garnet ruby. Aromas of ripe red fruit, raspberry, plum, spice, liquorice and garrigue herbs. Broad and warm on the palate, with soft tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Stews, lamb with herbs, Provençal daube, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
