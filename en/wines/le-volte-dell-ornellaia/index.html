Tenuta dell'Ornellaia

Le Volte dell'Ornellaia

RedToscana IGTWine region Tuscany

Tenuta dell’Ornellaia’s most approachable red, combining Merlot, Sangiovese and Cabernet Sauvignon in a fruity style.

Le Volte dell’Ornellaia is the Bolgheri estate’s entry-level wine and is conceived as a red to be enjoyed without waiting, reflecting the character of the Tuscan coast.

Unlike the house’s other wines, it combines Bordeaux varieties with Sangiovese, Tuscany’s traditional grape: Merlot, Sangiovese and Cabernet Sauvignon, in proportions that vary each year. That is why it is labelled as Toscana IGT rather than Bolgheri DOC.

The varieties are vinified separately in steel tanks, and the wine is aged for a few months in wood, partly in previously used barrels, so that the oak stays in the background. It is a fruity, smooth red that is versatile at the table.

Tasting profile

Bright ruby. Aromas of cherry, plum and red berries, with a touch of Mediterranean herbs and spice. Smooth and medium-bodied on the palate, with a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Pasta, pizza, burgers, grilled meat, young cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, medium-sized glass
