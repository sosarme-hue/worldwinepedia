San Felice Chianti Classico Gran Selezione La Pieve

RedChianti Classico Gran SelezioneControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Chianti Classico

A Gran Selezione from San Felice, the top category of Chianti Classico, a Sangiovese from Castelnuovo Berardenga built for ageing.

Gran Selezione is the highest category of the Chianti Classico pyramid, above Riserva. Introduced in the 2010s, it requires the grapes to come exclusively from the winery’s own vineyards and a longer minimum ageing period, and its rules have evolved to strengthen the role of Sangiovese and native varieties.

La Pieve is a Gran Selezione from San Felice, a winery in Castelnuovo Berardenga in the south of the appellation; its name refers to the pieve, the Tuscan term for old rural parish churches. It is made from Sangiovese from selected estate vineyards and aged in wood for an extended period, aiming to combine concentration, structure and elegance.

It is a full-bodied red with black cherry, plum, spice and tobacco notes, firm tannins and good acidity. It needs several years in bottle to express its full complexity.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby-garnet. Aromas of black cherry, plum, spice, tobacco and earth. Broad, structured palate with a long finish.

Food pairing

Game, wild boar stew, roast red meat, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decant
