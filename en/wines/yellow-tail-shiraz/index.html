[yellow tail] Shiraz

Red

Shiraz from Casella's [yellow tail] brand, a fruity, soft red that ranks among the best-selling Australian wines in the world.

Shiraz was one of the first wines of [yellow tail], the brand the Casella family launched in the early 2000s with an eye on the United States market. Its formula is based on a simple idea: to offer a fruity, soft and easy-to-understand red with a recognisable label and an affordable price.

The grapes come mainly from the Riverina region of New South Wales and from other areas of south-eastern Australia. The warm climate and irrigation make it possible to obtain ripe grapes and regular harvests. Winemaking aims to preserve fruit and soften tannins; the wine may have limited contact with oak, but it is not conceived as a barrel-aged red. The result is a round Shiraz with an indulgent profile and a slight fruity sweetness on the palate, designed for drinking young and on informal occasions.

Tasting profile

Aromas of ripe red and black fruit, plum and a touch of spice. Soft and juicy, medium-bodied, with light tannins.

Food pairing

Barbecue, burgers, pizza, tacos

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
