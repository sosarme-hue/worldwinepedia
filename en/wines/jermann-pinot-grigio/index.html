Jermann Pinot Grigio

WhiteVenezia Giulia IGT

Jermann Pinot Grigio from Friuli Venezia Giulia, a fresh, fruity unoaked white and one of the house’s most widely available wines.

Friuli Venezia Giulia is one of the Italian regions where Pinot Grigio (Pinot Gris) gives its most expressive results, thanks to a climate with Alpine and maritime influences and soils of alluvial and marl origin. Jermann, best known for its white blends, also makes a range of varietal wines, of which Pinot Grigio is one of the most widely available.

The grapes come from the house’s vineyards in the Isonzo area and the nearby hills. They are vinified in stainless steel at controlled temperature and the wine rests for some time on its fine lees before bottling, without oak ageing, to preserve its fruit and freshness.

It is a dry, balanced white with more character than many light lowland Pinot Grigios, intended to be drunk in its first few years. It is sold under the Venezia Giulia IGT geographical indication.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow with light copper hints. Aromas of pear, apple, peach and flowers. Dry, flavourful, fresh palate with a slightly almondy finish.

Food pairing

San Daniele ham, fish, pasta with vegetables, risotto, fresh cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
