Château Kefraya

Comte de M

RedWine region Bekaa Valley

Château Kefraya’s most ambitious red, named in tribute to Michel de Bustros. It is an aged wine based on Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah from the West Bekaa.

Comte de M is Château Kefraya’s flagship wine and owes its name to Michel de Bustros, the owner who planted the estate’s vineyards. It is made from a selection of grapes from the winery’s plots in the West Bekaa, on slopes at around a thousand metres, and is based mainly on Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah, in proportions that vary with the vintage.

After careful winemaking, the wine is aged in French oak barrels for an extended period and then refined in bottle before release. It is a red of concentrated style, in which the ripe fruit of the Bekaa combines with the structure of Cabernet Sauvignon and the spicy character of Syrah. It is designed for ageing and benefits from several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep, intense colour. Aromas of ripe black fruit, spice, liquorice and oak notes. Powerful and structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, red meat, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
