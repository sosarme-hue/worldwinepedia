El Enemigo

Gran Enemigo Gualtallary

RedWine region Valle de Uco

Top-end red from El Enemigo based on Cabernet Franc, from Gualtallary in the Uco Valley, an expression of a high-altitude site.

The Gran Enemigo wines are the most ambitious range in the project of Alejandro Vigil and Adrianna Catena. Each comes from a specific site in the Uco Valley, and all share the central role of Cabernet Franc, a variety Vigil considers particularly well suited to expressing Mendoza’s high-altitude soils. The Gualtallary bottling is probably the best known.

Gualtallary, in the department of Tupungato, is one of the highest areas of the Uco Valley, with stony, partly calcareous soils and a cool climate with sharp temperature differences between day and night. The wine is made mostly from Cabernet Franc, with a smaller proportion of Malbec that varies with the vintage. It is aged in French oak casks and barrels. It is a taut, spicy red with fine tannins, intended for long ageing.

Tasting profile

Deep purplish red. Aromas of red and black fruit, roasted pepper, wild herbs, spice and chalky notes. Taut palate with fine tannins and great length.

Food pairing

Chargrilled lamb, game, kid, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; may benefit from decanting
