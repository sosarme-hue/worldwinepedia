Château La Mission Haut-Brion

La Chapelle de la Mission Haut-Brion

RedSecond wineAOC Pessac-Léognan

Red second wine of Château La Mission Haut-Brion, a Pessac-Léognan of Merlot and Cabernet, more approachable in its youth than the grand vin.

La Chapelle de la Mission Haut-Brion is the red second wine of Château La Mission Haut-Brion, in Talence, within the Pessac-Léognan appellation. Its name refers to the estate’s chapel, a reminder of the time when it belonged to the priests of the Congregation of the Mission.

It is made from lots from the same gravel vineyard that are not used for the grand vin, often from younger vines. The blend brings together Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Cabernet Franc, and barrel ageing uses a smaller proportion of new oak.

It is a medium- to full-bodied red with ripe fruit, the smoky and spicy notes typical of Graves and softer tannins than the grand vin. It can be enjoyed in its first years, although good vintages develop favourably for a considerable time in bottle and gain complexity along the way.

Tasting profile

Dark ruby. Aromas of blackberry, plum, smoky and tobacco notes. Round, flavourful palate with soft tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, roast poultry, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass
