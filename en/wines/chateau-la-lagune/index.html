Château La Lagune

Château La Lagune

RedThird Growth (1855), Haut-MédocBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The grand vin of Château La Lagune, a Haut-Médoc third growth, a fine, balanced red from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Petit Verdot.

Château La Lagune is the grand vin of the estate of the same name in Ludon-Médoc, a third growth in 1855 and the southernmost of the Médoc’s classified growths. It is bottled under the Haut-Médoc appellation.

It comes from a single-block vineyard on very free-draining gravel and sandy soils, similar in character to those of Margaux. It is made from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and a proportion of Petit Verdot higher than usual in the Médoc, which brings colour, spicy notes and freshness. The proportions vary with the vintage.

Under the Frey family’s management, winemaking favours precise fruit and gentle extraction, with ageing in French oak barrels. The result is an elegant red with black and red fruit, floral and spicy notes and silky tannins, best enjoyed after a few years in bottle and able to age for a long time.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet. Aromas of blackcurrant, black cherry, violet, spice and cedar. Elegant, fresh palate with silky tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, beef fillet, game birds, mature cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, Bordeaux glass; decant when young
