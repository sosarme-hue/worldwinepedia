Tenuta San Guido

Sassicaia

RedBolgheri Sassicaia DOCControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Bolgheri

Tenuta San Guido’s Cabernet Sauvignon and Cabernet Franc red, the pioneer of the great wines of the Tuscan coast, with its own appellation.

Sassicaia grew out of Marchese Mario Incisa della Rocchetta’s interest in the wines of Bordeaux. The first Cabernet vines were planted in the 1940s on a stony plot of the estate, and after decades of family consumption the wine was first sold commercially with the 1968 vintage. Its name refers to the “sassi”, the stones in the soil.

Today it is made from a majority of Cabernet Sauvignon and a smaller share of Cabernet Franc, from several of the estate’s vineyards at different altitudes between the coast and the Bolgheri hills. After fermentation the wine is aged in oak barrels and completes its maturation in bottle before release.

It is a wine whose style is elegant rather than opulent, with a firm structure and remarkable ageing capacity. In time the area was given a specific appellation, Bolgheri Sassicaia, reserved for this wine.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry and black cherry, with notes of Mediterranean herbs, graphite and spice. Elegant and firm on the palate, with fine tannins, good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, beef fillet, game, mushroom dishes, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, large glass; benefits from decanting
