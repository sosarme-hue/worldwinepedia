Gratallops Vi de la Vila

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Priorat

Village wine from Álvaro Palacios made from Garnacha and Cariñena from various plots in the municipality of Gratallops.

Gratallops Vi de la Vila occupies the village wine tier in Álvaro Palacios' range, which is inspired by the way Burgundy's wines are organised. The Priorat officially recognises the term Vi de Vila, which identifies wines made from the grapes of a single municipality, and Gratallops is one of the villages with the strongest winegrowing tradition in the area.

The wine is made from Garnacha and Cariñena, in proportions that vary with the vintage, from various plots in the municipality, both the winery's own and those of local growers, many of them old vines on llicorella. Combining vineyards with different exposures and altitudes aims to offer a portrait of the village rather than of a single estate. Ageing takes place in oak barrels and large casks for a moderate period. It is a red with the intensity and mineral character typical of the Priorat, but more approachable in its youth than the house's estate wines.

Tasting profile

Intense ruby red. Aromas of ripe red and black fruit, liquorice, wild herbs and slate notes. Savoury on the palate, with ripe tannins and freshness.

Food pairing

Grilled meats, game stews, charcuterie, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
