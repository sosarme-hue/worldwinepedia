Planeta Santa Cecilia

RedNoto DOCWine region Sicily

Planeta Nero d’Avola from its estate in the Noto area of south-eastern Sicily, the historic home of this variety.

Nero d’Avola takes its name from the town of Avola, in south-eastern Sicily near Noto, considered its area of origin. Planeta, the Menfi family that has developed estates in different parts of the island, acquired vineyards in this area to make a Nero d’Avola that would express the character of its homeland. The result is Santa Cecilia.

It is made solely from Nero d’Avola from the vineyards of the Noto estate, in a landscape of white limestone soils and a warm climate influenced by the nearby sea. After fermentation with maceration, the wine is aged in oak for several months and then rests in bottle before release. It is sold under the Noto DOC appellation.

It is a Nero d’Avola of good structure and ripe fruit, with spicy notes and an acidity that adds freshness. It can evolve for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of black cherry, plum, carob, spice and Mediterranean herbs. Broad, juicy palate with soft tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Pasta with aubergine, caponata, grilled meat, cured meats, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
