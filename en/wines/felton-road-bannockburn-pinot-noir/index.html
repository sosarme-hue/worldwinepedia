Felton Road

Felton Road Bannockburn Pinot Noir

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Central Otago

Felton Road Pinot Noir blending grapes from several blocks of its biodynamically farmed vineyards at Bannockburn, in Central Otago.

The Bannockburn Pinot Noir is the core wine of Felton Road’s range. Unlike the block wines, which come from a single plot, it combines grapes from different blocks of the vineyards the winery farms at Bannockburn, on north-facing slopes above the Kawarau river, one of the warmest parts of Central Otago. All of them are farmed biodynamically.

The grapes are hand-picked and fermented with indigenous yeasts in small open vats, often with a proportion of whole bunches, using gentle extraction. The wine is moved by gravity and aged in French oak barrels, with a moderate proportion of new wood,, and is bottled after a period of settling. Its style combines the pure, intense fruit characteristic of Central Otago with a fine structure and good acidity, and it can be drunk young or after several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Cherry, raspberry and red plum, with notes of wild herbs, spice and flowers. Silky palate with fine tannins and fresh acidity.

Food pairing

Duck, lamb, mushrooms, salmon, game birds

Serving

14-16 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
