Planeta

Planeta La Segreta Rosso

RedSicilia DOCWine region Sicily

Blended red from Planeta based on Nero d’Avola, from the La Segreta line, designed as a fruity, approachable everyday wine.

The La Segreta line is the entry-level range of Planeta, the Sicilian family whose original winery is at Menfi, on the island’s south-west coast, beside the Arancio reservoir. Its name refers to one of the family’s historic estates in that area.

La Segreta Rosso is based on Nero d’Avola, Sicily’s great red variety, joined by other red varieties, some of them international, which complete the blend. The wine is labelled under the Sicilia DOC, which allows grapes from different parts of the island to be blended. Winemaking prioritises fruit, with brief ageing in which oak does not play a prominent role.

It is a medium-bodied, juicy, rounded red with aromas of cherry, plum and gentle spice. It is designed to be drunk in its first few years and is very versatile at the table.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby red. Aromas of cherry, plum, blackberry and gentle spice. Juicy, round palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Pizza, baked pasta, charcuterie, grilled meat, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, medium-sized glass
