Raventós i Blanc

Raventós i Blanc Blanc de Blancs

SparklingBrutDesignation of Origin (DO) Penedès

White sparkling wine from Raventós i Blanc made with organically grown Xarel·lo, Macabeo and Parellada, labelled Conca del Riu Anoia.

Blanc de Blancs is Raventós i Blanc's benchmark sparkling wine and the most widely distributed of the house. Since the winery left the Cava D.O. in 2012, it has been labelled under the geographical name Conca del Riu Anoia, which the house itself promoted to identify sparkling wines from the Anoia river basin made to more demanding rules.

It is made from the area's three native varieties: Xarel·lo, which brings structure and character, together with Macabeo and Parellada, organically grown and hand-harvested; the proportions vary with the vintage. The grapes come from the family estate, on calcareous soils rich in marine fossils, and from neighbouring growers. It follows the traditional method and rests on its lees for longer than the Cava minimum. It is a dry, taut sparkling wine with a fine mousse that favours freshness and the expression of origin over the richness of ageing.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with fine bubbles. Aromas of green apple, citrus, Mediterranean herbs and a mineral background. Dry, vibrant and saline on the palate.

Food pairing

Oysters, shellfish, raw fish, tempura, fresh cheeses

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass or flute
