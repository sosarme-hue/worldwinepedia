Domaines Ott Château de Selle Rosé

RoséCôtes de ProvenceWine region Provence

The rosé from Domaines Ott’s historic estate in Taradeau, a pale, elegant Côtes de Provence presented in the house’s distinctive bottle.

Château de Selle, in Taradeau, is the first property Marcel Ott acquired, in 1912, and the birthplace of Domaines Ott. Located inland in the Var on clay-limestone soils, it enjoys a Mediterranean climate with continental influence, with cooler nights than on the coast.

Its rosé is made mainly from Grenache and Cinsault, completed with Syrah and other Mediterranean varieties. The grapes are gently pressed to extract pale-coloured must, which ferments at controlled temperatures; part of the wine may be aged in large oak casks before blending, adding texture.

It is a dry, delicate rosé with good structure for its type, presented in the amphora-inspired bottle that has identified the house since the 1930s.

Tasting profile

Very pale pink with onion-skin tints. Aromas of white peach, citrus, flowers and Mediterranean herbs. Dry, delicate and fresh on the palate, with a smooth texture.

Food pairing

Mediterranean cuisine, bouillabaisse, chargrilled fish, salads, Asian cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
