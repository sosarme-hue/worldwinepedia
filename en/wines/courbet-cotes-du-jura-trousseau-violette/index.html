Courbet

Courbet Côtes du Jura Trousseau Violette

RedAOC Côtes du Jura

Trousseau red from the Jura producer Courbet, aged in wood, under the Côtes du Jura appellation.

Courbet is a family domaine in the Jura, the small wine region of eastern France between Burgundy and Switzerland, at the foot of the mountains of the same name. Like many houses in the area, it makes the traditional Jura range, including Chardonnay and Savagnin whites, light reds from Poulsard, Trousseau and Pinot Noir, and special wines such as vin jaune.

This wine is made from Trousseau, a red variety native to the Jura, known in Galicia as Merenzao and in Portugal as Bastardo. In the Jura it is less widely planted than Poulsard and is grown preferably on the warmest, stoniest soils, since it needs heat to ripen. It gives wines with deeper colour and more structure than Poulsard, though medium-bodied and very fresh.

The Côtes du Jura appellation covers most of the region’s vineyards. The wine is aged in wood, generally in used vessels that do not dominate the fruit. At 14% ABV, it shows notable ripeness for the region without losing the variety’s spicy, aromatic character.

Tasting profile

Medium ruby red. Aromas of cherry, red berries, pepper and earthy, floral notes. Fresh and savoury on the palate, with fine tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, roast poultry, mushrooms, Morteau sausage with lentils, pressed cheeses such as Comté

Serving

14-16 °C, Burgundy-style glass
