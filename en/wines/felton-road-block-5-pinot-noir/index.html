Felton Road

Felton Road Block 5 Pinot Noir

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Central Otago

Pinot Noir from a single block of Felton Road’s The Elms vineyard at Bannockburn, one of Central Otago’s benchmark wines.

Felton Road’s original vineyard, known as The Elms, is divided into numbered blocks that are harvested and vinified separately. Block 5 occupies part of the slope with loess and gravel soils over schist, and from the early vintages it stood out for giving a Pinot Noir of particular concentration and structure, so the winery decided to bottle it under its own name.

Winemaking follows the house philosophy: hand harvesting, fermentation with indigenous yeasts in open vats, a proportion of whole bunches depending on the vintage, gravity-fed movement of the wine and ageing in French oak barrels with a moderate proportion of new wood. The vineyard is farmed biodynamically. Compared with the Bannockburn Pinot Noir, Block 5 is a deeper, more structured wine with greater ageing potential, which usually benefits from several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Black cherry, plum and blackberry, with notes of spice, dried herbs and forest floor. Concentrated palate with firm, fine tannins and great length.

Food pairing

Roast duck, lamb, game birds, mushroom dishes

Serving

14-16 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
