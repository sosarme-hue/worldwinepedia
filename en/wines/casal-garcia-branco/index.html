Casal Garcia Vinho Verde Branco

WhiteControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Vinho Verde

White Vinho Verde from Aveleda launched in 1939, young, light and slightly spritzy, and one of the best-selling Portuguese wines in the world.

Casal Garcia was launched by Aveleda in 1939 and became one of the most recognisable Vinho Verde brands, with its traditionally inspired label. It represents the region’s most classic style: a young, light, refreshing white intended for drinking within the year.

It is made from native Minho varieties such as Trajadura (Treixadura), Loureiro, Arinto and Azal, from vineyards in the region. Fermentation takes place at controlled temperatures to preserve fruity and floral aromas, and the wine is bottled early, without ageing in wood. It has moderate alcohol, lively acidity and a slight spritz that enhances its freshness. Aveleda also makes rosé and red versions, as well as other variants under the same brand.

For many drinkers outside Portugal, this wine has for decades been the very image of Vinho Verde.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green glints. Aromas of citrus, green apple and white flowers. Light, fresh and slightly spritzy on the palate.

Food pairing

Appetisers, shellfish, grilled fish, salads, grilled sardines

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
