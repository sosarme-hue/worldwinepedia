Maison Louis Jadot

Louis Jadot Mercurey

RedBurgundy subregion (regional AOC Bourgogne Côte Chalonnaise and village AOCs) Côte Chalonnaise

Louis Jadot’s village red Mercurey, a fruity Pinot Noir from the largest appellation of the Côte Chalonnaise.

The Côte Chalonnaise extends the Côte de Beaune southwards in a more open landscape of hills, where vines alternate with meadows and woods. Its five village appellations, Bouzeron, Rully, Mercurey, Givry and Montagny, offer Burgundian-style wines with a slightly more rustic character and generally more moderate prices than those of the Côte d’Or. The area takes its name from the town of Chalon-sur-Saône, a few kilometres to the east.

Mercurey is the largest appellation in the area and produces mainly reds from Pinot Noir. This village wine blends grapes from different plots within the appellation. Louis Jadot ferments it in open vats and ages it in barrels, mostly used ones.

It is a medium-bodied, fruity and fresh red with soft tannins, intended to be drunk in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Bright ruby. Aromas of cherry, raspberry and spicy notes. Medium-bodied, fresh palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Roast poultry, charcuterie, white meat, soft cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, Burgundy glass
