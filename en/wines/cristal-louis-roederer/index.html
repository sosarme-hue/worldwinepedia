Champagne Louis Roederer

Louis Roederer Cristal

SparklingVintage brut, prestige cuvéeAOC (sparkling wine) Champagne

Louis Roederer’s prestige cuvée, created in 1876 for Tsar Alexander II and presented in a clear glass bottle.

Cristal was born in 1876 as a champagne reserved for the court of Tsar Alexander II, who, according to tradition, asked for a clear bottle to set it apart. Since the mid-20th century it has been sold generally and is one of the best-known prestige cuvées in Champagne.

It is made only in vintages considered exceptional, from the house’s own vineyards classified as grands crus, in the Montagne de Reims, the Vallée de la Marne and the Côte des Blancs. The blend is based on Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Some of the wines are aged in wood and the champagne spends several years on its lees before disgorgement. The clear bottle is protected from light by a yellow cellophane wrapper.

Cristal is characterised by its finesse and precision, and it can age for many years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Bright gold with very fine bubbles. Aromas of citrus, white fruit, white flowers and notes of toasted hazelnut. Delicate and precise on the palate, with a silky texture and a long, mineral finish.

Food pairing

Caviar, lobster, scallops, fine fish, poultry in creamy sauces

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
