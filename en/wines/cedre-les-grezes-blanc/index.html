Château du Cèdre Les Grèzes Blanc

WhiteIGP Côtes du LotAOC (South West France) Cahors

A white under the Côtes du Lot geographical indication from Château du Cèdre, fresh and fruity, in an area dominated by reds.

The Cahors appellation covers only red wines, so the whites produced in the Lot valley are sold under the Côtes du Lot protected geographical indication. This more flexible IGP allows local producers to work with white varieties and make wines in a lighter style.

Les Grèzes is the white under this indication made by Château du Cèdre, the Verhaeghe family property in Vire-sur-Lot. The name evokes the stony soils of the valley. It comes from organically farmed vineyards and is made with the aim of preserving the freshness and aromatic expression of the fruit, without ageing that would hide it.

It is a light- to medium-bodied white with aromas of white fruit and flowers and pleasant acidity. It is designed to be drunk young, as an aperitif or with simple fish and vegetable dishes.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of white fruit, citrus and blossom. Fresh, light palate with a clean finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, grilled fish, salads, fresh goat’s cheese

Serving

9-11 °C, white wine glass
