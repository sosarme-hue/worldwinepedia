Ata Rangi

Ata Rangi Pinot Noir

RedGeographical Indication (GI) Martinborough

Emblematic Martinborough Pinot Noir made by Ata Rangi from the estate’s old vines, including the selection known as the Abel clone.

Ata Rangi Pinot Noir is one of the wines that showed, in the 1980s and 1990s, that New Zealand could produce Pinot Noir of high quality. Since then it has become a benchmark for Martinborough and for the whole country.

It comes from the estate’s oldest vines and from other vineyards in the area farmed by the winery, on the gravels of the Martinborough terrace. An important part comes from the Pinot Noir selection known as the Abel clone, whose origin is associated with the well-known story of the cutting intercepted by customs at Auckland airport. The grapes are hand-picked and fermented in small open vats, sometimes with a proportion of whole bunches, and the wine is aged in French oak barrels. Its style combines concentration, structure and finesse, and it is known for its ability to develop for many years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Black cherry, plum and raspberry, with notes of spice, dried herbs and forest floor. Concentrated palate with firm, fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Duck, lamb, venison, mushroom dishes

Serving

14-16 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
