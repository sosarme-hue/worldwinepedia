Jean Leon

Jean Leon Vinya La Scala

RedGran ReservaDesignation of Origin (DO) Penedès

Long-aged Cabernet Sauvignon from Jean Leon, from a single Penedès plot and named after the founder's La Scala restaurant in Beverly Hills.

Vinya La Scala is Jean Leon's longest-aged red and pays tribute to the La Scala restaurant that the founder ran in Beverly Hills, where the winery's first wines were served. It comes from a specific plot on the Torrelavit estate, in the Penedès, planted with Cabernet Sauvignon, one of the French varieties that Jean Leon was among the first to grow in the area.

The wine is made as a red for long keeping: after fermentation, it is aged in oak barrels and then in bottle for a period that allows it to qualify as a Gran Reserva, the most demanding category of Spanish ageing. It is made only in vintages the winery considers suitable. Cabernet Sauvignon, which ripens well in the Mediterranean climate of the Penedès, gives a deeply coloured wine here, with firm tannins and evolved notes, in a style reminiscent of the Bordeaux classics.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red with a brick rim. Aromas of ripe black fruit, cedar, tobacco, spice and balsamic notes. Structured on the palate, with polished tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast red meats, game, stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
