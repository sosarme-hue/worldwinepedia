Williams & Humbert

Dry Sack

FortifiedMediumDesignation of Origin (DO) Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

Medium sherry from Williams & Humbert, one of the most internationally widespread sherry brands.

Dry Sack is Williams & Humbert's most famous brand and one of the best-known sherries outside Spain. For decades it was sold in a bottle wrapped in a small burlap sack, alluding to the word sack, the old English name for dry wines imported from Spain, including sherry.

Despite its name, it is not a completely dry wine but a medium sherry, the category of the Jerez D.O. covering blended wines with moderate sweetness. It is made by combining oxidatively aged Palomino wines, mainly amontillado and oloroso, with a share of sweet Pedro Ximénez wine; the proportions are not specified. The base wines are aged in the criaderas and solera system. The result is an amber sherry with nutty aromas and a slight sweetness that makes it accessible to those new to these wines.

Tasting profile

Dark amber. Aromas of walnut, hazelnut, raisins and caramel, with wood notes. Smooth and slightly sweet on the palate, with a broad finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif over ice, pâtés, aged cheeses, nuts

Serving

10-12 °C or over ice, white wine glass
