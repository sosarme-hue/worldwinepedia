Viñas del Vero

Viñas del Vero Gewürztraminer

WhiteDesignation of Origin (DO) Somontano

Aromatic Somontano white made by Viñas del Vero from Gewürztraminer, one of the wines that made this variety known in Spain.

Viñas del Vero's Gewürztraminer is one of the most popular whites from Somontano and one of the wines that showed that this variety, associated above all with Alsace and northern Italy, could do well at the foot of the Aragonese Pyrenees. Together with those of other wineries in the area, it helped Gewürztraminer become identified with Somontano in the Spanish market.

Gewürztraminer is a pink-skinned, highly aromatic variety that needs relatively cool climates to retain its acidity. In Somontano, cool nights during ripening help maintain balance. The grapes are picked at the right moment to preserve their aromas and vinified at low temperatures in stainless steel tanks, sometimes after a brief maceration on the skins to extract more aroma. There is no oak ageing. It is an expressive, medium-bodied white with a somewhat unctuous texture, best enjoyed young.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with golden hints. Intense aromas of rose petals, lychee, tropical fruit and sweet spice. Broad, silky and aromatic on the palate.

Food pairing

Asian cuisine, mild curry, foie gras, washed-rind cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
