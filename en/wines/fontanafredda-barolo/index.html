Fontanafredda

Fontanafredda Barolo

RedBarolo DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Barolo

Fontanafredda’s core Barolo, a widely available, classically styled Nebbiolo from the historic Serralunga d’Alba estate and other vineyards in the area.

Fontanafredda’s Barolo is one of the easiest Barolos to find on sale and a good gateway to the appellation. It is made from Nebbiolo from the estate’s vineyards in Serralunga d’Alba and from other municipalities in the Barolo area.

Serralunga, in the eastern part of the appellation, is associated with firmly structured, tannic Barolos, thanks to its more compact, calcareous soils. Blending with grapes from other areas adds balance and aroma.

After fermentation the wine is aged in large oak casks for an extended period and then rests in bottle, meeting the appellation’s minimum ageing of just over three years. It is a classic Barolo that gains complexity with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Garnet with orange glints. Aromas of cherry, faded rose, spice and liquorice, with notes of tobacco and leather. Firm on the palate, with pronounced tannins, good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Braised beef, roast red meat, game, truffle dishes, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, large Burgundy-style glass; benefits from decanting
