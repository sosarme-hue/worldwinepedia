Foradori

Foradori

RedWine region Trentino-Alto Adige

The emblematic red of the Foradori winery, a Teroldego from the Campo Rotaliano in Trentino, with dark fruit and alpine freshness.

The red that bears the family name, Foradori, is the most representative wine of the Mezzolombardo winery and its benchmark interpretation of Teroldego, the native variety of the Campo Rotaliano in Trentino. For years it was sold as Teroldego Rotaliano, and today it carries the Vigneti delle Dolomiti geographical indication.

It is made exclusively from Teroldego from various family plots on the plain’s alluvial gravel and sand soils, farmed biodynamically. The vines largely derive from the massal selection of old vines carried out by Elisabetta Foradori to restore the variety’s diversity. Vinification aims to preserve fruit and freshness, with ageing that does not mask the character of the grape.

It is a deeply coloured red with aromas of dark fruit, cherry and floral and spicy notes, medium body, lively acidity and fine tannins. It can be enjoyed young, although it develops well over a few years.

Tasting profile

Deep purple-red. Aromas of blackberry, black cherry, violet and pepper. Juicy, fresh palate with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, grilled meat, mushrooms, polenta with stews, mountain cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
