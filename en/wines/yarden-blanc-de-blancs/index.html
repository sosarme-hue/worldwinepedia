Golan Heights Winery

Yarden Blanc de Blancs

SparklingBrutWine region Golan Heights

A traditional-method sparkling wine made by Golan Heights Winery from Chardonnay grown in the coolest Golan vineyards. It spends a long period on its lees before disgorgement.

Yarden Blanc de Blancs is Golan Heights Winery’s best-known sparkling wine and one of the benchmarks for the traditional method in Israel. As its name indicates, it is made only from white grapes, in this case Chardonnay, from vineyards in the high, cool parts of the Golan, picked early to preserve acidity.

The base wine undergoes a second fermentation in bottle, in accordance with the traditional method, and then remains for several years in contact with the lees before disgorgement. This extended ageing brings complexity and a fine mousse. The result is a dry sparkling wine in the Brut style, combining the freshness of high-altitude Chardonnay with bakery notes from lees ageing. It is a kosher wine. It works well both as an aperitif and at the table, where its acidity pairs well with a wide range of dishes.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with fine bubbles. Aromas of citrus, apple, pastry and nut notes. Fresh and creamy on the palate, with good acidity and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, fish and shellfish, sushi, fried dishes, mild cheeses

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine or tulip glass
