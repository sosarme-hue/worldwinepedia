Schlumberger Pinot Gris Vendanges Tardives Cuvée Laure

SweetVendanges TardivesWine region (AOC Alsace, Alsace Grand Cru and Crémant d'Alsace) Alsace

Late-harvest Pinot Gris from Domaines Schlumberger in Guebwiller: a rich, spicy and balanced sweet Alsace white from overripe grapes.

Cuvée Laure is the Pinot Gris Vendanges Tardives of Domaines Schlumberger, the family house in Guebwiller in southern Alsace, which also makes a late-harvest Gewurztraminer, Cuvée Christine.

The Vendanges Tardives designation, regulated in Alsace, is reserved for Riesling, Gewurztraminer, Pinot Gris and Muscat wines made from overripe grapes, with a minimum sugar level set by the regulations, and is produced only in favourable vintages. Pinot Gris, with its pink skin and great natural richness, is well suited to this style: as ripening continues on the vine, sometimes helped by noble rot, it gains concentration and smoky, spicy notes.

The sunny, dry slopes of Guebwiller, sheltered by the Vosges, favour this late ripening. The result is a sweet, unctuous and complex wine whose richness is balanced by freshness, and which can be kept for many years.

Tasting profile

Deep gold. Aromas of quince, dried apricot, honey, smoky notes and spice. Sweet, unctuous palate balanced by freshness, with a long finish.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses, poultry with fruit sauces, fruit tarts

Serving

9-11 °C, white wine glass
