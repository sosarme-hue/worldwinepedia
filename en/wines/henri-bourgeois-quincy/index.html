Henri Bourgeois

Henri Bourgeois Quincy

WhiteWine region Loire

Quincy from Henri Bourgeois, a dry, fresh Sauvignon Blanc from a small Central Loire appellation beside the river Cher.

Henri Bourgeois is a family house in Chavignol, in the heart of Sancerre, with a long winegrowing tradition in the Central Loire. Besides its Sancerre, it makes wines in neighbouring appellations, always centred on Sauvignon Blanc, and pays particular attention to the expression of the area’s different soils: Kimmeridgian marls, limestone and flint.

Quincy is a small appellation west of Bourges, on the left bank of the Cher, and one of the oldest in the Loire. It allows only white wine from Sauvignon Blanc, grown on terraces of ancient sand and gravel, light soils that tend to give fruity, lively whites, somewhat more open than those of Sancerre. Henri Bourgeois makes it without oak, in a dry, fresh style meant to be drunk in its first years.

Tasting profile

Citrus, passion fruit, fresh herbs. Light, dry palate with lively acidity and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Goat’s cheese, grilled fish, shellfish, salads, aperitif

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
