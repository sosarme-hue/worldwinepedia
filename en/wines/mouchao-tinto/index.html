Herdade do Mouchão

Mouchão Tinto

RedControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) and Alentejano GI Alentejo

The main red of Herdade do Mouchão, made mostly from Alicante Bouschet by traditional methods, with long ageing in large vats and in bottle.

Mouchão red is one of the classic wines of the Alentejo and the best-known expression of Alicante Bouschet in Portugal. Herdade do Mouchão, owned by the Reynolds family, was one of the first estates in the country to grow this red-fleshed teinturier variety, which here gives wines of great colour and structure.

The wine is made mostly from Alicante Bouschet, completed in some years with a small proportion of Trincadeira; the composition varies with the vintage. Winemaking follows traditional methods: part of the fruit is trodden in lagares and fermentation involves long macerations. The wine then ages for years in large old wooden vats, which allow slow development without marking the wine with new-oak aromas, and spends an additional period in bottle before release.

It is a red for long cellaring, concentrated and tannic in youth, which develops great complexity over time. It should be decanted.

Tasting profile

Very dark, almost opaque. Aromas of ripe black fruit, plum, spice, and earthy, leather and scrubland notes with age. Concentrated, tannic palate with good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Large game, roast lamb, meat stews, Iberian pork, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
