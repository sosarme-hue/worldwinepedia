Napoleón Amontillado

FortifiedAmontilladoDesignation of Origin (DO) Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

Dry amontillado from Hidalgo-La Gitana, aged in Sanlúcar first under flor and then oxidatively.

Napoleón is the amontillado of Bodegas Hidalgo-La Gitana and a historic brand of the Sanlúcar house. Like every amontillado, it is a wine of dual ageing, combining the characteristics of biological and oxidative ageing.

It is made from Palomino from albariza vineyards. In a first stage the wine is aged like a manzanilla, under flor, in the humid bodegas of Sanlúcar de Barrameda. When the flor is exhausted, the wine is fortified and continues ageing in contact with the air, in a dedicated criaderas and solera system, for several years. In this second phase the wine gains its amber colour, concentration and aromas of nuts and wood, while keeping the finesse and saline note inherited from its Sanlúcar origin. The result is a dry, elegant amontillado, perhaps somewhat lighter than those of Jerez, very well suited to accompanying food.

Tasting profile

Golden amber. Aromas of hazelnut, toasted almond, wood, saline notes and a spicy background. Dry and savoury on the palate, with a long finish.

Food pairing

Consommés, artichokes, mushrooms, white meats, aged cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, white wine glass
