Château Musar

Château Musar Rouge

RedWine region Bekaa Valley

Château Musar’s emblematic red, a blend of Cabernet Sauvignon, Cinsault and Carignan from the Bekaa released several years after the harvest. It is known for its personal style and its ability to evolve over decades.

The red Château Musar is made from Cabernet Sauvignon, Cinsault and Carignan grapes from vineyards in the Bekaa, in proportions that vary with the vintage. The varieties are fermented separately and aged for a time in French oak barrels before blending. The wine then spends a long period in bottle in the cellars at Ghazir, so that it reaches the market several years after the harvest.

The house champions minimal intervention and a style that does not seek uniformity between vintages, so each year has its own character. The red Musar combines the structure of Cabernet Sauvignon with the finesse and spicy fruit of Cinsault and Carignan, and usually shows notes of evolution, sometimes with a perceptible volatile acidity that is part of its style. It is a wine made for ageing, which can go on developing in bottle for decades.

Tasting profile

Ruby or garnet in colour, often with an evolved rim. Aromas of ripe red and black fruit, spice, leather and earthy notes. Savoury on the palate, with already polished tannins, good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, kebabs and grilled meats, game, spiced Middle Eastern dishes, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
