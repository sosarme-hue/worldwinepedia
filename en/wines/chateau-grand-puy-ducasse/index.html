Château Grand-Puy Ducasse

Château Grand-Puy Ducasse

RedAOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Pauillac

Pauillac fifth growth classified in 1855, whose house stands on the quays of the town of Pauillac and whose vines are spread over several parts of the appellation.

Château Grand-Puy Ducasse is one of the cinquièmes grands crus of the 1855 classification in the Pauillac appellation. Unlike most Médoc châteaux, its main building stands in the town of Pauillac itself, facing the Gironde estuary, while its vineyards are spread over several plots in different parts of the appellation, some near great names in the north and south of the commune. It belongs to a group of estates linked to the Crédit Agricole bank.

Pauillac, in the heart of the Haut-Médoc, is home to three of the five first growths of 1855 and is the benchmark appellation for Cabernet Sauvignon, which finds ideal ground in its deep gravels and gives reds of great structure and longevity. The Grand-Puy Ducasse blend is based on Cabernet Sauvignon, with a significant share of Merlot.

Aged in oak barrels, it is a Pauillac of black fruit, notes of cedar and spice and firm tannins, somewhat more approachable in its youth than other wines of the appellation, yet able to develop for years. It is best decanted if drunk young.

Tasting profile

Cherry red. Aromas of blackcurrant, plum, cedar, spice and gentle toast. Well structured on the palate, with firm but approachable tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled red meat, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, Bordeaux glass
