Maison Louis Latour

Louis Latour Corton-Charlemagne Grand Cru

WhiteGrand CruBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Beaune

The Corton-Charlemagne of Louis Latour, one of the main owners of this white Grand Cru on the hill of Corton.

Corton-Charlemagne is the white Grand Cru of the hill of Corton, shared between the communes of Aloxe-Corton, Pernand-Vergelesses and Ladoix-Serrigny, in the north of the Côte de Beaune. Its name recalls the tradition that Charlemagne owned vineyards on this slope. Louis Latour is one of the largest owners in the appellation, where the family has held vines for generations.

The wine is made exclusively from Chardonnay, planted on the upper part of the hill on marl and limestone soils. It ferments and ages in oak barrels, partly made in the house’s own cooperage.

It is a white of great structure which may seem somewhat closed in its youth and develops more complex notes with age. It is one of Louis Latour’s benchmark wines.

Tasting profile

Golden. Aromas of yellow fruit, ripe citrus, butter, hazelnut and mineral notes. Powerful and broad on the palate, with good acidity and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Lobster, turbot, Bresse chicken in sauce, hard cheeses such as Comté

Serving

12-14 °C, Burgundy white wine glass
