Château de Fieuzal

L'Abeille de Fieuzal Blanc

WhiteAOC Pessac-Léognan

White second wine of Château de Fieuzal, a fresh blend of Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon from Pessac-Léognan.

L'Abeille de Fieuzal Blanc is the white version of the second wine of Château de Fieuzal, a Léognan property whose dry white enjoys a fine reputation in the Pessac-Léognan appellation.

Like the grand vin, it is made from a blend of Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon grown on the area's gravels, but from lots selected for a fruitier style for earlier drinking. Sauvignon brings freshness and citrus and herbaceous aromas, Sémillon roundness and texture. Ageing, partly in wood, is lighter than for the grand vin.

It is a lively, aromatic white with a pleasant texture and a mineral background, offering an accessible introduction to the style of Graves whites. It is enjoyed in its first few years, though it can develop a little in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of citrus, white fruit, boxwood and flowers. Fresh, rounded palate with a slightly mineral finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, shellfish, poultry, goat's cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
