Floramundi Cerasuolo di Vittoria

RedCerasuolo di Vittoria DOCGWine region Sicily

Cerasuolo di Vittoria from Donnafugata, a blend of Nero d’Avola and Frappato from Sicily’s only DOCG, fruity and fresh in style.

Cerasuolo di Vittoria is Sicily’s only DOCG. It is made in the south-east of the island, around Vittoria, and combines two native varieties: Nero d’Avola, which brings colour, body and dark fruit, and Frappato, which adds perfume, freshness and lightness. Its name refers to the cherry colour (cerasa) of the wine.

Donnafugata owns an estate in the Vittoria area, with vineyards on sandy and calcareous soils, which is the source of Floramundi. As is usual in this appellation, the wine aims for elegance and drinkability rather than power, and the winemaking respects the fruit of both varieties.

The result is a medium-bodied red with aromas of cherry, strawberry and flowers, gentle spicy notes and fine tannins. It is a versatile wine that can be enjoyed young and can be served a little cooler than other reds.

Tasting profile

Bright cherry red. Aromas of cherry, strawberry, violet and gentle spice. Fresh, harmonious palate with fine tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Baked pasta, charcuterie, roast poultry, tuna, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, medium-sized glass
