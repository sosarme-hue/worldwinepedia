Argiolas Iselis Nasco di Cagliari

WhiteNasco di Cagliari DOCWine region Sardinia

White from Argiolas made from Nasco, an ancient native variety of southern Sardinia, under the Nasco di Cagliari appellation.

Iselis is the name of a line from Argiolas, the family winery in Serdiana, dedicated to traditional varieties of southern Sardinia. As a white, Iselis is made from Nasco, one of the island’s oldest and least widespread grapes, grown mainly in the province of Cagliari.

The Nasco di Cagliari appellation allows both dry and sweet or fortified wines; historically Nasco was associated with sweet dessert wines, but it also gives characterful dry whites. Its aromatic profile, with notes recalling ripe fruit, aromatic herbs and sometimes a musky touch, sets it apart from other Sardinian varieties such as Vermentino.

Argiolas makes it as a dry white with some structure, with partial ageing that adds texture and roundness. The result is an original wine with body and Mediterranean freshness, a good match for fish, shellfish and Sardinian dishes.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of ripe yellow fruit, Mediterranean herbs and a musky hint. Broad, savoury palate with a slightly saline finish.

Food pairing

Baked fish, shellfish, bottarga, fresh cheeses

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
