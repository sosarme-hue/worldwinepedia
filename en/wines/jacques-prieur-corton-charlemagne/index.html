Domaine Jacques Prieur

Corton-Charlemagne Grand Cru

WhiteGrand CruBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Beaune

White grand cru from the hill of Corton made by Domaine Jacques Prieur from Chardonnay.

Corton-Charlemagne covers the upper slopes of the hill of Corton, between Aloxe-Corton, Pernand-Vergelesses and Ladoix-Serrigny, in the north of the Côte de Beaune. Its marl and limestone soils, with exposures ranging from south-east to west, give whites of tension and mineral character, very different from those of Meursault or Puligny. Domaine Jacques Prieur, based in Meursault, vinifies each of its plots across the Côte d’Or separately and ages its wines in oak barrels, in a style that seeks concentration, texture and ageing potential.

It is a firm, deep white, often somewhat closed in its youth, which needs several years in bottle to show its breadth and complexity. It is best served not too cold and, when young, opened somewhat in advance or decanted, so that the fruit and barrel ageing come together and the wine fully expresses its terroir.

Tasting profile

White and yellow fruit, citrus, hazelnut, butter and toasty notes. Rich, broad palate with fresh acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Lobster, turbot, Bresse chicken in cream, soft cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, large glass
