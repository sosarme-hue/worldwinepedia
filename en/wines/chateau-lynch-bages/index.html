Château Lynch-Bages

Château Lynch-Bages

RedCinquième Grand Cru Classé (1855)Bordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The grand vin of Château Lynch-Bages, a Cabernet Sauvignon-led Pauillac known for its generous style.

Lynch-Bages’ grand vin comes from the vineyards of the Bages plateau, on the deep gravels typical of Pauillac. Cabernet Sauvignon is the majority variety in the blend, completed with Merlot and small proportions of Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot depending on the vintage.

After fermentation, the wine is aged in French oak barrels, with a significant proportion of new wood, for a period that usually runs to about eighteen months. The plots are vinified separately and selection determines which lots go into the grand vin and which into the second wine, Echo de Lynch-Bages.

Lynch-Bages is a classic-profile Pauillac, with concentrated black fruit and firm tannins, which benefits from several years in bottle and can age for decades.

Tasting profile

Dark ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, blackberry, cedar, graphite and spice, with toasty notes. Broad and structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Pauillac lamb, rib steak, entrecôte bordelaise, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass; decanting recommended
