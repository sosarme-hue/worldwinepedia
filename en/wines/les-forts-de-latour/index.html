Château Latour

Les Forts de Latour

RedSecond wineBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The second wine of Château Latour, made mainly from plots outside L’Enclos together with part of its production, deep in style but somewhat more approachable.

Les Forts de Latour is the second wine of Château Latour. It takes its name from plots lying to the west of the estate, and today it comes from several of the property’s vineyards in Pauillac that are not part of L’Enclos, together with a share of the grapes from that historic vineyard, mainly from younger vines.

The blend is based on Cabernet Sauvignon, completed with Merlot and, in some years, small amounts of Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot; the proportions vary from vintage to vintage. Vinification and barrel ageing follow the same principles as for the grand vin, with a somewhat smaller proportion of new oak.

Like the grand vin, Les Forts de Latour stopped being sold en primeur from the 2012 vintage and is released several years after the harvest. It shares the château’s firm, deep character but tends to be more open at an earlier age, although it too ages for a long time.

Tasting profile

Dark garnet. Aromas of blackcurrant, plum and cedar, with notes of graphite and spice. Well-concentrated palate with firm but polished tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled rib steak, game, hard aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large Bordeaux glass
