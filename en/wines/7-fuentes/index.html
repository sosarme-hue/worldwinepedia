Suertes del Marqués

7 Fuentes

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Valle de La Orotava

Red from the Orotava Valley, Tenerife, based on Listán Negro, fresh and volcanic in character.

7 Fuentes is the most widely available red from Suertes del Marqués and a good introduction to the wines of the Orotava Valley, in northern Tenerife. It is made as a village wine, from grapes from different plots in the valley, rather than from a single plot like the house's more exclusive wines.

Its base is Listán Negro, completed with small amounts of other local varieties such as Tintilla; the proportions vary with the vintage. The vines, many of them old and ungrafted, grow on volcanic soils at different altitudes, often in the valley's traditional braided cordon, and are influenced by the humid trade winds. Winemaking is low-intervention, with native yeasts, and the wine is aged in tanks and large used wooden vessels so as not to mask the fruit. The result is a medium-bodied, fresh, spicy red with notes often described as volcanic.

Tasting profile

Medium-deep cherry red. Aromas of red fruit, black pepper, herbs and smoky, mineral notes. Fresh and flowing on the palate, with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled meats, kid goat, tuna, mushrooms, aged Canarian cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
