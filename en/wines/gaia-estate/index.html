Gaia Wines

Gaia Estate

RedNemea PDOProtected designation of origin (PDO) Nemea

An Agiorgitiko red from Gaia, from high-altitude vineyards in Koutsi, within the Nemea PDO. It is the house’s benchmark red.

Gaia Estate is made exclusively from Agiorgitiko, as the Nemea PDO requires, from vineyards on the high slopes of Koutsi, one of the appellation’s highest villages. At these elevations the variety ripens more slowly than on the valley floor and keeps livelier acidity, which makes it possible to produce a structured red with ageing potential.

After fermentation, the wine is aged in oak barrels for an extended period and rests in bottle before release. It aims to show the more serious side of Agiorgitiko, a variety often known for soft, young reds: a deeply coloured wine with concentrated fruit and firmer tannins, which gains complexity with a few years in bottle. It is one of the reds that, since the 1990s, have contributed to Nemea’s new reputation.

Tasting profile

Intense garnet red. Aromas of black cherry, plum and blackberry, with sweet spice, vanilla and cocoa notes. Broad on the palate, with ripe but firm tannins and acidity that keeps it fresh.

Food pairing

roast lamb, moussaka, grilled red meat, aged sheep’s cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
