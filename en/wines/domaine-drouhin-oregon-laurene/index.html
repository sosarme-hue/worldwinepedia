Laurène Pinot Noir

RedAVA Willamette Valley

A selection of Domaine Drouhin Oregon’s best Pinot Noir barrels, named after Véronique Drouhin-Boss’s elder daughter.

Laurène is Domaine Drouhin Oregon’s top Pinot Noir. Each year Véronique Drouhin-Boss and her team select from the lots of the Dundee Hills estate the barrels that show the greatest concentration, complexity and balance. The wine is named after her elder daughter.

The grapes come from the estate vineyards, on volcanic soils of the Jory series, and the winemaking follows the same principles as the rest of the range: hand harvesting, a sorting table, moving the wine by gravity and ageing in French oak barrels, in this case with a somewhat higher proportion of new wood. It is a deeper, more structured Pinot Noir than the Dundee Hills bottling, intended to evolve for several years in bottle without losing the freshness and finesse that characterise the house style.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby red. Aromas of black cherry, raspberry, spice and forest-floor notes. Deep and silky on the palate, with fine tannins, good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast duck, squab, wild mushrooms, pork tenderloin, washed-rind cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
