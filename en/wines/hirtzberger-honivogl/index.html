Grüner Veltliner Smaragd Honivogl

WhiteSmaragd (Vinea Wachau)DAC (Districtus Austriae Controllatus) Wachau

Grüner Veltliner Smaragd from the Honivogl vineyard, one of Hirtzberger’s most highly regarded wines, full-bodied and age-worthy.

The Honivogl is a vineyard in the western Wachau, near Spitz, long known for its Grüner Veltliners. Its soils combine primary rock and deeper deposits, which retain water and allow full ripening, while the climate, cooler in this part of the region, preserves acidity.

Franz Hirtzberger makes a Smaragd-category Grüner Veltliner from these grapes, Vinea Wachau’s category for its ripest dry wines. The grapes are picked by hand, often late, and the wine ferments in tanks and casks. The result is a full-bodied dry Grüner Veltliner, rich and concentrated, with ripe fruit and spicy notes, supported by acidity that gives it balance and ageing potential.

It is best not served too cold and left to breathe in the glass, as its aromas open up with air; with a few years in bottle it usually gains complexity.

Tasting profile

Aromas of ripe yellow fruit, quince, white pepper, herbs and spicy notes. Dry, broad and creamy on the palate, with fresh acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Wiener Schnitzel, vegetables, asparagus, freshwater fish, Asian food

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
