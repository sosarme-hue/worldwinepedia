Casa Castillo

Casa Castillo Monastrell

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Jumilla

Casa Castillo's most accessible Monastrell, a juicy, fresh red expressing the estate's style in Jumilla.

Casa Castillo Monastrell is the gateway to the wines of the Vicente family's estate in Jumilla. It is made from Monastrell from different plots on the property, located on a high plateau surrounded by mountains, with limestone, gravel and sandy soils.

The aim is to show the variety's fresher, fruitier side, far from the heavily extracted, oaky reds that once characterised the area. The grapes are harvested seeking a good balance between ripeness and acidity, and the wine has a short ageing in which oak plays a secondary role. The result is a juicy, Mediterranean red offering good value, which helps to understand the recent evolution of Monastrell in Jumilla.

Tasting profile

Fresh black fruit, notes of wild herbs, pepper and an earthy background. Juicy palate with fine tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, grilled meats, meat rice dishes, roasted vegetables

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
