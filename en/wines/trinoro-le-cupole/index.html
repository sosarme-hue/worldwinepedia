Tenuta di Trinoro

Le Cupole

RedToscana IGTWine region Tuscany

Second wine of Tenuta di Trinoro, a blend of Bordeaux varieties from the Val d’Orcia, more approachable than the estate’s main wine.

Tenuta di Trinoro, created by Andrea Franchetti near Sarteano in the Val d’Orcia, is devoted to Bordeaux varieties, planted at high density and harvested late in search of full ripeness. Its wines are made under the Toscana IGT designation.

Le Cupole is the estate’s second wine. It is made from a blend of the house’s Bordeaux varieties, mainly Cabernet Franc and Merlot, together with Cabernet Sauvignon and Petit Verdot, in proportions that change with the vintage. It comes both from younger vines and from lots not used for the main wine.

It shares the rest of the range’s pursuit of ripeness and concentration, but with shorter ageing and a more direct profile. It is a generous, fruity red with soft tannins, which can be enjoyed in its first few years and offers an introduction to Trinoro’s distinctive style.

Tasting profile

Ripe black fruit, plum, herbs and gentle spice. Generous, rounded palate with friendly tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, game, lamb, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
