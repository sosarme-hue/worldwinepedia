Opus One Winery

Opus One

RedAVA Napa Valley

A Bordeaux-style red born of the alliance between Baron Philippe de Rothschild and Robert Mondavi, first made in 1979.

Opus One is the flagship wine of the winery of the same name and one of California’s best-known reds. It was born of the collaboration between the owner of Château Mouton Rothschild and Robert Mondavi, and its first vintage was 1979. From the start it was conceived as a great Bordeaux-style blend made from Oakville vineyards.

Cabernet Sauvignon is the dominant variety, accompanied by varying proportions of Merlot, Cabernet Franc, Petit Verdot and Malbec depending on the vintage. Harvesting is by hand and the grapes are carefully sorted; the wine is aged in new French oak barrels for an extended period before bottling. The result is a red with ripe fruit and a silky texture, with the structure needed to evolve in bottle for many years.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet red. Nose of blackcurrant, black cherry and violet with notes of cedar, graphite and cocoa. Enveloping and balanced on the palate, with fine tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, roast lamb, rib steak, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
