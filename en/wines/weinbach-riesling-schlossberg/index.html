Domaine Weinbach

Riesling Grand Cru Schlossberg

WhiteAlsace Grand CruWine region (AOC Alsace, Alsace Grand Cru and Crémant d'Alsace) Alsace

Domaine Weinbach Riesling from the Schlossberg grand cru, a granite slope in Kaysersberg, in a dry, taut, mineral style.

The Schlossberg, which rises above Kaysersberg and Kientzheim beneath the ruins of a medieval castle, was the first vineyard in Alsace to be recognised as a grand cru. Its terraces, on steep south-facing slopes, sit on poor, well-drained soils of weathered granite, particularly suited to Riesling.

Domaine Weinbach owns a significant share of this grand cru, which it farms biodynamically. The wine is made exclusively from Riesling, picked by hand and fermented with indigenous yeasts in large old oak casks, which impart no oak flavour. It is usually made in a dry style, although the character of each vintage influences its profile.

It is a precise, luminous Riesling, with citrus fruit and marked mineral tension, which may seem somewhat reserved in its youth and gains complexity with years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Bright pale yellow. Aromas of lemon, grapefruit, white peach, flowers and stony notes. Dry, taut, vibrant palate with a long, saline finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, choucroute, poultry, Asian cuisine

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
