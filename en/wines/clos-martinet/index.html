Mas Martinet

Clos Martinet

RedCrianzaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Priorat

A red from Mas Martinet, one of the five pioneering wineries that drove Priorat's revival in the late 1980s.

Mas Martinet is one of the founding wineries of modern Priorat. Its creator, Josep Lluís Pérez, a winemaker and teacher, was part of the group of five pioneers who, in the late 1980s, set out to revive the region's terraced vineyards and make highly ambitious reds. Clos Martinet is one of those five historic "clos". For some years now the winery has been in the hands of the next generation, led by Sara Pérez.

The vineyards are farmed on llicorella, the slate that defines the Priorat landscape, using organic methods. The wine is based on the region's traditional varieties, Grenache and Carignan, with contributions from varieties such as Syrah or Cabernet Sauvignon, planted in the pioneers' era.

It carries the Crianza designation and is aged in barrel, although in recent years the winery has sought a style less marked by oak and more focused on freshness and the expression of terroir. It is a deep, mineral Priorat with black fruit, herbal notes and a structure suited to ageing.

Tasting profile

Intense cherry red. Aromas of black fruit, liquorice, wild herbs, spice and mineral notes. Deep, fresh palate with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, game, grilled meats, mushroom stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decanting recommended
