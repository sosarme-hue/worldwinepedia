Andrew Will

Andrew Will Ciel du Cheval

RedVashon

Bordeaux-style red from Andrew Will sourced from Ciel du Cheval Vineyard on Red Mountain, one of the warmest and most highly regarded areas of Washington State.

Andrew Will is a Washington State winery founded in the late 1980s by Chris Camarda, who named it after his nephew Andrew and his son Will. The winery is on Vashon Island, in Puget Sound, but the grapes come from the east of the state, in the Columbia River basin, where a dry, sunny climate with large temperature swings ripens the Bordeaux varieties well. The house made its name by bottling wines from individual vineyards separately.

Ciel du Cheval is a vineyard in the Red Mountain AVA, a small slope above the Yakima River known for its hot summers, its exposure to wind and its poor, sandy soils, which give thick-skinned grapes and marked tannins. It is one of Washington’s benchmark vineyards and supplies several wineries in the state.

Andrew Will makes from its fruit a red that blends Bordeaux varieties. It is fermented separately and aged in oak barrels, aiming to express the tannic power typical of Red Mountain without losing freshness. It tends to be closed and firm in youth and needs time in bottle or aeration to open up; with age it gains complexity.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red. Aromas of cassis, black cherry, dried herbs, cocoa and spicy oak. Structured and fresh on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, roast lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
