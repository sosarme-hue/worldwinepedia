Casa de Piedra

Vino de Piedra

RedWine valley Valle de Guadalupe

Tempranillo and Cabernet Sauvignon red blend created by Hugo D’Acosta at Casa de Piedra, one of the benchmark wines of the Valle de Guadalupe.

Vino de Piedra is the signature wine of Casa de Piedra, the winery founded in 1997 by the winemaker Hugo D’Acosta in San Antonio de las Minas, in the southern Valle de Guadalupe. Its appearance coincided with the start of a new era for Baja California wine, marked by small-production artisan projects, and it has become one of the region’s benchmark reds.

It is a blend of Tempranillo and Cabernet Sauvignon, two varieties well suited to the valley’s semi-arid Mediterranean climate: the former brings red fruit and suppleness, the latter structure and black-fruit notes. The grapes come from selected vineyards in Baja California, on poor, sandy soils of granitic origin. The wine is aged in oak barrels and then rests in bottle before release. It is a full-bodied red with ripe fruit and a structure that allows it to develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red. Aromas of cherry, plum and blackberry with notes of spice, tobacco and toasted oak. Broad and structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Grilled meats, lamb, mole, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
