Lungarotti

Lungarotti Rubesco Riserva Vigna Monticchio

RedTorgiano Rosso Riserva DOCG

Riserva version of Rubesco from the Monticchio vineyard, Lungarotti’s top red and a benchmark of the Torgiano Rosso Riserva DOCG.

The Vigna Monticchio Riserva comes from a specific vineyard on the Lungarotti estate in Torgiano, in the hills near Perugia, and is the house’s top wine. It is sold under the Torgiano Rosso Riserva DOCG, one of Umbria’s first DOCGs, which requires extended ageing before release.

It is made mainly from Sangiovese, possibly with a contribution of other traditional varieties, depending on the vintage. After fermentation the wine is aged in oak and then spends a long period in bottle in the cellar, so that it reaches the market several years after the harvest.

It is a red of firm structure and great ageing potential which, over time, develops complex aromas of ripe fruit, spice and forest floor. It is regarded as one of the great classic reds of central Italy.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet. Aromas of ripe cherry, plum, spice, tobacco and forest floor. Firm, elegant palate with ripe tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast red meat, game, pappardelle with wild boar, mushrooms, aged pecorino

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
