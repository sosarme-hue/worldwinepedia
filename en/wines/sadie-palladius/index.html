The Sadie Family Wines

Sadie Family Palladius

WhiteWine of Origin (WO) Swartland

White blend from The Sadie Family Wines based on Chenin Blanc and numerous varieties from old Swartland vineyards, a benchmark for the region’s whites.

Palladius is Eben Sadie’s founding white, and its name pays tribute to Palladius, a Roman author of a treatise on agriculture. It was one of the wines that showed the potential of old-vine white blends from the Swartland and inspired other producers in the region.

Chenin Blanc is the basis of the blend, to which numerous varieties are added that may include, depending on the vintage, Grenache Blanc, Clairette, Palomino, Semillon, Viognier or Chardonnay, among others. The grapes come from various Swartland plots, often old, dry-farmed bush vines. Fermentation is spontaneous and the wine is aged for an extended period on its lees in vessels of different kinds, such as used barrels, large casks and clay or concrete tanks. The result is a white of great texture, complex and fresh, able to develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Quince, citrus, stone fruit and dried herbs, with notes of wax, nuts and a saline background. Broad, textured palate with fresh acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Baked fish, poultry, mildly spiced dishes, aged cheeses

Serving

11-13 °C, large white wine glass
