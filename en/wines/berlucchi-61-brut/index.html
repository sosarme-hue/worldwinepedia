Berlucchi '61 Franciacorta Brut

SparklingFranciacorta DOCG BrutControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Franciacorta

The Franciacorta Brut from Berlucchi’s ’61 line, a traditional-method sparkling wine from Chardonnay and Pinot Noir recalling the year of the house’s first bottles.

Berlucchi’s ’61 line pays tribute to 1961, the year the Borgonato house released its first bottles of traditional-method sparkling wine, regarded as the origin of modern Franciacorta. The Brut is the most widely available wine in the range.

It is made mainly from Chardonnay, with some Pinot Noir, from Franciacorta vineyards. After the base wines are fermented and blended, the wine undergoes its second fermentation in bottle and stays on its lees for a number of months, at least the period required by the appellation, before disgorgement.

It is a dry, fresh sparkling wine with fine bubbles, notes of white fruit and a touch of bakery. It is versatile, both as an aperitif and with light meals.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow with fine bubbles. Aromas of green apple, citrus and white flowers, with notes of yeast and bread. Fresh, dry and balanced on the palate, with a clean finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, starters, fish and shellfish, fried food, sushi

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass or tulip
