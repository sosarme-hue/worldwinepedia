Esporão

Esporão Reserva Branco

WhiteReservaControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) and Alentejano GI Alentejo

Esporão’s Reserva white, a blend of Alentejo varieties such as Antão Vaz, Arinto and Roupeiro, partly aged in barrel.

Esporão Reserva white is the counterpart to the house’s Reserva red and one of the best-known whites of the Alentejo. It comes from the vineyards of the Herdade do Esporão, in Reguengos de Monsaraz.

It is made mainly from native varieties: Antão Vaz, typical of the Alentejo, which brings body and ripe fruit; Arinto, which contributes acidity and freshness; and the aromatic Roupeiro. The proportion of each may vary with the vintage. Part of the wine is fermented and aged in oak barrels and the rest in tank, which makes it possible to combine texture and ageing notes with fruit. In a warm region like the Alentejo, Arinto plays an important role in maintaining balance. It is a full-bodied white, suited to substantial dishes, that can develop for a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of ripe tropical and stone fruit, citrus and toasty notes from the barrel. Broad and creamy on the palate, with balanced acidity.

Food pairing

Salt cod, baked fish, roast poultry, seafood rice

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
