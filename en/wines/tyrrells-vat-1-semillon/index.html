Tyrrell's Vat 1 Hunter Semillon

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Hunter Valley

Hunter Valley Semillon from Tyrrell’s, a classic example of this light, unoaked style able to age for decades.

Vat 1 is Tyrrell’s top Semillon and one of the best-known examples of the traditional Hunter Valley style. Its name comes from the system of numbering the vats that the winery used to identify its best wines.

The grapes come from the family’s old vineyards in the Lower Hunter, on the sandy soils of the flats, and are picked early, at low sugar levels. The wine is fermented in stainless steel tanks, without oak or malolactic fermentation, to preserve its acidity and purity. When young it is light, citrusy and very fresh, but with years in bottle it develops toasty, honeyed and buttered-toast notes without ever having been in barrel. The winery usually releases it after several years of cellaring.

Tasting profile

When young, lime, lemon and fresh grass with cutting acidity; with age, notes of toast, honey and wax. Light body and a long finish.

Food pairing

Oysters, grilled fish, shellfish, salads

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
