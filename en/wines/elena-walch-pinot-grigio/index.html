Elena Walch

Elena Walch Pinot Grigio

WhiteAlto Adige DOCWine region Trentino-Alto Adige

Elena Walch’s Alto Adige Pinot Grigio, a fresh, fruity mountain white made without oak.

Pinot Grigio (Pinot Gris) is one of the most widely planted white varieties in Alto Adige, where it benefits from altitude and the contrast between day and night temperatures. Elena Walch makes it from vineyards in different parts of the region, on slopes at various altitudes and on varied soils.

Fermentation takes place at controlled temperature in stainless steel tanks, and the wine rests on its fine lees for a few months before bottling, which gives it a little more texture without reducing its freshness. It sees no oak, to preserve the purity of the fruit.

Compared with many light lowland Pinot Grigios, those from Alto Adige usually show more body and aromatic concentration. This is a dry, flavourful, balanced white, intended to be drunk in its first few years and very versatile with fish and vegetable dishes.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow with copper hints. Aromas of pear, yellow apple, melon and flowers. Dry, flavourful, fresh palate of medium body.

Food pairing

Baked fish, pasta with vegetables, white meat, speck, mild cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
