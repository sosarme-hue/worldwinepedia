CVNE (Compañía Vinícola del Norte de España)

Imperial Reserva

RedReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Classic CVNE reserva made in Haro from Rioja Alta grapes, one of the house’s historic brands.

Imperial is one of CVNE’s oldest brands. Its name is usually linked to a bottle size the winery used for the British market in the early twentieth century. Today it groups the house’s most classically styled reservas and gran reservas, made in Haro from Rioja Alta grapes.

Imperial Reserva is based on Tempranillo, accompanied by smaller proportions of Graciano and Mazuelo (Carignan). It is aged in oak barrels and then rests in bottle before release, in accordance with the minimum periods required for the Reserva category.

It is a traditional-style Rioja that seeks elegance and balance rather than power, with good acidity and the capacity to evolve for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Ruby with a garnet rim. Aromas of ripe red and black fruit, spice, liquorice and ageing notes. Elegant and balanced on the palate, with fine tannins and a persistent finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, red meat, game, stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
