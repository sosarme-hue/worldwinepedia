Château Quinault l'Enclos

Château Quinault l'Enclos

RedAOC Saint-Émilion Grand Cru

A Saint-Émilion Grand Cru red from an estate surrounded by the town of Libourne, linked to the owners of Château Cheval Blanc.

Château Quinault l'Enclos is an unusual estate within the Saint-Émilion Grand Cru appellation: its vineyard, enclosed by a wall as its name suggests, lies within the municipal boundaries of Libourne, surrounded by the town. In the late 2000s it passed into the hands of the owners of Château Cheval Blanc, whose technical team has managed it ever since.

The vineyard lies on gravel, sand and clay soils, closer to those of Pomerol than to Saint-Émilion's limestone plateau. It is planted mainly with Merlot, along with Cabernet Franc and some Cabernet Sauvignon.

Harvesting is by hand and the wine is aged in French oak barrels. Under the new management, the style has moved towards greater freshness and precision, aiming for a red with clear fruit, fine tannins and a balance that allows it to be enjoyed relatively young as well as after a few years of cellaring.

Tasting profile

Intense ruby red. Aromas of cherry, plum, floral notes and gentle spice. Fresh, balanced palate with fine tannins and an elegant finish.

Food pairing

Roast veal, duck, lamb, sautéed mushrooms, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large Bordeaux glass
