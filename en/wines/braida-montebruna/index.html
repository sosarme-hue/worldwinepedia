Braida

Braida Montebruna Barbera d’Asti

RedBarbera d’Asti DOCGWine region Piedmont

Braida Barbera d’Asti aged in large casks, fresher and more direct than the house’s single-vineyard wines and designed for everyday dining.

Montebruna is Braida’s most widely available Barbera d’Asti and represents the more traditional face of the variety at the Bologna family’s winery. Unlike Bricco dell’Uccellone or Bricco della Bigotta, which are aged in barriques, this wine seeks a direct expression of Barbera’s fruit and freshness.

The grapes come from vineyards in and around Rocchetta Tanaro. After fermentation with maceration, the wine is aged in large oak casks, which allow gentle development without adding marked oak aromas, and it is bottled relatively young.

It is a deeply coloured, fruity red with the lively acidity typical of the variety, which makes it very well suited to tomato-based dishes, cured meats or pasta. It is best enjoyed in its first few years.

Tasting profile

Deep purple-ruby. Aromas of cherry, morello cherry, black fruit and a touch of spice. Juicy, fresh, fruity palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Pasta with ragù, cured meats, grilled meat, bollito misto, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
