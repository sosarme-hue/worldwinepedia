La Chablisienne

Chablis 1er Cru Vaulorent

WhitePremier CruAOC (Petit Chablis, Chablis, Chablis Premier Cru and Chablis Grand Cru) Chablis

Chablis premier cru from La Chablisienne sourced from Vaulorent, on the right bank next to the grands crus.

Vaulorent is a climat on the right bank of the Serein, part of the Fourchaume premier cru, bordering the grand cru Les Preuses. Because of its position and exposure, it is often considered one of the most complete premiers crus of Chablis. Its Kimmeridgian marl soils give a Chardonnay with more body than those of the left bank, combining ripe fruit with the acidity and mineral tension of the area.

La Chablisienne is the Chablis cooperative, founded in 1923 by local growers. Its members farm vineyards spread across the whole area, which allows it to make wines from every tier of the appellation as well as other Auxerrois appellations, in a style that favours freshness and the expression of terroir. The cooperative makes, ages and bottles the wines at its own facilities in Chablis.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green glints. Aromas of citrus, white fruit, flowers and stony mineral notes. Taut and saline on the palate, with lively acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, lobster, scallops, poultry in cream, soft cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, large glass
