Domaine du Castel

Grand Vin Castel

Red

Domaine du Castel’s main red, a barrel-aged Bordeaux-style blend based on Cabernet Sauvignon from the Judean Hills. It is one of the wines that drove the rise of small-production wineries in Israel.

Grand Vin is Domaine du Castel’s flagship wine and the one that made the winery’s name in the early 1990s. It is made from grapes from the estate’s vineyards in the Judean Hills, several hundred metres above sea level, on limestone and terra rossa soils. It is a Bordeaux-style blend dominated by Cabernet Sauvignon, with Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, in proportions that vary with the vintage.

The varieties are vinified separately and the wine is aged in French oak barrels before final blending and bottling. The style seeks elegance and balance rather than power, with ripe fruit typical of the Mediterranean climate tempered by the freshness of altitude. It is a red suited to ageing. It is a kosher wine.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red. Aromas of blackcurrant, plum, cedar and sweet spice. Balanced and elegant on the palate, with fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast red meat, lamb, duck, mushrooms

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
