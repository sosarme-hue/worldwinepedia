Kanonkop

Kanonkop Kadette Cape Blend

RedWine of Origin (WO) Stellenbosch

Kanonkop blended red based on Pinotage and Bordeaux varieties, one of the most widely available South African labels from Stellenbosch.

Kanonkop, on the slopes of the Simonsberg in Stellenbosch, is one of South Africa's most highly regarded estates, above all for its Pinotage and its Bordeaux blend Paul Sauer. Kadette is its more approachable range, and the wine that gives it its name is a Cape Blend, the term used in South Africa for red blends in which Pinotage, the variety created in the country, plays a prominent role.

It is made with Pinotage as its base, complemented by Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Cabernet Franc; the proportions vary with the vintage. Fermentation follows the house tradition of open fermenters and manual punching down, and the wine is aged in French oak barrels, most of them used. The result is a fruity, structured red with the firm character of Kanonkop's wines but easier to enjoy in its youth.

Tasting profile

Aromas of plum, blackberry and cherry, with notes of spice, smoke and cocoa. Medium-bodied and juicy, with firm but ripe tannins.

Food pairing

Chargrilled meats, sausages, bobotie, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
