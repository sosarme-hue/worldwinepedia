R. López de Heredia Viña Tondonia

Viña Tondonia Reserva

RedReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

López de Heredia’s emblematic red from the Tondonia estate, embodying classic Rioja with long barrel ageing.

Viña Tondonia Reserva is made from grapes grown on the Tondonia estate, beside the Ebro near Haro, from a blend dominated by Tempranillo and completed with Grenache, Graciano and Mazuelo (Carignan). Fermentation takes place in large oak vats, following the house’s traditional methods.

The wine then spends several years in used oak barrels, with regular manual racking, and is fined with egg whites before bottling. The winery releases it many years after the harvest, when it judges it to be ready, so the vintages on sale are usually older than those of other reservas.

It is a deliberately classic red, in which oxidative ageing in wood dominates over primary fruit. Its acidity and structure give it remarkable ageing potential, and many enthusiasts regard it as a benchmark for understanding traditional Rioja.

Tasting profile

Medium-intensity ruby with a brick-coloured rim. On the nose, ripe red fruit alongside notes of leather, tobacco, spice and old wood. Medium-bodied on the palate, with lively acidity and polished tannins.

Food pairing

Lamb chops, roasts, game stews, mushrooms, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decant if there is sediment
