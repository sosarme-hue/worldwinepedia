Cape of Good Hope Riebeeksrivier Chenin Blanc

WhiteSwartlandWine of Origin (WO) Swartland

Single-vineyard Chenin Blanc from Anthonij Rupert’s Cape of Good Hope range, from the Riebeeksrivier farm in the Swartland.

Anthonij Rupert Wyne’s Cape of Good Hope range is dedicated to single-vineyard wines that seek to show the character of a specific farm. This Chenin Blanc comes from Riebeeksrivier, a property of the group in the Swartland, on the slopes around Riebeek-Kasteel.

The Swartland is a warm, dry region north of Cape Town, with poor schist and granite soils, where dry-farmed bush vines are common. In recent decades it has become one of South Africa’s most dynamic areas, and its Chenin Blanc, often from old vines, is one of its most characteristic wines.

Winemaking aims for a white with texture and concentration, but also the natural freshness of the variety; it may include some ageing in wood or on the lees. The result is a food-friendly Chenin expressing ripe fruit and mineral notes, which can develop for a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of quince, peach, honey and floral notes. Broad, textured palate with fresh acidity and a mineral finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, roast pork, poultry, mild curry

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
