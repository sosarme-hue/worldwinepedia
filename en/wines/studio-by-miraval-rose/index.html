Château Miraval

Studio by Miraval Rosé

RoséIGP MéditerranéeWine region Provence

Rosé from Miraval’s Studio line, under the IGP Méditerranée, pale, light and fruity, designed for casual drinking.

Studio by Miraval rosé is the most accessible version of the Provençal style that made Château Miraval famous, the inland Provence estate that makes its wines in partnership with the Perrin family. The Studio line owes its name to the recording studio that operated on the property for decades.

Unlike the house’s Côtes de Provence rosé, this wine is labelled under the IGP Méditerranée, a broad geographical indication in south-eastern France that allows grapes to be selected from different areas near the coast. Winemaking follows the usual method for Provence rosés: gentle pressing of Mediterranean red varieties to obtain a very pale colour, and low-temperature fermentation to preserve the fruit.

It is a dry, light rosé with aromas of strawberry, peach and citrus and a fresh, flowing palate. It is meant to be drunk young and well chilled, as an aperitif or with simple summer dishes.

Tasting profile

Pale pink. Aromas of strawberry, peach, grapefruit and flowers. Dry, light, fresh palate.

Food pairing

Aperitif, salads, tapas, grilled fish, light Mediterranean cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
