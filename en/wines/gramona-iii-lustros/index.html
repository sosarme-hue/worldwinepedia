Gramona

Gramona III Lustros

SparklingBrut NatureDesignation of Origin (DO) Penedès

Very long-aged Brut Nature sparkling wine from Gramona, based on Xarel·lo, one of the wines that best show the ageing potential of Penedès sparkling wines.

III Lustros is one of Gramona's most acclaimed sparkling wines. Its name refers to «lustra», periods of five years, and to the idea of time that defines this wine. It is made from grapes from the family's vineyards, mainly Xarel·lo together with Macabeo, from a single vintage.

After the second fermentation in bottle, carried out under a cork stopper rather than a crown cap, the wine spends many years in contact with its lees before disgorgement, which is done by hand. It is a Brut Nature, with no added sugar, so it is the acidity of Xarel·lo that sustains such long ageing. The result is a sparkling wine of great complexity, more vinous than most, released under the Corpinnat brand.

Tasting profile

Notes of ripe white fruit, candied citrus, toasted nuts, bread and a saline background. Dry, vinous palate, very fine bubbles and a long finish.

Food pairing

Shellfish, fine fish, rice dishes, poultry, aged cheeses

Serving

9-11 °C, white wine glass
