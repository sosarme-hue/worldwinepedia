Donnafugata

Donnafugata Mille e una Notte

RedSicilia DOCWine region Sicily

Donnafugata’s top red, based on Nero d’Avola from the Contessa Entellina estate and aged in barrel.

Mille e una Notte, “A Thousand and One Nights”, is Donnafugata’s most ambitious red. Like other wines from the house, its name and label evoke literature and imagination, in this case the famous collection of oriental tales. It comes from the Contessa Entellina estate, in the inland hills of western Sicily, where the Rallo family has most of its vineyards.

It is made mainly from Nero d’Avola, to which small proportions of other varieties may be added depending on the vintage. The grapes come from a selection of plots. After fermentation with maceration, the wine is aged in French oak barriques and then rests in bottle for a time before release.

It is a red of great concentration and structure, with ripe fruit, spicy notes and rounded tannins. It has good ageing potential and gains complexity over the years.

Tasting profile

Very deep ruby. Aromas of black cherry, plum, wild berries, spice, cocoa and balsamic notes. Broad, velvety palate with a long finish.

Food pairing

Chargrilled red meat, roast lamb, game, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
