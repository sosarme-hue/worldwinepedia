Bodegas Borsao

Borsao Selección Tinto

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Campo de Borja

Young, very affordable red from Bodegas Borsao, based on Garnacha and designed for everyday drinking.

Borsao Selección is the entry-level red in Bodegas Borsao's range and one of the easiest Campo de Borja D.O. wines to find. Garnacha is the main variety, completed by Syrah and Tempranillo, which add colour, structure and complementary nuances.

The grapes come from the cooperative members' vineyards, spread across the plains and slopes near the Moncayo. The wine is made to preserve the variety's fresh fruit, without extended oak ageing, and is bottled young. It is a simple, fruity, easy-drinking red that illustrates the open, generous style of Aragonese Garnacha at a very modest price.

It is best served slightly below room temperature, so that its fruit and freshness show clearly, and preferably enjoyed within its first few years after the harvest.

Tasting profile

Fresh red fruit, strawberries and cherries, with a touch of spice. Light to medium-bodied, juicy palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Tapas, pasta, burgers, charcuterie, everyday dishes

Serving

14-16 °C, red wine glass
