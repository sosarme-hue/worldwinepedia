Mouton Cadet Réserve Sauternes

SweetAOC (sweet wine) Sauternes

Sweet Sauternes in the Mouton Cadet Réserve range, made from Sémillon and Sauvignon Blanc affected by noble rot.

The Mouton Cadet Réserve range brings together wines from different Bordeaux appellations made by Baron Philippe de Rothschild, the Pauillac house that owns Château Mouton Rothschild. Unlike the classic Mouton Cadet, a generic Bordeaux, each Réserve comes from a specific appellation and aims to reflect its character, using grapes from growers the house works with.

Sauternes, beside the Ciron river, is the great sweet wine appellation of Bordeaux. Autumn mists favour the development of Botrytis cinerea, which concentrates the sugar and acidity of the grapes, mainly Sémillon, completed with Sauvignon Blanc. Harvesting is often carried out in several passes.

It is an approachable Sauternes, with sweetness balanced by acidity, that can be enjoyed young for its fruit or kept for a few years to gain complexity.

Tasting profile

Apricot, peaches in syrup, honey and candied citrus. Unctuous, sweet palate supported by fresh acidity.

Food pairing

Foie gras, blue cheeses, fruit desserts, apricot tart

Serving

8-10 °C, small white wine glass
