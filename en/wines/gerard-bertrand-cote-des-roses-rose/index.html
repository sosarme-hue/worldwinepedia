Gérard Bertrand

Gérard Bertrand Côte des Roses Rosé

RoséAOP LanguedocWine region Languedoc-Roussillon

A Languedoc rosé from Gérard Bertrand, highly recognisable for its bottle with a rose-shaped base.

Côte des Roses is one of Gérard Bertrand’s most widely available ranges. Its bottle, whose base is moulded in the shape of a rose, makes it easy to spot in shops. The rosé carries the Languedoc appellation, which covers a large part of the region’s vineyards between the Rhône and the Pyrenees.

It is made from Grenache, Cinsault and Syrah from vineyards near the Mediterranean coast. The grapes are picked early to retain freshness, and the wine is obtained by pressing or brief maceration, with fermentation at controlled temperatures.

It is a dry, fruity, easy-drinking rosé, designed to be enjoyed in its first months, as an aperitif or with summer food.

Tasting profile

Bright pale pink. Aromas of strawberry, redcurrant, citrus and flowers. Dry, fresh and fruity on the palate, with a light finish.

Food pairing

Aperitif, salads, tapas, grilled fish, Mediterranean cuisine

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
