Ameztoi

Rubentis

RoséDesignation of Origin (DO) Getariako Txakolina

Rosé Txakoli from Ameztoi made from Hondarrabi Zuri and Hondarrabi Beltza, which has helped to make pink Txakoli known outside the Basque Country.

Rubentis is the rosé Txakoli from Ameztoi, the family winery in Getaria, and one of the wines that has done most to spread pink Txakoli beyond the Basque Country, especially in export markets. It comes from vineyards in the Getariako Txakolina Denominación de Origen, on the coastal hills around the town, facing the Bay of Biscay.

It is made from a blend of Hondarrabi Zuri, the local white variety, and Hondarrabi Beltza, the native red variety, whose proportions vary with the vintage. The presence of red grapes gives it its pale pink colour. Fermentation takes place at low temperature, retaining some of the natural carbon dioxide, which gives it a characteristic slight spritz. It does not see oak and is bottled early.

The result is a light, fresh, low-alcohol rosé with delicate red fruit, lively acidity and a saline finish, designed to be drunk within the year.

Tasting profile

Pale pink with a fine spritz. Aromas of strawberry, redcurrant and citrus peel. Light, very fresh and lively on the palate, with a saline finish.

Food pairing

Pintxos, aperitifs, shellfish, salads, light fish dishes

Serving

6-8 °C, white wine glass
