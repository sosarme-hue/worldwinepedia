Graham’s Six Grapes Reserve Port

FortifiedReserve (ruby)Controlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Port

Reserve ruby Port from Graham’s, fruity and rich in style, made from wines from the same quintas that supply its Vintage Ports.

The name Six Grapes comes from the mark used in the house’s records to identify its finest wines, drawn as six bunches of grapes. Graham’s presents it as a Reserve Port made from wines from its Douro quintas, including Quinta dos Malvedos, which also supply grapes for its Vintage Ports.

It is made from traditional Douro varieties such as Touriga Nacional and Touriga Franca, with fermentation stopped by grape spirit. It then ages for a few years in large oak vats, a form of ageing that preserves the wine’s deep colour and fruit. It is a ruby-style Port intended for drinking without cellaring, and once opened it keeps well for a few weeks if stoppered and stored somewhere cool.

Stylistically, it is often described as a Port that recalls the character of a young Vintage, though without its ageing capacity.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby, with aromas of black cherry, plum and blackberry and notes of chocolate. Sweet and broad on the palate, with rounded tannins.

Food pairing

Chocolate desserts, blue cheeses, red berry tarts

Serving

15-17 °C, Port glass
