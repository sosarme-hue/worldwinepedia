Château Lynch-Bages

Echo de Lynch-Bages

RedSecond wineBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

The second wine of Château Lynch-Bages, a more approachable Pauillac that develops more quickly than the grand vin.

Echo de Lynch-Bages is the estate’s second wine and carries the Pauillac appellation. Until the 2007 vintage it was sold under the name Château Haut-Bages Averous. It is made from grapes from the Lynch-Bages vineyards, in particular younger vines and lots not selected for the grand vin.

The blend combines Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, sometimes with a little Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot, and ageing takes place in oak barrels with a lower proportion of new wood than the grand vin.

It is a red that offers the character of Pauillac — black fruit, cedar notes and firm tannins — with a fruitier profile and softer texture, so it can be enjoyed within a few years of the harvest.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, plum, cedar and toasty notes. Medium-bodied and fruity on the palate, with well-integrated tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, lamb, gourmet burgers, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, Bordeaux glass
