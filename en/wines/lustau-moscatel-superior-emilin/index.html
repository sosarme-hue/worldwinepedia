Lustau Moscatel Superior Emilín

SweetDesignation of Origin (DO) Jerez-Xérès-Sherry

Lustau Moscatel Superior Emilín is a sweet wine from Bodegas Lustau, made under the DO Jerez-Xérès-Sherry appellation in Spain.

It has 17% ABV.

Jerez house founded in 1896, known for its broad range of styles, its collection of almacenista wines and for ageing wines in all three towns of the Sherry Triangle: Jerez, El Puerto de Santa María and Sanlúcar.

Jerez-Xérès-Sherry, in the province of Cádiz, is the designation of origin for sherry, made mainly from Palomino and aged in the criaderas and solera system. Its range runs from biologically aged finos to olorosos and sweet Pedro Ximénez.
