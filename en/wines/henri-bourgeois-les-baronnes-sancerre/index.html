Henri Bourgeois

Henri Bourgeois Sancerre Les Baronnes

WhiteSancerreAOC Sancerre

Henri Bourgeois’ most widely available white Sancerre, a blend of plots on different soils around Chavignol.

Les Baronnes is the Bourgeois family’s benchmark Sancerre and one of the easiest to find in the appellation. It is made from Sauvignon Blanc from vineyards around Chavignol and other Sancerre villages, combining the area’s three main soil types: Kimmeridgian marls (terres blanches), stony limestone (caillottes) and flint.

Each soil type brings different nuances — breadth, finesse or mineral character — and the blend aims for a balanced wine that represents the appellation. Winemaking takes place in stainless-steel tanks, with ageing on lees, without oak.

It is a dry, fresh, aromatic white with a classic profile, enjoyed in its early years and an excellent match for Chavignol goat’s cheese.

It also exists in red and rosé versions, made from Pinot Noir.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Aromas of citrus, white currant, white flowers and mineral and herbaceous notes. Dry, taut and fresh on the palate.

Food pairing

Crottin de Chavignol and other goat’s cheeses, oysters, grilled fish, salads

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
