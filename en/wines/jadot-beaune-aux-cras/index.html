Maison Louis Jadot

Louis Jadot Beaune 1er Cru Aux Cras

RedPremier CruBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Beaune

Beaune premier cru made from Pinot Noir by Louis Jadot under the Famille Gagey name.

Beaune, the wine capital of Burgundy and home of Louis Jadot, is one of the communes with the most premiers crus in the Côte d’Or. It has no grands crus, but its best vineyards, on the slope west of the town, mainly produce reds from Pinot Noir, generally rounder and more approachable than those of Pommard or the Côte de Nuits.

Aux Cras is one of the premiers crus on the Beaune slope. Its name comes from a Burgundian word for stony, limestone ground. Louis Jadot makes it from Pinot Noir and releases it under the Famille Gagey name, after the family that ran the house for decades.

Aged in oak barrels, it is a medium-bodied red with red fruit and fine tannins, gaining complexity after a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Ruby. Aromas of cherry, red berries, gentle spice and floral notes. Round palate with fine tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Roast poultry, veal, parsleyed ham, soft cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, Burgundy glass
