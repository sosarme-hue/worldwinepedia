Château Cantemerle

Les Allées de Cantemerle

RedSecond wineBordeaux subregion (AOC Médoc, Haut-Médoc and communal appellations) Médoc

Second wine of Château Cantemerle, a fruity, approachable Haut-Médoc intended to be drunk earlier than the grand vin.

Les Allées de Cantemerle is the second wine of Château Cantemerle, a fifth growth located in Macau, in the southern Haut-Médoc. Its name evokes the tree-lined avenues of the park surrounding the château.

It comes from the same gravel and sandy vineyards as the grand vin, with a selection that usually includes younger vines and lots not destined for the first wine. It is based on the classic Bordeaux blend of Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, the latter often playing a significant role and bringing roundness and fruit.

Ageing is shorter and less marked by oak than for the grand vin. The result is a red with frank fruit, soft tannins and an elegant style, sharing the house character and enjoyable in its first few years without the need for long cellaring.

Tasting profile

Ruby red. Aromas of cherry, black fruit and a hint of vanilla. Smooth, fruity palate with round tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled meats, roast poultry, pasta with ragù, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, Bordeaux glass
