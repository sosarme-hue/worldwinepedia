Viña Montes

Montes Alpha Carmenère

RedDesignation of Origin (zone within the Rapel Valley) Colchagua Valley

Colchagua Valley Carmenère from the Montes Alpha line, with a small proportion of Cabernet Sauvignon and aged in French oak barrels.

The Montes Alpha line is the range with which Viña Montes became known internationally, and its Carmenère is one of its most characteristic wines. Carmenère, a Bordeaux variety that was confused with Merlot in Chile for decades, finds favourable conditions in the Colchagua Valley: hot, dry summers, enough sunshine to fully ripen a late-ripening grape, and well-drained hillside soils.

The wine is made mostly from Carmenère and usually includes a small proportion of Cabernet Sauvignon, which adds structure; the proportions vary with the vintage. It is aged for about a year in French oak barrels. It is a deeply coloured red, with ripe black fruit, spicy notes and the herbal touch characteristic of the variety, with soft tannins and a good capacity to develop for a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Deep purple. Aromas of blackberry, plum, roasted pepper, black pepper and cocoa. Rounded palate with soft tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Lamb, grilled meats, spiced dishes, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
