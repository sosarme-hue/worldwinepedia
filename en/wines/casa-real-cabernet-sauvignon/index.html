Viña Santa Rita

Casa Real Cabernet Sauvignon

RedDesignation of Origin (subregion) Maipo Valley

Top-end Cabernet Sauvignon from Viña Santa Rita, made from Maipo Valley grapes. Its name comes from the manor house on the Alto Jahuel estate.

Casa Real is Viña Santa Rita’s benchmark red and takes its name from the nineteenth-century manor house preserved on the Alto Jahuel estate, now a hotel. It is made solely from Maipo Valley Cabernet Sauvignon, the variety and area on which the estate has built much of its reputation.

The grapes come from the winery’s own vineyards in the Maipo and are carefully selected at harvest, which is done by hand. After fermentation, the wine is aged in French oak barrels and then rests in bottle before release. Casa Real’s style is that of a classic Maipo Cabernet Sauvignon: ripe black fruit, notes of herbs, mint and spice, and a firm structure that allows it to age for many years. Over time it develops aromas of cedar, tobacco and leather. It is one of the Chilean reds with the longest track record in the premium segment, and decanting is advisable when it is young.

Tasting profile

Deep garnet red. Cassis, plum, mint, eucalyptus and spice, with cedar notes as it ages. Firm, elegant palate with fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast lamb, grilled red meat, game stews, aged cheeses

Serving

17-18 °C, decanted, large glass
