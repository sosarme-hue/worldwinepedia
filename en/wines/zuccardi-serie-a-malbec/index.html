Familia Zuccardi

Zuccardi Serie A Malbec

RedWine region Valle de Uco

Uco Valley Malbec from Zuccardi’s Serie A, a red with fresh fruit and an expressive style that reflects the family’s high-altitude vineyards.

The Serie A is a line of varietal wines from Zuccardi designed to show the character of the family’s high-altitude grapes at a mid-range price. Malbec is its main wine and comes from vineyards in the Uco Valley, where the Zuccardis have concentrated much of their work in recent decades, with their Paraje Altamira winery at the centre.

The grapes come from different parts of the valley, such as San Carlos, Tunuyán or Tupungato, with stony alluvial soils and cool nights. True to the current house style, the wine seeks to express fruit and place rather than the influence of oak: it is fermented in concrete tanks and part of it is aged in casks or used barrels. The result is a fresh, floral Malbec with fine tannins and good acidity, enjoyable young and able to age for a few years.

Tasting profile

Intense purple. Aromas of fresh plum, red fruit, violet and herbs. Fresh, juicy palate with fine tannins.

Food pairing

Argentine asado, grilled meats, empanadas, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
