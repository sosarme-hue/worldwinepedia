Lytton Springs

RedCounty with several AVAs Sonoma

A Ridge red from old vines in Dry Creek Valley, Sonoma, based on Zinfandel with Petite Sirah and Carignan.

The Lytton Springs vineyard lies in the Dry Creek Valley AVA, north of Healdsburg, and retains parcels planted in the early twentieth century according to the custom of the time: Zinfandel vines interplanted in the same field with Petite Sirah, Carignan and other varieties. Ridge began making wine from this vineyard in the 1970s, eventually bought it, and built its second winery there.

The wine reflects that field blend. Zinfandel, identical to Italy’s Primitivo, brings ripe fruit and spice; Petite Sirah, colour and tannin; and Carignan, freshness. Because the proportion of Zinfandel varies with the vintage, the wine is sold under the vineyard name rather than always under the variety. It is fermented with native yeasts and aged in American oak barrels, in keeping with the house philosophy.

Tasting profile

Deep cherry red. Aromas of bramble fruit, ripe raspberry, black pepper and Mediterranean herbs. Juicy and structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a spicy finish.

Food pairing

Barbecued ribs, spiced lamb, charcuterie, pizza, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; may be decanted
