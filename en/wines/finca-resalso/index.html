Finca Resalso

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Ribera del Duero

Young red from Emilio Moro made from young-vine Tinto Fino, with a short spell in barrel and a fruity profile.

Finca Resalso is the entry-level wine in Emilio Moro’s range. It is made from Tinto Fino (Tempranillo) from the family’s youngest vines around Pesquera de Duero, and its name refers to one of the winery’s vineyard sites.

Winemaking aims for an expressive, easy-drinking red: after fermentation the wine spends a short period in oak barrels, which adds some roundness without masking the fruit, and is bottled relatively early.

It is a wine for everyday drinking that shows the fruity character of Ribera del Duero Tempranillo at a moderate price. It is best enjoyed young.

Young vines tend to give lighter, fruitier wines than old vines, whose roots run deeper and whose yields are lower. The Moro family uses this difference to build a tiered range, in which Finca Resalso represents the most direct and immediate expression of its vineyards.

Tasting profile

Cherry red with violet highlights. Aromas of fresh red and black fruit, with floral touches and a light hint of oak. Fresh, fruity and gently tannic on the palate.

Food pairing

Tapas, charcuterie, pasta, pizza, grilled meat

Serving

14-16 °C, red wine glass
