Meiomi

Meiomi Chardonnay

White

Californian Chardonnay from Meiomi, made from grapes grown in several coastal areas in a fruity, creamy style.

After the success of its Pinot Noir, Meiomi extended its range with a Chardonnay made on the same principle: blending grapes from different parts of the California coast, such as Monterey, Santa Barbara and Sonoma counties. This diversity of origins makes it possible to combine the ripe fruit of the sunnier areas with the acidity of the vineyards most exposed to ocean breezes and fogs.

The wine is made partly with fermentation and ageing in oak barrels and partly in tank, which contributes vanilla notes and a creamy texture without entirely losing freshness. It is sold under the general California designation and is intended for drinking young, in a broad, fruity, easy-drinking style that has made it one of the branded Chardonnays most widely found in American shops.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow. Aromas of peach, pineapple, baked apple and vanilla. Round, creamy palate with enough acidity to keep it fresh.

Food pairing

Roast poultry, lobster and shellfish, fish in sauce, creamy pasta

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
