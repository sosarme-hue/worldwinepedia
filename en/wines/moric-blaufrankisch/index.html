Moric

Moric Blaufränkisch

RedWine-growing region (Weinbaugebiet), with several DACs Burgenland

Blaufränkisch from Roland Velich made from grapes from various plots in Burgenland, in a fresh, spicy style with fine tannins.

This is the entry-level wine of Moric, the project through which Roland Velich set out to show Blaufränkisch as a variety capable of producing fine, expressive reds. It is made from grapes from several plots in Burgenland, largely in Mittelburgenland, the part of the region most associated with this variety.

Winemaking is minimal-intervention, with spontaneous fermentations and gentle extraction, and the wine is aged in used barrels and larger casks so as not to mask the fruit. In contrast to the more concentrated, new-oak-driven styles that long dominated the region, this Blaufränkisch seeks freshness, lively acidity and fine tannins, with notes of red and black fruit and spice. It offers an accessible introduction to the house style.

It is best served at a moderate temperature and, when young, aerated in a decanter or a large glass so that its aromas can fully express themselves.

Tasting profile

Aromas of cherry, blackberry, plum, pepper and floral notes. Medium-bodied, fresh and spicy on the palate, with fine tannins and lively acidity.

Food pairing

Roast meats, game birds, veal stews, aged cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, large glass
