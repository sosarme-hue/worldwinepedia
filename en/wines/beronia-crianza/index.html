Beronia Crianza

RedCrianzaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Classic-style Crianza from Bodegas Beronia in Ollauri, made from Tempranillo, Garnacha and Mazuelo and aged for at least a year in barrel.

Beronia Crianza is one of the best-known wines from the Ollauri winery in Rioja Alta. It is made from a blend dominated by Tempranillo, accompanied by Garnacha, which brings fruit and roundness, and Mazuelo (Carignan), which adds acidity and structure. The proportions change with the vintage.

After fermentation the wine is aged in oak barrels for at least a year, as required for the Crianza category in Rioja, and completes its ageing with a period of bottle rest before release. Ageing in wood, in barrels involving American oak, provides the notes of vanilla, coconut and spice that characterise classic Rioja.

It is a medium-bodied, balanced and easy-drinking red that can be enjoyed in its first years and suits a wide variety of dishes.

Tasting profile

Medium-intensity deep cherry red. Ripe red fruit, vanilla, coconut and spice. Round on the palate, with soft tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Tapas, charcuterie, grilled meat, meat rice dishes, pasta

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
