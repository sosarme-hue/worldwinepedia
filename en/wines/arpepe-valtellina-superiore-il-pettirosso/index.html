ARPEPE Valtellina Superiore Il Pettirosso

RedDOC Valtellina Superiore

Valtellina Superiore from Ar.Pe.Pe., a mountain Nebbiolo from the house’s terraces, aged in large casks.

Ar.Pe.Pe. (from Arturo Pelizzatti Perego) is a family winery in Sondrio founded in the 1980s by an heir to an old family of wine producers in Valtellina, in northern Lombardy. Today it is run by his children. Its vineyards occupy terraces held up by dry-stone walls on the south-facing slope of the Rhaetian Alps, above the Adda river, where Nebbiolo, here called Chiavennasca, ripens thanks to the sunshine and the shelter of the mountains. The house uses long macerations and patient ageing in large casks, and holds back its wines until it considers them ready.

Valtellina Superiore is the DOCG that brings together the valley’s best vineyards, with subzones such as Sassella, Grumello and Inferno, and requires a minimum of 90% Nebbiolo. Il Pettirosso (“the robin”) is one of the house’s Valtellina Superiore wines without the Riserva designation, from its terraces at the foot of the Rhaetian Alps. It is made with a long maceration and aged in large casks before bottle rest. It is a pale, aromatic and fresh red, with red fruit, spice and a mineral background, which can be drunk in its first years but also develops well.

Tasting profile

Light garnet red. Aromas of cherry, red berries, faded rose, spice, alpine herbs and mineral notes. Fresh and elegant on the palate, medium-bodied, with fine tannins and lively acidity.

Food pairing

Pizzoccheri, bresaola, stewed meat, mushrooms, aged mountain cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
