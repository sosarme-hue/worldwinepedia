Blandy’s 10 Year Old Malmsey

Fortified10 Year Old · Malmsey (rich)Protected Designation of Origin (PDO) Madeira

Sweet Malmsey Madeira with a 10-year age indication, made by Blandy’s and aged in cask using the island’s traditional method.

Malvasia, known in English as Malmsey, is the traditional variety used for the sweetest Madeiras. In this wine from Blandy’s, fermentation is stopped early with grape spirit, so that the wine retains a high level of the grapes’ natural sugar.

The wine then ages in oak casks in the house’s lodges in Funchal, following the canteiro system: the casks are placed in warm spaces, where the island’s natural heat encourages slow oxidation and gradual development. The 10-year indication corresponds to the average age of the blend, approved by the regulatory body. The result combines rich sweetness with the very lively acidity typical of Madeira, which keeps it from feeling heavy. Like all Madeiras, it keeps well for a long time once the bottle has been opened.

Tasting profile

Dark amber with golden glints. Aromas of nuts, caramel, dried fig, candied orange peel and spice. Sweet and unctuous on the palate, balanced by vibrant acidity and a very long finish.

Food pairing

Chocolate and nut desserts, honey cake, aged cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, small fortified wine glass
