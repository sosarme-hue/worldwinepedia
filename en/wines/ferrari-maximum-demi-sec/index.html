Ferrari Trento

Ferrari Maximum Demi-Sec

SparklingTrento DOC Demi-SecWine region Trentino-Alto Adige

Trento DOC sparkling wine from Ferrari in a demi-sec style, made from Chardonnay by the traditional method.

Maximum Demi-Sec shares the Chardonnay base and traditional-method production of the Maximum line, but receives a dosage with more sugar than the brut versions. The result is a sparkling wine with perceptible sweetness balanced by the acidity of mountain-grown grapes, so it does not feel heavy. This style, less common today than brut, is especially suited to desserts or as an alternative to sweet wines.

Ferrari, founded in Trento in 1902 by Giulio Ferrari and owned by the Lunelli family since 1952, is one of the benchmark houses of Italian traditional-method sparkling wine. Its wines are covered by the Trento DOC and come from mountain vineyards, where altitude and temperature swings favour freshness and acidity. Giulio Ferrari was also one of the pioneers of Chardonnay growing in Italy.

Tasting profile

Straw yellow. Aromas of ripe white fruit, honey, flowers and pastry. Soft and slightly sweet on the palate, with fine bubbles and refreshing acidity.

Food pairing

Fruit desserts, pastries, panettone, foie gras, blue cheeses

Serving

6-8 °C, flute or tulip glass
