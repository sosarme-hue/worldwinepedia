Barone Ricasoli

Brolio Chianti Classico

RedChianti Classico DOCGControlled and Guaranteed Designation of Origin (DOCG) Chianti Classico

Barone Ricasoli Chianti Classico made from grapes grown in the vineyards surrounding the castle of Brolio in Gaiole in Chianti.

Brolio Chianti Classico is Barone Ricasoli’s most widely available wine and bears the name of the castle the family has owned since the Middle Ages. The grapes come from the estate’s vineyards in the commune of Gaiole in Chianti, in the south of the appellation.

It is made mainly from Sangiovese, complemented by small proportions of varieties such as Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon; the proportions may vary with the vintage. After fermentation the wine is aged in oak for a few months, in vessels of different sizes, so that the wood accompanies rather than dominates.

It is a medium-bodied Chianti Classico with the red fruit and acidity typical of Sangiovese and soft tannins that make it enjoyable in its youth. It is very versatile at the table and a good introduction to the wines of Brolio.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of cherry, red berries, violet and spicy notes. Medium-bodied, fresh palate with soft tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Pasta with ragù, grilled meat, bistecca alla fiorentina, Tuscan cured meats, pecorino

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
