Maison Louis Jadot

Louis Jadot Corton Grand Cru Les Grèves

RedGrand CruBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Beaune

Red Corton grand cru from the Les Grèves plot on the hill of Corton, made by Louis Jadot from Pinot Noir.

The hill of Corton, in the north of the Côte de Beaune, is home to the only red grand cru in this part of the Côte d’Or. The Corton appellation is divided into numerous climats, whose name may be added to that of the grand cru on the label; Les Grèves is one of them, located on the well-exposed slope of the hill.

Louis Jadot makes this wine exclusively from Pinot Noir from this plot. After a traditional vinification with long macerations, the wine is aged in French oak barrels, part of them new.

Red Cortons are characterised by their structure and power, with firm tannins that demand time in bottle. In its youth this wine shows black fruit, spice and toasty notes, and with cellaring it develops aromas of forest floor, leather and truffle. It is a red designed to age for many years and is best appreciated after decanting.

Tasting profile

Dark ruby. Aromas of black cherry, blackberry, spice and toasty notes, with leather and truffle with age. Powerful palate with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Game birds, duck, red meat in sauce, mushrooms

Serving

16-18 °C, Burgundy glass; decant
