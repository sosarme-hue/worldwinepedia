Domaine du Mont d'Or

Petite Arvine

WhiteAOC Valais Valais

A dry Petite Arvine from the Mont d’Or terraces near Sion, a classic expression of the Valais’s great native variety.

This wine comes from the vines Domaine du Mont d’Or grows on the terraced slopes of the Mont d’Or at Pont-de-la-Morge, in the municipality of Sion. There, on south-facing terraces held up by dry-stone walls, Petite Arvine finds the heat and light this late-ripening variety needs. Harvesting is by hand, as the slope demands.

The house makes both dry and sweet wines from this grape; this dry version aims to show the variety’s character: lively acidity, a savoury texture and a characteristic salty finish. The winemaking is geared to preserving fruit and freshness, without prominent oak. It is a good example of the style of the Valais mountain whites, in which the ripeness of sunny slopes is balanced by the freshness of the Alpine climate. It can be drunk young but usually gains complexity after a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of grapefruit, rhubarb, citrus and white flowers. Lively and savoury on the palate, with a long, salty finish.

Food pairing

Lake fish, shellfish, mountain cheese, herb-based dishes

Serving

8-10 °C, white-wine glass
