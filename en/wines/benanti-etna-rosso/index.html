Benanti Etna Rosso

RedEtna Rosso DOCControlled Designation of Origin (DOC) Etna

Benanti’s Etna Rosso based on Nerello Mascalese with Nerello Cappuccio, a fresh, aromatic, medium-bodied volcanic red.

The Etna Rosso DOC covers the northern, eastern and southern slopes of the volcano, with vineyards that often lie above 500 metres on sandy, mineral-rich volcanic soils. The climate, Mediterranean but mountainous, combines intense sunshine with cool nights and more rainfall than the rest of Sicily.

Benanti’s Etna Rosso is made from Nerello Mascalese, the volcano’s great red grape, completed with a small share of Nerello Cappuccio, which adds colour and softness. The grapes come from vineyards on different sides of Etna. After fermentation the wine is aged in wood and then in bottle.

It is a relatively pale red whose profile is often compared to Pinot Noir or Nebbiolo: red fruit, floral and spicy notes, lively acidity and fine tannins. It is a good introduction to the wines of Etna.

Tasting profile

Bright, light ruby. Aromas of cherry, raspberry, rose petals and spice, with a smoky, mineral touch. Medium-bodied on the palate, with lively acidity, fine tannins and a fresh finish.

Food pairing

Grilled tuna or swordfish, pasta with mushrooms, white meat, cured meats, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

15-17 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
