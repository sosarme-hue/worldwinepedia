Champagne Billecart-Salmon

Billecart-Salmon Cuvée Elisabeth Salmon Brut Rosé

SparklingBrut RoséAOC (sparkling wine) Champagne

Vintage rosé Champagne and prestige cuvée of Billecart-Salmon, named after the house’s co-founder.

Billecart-Salmon is a family house in Mareuil-sur-Aÿ founded in 1818 by Nicolas François Billecart and his wife Elisabeth Salmon. Its fresh, elegant style is associated with cold settling of the musts and long, low-temperature fermentations, which preserve purity of fruit and finesse of the bubbles.

The Cuvée Elisabeth Salmon is the prestige rosé of a house known for the quality of its rosé Champagnes. It is made only in selected years from Pinot Noir and Chardonnay from top vineyards, and spends a long time on its lees before release.

Its style combines the house’s characteristic delicacy with the depth brought by the vintage and time in bottle. It is a Champagne for keeping, suited both to special occasions and to the table.

Tasting profile

Pale pink. Wild strawberry, raspberry, citrus and brioche notes. Delicate, precise palate with very fine bubbles and a long finish.

Food pairing

Salmon, carpaccio, Japanese cuisine, duck, red-berry desserts that are not too sweet

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass rather than a flute
