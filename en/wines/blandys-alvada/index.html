Blandy’s Alvada 5 Year Old

Fortified5 Year Old, RichProtected Designation of Origin (PDO) Madeira

Five-year-old sweet Madeira from Blandy’s blending Bual and Malmsey, presented with a modern image to bring Madeira to new drinkers.

Alvada is a Madeira from Blandy’s that departs from the island’s custom of bottling the noble varieties separately: it is a blend of two of them, Bual and Malmsey, which give the sweetest styles. It is presented with a modern label and bottle, designed to appeal to people unfamiliar with traditional Madeira.

Like all Madeiras, it is made by stopping fermentation with grape spirit at the point when the wine retains the desired sweetness. It then ages in oak casks in the house’s lodges in Funchal, where the island’s warmth encourages slow oxidative development. The five-year statement indicates the average age and character of the blend. Bual contributes notes of nuts and caramel, and Malmsey richness and aromas of candied fruit; both keep the lively acidity typical of the island.

Once opened, like all Madeiras, it stays in good condition for a long time.

Tasting profile

Golden amber. Aromas of caramel, raisins, walnut, candied orange peel and sweet spice. Sweet, silky palate with a lively acidity that brings freshness, and a long finish.

Food pairing

Nut tart, honey cake, dark chocolate, aged cheeses, on its own with coffee

Serving

14-16 °C, fortified or white wine glass
