Marqués de Murrieta

Marqués de Murrieta Reserva

RedReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Reserva from the Ygay estate, Marqués de Murrieta’s most widely available red, classically styled and barrel-aged.

Marqués de Murrieta Reserva comes entirely from the Ygay estate, southeast of Logroño, where the winery’s vineyards are concentrated. It is made mainly from Tempranillo, completed with Grenache, Mazuelo (Carignan) and Graciano.

After fermentation, the wine is aged in American oak barrels and then rests in bottle at the winery before release, in line with the periods required for the Reserva category.

It is a classically styled Rioja, with ripe fruit, ageing notes and a structure that allows it to evolve for years in bottle. It makes a good introduction to the style of one of the region’s oldest houses.

Sourcing all the wine from a single estate is unusual among Rioja’s great historic houses, which traditionally bought grapes from different areas. In Murrieta’s case, the Ygay estate allows the winery to control the whole process from the vineyard onwards.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of ripe black and red fruit, spice, vanilla and toasty notes. Broad on the palate, with ripe tannins, good acidity and a long finish.

Food pairing

Red meat, lamb, game, stews, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
