Chateau Ste. Michelle

Eroica Riesling

WhiteAVA Columbia Valley

A Columbia Valley Riesling created in the late 1990s by Chateau Ste. Michelle together with Ernst Loosen of the Dr. Loosen estate in the Mosel.

Eroica was born of the collaboration between Chateau Ste. Michelle and the German producer Ernst Loosen, head of the Dr. Loosen estate in the Mosel. The aim was to make a benchmark Riesling in Washington State that would combine the brightness and ripeness of Columbia Valley fruit with the tension and finesse of great German Rieslings. The name pays tribute to Beethoven’s Third Symphony.

The grapes come from vineyards in cool parts of the Columbia Valley, chosen for their ability to retain acidity. Fermentation takes place at low temperature in stainless steel tanks to preserve the aromas, and the wine usually keeps a little residual sugar, balanced by marked acidity. It is an aromatic, vibrant Riesling to enjoy young, but it can develop more complex notes with a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow with green tints. Aromas of lime, white peach, apricot and flowers. Vibrant on the palate, with a touch of sweetness balanced by lively acidity.

Food pairing

Spiced Asian dishes, shellfish, white fish, pork, fresh cheeses

Serving

8-10 °C, white wine glass
