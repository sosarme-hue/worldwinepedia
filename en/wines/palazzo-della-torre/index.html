Allegrini

Palazzo della Torre

RedVeronese IGTWine region Veneto

Allegrini red based on Corvina in which part of the grapes is dried and added to the wine in a second fermentation, halfway between a Valpolicella and an Amarone.

Palazzo della Torre takes its name from the vineyard beside Villa Della Torre, the Renaissance palace in Fumane linked to the Allegrini family. It is one of the winery’s best-known wines and a good example of the local reds that draw on the Amarone method without reaching its concentration.

It is made mainly from Corvina Veronese, together with Rondinella and a small share of Sangiovese. Most of the grapes are vinified right after harvest; the rest are left to dry for several weeks and, once raisined, are added to the wine, which then starts a second fermentation. This technique adds body, colour and ripe-fruit aromas.

After barrel ageing, the result is a round, fruity red with some of Amarone’s richness but lighter and easier to pair with food.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of ripe cherry, plum and dried red fruit, with notes of spice and vanilla. Round and smooth on the palate, with gentle tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Pasta with meat sauces, roast meat, polenta with stew, semi-aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
