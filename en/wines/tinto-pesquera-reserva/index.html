Tinto Pesquera (Familia Fernández Rivera)

Tinto Pesquera Reserva

RedReservaDesignation of Origin (DO) Ribera del Duero

Tempranillo reserva from Pesquera, with longer ageing and more structure than the Crianza, made in selected vintages.

Tinto Pesquera Reserva, like the rest of the range, is made exclusively from Tempranillo grown around Pesquera de Duero, selecting the most concentrated grapes in suitable vintages.

The Reserva category in Ribera del Duero requires a minimum of thirty-six months of ageing, at least twelve of them in barrel. The wine is aged in oak, mostly American, and then rests in bottle before release.

The result is a classic, structured Ribera red, with more complexity and ageing notes than the Crianza and the capacity to evolve for several years in bottle.

The vineyards around Pesquera de Duero lie in the central part of the appellation, at high altitude and with a continental climate of cold winters, hot summers and sharp day-night contrasts during ripening, conditions that favour deeply coloured reds with good acidity.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby with a garnet rim. Aromas of ripe black fruit, spice, leather, tobacco and toast. Broad and structured on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Roast suckling lamb, red meat, game, aged sheep’s cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass; decanting is advisable
