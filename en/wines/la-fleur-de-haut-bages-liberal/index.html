Château Haut-Bages Libéral

La Fleur de Haut-Bages Libéral

RedSecond wineAOC (communal appellation of the Haut-Médoc, Bordeaux) Pauillac

The second wine of Château Haut-Bages Libéral, a Pauillac of Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot in a fruitier, more approachable style.

La Fleur de Haut-Bages Libéral is the second wine of Château Haut-Bages Libéral, a Pauillac fifth growth. It is made from estate grapes not selected for the grand vin, often from younger vines, from the property’s plots farmed according to organic and biodynamic principles.

Like the main wine, it combines Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, although Merlot usually plays a somewhat larger role here, giving it more roundness. Barrel ageing is shorter and less marked by new oak, with the aim of preserving the fruit.

It is a Pauillac of juicy dark fruit, with the cedar and graphite notes typical of the appellation, but with softer tannins and a gentler texture than the grand vin. It can be enjoyed after a few years in bottle and offers a good introduction to the house style.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of blackcurrant, cherry, cedar and a touch of graphite. Juicy palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Grilled red meat, lamb, charcuterie, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
