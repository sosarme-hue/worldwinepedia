Comtesse de Chérisey

Comtesse de Chérisey Meursault Bois de Blagny

WhiteAOC Meursault / Côte-d'Or

Meursault from Domaine Comtesse de Chérisey sourced from the Bois de Blagny sector, on the upper slopes of the Côte de Beaune, aged in barrel.

Domaine Comtesse de Chérisey is a small family property based in Blagny, a hamlet high up in the Côte de Beaune between Meursault and Puligny-Montrachet. The family farms vineyards in both villages, many of them on the upper slopes around the hamlet, where the greater altitude and exposure give wines of a taut, mineral profile.

This wine comes from the Bois de Blagny sector, in the upper part of the Meursault commune, near the woods that crown the slope. It is a cooler, airier area than the heart of the appellation, with shallow limestone soils, giving slowly ripening Chardonnay with good acidity. The wine is sold under the village Meursault appellation.

It is made from Chardonnay, fermented and aged in oak barrels with a discreet proportion of new wood. The result is a Meursault of a tauter, more direct style than those from the lower areas, in which the creamy texture typical of the appellation is balanced by marked freshness. At 13% ABV, it can be drunk in its first years or kept a few more years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of apple, pear, citrus and nuts, with a touch of butter and toast. Fresh and balanced on the palate, with a smooth texture and a clean finish.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, shellfish, farmyard poultry, veal in cream sauce, soft cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, large Burgundy-style glass
