Familia Torres

Grans Muralles

RedDesignation of Origin (DO) Conca de Barberà

Estate red from Familia Torres in the Conca de Barberà, beside the walls of Poblet monastery, including ancestral Catalan varieties recovered by the family.

Grans Muralles is one of Familia Torres' most ambitious reds. Its name, “great walls”, refers to the walls of the monastery of Poblet, beside which the vineyard lies, in the Conca de Barberà D.O. The Cistercian monks of Poblet grew vines on this land for centuries.

The wine is a blend of traditional Mediterranean varieties —Garnacha, Cariñena (known locally as Samsó) and Monastrell— together with ancestral Catalan varieties such as Garró and Querol, which had almost disappeared after phylloxera and were recovered by the family's research programme. The proportions vary with the vintage. The grapes grow on stony slate soils and are vinified separately before ageing in oak barrels. It is a structured, concentrated red with a spicy character, designed for long ageing.

Tasting profile

Very deep cherry red. Aromas of black fruit, spice, Mediterranean herbs, mineral and oak notes. Powerful on the palate, with firm tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Game, roast lamb, braised meats, aged cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, decanted, large glass
