Elena Walch

Elena Walch Lagrein

RedAlto Adige DOCWine region Trentino-Alto Adige

Alto Adige Lagrein from Elena Walch, a red from the region’s native variety, deep in colour, with dark fruit and soft tannins.

Lagrein is a native red variety of Alto Adige, grown mainly around Bolzano and in the Adige valley. It is related to other Alpine varieties and gives wines of very deep colour, dark fruit and fresh acidity, with tannins that can be firm in youth.

Elena Walch, a family winery in Tramin, makes it in its classic line from vineyards in southern Alto Adige, where the warmer temperatures of the valley floor help this late-ripening variety to mature. Winemaking aims for an approachable red, with ageing that rounds out the tannins without stripping the fruit.

The result is a medium-bodied red of deep violet colour, with aromas of blackberry, plum, violet and notes of cocoa, and a juicy, smooth palate. It is enjoyed in its first few years and is a good example of the region’s red identity.

Tasting profile

Deep violet red. Aromas of blackberry, plum, violet and cocoa. Juicy, fresh, smooth palate with rounded tannins.

Food pairing

Speck, charcuterie, goulash, grilled meat, mountain cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, medium-sized glass
