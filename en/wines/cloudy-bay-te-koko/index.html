Cloudy Bay

Cloudy Bay Te Koko

WhiteGeographical Indication (GI) Marlborough

Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc fermented with indigenous yeasts and aged in barrel, a more complex, textured version of the regional style.

Te Koko takes its name from Te Koko-o-Kupe, the Māori name for the bay that also gives the winery its name, associated with the legend of the navigator Kupe. Cloudy Bay created it as a different interpretation of Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc, far from the aromatic, direct style that had made the house famous.

The grapes, selected from the winery’s vineyards, are fermented with naturally occurring yeasts, largely in French oak barrels, most of them used. The wine undergoes full or partial malolactic fermentation and is aged on its lees for an extended period before bottling, and it is usually released somewhat later than the classic Sauvignon Blanc. The result is a white of greater texture and complexity, in which the fruit of the variety combines with notes from ageing, and which can develop for several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Ripe citrus, tropical fruit and peach, with notes of hazelnut, brioche and spice. Broad, creamy palate supported by firm acidity.

Food pairing

Fish in sauce, scallops, tarragon chicken, aged goat’s cheese

Serving

10-12 °C, large white wine glass
