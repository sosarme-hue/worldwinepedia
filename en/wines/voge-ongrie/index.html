Domaine Alain Voge

Domaine Alain Voge Saint-Péray Ongrie

WhiteSaint-PérayRhône valley subregion Northern Rhône

A single-site Saint-Péray from Domaine Alain Voge, a northern Rhône white with a broad texture and good freshness.

The Saint-Péray appellation, at the southern tip of the northern Rhône opposite Valence, is devoted exclusively to white wines made from Marsanne and Roussanne. Its vineyards, at the foot of the hill of Crussol, lie on varied soils alternating between granite and limestone, which allows plots of different character to be distinguished.

Ongrie is one of the site-specific Saint-Pérays of Domaine Alain Voge, the Cornas estate that has contributed notably to the reputation of this small appellation. It comes from vineyards farmed along organic and biodynamic lines and is made with ageing designed to bring texture and complexity while preserving the tension of the soil.

It is a medium- to full-bodied white with ripe white fruit, floral and spicy notes and a mineral finish. It can be enjoyed young, although it benefits from a few years of cellaring.

Tasting profile

Pale gold. Aromas of ripe white fruit, blossom, gentle spice and mineral notes. Broad yet taut palate with a long finish.

Food pairing

Fine fish in sauce, poultry, mushroom risotto, aged cheeses

Serving

12-14 °C, large white wine glass
