Monzinger Halenberg Riesling GG

WhiteGrosses GewächsAnbaugebiet (specified wine region) Nahe

Riesling Grosses Gewächs from Emrich-Schönleber from the Halenberg, a steep slope of blue slate and quartzite in Monzingen.

The Halenberg is the most highly regarded vineyard in Monzingen and one of the great sites of the Nahe. It is a very steep south-facing slope with stony, shallow soils of blue slate and quartzite, which warm up quickly and force the vines to root deeply.

The Halenberg Grosses Gewächs is Emrich-Schönleber’s top dry Riesling. It is made exclusively from Riesling, from a selection of the best parcels and hand-picked grapes. Fermentation is slow, in tank and in large casks, and the wine rests on its lees before bottling, in line with the VDP rules for wines in this category.

It is a dry Riesling of great precision and tension, in which the slate is expressed in notes often described as smoky or mineral. Its firm acidity gives it good ageing potential, and it usually gains complexity after a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Pale yellow. Aromas of citrus, peach, herbs and smoky, slaty notes. Dry, taut and precise palate with a long, saline finish.

Food pairing

Fine fish, roast poultry, veal, hard cheeses

Serving

11-13 °C, large white wine glass
