Maison Louis Jadot

Domaine Louis Jadot Gevrey-Chambertin 1er Cru Estournelles-Saint-Jacques

RedPremier CruBurgundy subregion (Côte d'Or) Côte de Nuits

Gevrey-Chambertin premier cru on the upper Côte Saint-Jacques slope, made by Louis Jadot from Pinot Noir from its own vines.

Estournelles-Saint-Jacques is a premier cru of Gevrey-Chambertin lying west of the village on the slope known as the Côte Saint-Jacques, next to Lavaut-Saint-Jacques and the famous Clos Saint-Jacques. It is one of the highest sites in the appellation, with shallow limestone soils and an exposure that favours slow ripening.

The word “Domaine” indicates that the wine comes from vines owned by Louis Jadot rather than from bought-in grapes. It is made from Pinot Noir and aged in oak barrels, with a proportion of new wood adjusted to each vintage.

Because of the altitude and coolness of the site, Estournelles usually shows a tauter, more mineral profile than other Gevrey premiers crus, with red and black fruit, lively acidity and fine but firm tannins. It needs a few years in bottle to show its full character.

Tasting profile

Deep ruby. Aromas of cherry, black fruit, violet and mineral and spicy notes. Taut palate with lively acidity, fine tannins and a long finish.

Food pairing

Game birds, beef fillet, roast duck, aged cheeses

Serving

16-17 °C, Burgundy glass; may be decanted
