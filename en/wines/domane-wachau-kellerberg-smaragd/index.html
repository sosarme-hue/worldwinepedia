Domäne Wachau

Grüner Veltliner Smaragd Ried Kellerberg

WhiteSmaragd (Vinea Wachau)DAC (Districtus Austriae Controllatus) Wachau

Grüner Veltliner Smaragd from Domäne Wachau from the Kellerberg vineyard in Dürnstein, a dry, full-bodied white with strong expression of its site.

Domäne Wachau is a cooperative based in Dürnstein that brings together many growers in the Wachau, the narrow Danube valley in Lower Austria whose dry-stone terraced vineyards are part of a cultural landscape listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Kellerberg is one of the most highly regarded vineyards (Ried) in the Dürnstein area, with steep terraces on primary rock soils. This Grüner Veltliner comes from there and is bottled in Vinea Wachau’s Smaragd category, the ripest level, covering dry wines with at least 12.5% alcohol.

It is a full-bodied white with a broad texture, in which ripe fruit and the variety’s characteristic white pepper note are balanced by firm acidity and a mineral core. It can be enjoyed young but gains complexity over several years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Golden yellow with green tints. Aromas of stone fruit, citrus, white pepper and herbs. Broad, firm and mineral palate with a long finish.

Food pairing

Wiener schnitzel, roast pork, freshwater fish, Asian cuisine, asparagus

Serving

10-12 °C, white wine glass
