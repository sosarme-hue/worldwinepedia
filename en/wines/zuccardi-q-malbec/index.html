Familia Zuccardi

Zuccardi Q Malbec

RedWine region Valle de Uco

Uco Valley Malbec from Familia Zuccardi’s Q line, made from grapes from different parts of the valley and aged in oak.

The Q line is one of Familia Zuccardi’s historic ranges, and its Malbec has followed the winery’s evolution from a riper, more oak-marked style towards a fresher one focused on origin. Today the grapes come from vineyards in the Uco Valley, from different areas, whose differences in altitude and soil are combined in the blend.

The plots are vinified separately and the wine is aged in oak before blending. The aim is a Malbec reflecting the general character of the valley: deep colour, black and red fruit, floral notes and an acidity that gives it freshness. It is a well-structured red, more ambitious than entry-level varietals, enjoyable in its first few years and able to keep for some time.

It is a good starting point for getting to know the house’s current style.

Tasting profile

Deep violet-red. Plum, blackberry, violet and gentle spice. Fresh, balanced palate with firm tannins and a fruity finish.

Food pairing

Argentine asado, grilled red meat, beef empanadas, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

16-18 °C, large glass
