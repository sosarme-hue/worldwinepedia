Clos Henri Vineyard

Clos Henri Estate Pinot Noir

RedMarlboroughGeographical Indication (GI) Marlborough

Clos Henri’s estate Pinot Noir from Marlborough, a fresh, fruity red made with a Burgundian sensibility.

The Estate Pinot Noir is the main red of Clos Henri, the estate created by the Bourgeois family of Sancerre in the Wairau Valley, Marlborough. It comes from several plots on the property and offers an overall view of its Pinot Noir.

Although Marlborough is best known for Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir is the region’s most widely planted red variety. The sunny but cool climate, with large differences between day and night temperatures, favours wines with clear-cut fruit and lively acidity. At Clos Henri, the more clay-rich soils add body, while the stony areas bring finesse.

Winemaking aims for gentle extraction, and ageing in French oak barrels, mostly used, respects the fruit. The result is a medium-bodied Pinot Noir with red-berry aromas and a spicy touch, enjoyable young and able to improve for a few years in bottle.

Tasting profile

Light ruby. Aromas of cherry, raspberry, gentle spice and an earthy hint. Light- to medium-bodied, fresh palate with silky tannins.

Food pairing

Roast poultry, duck, salmon, mushrooms

Serving

14-16 °C, Burgundy glass
