Viña Monty Garnacha Reserva

RedReservaQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Viña Monty Garnacha Reserva is a red wine from Montecillo, made under the DOCa Rioja appellation in Spain.

It is made from Grenache. It carries the Reserva designation. It has 13.5% ABV.

Rioja, in the Ebro valley, became Spain's first Qualified Designation of Origin in 1991. It is best known for its Tempranillo-based reds and its long tradition of ageing in oak barrels.

Food pairing

Red meat, roasts, stews, aged cheeses

Serving
