Crozes-Hermitage Les Jalets Rouge

RedRhône valley subregion Northern Rhône

Red Crozes-Hermitage from Paul Jaboulet Aîné, a fruity, spicy Syrah intended to be enjoyed young.

Crozes-Hermitage, the largest appellation in the northern Rhône, surrounds the hill of Hermitage on the left bank of the river. It combines granite slopes in the north with alluvial terraces covered in rounded pebbles in the south, and its reds are made from Syrah.

Les Jalets is Paul Jaboulet Aîné’s entry-level range in this appellation, below its well-known Domaine de Thalabert. With this wine, the house, founded in 1834, seeks a direct, fruity expression of Syrah, with ageing that does not overshadow the fruit.

It is a medium-bodied red, with aromas of red and black fruit, notes of pepper and violet, and soft tannins. It offers a good introduction to the northern Rhône style and is best enjoyed in its first few years, while it keeps all its freshness. Its versatility makes it well suited to varied everyday cooking.

Tasting profile

Purple-tinged ruby. Aromas of raspberry, blackberry, pepper and violet. Juicy, medium-bodied palate with soft tannins.

Food pairing

Charcuterie, burgers, grilled meat, pasta with ragù, semi-hard cheeses

Serving

14-16 °C, red wine glass
