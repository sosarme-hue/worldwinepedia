Compañía de Vinos Telmo Rodríguez

LZ

RedQualified Designation of Origin (DOCa) Rioja

Young red from Telmo Rodríguez in Rioja Alavesa, made from Tempranillo, Garnacha and Graciano from vineyards around Lanciego, in a fresh, fruity style.

LZ is the entry-level wine of the Lanzaga range, the project of the Compañía de Vinos Telmo Rodríguez in Rioja Alavesa. Its name is an abbreviation of Lanzaga, and it comes from vineyards around Lanciego, a village at the foot of the Sierra de Cantabria where the company farms vines organically.

It is made from a blend of Tempranillo, Garnacha and Graciano, the area’s traditional varieties, whose proportions vary with the vintage. Winemaking seeks fruit and freshness: fermentation in tank and brief or no ageing in wood, so that the wine keeps the character of the grape.

LZ reflects the idea, championed by Telmo Rodríguez, that a simple wine can clearly express its origin. It is a light, juicy red designed to be drunk young and slightly cool, very different from long barrel-aged Rioja.

Tasting profile

Bright cherry red. Aromas of fresh red fruit such as strawberry and cherry, with floral notes. Light, juicy and fresh on the palate.

Food pairing

Tapas, charcuterie, grilled meat, meat rice dishes, pasta

Serving

14-16 °C, red wine glass
